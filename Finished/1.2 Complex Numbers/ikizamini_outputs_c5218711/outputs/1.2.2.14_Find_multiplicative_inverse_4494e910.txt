Problem 1 (Inverse1):
What is the multiplicative inverse of 7?
A) 0.14
B) 1.42857
C) 7
D) 1/7
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The multiplicative inverse of a number x is 1/x. So, the inverse of 7 is 1/7.

Explanation:
To find the multiplicative inverse, divide 1 by the given number.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose option A (0.14), which is 7 divided by 50, instead of dividing 1 by 7.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Inverse2):
What is the multiplicative inverse of 0.2?
A) 5
B) 0.5
C) 2
D) 5/1
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The multiplicative inverse of a number x (in decimal form) is 1/x. So, the inverse of 0.2 is 1/0.2 = 5.

Explanation:
To find the multiplicative inverse, divide 1 by the given decimal number.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose option C (2), which is 1 divided by 0.2, instead of dividing 1 by 0.2.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Inverse3):
What is the multiplicative inverse of -4?
A) -1/4
B) -4
C) 1/4
D) 4
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The multiplicative inverse of a negative number x is -1/x. So, the inverse of -4 is -1/-4 = 1/4.

Explanation:
To find the multiplicative inverse of a negative number, divide -1 by the given number.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose option D (4), which is the absolute value of -4, instead of dividing -1 by -4.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Inverse4):
What is the multiplicative inverse of 2/3?
A) 3/2
B) 2/5
C) 1/2
D) 3
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The multiplicative inverse of a fraction x/y is y/x. So, the inverse of 2/3 is 3/2.

Explanation:
To find the multiplicative inverse of a fraction, swap the numerator and denominator.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose option C (1/2), which is the reciprocal of the numerator 2, instead of swapping the numerator and denominator.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (Inverse5):
What is the multiplicative inverse of -3/4?
A) -4/3
B) 3/4
C) 4/3
D) 1
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The multiplicative inverse of a negative fraction x/y is -(y/x). So, the inverse of -3/4 is -(4/3) = -4/3.

Explanation:
To find the multiplicative inverse of a negative fraction, change the sign and swap the numerator and denominator.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose option B (3/4), which is the reciprocal of the positive fraction 3/4, instead of considering the negative sign.

Problem 6 (Inverse6):
What is the multiplicative inverse of 1.5?
A) 0.66667
B) 2/3
C) 1/3
D) 2
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The multiplicative inverse of a decimal number x is 1/x in its simplest form. So, the inverse of 1.5 (which is 3/2) is 2/3.

Explanation:
To find the multiplicative inverse of a decimal, convert it to a fraction and simplify if necessary.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose option A (0.66667), which is the decimal form of 2/3, instead of converting 1.5 to its simplest fraction form.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Inverse7):
What is the multiplicative inverse of 0?
A) No inverse exists
B) Indeterminate
C) 0
D) 1
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The multiplicative inverse of 0 does not exist, as division by zero is undefined.

Explanation:
Zero has no multiplicative inverse because dividing any number by zero is mathematically undefined.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose option C (0), which is not the multiplicative inverse of any number.
