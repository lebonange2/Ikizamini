Problem 1 (AsdFgHjK):
Given the complex number z = 3 + 4i, what is its complex conjugate?
A) 2 - 4i
B) 3 - 4i
C) 4 + 3i
D) 5 + 0i
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The complex conjugate of a complex number a + bi is a - bi. For z = 3 + 4i, the complex conjugate is 3 - 4i.

Explanation:
Complex conjugates are formed by changing the sign of the imaginary part while keeping the real part unchanged.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to swap the real and imaginary parts instead of just negating the imaginary part.

Problem 2 (ZxcVbNmL):
What is the product of a complex number z = 2 + 3i and its conjugate?
A) -1 - 6i
B) 13
C) 7 + 6i
D) 7 - 6i
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
(2 + 3i)(2 - 3i) = 4 - (3i)^2 = 4 - (-9) = 13.

Explanation:
The product of a complex number and its conjugate results in the square of the real part minus the square of the imaginary part.

Common mistake note:
Students might forget to square the imaginary part or might accidentally multiply the real parts with the imaginary parts.

Problem 3 (QweRtYui):
If the complex number z = a + bi and its conjugate are plotted on the complex plane, what shape do they form?
A) Line segment
B) Circle
C) Parabola
D) Ellipse
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The two points (a, b) and (a, -b) form a line segment when plotted on the complex plane.

Explanation:
Complex conjugates are reflections of each other across the real axis; thus, they lie on a straight line.

Common mistake note:
Students might confuse the shape with other geometric figures formed by different combinations of complex numbers.

Problem 4 (BvnMjHgl):
Given the equation z^2 + 4 = 0, what are the solutions in terms of their complex conjugates?
A) -2i and 2i
B) 2 - 2i and 2 + 2i
C) 1 - 2i and 1 + 2i
D) 4i and -4i
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(z + 2i)(z - 2i) = 0, so z = -2i or z = 2i. These are complex conjugates.

Explanation:
The quadratic equation can be factored using difference of squares, revealing the complex conjugate solutions.

Common mistake note:
Students might forget to consider complex roots or incorrectly factor the equation.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (XcvBnMkl):
If a complex number z = 1 + 2i is graphed on the complex plane, what is the distance between z and its conjugate?
A) 1
B) sqrt(2)
C) 2
D) sqrt(5)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The distance between (1, 2) and (1, -2) is the length of the vertical line segment, which is sqrt((0)^2 + (-2 - 2)^2) = sqrt(4^2) = 2.

Explanation:
Distance between two points on a coordinate plane can be calculated using the distance formula. Here, only the imaginary part changes.

Common mistake note:
Students might calculate the distance horizontally instead of vertically or forget to square and take the square root.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (PoIuYtrew):
The complex numbers z = 5 + 3i and w = 5 - 3i are plotted on the complex plane. What is the angle between the line connecting the origin to z and the line connecting the origin to w?
A) 0°
B) 45°
C) 90°
D) 180°
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The lines form a right angle because they are complex conjugates and their imaginary parts have opposite signs.

Explanation:
Complex conjugate pairs create lines that intersect at right angles with the real axis.

Common mistake note:
Students might confuse the angle between the lines with the angle between z and w, which would be 0°.

Problem 7 (QweRtYuiO):
The complex number z = -2 + 3i is reflected across the real axis to form its conjugate. What is the new imaginary part of the conjugate?
A) -3i
B) 3i
C) 5i
D) 0
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The reflection changes the sign of the imaginary part, so the new imaginary part is -3i.

Explanation:
Reflecting a complex number across the real axis negates its imaginary part.

Common mistake note:
Students might forget to change the sign of the imaginary part or apply the reflection incorrectly.
