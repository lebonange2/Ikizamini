Problem 1 (:1):
Simplify the expression (3x + 4)(2x - 5):
A) (6x^2 - 7x - 20)
B) (6x^2 + 7x - 20)
C) (6x^2 + 13x - 20)
D) (6x^2 - 13x - 20)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To simplify the expression (3x + 4)(2x - 5), use the distributive property of multiplication over addition and subtraction:

(3x * 2x) + (3x * -5) + (4 * 2x) + (4 * -5)
6x^2 - 15x + 8x - 20
Combine like terms: 6x^2 - 7x - 20

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by multiplying each term in the first polynomial with each term in the second polynomial and then combining like terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to distribute one of the terms or to incorrectly multiply the terms. For example, some students might mistakenly multiply (3x + 4) as a single unit with (2x - 5), leading to an incorrect result.

Problem 4 (4):
Simplify (5x - 2)(3x + 1) - (2x + 3)(x - 4):
A) (15x^2 - 7x - 2) - (2x^2 - 5x - 12)
B) (15x^2 - 7x - 2) - (2x^2 + 5x - 12)
C) (15x^2 + 7x - 2) - (2x^2 - 5x - 12)
D) (15x^2 + 7x - 2) - (2x^2 + 5x + 12)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
First, multiply each pair of polynomials:

(5x * 3x) + (5x * 1) - (2 * 3x) - (2 * 1)
15x^2 + 5x - 6x - 2
Combine like terms: 15x^2 - x - 2
Next, multiply the second pair of polynomials:

(2x * x) + (2x * -4) + (3 * x) + (3 * -4)
2x^2 - 8x + 3x - 12
Combine like terms: 2x^2 - 5x - 12
Now, subtract the second polynomial from the first:

(15x^2 - x - 2) - (2x^2 - 5x - 12)
15x^2 - x - 2 - 2x^2 + 5x + 12

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by multiplying each pair of polynomials, combining like terms, and then subtracting one polynomial from the other.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to distribute or to incorrectly multiply the terms. Students might also make errors when combining like terms or performing subtraction.

Problem 5 (5):
Factor the expression 12x^2 - 36x + 24:
A) (6x - 4)(2x - 3)
B) (6x - 8)(2x - 3)
C) (6x - 4)(2x + 3)
D) (6x - 8)(2x + 3)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To factor the expression, find two numbers that multiply to give 12x^2 * 24 = 288 and add up to give -36x. These numbers are -4 and -8. So, rewrite the expression as:

12x^2 - 4x - 8x + 24
Factor by grouping:
(12x^2 - 4x) + (-8x + 24)
4x(3x - 1) - 8(3x - 1)
Now, factor out the common binomial (3x - 1):
(6x - 4)(2x - 3)

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by factoring the expression using grouping and identifying the common factors.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to choose incorrect numbers for factorization or to forget to distribute the negative sign when factoring. Some students might also make errors in combining like terms during the process.

Problem 6 (6):
Simplify (x + 3)^2:
A) x^2 + 9
B) x^2 + 6x + 9
C) x^2 + 6x - 9
D) x^2 - 6x + 9
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To simplify (x + 3)^2, square the binomial by multiplying it by itself:

(x + 3)(x + 3)
x * x + x * 3 + 3 * x + 3 * 3
x^2 + 3x + 3x + 9
Combine like terms: x^2 + 6x + 9

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by squaring the binomial and then combining like terms.

Common mistake note:

Problem 7 (7):
Factor the expression 9x^2 - 6xy + y^2:
A) (3x - y)^2
B) (3x + y)^2
C) (9x - y)^2
D) (9x + y)^2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The expression is a perfect square trinomial, which can be factored as (3x - y)^2. This form represents the square of the binomial 3x - y.

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by recognizing that the given expression is a perfect square trinomial and applying the identity (a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2 with a = 3x and b = y.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to misidentify the terms or not recognize the pattern of a perfect square trinomial. Some students might also attempt to factor it as (3x + y)(3x - y), which would be incorrect for this specific problem.
