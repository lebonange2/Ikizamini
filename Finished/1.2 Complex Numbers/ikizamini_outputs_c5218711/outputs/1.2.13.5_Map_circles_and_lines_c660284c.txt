Problem 1 (:1):
Which of the following represents a circle on a map?
A) A straight line with many dots along it
B) A squiggly line that goes around an area
C) A closed shape with curved sides and no corners
D) A triangle with three equal sides
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
A circle on a map is represented by a closed shape with curved sides and no corners. This allows for clear visualization of a round object or area on a flat surface.

Explanation:
Option A represents a dotted line, which usually indicates a path or route. Option B represents a squiggly boundary line, and option D represents an equilateral triangle, neither of which are circles.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B, as some students might confuse it with the border around an area on a map.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (2):
What symbol is used to indicate a river on a topographic map?
A) A solid blue line
B) A dashed green line
C) A dotted black line
D) A thick brown line
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
On a topographic map, a solid blue line is used to represent a river or stream.

Explanation:
Option B represents forests or shrublands, option C indicates roads or paths, and option D typically denotes major highways or boundaries. A solid blue line specifically indicates water bodies like rivers.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing D, as some students might associate brown with a natural feature but forget that it usually refers to major roads.

Problem 4 (4):
Which symbol indicates a city on a road map?
A) A small circle with a dot inside
B) A star- shaped symbol
C) A square with a letter in it
D) A triangle with an arrow pointing towards it
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
On a road map, a square with a letter or number inside is used to represent a city.

Explanation:
Option A represents a town or village, option B might be used for landmarks like tourist attractions, and option D usually indicates a direction or exit. A square with an identifier is specific to cities.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A, as some students might think that a small circle represents a city but forget the dot inside.

Problem 5 (5):
What does a dashed black line on a nautical chart represent?
A) Shallow water
B) Deep water
C) Navigation buoys
D) Underwater
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
A dashed black line on a nautical chart indicates the location of navigation buoys or markers.

Explanation:
Option A represents areas with less than 10 meters of water depth, option B shows deeper waters, and option D is not a standard symbol. Dashed black lines are used for navigational aids.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or B, as some students might混淆浅水和深水的符号，而忽略了导航浮标的重要性。

Problem 6 (6):

Explanation:
Option A refers to a solid double yellow line, option B represents a solid white or yellow line, and option C usually has a solid yellow line with dashes. Dotted yellow lines allow for limited use of the shoulder when safe.
