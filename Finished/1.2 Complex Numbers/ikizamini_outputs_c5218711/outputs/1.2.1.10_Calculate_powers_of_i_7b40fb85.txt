Problem 1 (Asdf):
What is the value of i^4?
A) -1
B) 1
C) i
D) -i
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The powers of i follow a repeating pattern: i, -1, -i, 1. Since 4 is an even number, i^4 will be 1.

Explanation:
The value of i raised to an even power is always 1 because the imaginary unit cancels itself out.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be selecting A (-1) or C (i), which are correct for odd powers of i.
