Problem 1 (Asd1):
Find the conjugate of the complex number (3 + 4i):
A) (3 - 4i)
B) (-3 + 4i)
C) (-3 - 4i)
D) (4 + 3i)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The conjugate of a complex number (a + bi) is obtained by changing the sign of the imaginary part. So, the conjugate of (3 + 4i) is (3 - 4i).

Explanation:
Conjugation flips the sign of the imaginary part while keeping the real part unchanged.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to change both parts of the complex number, which results in choice B or C. Remember, only the imaginary part changes sign.

Problem 2 (Asd2):
Simplify (5 - 6i)(π + i):
A) (5π - 11i)
B) (5π + 11i)
C) (-5π - 11i)
D) (-5π + 11i)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Use the distributive property (FOIL) to expand and then apply conjugation on (i)(-π): (5 - 6i)(π + i) = 5π + 5i - 6πi - 6i^2 = 5π + 5i - 6πi + 6 = (5π - 11i)

Explanation:
FOIL stands for First, Outer, Inner, Last and is used to multiply binomials. Remember that i^2 = -1.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be forgetting to apply conjugation on (i)(-π), which could lead to choice B or C.

Problem 3 (Asd3):
Find the difference of the complex numbers (7 + 3i) - (2 - 4i):
A) (5 - 7i)
B) (9 + 7i)
C) (5 + 7i)
D) (9 - 7i)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Subtract the real parts and the imaginary parts separately: (7 + 3i) - (2 - 4i) = 7 - 2 + (3i + 4i) = 5 + 7i

Explanation:
When subtracting complex numbers, treat the real part and the imaginary part as separate entities.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to combine the terms incorrectly, which could result in choice A or D.

Problem 5 (Asd5):
Find the sum of the complex numbers (8 - 6i) + (-4 + 9i):
A) (12 + 3i)
B) (4 + 3i)
C) (4 - 3i)
D) (12 - 3i)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
(8 - 6i) + (-4 + 9i) = 8 - 4 - 6i + 9i = 4 + 3i

Explanation:
When adding complex numbers, combine the real parts and the imaginary parts separately.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to add or subtract the terms incorrectly, which could result in choice A, C, or D.

Problem 6 (Asd6):
Simplify (2 + 3i)(4 - 5i):
A) (8 + 7i)
B) (8 - 7i)
C) (14 - 7i)
D) (14 + 7i)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
(2 + 3i)(4 - 5i) = 8 - 10i + 12i - 15i^2 = 8 + 2i + 15 = (8 - 7i)

Explanation:
Use the distributive property (FOIL) to expand and remember that i^2 = -1.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to apply conjugation on (3i)(-5i), which could lead to choice A or C.

Problem 7 (Asd7):
Find the product of the complex numbers (6 + 4i) and its conjugate:
A) (20 - 24i)
B) (20 + 24i)
C) (36 - 16i)
D) (36 + 16i)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The product of a complex number (a + bi) and its conjugate is (a + bi)(a - bi) = a^2 - (bi)^2 = a^2 + b^2. For (6 + 4i), the product with its conjugate is (6 + 4i)(6 - 4i) = 36 - 16i^2 = 36 + 16 = (36 - 16i)

Explanation:
The product of a complex number and its conjugate results in the square of the real part plus the square of the imaginary part.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget that i^2 = -1, which could lead to choice A or B.
