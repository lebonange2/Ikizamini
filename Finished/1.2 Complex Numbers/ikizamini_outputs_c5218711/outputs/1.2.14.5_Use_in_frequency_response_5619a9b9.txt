Problem 1 (:1):
A system's transfer function is given by H(s) = (s + 3)/(s^2 + 4s + 5). What is the system's frequency response at a frequency of 1 rad/s?
A) -0.6
B) 0
C) 0.6
D) 1
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The frequency response H(jω) is calculated by substituting s with jω in the transfer function, where ω is the angular frequency. At 1 rad/s, ω = 1. So, H(1) = (j + 3)/(j^2 + 4j + 5). Simplifying this expression gives H(j1) = (j + 3)/(1 + 4j + 5) = (j + 3)/(6 + 4j) = 0.6 - 0.6j. The magnitude of the frequency response is |H(ω)|, which for ω = 1 rad/s is |0.6 - 0.6j| = 0.6. Therefore, the correct answer is C: 0.6.

Explanation:
The frequency response measures how a system responds to different frequencies. It's important to substitute s with jω and calculate the magnitude of the resulting complex number.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to convert the frequency from rad/s to Hz, which is not necessary in this case since the calculation is done directly in radians.

Problem 2 (2):
What does a Bode plot represent for a linear time-invariant system?
A) The phase shift vs. frequency
B) The output signal vs. input signal
C) The impulse response vs. time
D) The step response vs. time
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
A Bode plot is a graphical representation of the frequency response of a linear time-invariant system, showing the magnitude (in decibels) and phase shift (in degrees) as functions of frequency. Therefore, the correct answer is A: The phase shift vs. frequency.

Explanation:
Bode plots are useful for analyzing the stability and overall behavior of control systems at different frequencies.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to confuse Bode plots with time-domain responses like impulse or step responses.

Problem 3 (3):
In a frequency response analysis, what does a pole represent?
A) An increase in output amplitude at that frequency
B) A decrease in output amplitude at that frequency
C) A phase shift of 180 degrees at that frequency
D) The system's natural frequency
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
In the context of a transfer function, poles represent frequencies where the denominator goes to zero, causing the magnitude of the response to decrease. Therefore, the correct answer is B: A decrease in output amplitude at that frequency.

Explanation:
Poles are important for understanding the stability and dynamics of a system, as they can lead to instability if not properly compensated.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to associate poles with an increase in amplitude or phase shifts.

Problem 5 (5):
The magnitude of a system's frequency response at a particular frequency is 0 dB. What does this indicate?
A) No signal passes through the system at that frequency
B) The system amplifies the signal by a factor of 1 at that frequency
C) The system attenuates the signal by a factor of 1 at that frequency
D) The phase shift is undefined at that frequency
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
A magnitude of 0 dB in a frequency response indicates that the system neither amplifies nor attenuates the signal at that frequency; it maintains the same amplitude. Therefore, the correct answer is B: The system amplifies the signal by a factor of 1 at that frequency.

Explanation:
0 dB means the system has unity gain at that frequency, meaning it doesn't change the input amplitude.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to think that 0 dB indicates no signal passing through or an undefined phase shift.

Problem 6 (6):
What is the relationship between a system's phase margin and its stability in frequency response analysis?
A) Phase margin has no effect on stability
B) Greater phase margin means greater stability
C) Smaller phase margin means greater stability
D) Phase margin only affects overshoot in time domain
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The phase margin is a measure of how much additional phase shift the system can tolerate before it becomes unstable. A greater phase margin indicates that the system is more stable, as it has more room for phase variation without entering an instability region. Therefore, the correct answer is B: Greater phase margin means greater stability.

Explanation:
Phase margin is a crucial factor in assessing the stability of control systems under feedback.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to associate a smaller phase margin with greater stability or to混淆 it with overshoot in time domain analysis.

Problem 7 (7):
In a frequency response plot, what does a 45° phase shift at low frequencies indicate about the system?
A) The system has a dominant pole
B) The system has a dominant zero
C) The system is marginally stable
D) The system is critically damped
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
A 45° phase shift at low frequencies typically indicates the presence of a dominant pole in the system's transfer function. Poles contribute to phase shifts, and a first-order pole would result in a phase shift of -90°. Since we have a +45° phase shift, it means there is an additional 135° phase shift from other poles or zeros that compensate for the negative phase shift. Therefore, the correct answer is A: The system has a dominant pole.

Explanation:
The phase shift at low frequencies can provide insights into the system's dynamics and the presence of dominant poles.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to associate a 45° phase shift with zeros or stability classifications.
