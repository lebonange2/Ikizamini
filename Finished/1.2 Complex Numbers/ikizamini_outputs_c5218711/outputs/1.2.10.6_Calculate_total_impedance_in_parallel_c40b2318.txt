Problem 1 (:1):
Find the total impedance of two components connected in parallel: a 5 Ω resistor and a 3亨利电感.
A) 2 Ω
B) 8 Ω
C) 7.07 Ω
D) 6 Ω
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
When components are connected in parallel, the total impedance (Z_total) is found using the formula: 1/Z_total = 1/Z1 + 1/Z2. For a resistor (R) and an inductive reactance (XL), Z = R + jXL. Given Z1 = 5 Ω (resistor) and Z2 = j3亨利, we can calculate Z_total as follows: 1/Z_total = 1/5 + 1/(j3) = (1/5) - (j/3). To find the total impedance, we take the reciprocal of this complex number: Z_total = 1 / (1/5 - j/3) = 7.07 Ω.

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by applying the formula for parallel impedances and converting the inductive reactance to its imaginary form. Choice A is too low, choice B is too high, and choice D does not follow the correct calculation method.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is forgetting to convert the inductive reactance (XL) to an imaginary number (jXL) before calculating the reciprocal.

Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The total impedance in parallel for a resistor, an inductive reactance (XL), and a capacitive reactance (XC) is given by: 1/Z_total = 1/R + 1/XL - 1/XC. Taking the reciprocal gives Z_total ≈ 8.9 Ω.

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by applying the formula for parallel impedances with all three components and using the given frequency to calculate the reactances. Choice A is too low, choice C is too low, and choice D does not follow the correct calculation method.
