Problem 1 (:1):
What is the square root of (-8 + 6i)?
A) -2 + i
B) 2 - 3i
C) 4 + 3i
D) -2 - 3i
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
(a + bi)^2 = a^2 - b^2 + 2abi. For (-8 + 6i), a = -2, b = 3. So, we need to find complex numbers (x + yi) such that x^2 - y^2 = -8 and 2xy = 6. Solving these equations gives us x = -2 and y = -3. Thus, the square root is -2 - 3i.

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by solving for the square roots of the given complex number using the properties of complex numbers.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose A (-2 + i) because it seems close, but it does not satisfy the condition that its square equals the original complex number.

Problem 2 (:2):
Find the principal square root of (1 - 3i).
A) 1 - √3i
B) √3 + i
C) -√3 - i
D) √3 - i
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The principal square root is the one with a non-negative real part. For (1 - 3i), we have a = 1, b = -3. Solving for x and y gives us x = √(1/2) and y = -√(3/2). The principal square root is √3 - i.

Explanation:
The principal square root of a complex number has a non-negative real part. We solve for the values of x and y that satisfy the given complex number.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose B (√3 + i) because it is also a valid square root but not the principal one.

Problem 4 (:4):
Find the square root of (-16 + 0i).
A) -4
B) 4
C) 4i
D) -4i
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(a + bi)^2 = a^2 - b^2 + 2abi. For (-16 + 0i), a = -4, b = 0. The square root is simply -4 because there is no imaginary part to consider.

Explanation:
When the complex number has zero imaginary part, its square root will also have zero imaginary part. We only need to take the square root of the real part.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose C (4i) or D (-4i), but these are not correct because they do not satisfy the condition that their square equals the original complex number.
