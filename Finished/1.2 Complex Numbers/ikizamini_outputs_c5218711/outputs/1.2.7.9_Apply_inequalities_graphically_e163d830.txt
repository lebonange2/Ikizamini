Problem 1 (One):
Which graph represents the inequality 3x + 2y < 6?
A) [Image: Graph A, where the line 3x + 2y = 6 is shown and the area below the line is shaded.]
B) [Image: Graph B, where the line 3x + 2y = 6 is shown and the area above the line is shaded.]
C) [Image: Graph C, where the line 3x + 2y = 6 is dotted and the area below it is shaded, with an open circle at the origin.]
D) [Image: Graph D, where the line 3x + 2y = 6 is dotted and the area above it is shaded, with a closed circle at the origin.]
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The inequality states that 3x + 2y must be less than 6. This means we shade the area below the line because any point in that region satisfies the condition. Since there's no mention of equality, we use a dotted line to indicate that points on the line do not satisfy the inequality.

Explanation:
Graph B correctly represents the given inequality with the area above the line 3x + 2y = 6 shaded, indicating that all points in that region make the inequality true.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to choose Graph A, which shades the area below the line but uses a solid line. This would indicate that the points on the line are included, which is not the case for an inequality with '<'.

Problem 2 (Two):
Which graph represents the solution set of the system of inequalities: 2x + y > 4 and x - y ≤ 3?
A) [Image: Graph A, where Region A is above the line 2x + y = 4 and to the left of the line x - y = 3.]
B) [Image: Graph B, where Region B is below the line 2x + y = 4 and to the right of the line x - y = 3.]
C) [Image: Graph C, where Region C is above the line 2x + y = 4 and to the right of the line x - y = 3, with an open circle at the origin.]
D) [Image: Graph D, where Region D is below the line 2x + y = 4 and to the left of the line x - y = 3, with a closed circle at the origin.]
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The first inequality (2x + y > 4) requires points above the line. The second inequality (x - y ≤ 3) requires points to the right of the line or on it. Combining both, we shade the region that satisfies both conditions and use a dotted line for x - y = 3 because of the '<=' sign.

Explanation:
Graph C correctly represents the solution set with Region C above the line 2x + y = 4 and to the right of the line x - y = 3, using a dotted line for x - y ≤ 3. The origin is not included since it doesn't satisfy both inequalities.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose Graph D, which shades the region below the line 2x + y = 4 and to the left of the line x - y = 3, but this does not meet the conditions for both inequalities.

Problem 3 (Three):
The inequality 5x - 3y ≥ 6 is graphed as shown below. Which of the following points satisfies this inequality?
A) [Image: Point A at coordinates (1, 2)]
B) [Image: Point B at coordinates (-1, 0)]
C) [Image: Point C at coordinates (3, 4)]
D) [Image: Point D at coordinates (0, -2)]
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
To check if a point satisfies the inequality, substitute its x and y values into the inequality. For example, for point D (0, -2): 5(0) - 3(-2) ≥ 6 becomes 6 ≥ 6, which is true.

Explanation:
Point D at coordinates (0, -2) satisfies the given inequality because when we substitute x = 0 and y = -2 into 5x - 3y ≥ 6, we get 0 - (-6) ≥ 6, or 6 ≥ 6, which is true. The other points do not satisfy the inequality.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose Point C (3, 4), as some might think that since it's above the line, it satisfies the inequality. However, substituting x = 3 and y = 4 into the inequality gives 15 - 12 ≥ 6, which is not true.

Problem 7 (Seven):
The graph of the inequality 2x + y ≤ 8 is shown below. Which point lies on the boundary line?
A) [Image: Point A at coordinates (4, 0)]
B) [Image: Point B at coordinates (0, 8)]
C) [Image: Point C at coordinates (2, 4)]
D) [Image: Point D at coordinates (1, 6)]
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The boundary line for an inequality with '=' or '<=' has a solid line, indicating that points on the line satisfy the inequality. For the given inequality, the point (0, 8) lies on the line 2x + y = 8.

Explanation:
Point B at coordinates (0, 8) lies on the boundary line of the given inequality because it satisfies the equation 2(0) + 8 = 8. The other points do not lie on the line or are not part of the solution set.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose Point C (2, 4), as some might think that it's a solution point. However, it does not lie on the boundary line.
