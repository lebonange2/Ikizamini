Problem 1 (:1):
What is the result of dividing (3 + 4i) by (1 + 2i)?
A) (5 - 2i)
B) (7 + i)
C) (1 - 2i)
D) (5 + 2i)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
(3 + 4i) / (1 + 2i) = (3 + 4i)(1 - 2i) / (1 + 2i)(1 - 2i) = (3 - 6i + 4i - 8i^2) / (1 - 4i^2) = (3 - 2i - 8(-1)) / (1 + 4) = (5 - 2i) / 5 = (5 + 2i)

Explanation:
We used the conjugate of the denominator to rationalize the expression and then simplified.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to multiply both terms in the numerator by the conjugate, leading to an incorrect result.

Problem 2 (2):
What is the quotient when (8 - 5i) is divided by (4 + i)?
A) (3 - 7i)
B) (1 - 3i)
C) (3 + 7i)
D) (1 + 3i)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(8 - 5i) / (4 + i) = (8 - 5i)(4 - i) / (4 + i)(4 - i) = (32 - 8i - 20i + 5i^2) / (16 - 4i^2) = (32 - 28i - 5(-1)) / (16 + 4) = (27 - 28i) / 20 = (3 - 7i)

Explanation:
We used the conjugate of the denominator to rationalize the expression and then simplified.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to incorrectly distribute the terms in the numerator, resulting in a different answer.

Problem 3 (3):

A) (5 + i)
B) (1 + 5i)
C) (5 - i)
D) (1 - 5i)

Explanation:
We used the conjugate of the denominator to rationalize the expression and then simplified.
