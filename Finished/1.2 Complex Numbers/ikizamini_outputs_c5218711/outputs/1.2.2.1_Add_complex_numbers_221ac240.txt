Problem 1 (Asd123):
What is the sum of (3 + 4i) and (-1 - 2i)?
A) (2 + 6i)
B) (4 + 2i)
C) (2 + 2i)
D) (4 + 6i)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(3 + 4i) + (-1 - 2i) = (3 - 1) + (4i - 2i) = 2 + 2i

Explanation:
When adding complex numbers, you add the real parts and the imaginary parts separately.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to not separate the real and imaginary parts correctly. For example, adding the real parts together and the imaginary parts together would result in (2 + 2i), which is choice C.

Problem 2 (Xcv123):
Find the sum of (-4 - 5i) and (7 + 8i).
A) (11 + 3i)
B) (3 + 3i)
C) (3 + 13i)
D) (11 - 3i)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
(-4 - 5i) + (7 + 8i) = (-4 + 7) + (-5i + 8i) = 3 + 3i

Explanation:
Remember to add the real parts and the imaginary parts separately.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to mix up the signs when adding the real parts or the imaginary parts. For example, if you mistakenly added -4 and 7 as -11 instead of +3, you would get (3 - 11i), which is not one of the choices.

Problem 3 (Zxc123):
What is the result of adding (2 + 3i) and (-2 + 5i)?
A) (0 + 8i)
B) (4 - 2i)
C) (4 + 2i)
D) (0 - 8i)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(2 + 3i) + (-2 + 5i) = (2 - 2) + (3i + 5i) = 0 + 8i

Explanation:
When adding complex numbers, combine like terms: the real parts and the imaginary parts.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to not distribute the addition across both parts of each complex number. For example, if you added (2 + -2) as 0 and (3i + 5i) as 8i separately, you might mistakenly conclude that the sum is (0 + 8i), which would be choice A.

Problem 4 (Qwe123):
Find the sum of (6 - 7i) and (-3 + 4i).
A) (3 - 3i)
B) (9 - 3i)
C) (3 + 3i)
D) (9 + 3i)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(6 - 7i) + (-3 + 4i) = (6 - 3) + (-7i + 4i) = 3 - 3i

Explanation:
Add the real parts and the imaginary parts separately, paying attention to their signs.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to add the real parts with the same sign and the imaginary parts with the opposite sign. For example, if you added 6 and -3 as 3 and -7i and 4i as -3i, you would get (3 - 3i), which is choice A.

Problem 5 (Rty123):
What is the sum of (8 + 2i) and (-6 - 9i)?
A) (2 + 7i)
B) (2 - 7i)
C) (14 - 7i)
D) (2 + 11i)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
(8 + 2i) + (-6 - 9i) = (8 - 6) + (2i - 9i) = 2 - 7i

Explanation:
When adding complex numbers, combine like terms: the real parts and the imaginary parts.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to add the real parts with different signs incorrectly. For example, if you added 8 and -6 as -4 instead of +2, you would get (2 - 7i), which is choice B.

Problem 6 (Uij123):
Find the sum of (-5 + 3i) and (9 - 4i).
A) (4 + 7i)
B) (4 - 7i)
C) (4 + 1i)
D) (4 - 1i)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
(-5 + 3i) + (9 - 4i) = (-5 + 9) + (3i - 4i) = 4 - 1i

Explanation:
Add the real parts and the imaginary parts separately, considering their signs.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to add the real parts with different signs incorrectly or to combine the imaginary parts with the wrong sign. For example, if you added -5 and 9 as 4 and 3i and -4i as -1i, you would get (4 + 1i), which is choice C.

Problem 7 (Oop123):
What is the sum of (7i) and (-3i)?
A) (4i)
B) (-10i)
C) (10i)
D) (4)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
7i + (-3i) = 7i - 3i = 4i

Explanation:
When adding complex numbers with only imaginary parts, combine the coefficients of i.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to treat the imaginary parts as real numbers and add them together. For example, if you added 7 and -3 as 4, you would get (4i), which is choice A.
