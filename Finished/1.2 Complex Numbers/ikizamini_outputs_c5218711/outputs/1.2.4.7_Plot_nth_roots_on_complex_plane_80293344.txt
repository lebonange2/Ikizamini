C) (2rac{π}{3}, -2)
D) (4rac{π}{3}, -2)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The polar form of a complex number is (r, θ), where r is the modulus and θ is the argument. For (-8 + 0i), r = 8 and θ = π since it lies on the negative real axis. To find the cube root, we divide the angle by 3: π / 3 = 2π/3. The modulus is the cube root of the original modulus: (8)^(1/3) = 2. So, the polar form is (2rac{π}{3}, 2).

Explanation:
The correct answer has the correct angle and modulus for the cube root.

Common mistake note:

Problem 3 (Incremental Difficulty - Easy):
Plot the two square roots of (3 + 4i) on the complex plane.
A) (1+2i), (-1-2i)
B) (2+1i), (-2-1i)
C) (1-2i), (-1+2i)
D) (2-1i), (-2+1i)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The modulus of (3 + 4i) is √(3^2 + 4^2) = 5. The argument is arctan(4/3) ≈ 0.93 radians or 53.13 degrees. The two square roots are obtained by adding and subtracting the angle by π/2: (5, 0.93 ± π/2). Converting to rectangular form gives (1+2i) and (-1-2i).

Explanation:
The correct answer correctly plots the two square roots with the appropriate angles and moduli.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to plot only one root, forgetting that there are two distinct square roots for a complex number.
