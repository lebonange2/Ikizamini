Problem 1 (:1):
What is the product of (3 + 4i) and (2 - i)?
A) (5 + 5i)
B) (7 + 2i)
C) (6 + 7i)
D) (10 + 2i)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
(3 + 4i)(2 - i) = 6 - 3i + 8i - 4i^2 = 6 + 5i + 4 = 10 + 5i. The correct choice is (6 + 7i).

Explanation:
Multiplying complex numbers involves distributing the terms and remembering that i^2 = -1.

Common mistake note:
Forgetting to multiply the imaginary parts or not handling the i^2 term correctly.
