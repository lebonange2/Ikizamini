Problem 1 (Asdf):
Find the principal cube root of -64i.
A) -2 + 4i
B) 2 - 4i
C) -4 + 2i
D) 4 - 2i
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The given complex number is -64i, which can be written in polar form as (-64)(rac{π}{2}) = 8(4π) or 8e^(iπ). The principal cube root of a complex number is the one with the smallest argument. To find the cube roots, we divide the angle by 3. So, the angle for the principal cube root is rac{π}{3}. Now, let's find the modulus of the cube root: (8)^(1/3) = 2. Thus, the principal cube root is 2e^(iπ/3). Converting this back to rectangular form gives us -4 + 2i.

Explanation:
The principal cube root has the smallest argument among all cube roots. In this case, it's important not to mistake the angle for π, which would lead to an incorrect result.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to use π as the angle instead of rac{π}{3}, resulting in answer A (-2 + 4i) or B (2 - 4i).
