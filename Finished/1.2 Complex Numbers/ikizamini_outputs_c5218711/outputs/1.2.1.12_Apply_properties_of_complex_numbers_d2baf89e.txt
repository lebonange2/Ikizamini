Problem 1 (Asd123):
What is the result of multiplying (3 + 4i) and (2 - 3i)?
A) (5 + 6i)
B) (7 - 6i)
C) (11 - i)
D) (11 + i)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To multiply complex numbers, use the distributive property (FOIL method):
(3 + 4i)(2 - 3i) = 3 	imes 2 + 3 	imes (-3i) + 4i 	imes 2 + 4i 	imes (-3i)
= 6 - 9i + 8i - 12i^2
Since i^2 = -1, substitute -1 for i^2:
= 6 - i - 12(-1)
= 6 - i + 12
= 18 - i
The correct answer is (C): (11 - i).

Explanation:
Use the FOIL method to multiply binomials. A common mistake is forgetting to substitute i^2 with -1, resulting in an incorrect solution.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Forgetting that i^2 = -1.

Problem 2 (Xyz456):
What is the conjugate of the complex number 7 + 9i?
A) -7 + 9i
B) -7 - 9i
C) 7 - 9i
D) 9 + 7i
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The conjugate of a complex number (a + bi) is (a - bi). For the complex number 7 + 9i, the conjugate is 7 - 9i.

Explanation:
Find the conjugate by changing the sign of the imaginary part. A common mistake is reversing the real and imaginary parts.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Reversing the real and imaginary parts.

Problem 3 (Qwe789):
What is the modulus (magnitude) of the complex number -4 + 3i?
A) 5
B) 1
C) 7
D) sqrt(26)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The modulus (magnitude) of a complex number (a + bi) is sqrt(a^2 + b^2). For -4 + 3i, calculate:
Modulus = sqrt((-4)^2 + (3)^2)
= sqrt(16 + 9)
= sqrt(25)
= 5

Explanation:
Use the Pythagorean theorem to find the modulus. A common mistake is forgetting to square and sum the real and imaginary parts.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Forgetting to square the real and imaginary parts.

Problem 5 (Uij102):
What is the real part of the complex number (5 + 3i)/(7 - 4i)?
A) (35 + 28i)/(65)
B) (35 - 28i)/(65)
C) (35 + 28)/(65)
D) (35 - 28)/(65)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To find the real part of a complex fraction, multiply both numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator:
(5 + 3i)/(7 - 4i) * (7 + 4i)/(7 + 4i)
= [(5 + 3i)(7 + 4i)]/[(7 - 4i)(7 + 4i)]
= (35 + 20i + 21i - 12i^2)/49 - 16i^2
Since i^2 = -1, substitute -1 for i^2:
= (35 + 41i + 12)/(49 + 16)
= (47 + 41i)/65
The real part is 47/65, which simplifies to (35 - 28i)/(65).

Explanation:
Multiply by the conjugate and simplify to find the real part. A common mistake is forgetting to multiply both numerator and denominator.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Forgetting to multiply both numerator and denominator by the conjugate.

Problem 6 (Kol103):
What is the complex number that, when squared, gives 25 - 10i?
A) 5 + 3i
B) 5 - 3i
C) -5 + 3i
D) -5 - 3i
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Let the complex number be a + bi. Squaring it gives (a + bi)^2 = a^2 + 2abi - b^2i^2
Since i^2 = -1, substitute -1 for i^2:
(a + bi)^2 = a^2 + 2ab - b^2(-1)
= a^2 + 2ab + b^2
Set the real and imaginary parts equal to 25 - 10i:
a^2 + b^2 = 25 (real part)
2ab = -10 (imaginary part)
Solve for a and b, choosing positive values since the real part of the square root should be positive. The complex number is 5 + 3i.

Explanation:
Set up equations for the real and imaginary parts after squaring. A common mistake is overlooking the sign of the imaginary part when solving for a and b.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Forgetting to consider both positive and negative solutions.

Problem 7 (Mno104):
What is the sum of (2 + 3i) and (-4 - 5i)?
A) (6 - 2i)
B) (-2 - 2i)
C) (2 - 8i)
D) (-2 - 8i)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Add complex numbers by combining the real parts and imaginary parts separately:
(2 + 3i) + (-4 - 5i) = (2 - 4) + (3i - 5i)
= -2 - 2i
The correct answer is (B): (-2 - 2i).

Explanation:
Add the real parts and imaginary parts separately. A common mistake is mixing up the addition of real and imaginary parts.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Mixing up the addition of real and imaginary parts.
