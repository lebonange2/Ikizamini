Problem 1 (:1):
Find the distance between the complex numbers (3 + 4i) and (-1 - 2i).
A) 2sqrt(5)
B) sqrt(10)
C) 5
D) sqrt(26)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The distance between two complex numbers (a + bi) and (c + di) is given by sqrt((c - a)^2 + (d - b)^2). For the given numbers, a = 3, b = 4, c = -1, and d = -2. Plugging these values into the formula gives us:\nsqrt((-1 - 3)^2 + (-2 - 4)^2)\n= sqrt((-4)^2 + (-6)^2)\n= sqrt(16 + 36)\n= sqrt(52)\n= 2sqrt(5).

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by applying the distance formula between two complex numbers.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to add the real parts and imaginary parts separately, which would result in (c + a) and (d + b), instead of subtracting them as required by the formula.

Problem 2 (:2):
Find the distance between the complex numbers (-2 - 3i) and (4 + 5i).
A) sqrt(58)
B) 7
C) sqrt(62)
D) sqrt(10)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Using the distance formula between two complex numbers (a + bi) and (c + di):\n\nsqrt((c - a)^2 + (d - b)^2)\nFor the given numbers, a = -2, b = -3, c = 4, and d = 5. Plugging these values into the formula gives us:\n\nsqrt((4 - (-2))^2 + (5 - (-3))^2)\n= sqrt(6^2 + 8^2)\n= sqrt(36 + 64)\n= sqrt(100)\n= 10\nSo the distance is 10, which simplifies to 7.

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by applying the distance formula between two complex numbers.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to square the real parts and imaginary parts separately before adding them, rather than squaring their differences.

Problem 4 (:4):
Find the distance between the complex numbers (5 + 0i) and (-3 - 0i).
A) 2sqrt(17)
B) 8
C) sqrt(34)
D) 2
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Since both complex numbers have zero imaginary parts, they are actually just real numbers. The distance between two real numbers a and b is simply |b - a|:\n\n|-3 - 5| = |-8| = 8\nSo the distance is 8.

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by recognizing that the problem can be simplified to finding the absolute difference between two real numbers.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to try using the complex number distance formula, which would lead to a more complicated calculation than necessary.
