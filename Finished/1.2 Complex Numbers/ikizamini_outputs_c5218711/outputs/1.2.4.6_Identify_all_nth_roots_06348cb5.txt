Problem 1 (Week1_Q1):
Find all the cube roots of -8.
A) -3
B) 2
C) -2
D) 3
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The cube root of -8 is -2 because (-2) · (-2) · (-2) = -8.

Explanation:
Cube roots involve raising a number to the power of 1/3. Since -8 is a perfect cube, it has only one real cube root.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be selecting A (-3), which is incorrect because (-3) · (-3) · (-3) = -27, not -8. Another mistake might be selecting B (2), which is incorrect because 2 is a positive number and the cube root of -8 must be negative.

Problem 2 (Week1_Q2):
What are the square roots of 9?
A) -3, 3
B) 0, 9
C) 3
D) 9
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The square roots of 9 are -3 and 3 because (-3) · (-3) = 9 and (3) · (3) = 9.

Explanation:
Square roots involve raising a number to the power of 1/2. A positive number has two square roots: one positive and one negative.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be selecting C (3), which is only one of the two possible square roots. Another mistake might be selecting D (9), which is not a square root but rather the number itself squared.
