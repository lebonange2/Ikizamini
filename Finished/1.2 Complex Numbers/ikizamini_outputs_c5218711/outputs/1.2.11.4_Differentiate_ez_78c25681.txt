Problem 1 (:1):
Find the derivative of f(z) = e^(-3z).
A) e^(2z)
B) -3e^(-3z)
C) e^(4z)
D) 3e^(3z)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The derivative of e^(-3z) is -3 * e^(-3z) using the chain rule. Therefore, the correct answer is B.

Explanation:
When differentiating e raised to a function, use the chain rule where you multiply by the derivative of the inner function.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be forgetting to multiply by the derivative of -3z (-3) when applying the chain rule.

Problem 2 (2):
Find the derivative of f(z) = e^(2iz).
A) -2ie^(2iz)
B) 2e^(2iz)
C) 4ie^(2iz)
D) e^(-2iz)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The derivative of e^(2iz) is i * 2 * e^(2iz) using the chain rule. Simplifying, we get -2ie^(2iz). So, the correct answer is A.

Explanation:
When differentiating e raised to a complex function, remember to apply the chain rule and include the imaginary unit (i) in your result.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be forgetting to multiply by i when applying the chain rule for a complex exponential.

Problem 5 (5):
Find the derivative of f(z) = e^(ln(z)).
A) e^(z)
B) 1/z
C) ze^(z)
D) e^(ln(z))/z
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The derivative of e^(ln(z)) is (e^(ln(z)))' = 1 * e^(ln(z))/z = e^(ln(z))/z. So, the correct answer is D.

Explanation:
When differentiating e raised to a logarithmic function, use the chain rule and the fact that (e^u)' = e^u * u'. In this case, u = ln(z).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be forgetting to divide by z after applying the chain rule.
