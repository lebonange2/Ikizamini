Problem 1 (Asdf):
Convert the polar coordinate (5, 60°) to rectangular form.
A) (4.33, 2.5)
B) (5
cos(60), 5
sin(60))
C) (2.5, 4.33)
D) (5
cos(30), 5
cos(30))
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
(5cos(60°), 5sin(60°)) = (2.5, 4.33)

Explanation:
In polar coordinates, (r, θ) represents the distance r from the origin and the angle θ measured counterclockwise from the positive x-axis. To convert to rectangular form (x, y), use the formulas x = rcos(θ) and y = rsin(θ).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to confuse sine with cosine or to apply the wrong angle (e.g., using θ instead of 60°).

Problem 2 (Asdf2):
Convert the polar coordinate (3, -45°) to rectangular form.
A) (1.5
cos(-45), 1.5
cos(-45))
B) (3
cos(-45), 3
cos(-45))
C) (
-1.5
cos(45), -1.5
cos(45))
D) ((3cos(45), -3cos(45))
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
(3cos(-45°), -3cos(-45°)) = (2.12, -2.12)

Explanation:
When the angle is negative, remember to adjust the cosine and sine accordingly. In this case, cos(-45°) = cos(45°) and sin(-45°) = -sin(45°).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that only the sine changes sign when the angle is negative.

Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(-7cos(210°), -7cos(210°)) = (-2.45, 6.08)

Explanation:

Problem 4 (Asdf4):
Convert the polar coordinate (8, 270°) to rectangular form.
A) (8
cos(270), 8
cos(270))
B) (8
cos(90), 8
cos(90))
C) (
-8
cos(180), -8
cos(180))
D) ((8cos(0), 8cos(0))
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
(8cos(90°), 8cos(90°)) = (0, 8)

Explanation:
At 270°, the angle is in the third quadrant where the cosine is negative and sine is positive. However, cos(90°) = 0, so the x-coordinate will be zero.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that cos(90°) is zero.

Solution:
