Problem 1 (One):
What is the complex conjugate of the complex number 3 + 4i?
A) 2 + 4i
B) 3 - 4i
C) 4 + 3i
D) 5 + 0i
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The complex conjugate of a complex number a + bi is a - bi. For the given number 3 + 4i, its complex conjugate would be 3 - 4i.

Explanation:
Complex conjugates are formed by changing the sign of the imaginary part while keeping the real part unchanged.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to switch both parts of the complex number, which results in option A. Remember that only the imaginary part changes sign.

Problem 2 (Two):
Find the complex conjugate of (-2 + 6i)^2.
A) 4 - 36i
B) -4 + 36i
C) 10 + 24i
D) -10 - 24i
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
First, calculate the square of (-2 + 6i): (-2 + 6i)^2 = (-2)^2 + 2(-2)(6i) + (6i)^2 = 4 - 24i + 36i^2. Since i^2 = -1, we have: 4 - 24i - 36 = -32 - 24i. Now find the complex conjugate by changing the sign of the imaginary part: -32 + 24i.

Explanation:
First square the given complex number and then find its conjugate by reversing the sign of the imaginary term.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that i^2 = -1, which would lead to incorrect choices. Also, not squaring both parts of the binomial can result in errors.

Problem 3 (Three):
Identify the complex conjugate pair among the following: 7i, -7i, 5 + 3i, and 5 - 3i.
A) 7i and -7i
B) 5 + 3i and 5 - 3i
C) 7i and 5 + 3i
D) -7i and 5 - 3i
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The complex conjugate pair is the one where the real parts are identical, and the imaginary parts have opposite signs. In this case, 5 + 3i and 5 - 3i form a conjugate pair.

Explanation:
Look for pairs with the same real part and opposite imaginary parts to identify the complex conjugates.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to confuse the concept of conjugates with simply adding or subtracting numbers, which would lead to incorrect choices.

Problem 4 (Four):
What is the product of the complex number 2 + 3i and its complex conjugate?
A) -4 - 5i
B) 13
C) 4 + 5i
D) 4 - 5i
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The product of a complex number (a + bi) and its conjugate (a - bi) is always a real number, specifically (a^2 + b^2). For the given number 2 + 3i, the product with its conjugate is (2 + 3i)(2 - 3i) = 4 - 9i^2. Since i^2 = -1, we have: 4 + 9 = 13.

Explanation:
The product of a complex number and its conjugate results in a real number; calculate the product using the formula (a^2 + b^2).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that i^2 = -1, which would lead to an incorrect result. Also, not multiplying both parts of the binomial can cause errors.

Problem 5 (Five):
Find the complex conjugate of the expression (3 + 4i)/(5 - 6i).
A) (3 - 4i)/(5 + 6i)
B) (3 + 4i)/(6 + 5i)
C) (18 + 22i)/(25 + 30i)
D) (18 - 22i)/(25 + 30i)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To find the complex conjugate of a fraction, multiply both numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator: (3 + 4i)/(5 - 6i) * (5 + 6i)/(5 + 6i) = (15 + 18i + 20i + 24i^2)/(25 + 30i - 30i - 36i^2). Since i^2 = -1, we have: (15 + 38i - 24)/(25 + 36) = (1 + 38i)/(61) * (61)/(61) = (1 - 38i)/(61). The conjugate of this is (1 + 38i)/(61), which simplifies to (3 + 4i)/(5 + 6i).

Explanation:
To find the conjugate of a complex fraction, multiply both numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator, then simplify.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to multiply both parts of the fraction or to incorrectly apply the conjugation rule, leading to incorrect choices.

Problem 6 (Six):
Given the complex number 1 - 7i, what is its complex conjugate raised to the power of 3?
A) -46 + 21i
B) 46 + 21i
C) 46 - 21i
D) -46 - 21i
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The complex conjugate of 1 - 7i is 1 + 7i. Raising this to the power of 3: (1 + 7i)^3 = 1^3 + 3(1^2)(7i) + 3(1)(7i)^2 + (7i)^3 = 1 + 21i - 147 - 343i. Simplify and find the conjugate by changing the sign of the imaginary part: -136 + 322i.

Explanation:
First, find the complex conjugate, then raise it to the given power, and finally find its conjugate by reversing the sign of the imaginary term.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that i^2 = -1 or to incorrectly apply exponentiation rules, leading to incorrect choices.
