Problem 1 (:1):
Find the Maclaurin series for f(x) = cos(x). Which of the following is correct?
A) Σ(-1)^n  * x^(2n)/(2n!) for n=0 to ∞
B) Σ(-1)^n  * x^(2n+1)/(2n+1)! for n=0 to ∞
C) Σ(-1)^n  * x^n/‌​n! for n=0 to ∞
D) Σ(-1)^(n+1)  * x^(2n)/(2n)! for n=0 to ∞
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The Maclaurin series for cos(x) is Σ((-1)^n  * x^n/‌​n!) for n = 0 to ∞.

Explanation:
The Maclaurin series expansion of cosine function has a coefficient of (-1)^n and the factorial in the denominator follows n!

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A, which is the Taylor series for sin(x) instead of cos(x). The key difference is the absence of (2n+1) in the exponent.

Problem 2 (:2):
Find the Maclaurin series for f(x) = e^x. Which of the following is correct?
A) Σ(-1)^n  * x^n/‌​n! for n=0 to ∞
B) Σx^n/‌​n! for n=0 to ∞
C) Σx^(2n)/(2n!) for n=0 to ∞
D) Σx^(2n+1)/(2n+1)! for n=0 to ∞
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The Maclaurin series for e^x is Σ(x^n/‌​n!) for n = 0 to ∞.

Explanation:
The Maclaurin series expansion of the exponential function has a constant coefficient of 1 and the factorial in the denominator follows n!

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A, which is the Taylor series for (-e^x), not e^x. The key difference is the absence of (-1)^n.

Problem 4 (:4):
Find the Maclaurin series for f(x) = 1/(1-x), valid for |x| < 1. Which of the following is correct?
A) Σx^‌​n/‌​n! for n=0 to ∞
B) Σ(-1)^n  * x^(n+1)/(n+1)! for n=0 to ∞
C) Σx^‌​n for n=0 to ∞
D) Σ(-1)^n  * x^n for n=0 to ∞
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The Maclaurin series for 1/(1-x) is Σ(x^n) for n = 0 to ∞.

Explanation:
This is the geometric series expansion, which has a constant coefficient of 1 and no factorial in the denominator.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A, which is the Maclaurin series for e^x. The key difference is that this series does not have a factorial in the denominator.

Problem 5 (:5):
Find the Maclaurin series for f(x) = ln(1+x), valid for |x| < 1. Which of the following is correct?
A) Σ(-1)^n  * x^(n+1)/(n+1)!
B) Σx^‌​n/‌​n for n=1 to ∞
C) Σ(-1)^n  * x^n/‌​n for n=1 to ∞
D) Σx^‌​n/(n+1)! for n=0 to ∞
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The Maclaurin series for ln(1+x) is Σ((-1)^n  * x^n/‌​n) for n = 1 to ∞.

Explanation:
This is the Maclaurin series expansion of the natural logarithm, with a coefficient of (-1)^n and the factorial in the denominator follows n, starting from n=1.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A, which is the Taylor series for -ln(1-‌​x). The key difference is that this series starts from n=1 and has a negative sign.

Problem 6 (:6):
Find the Maclaurin series for f(x) = sqrt(1+x), valid for |x| < 1. Which of the following is correct?
A) Σ(-1)^n  * x^(2n)/(4^‌​n*(2n)!) for n=0 to ∞
B) Σx^(2n+1)/(2n+1)! for n=0 to ∞
C) Σx^‌​n/(2^‌​n*n!) for n=0 to ∞
D) Σ(-1)^n  * x^(2n+1)/(2^‌​n*(2n+1)!) for n=0 to ∞
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The Maclaurin series for sqrt(1+x) is Σ(x^n/(2^‌​n*n!)) for n = 0 to ∞.

Explanation:
This is the binomial expansion of (1+x)^1/2, with a coefficient of 1 and the factorial in the denominator follows n!

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A, which is the Maclaurin series for sqrt(1-‌​x). The key difference is that this series has a positive sign and starts from n=0.

Problem 7 (:7):
Find the Maclaurin series for f(x) = (1+x)^2, valid for all x. Which of the following is correct?
A) Σx^‌​n/‌​n! for n=0 to ∞
B) Σ(2n+1)‌​x^(2n)/(2n)! for n=0 to ∞
C) Σ(2^‌​n*x^‌​n) for n=0 to ∞

Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The Maclaurin series for (1+x)^2 is Σ(2^‌​n  * x^‌​n) for n = 0 to ∞.

Explanation:
This is the binomial expansion of (1+x)^2, with a coefficient of 2^‌​n and no factorial in the denominator.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B, which is the Maclaurin series for sin(2x). The key difference is that this series has a constant coefficient of 2^‌​n.
