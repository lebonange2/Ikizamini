Problem 1 (One):
Which of the following is a purely real number?
A) 3 + 4i
B) 5
C) 2i
D) -1
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
A purely real number has no imaginary part. The only choice without an 'i' is B, which represents the real number 5.

Explanation:
Purely real numbers are those with no imaginary component (no 'i').

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing C or D, which are purely imaginary numbers.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Two):
Identify the purely imaginary number from the following:
A) 7
B) -3i
C) 4 + 2i
D) sqrt(2)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
A purely imaginary number has an 'i' and no real part. Choice B, -3i, is the only one that meets this criteria.

Explanation:
Purely imaginary numbers have an 'i' and no corresponding real number.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or D, which are purely real numbers.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Three):
Which of the following expressions represents a purely real number?
A) (2 + 3i)(2 - 3i)
B) i^2
C) sqrt(-9)
D) 5i + 5i
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
When we multiply conjugates, like (2 + 3i) and (2 - 3i), the result is a purely real number: (2^2 - (3i)^2) = 4 + 9 = 13.

Explanation:
Multiplying complex conjugates eliminates the imaginary part.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing C, which simplifies to -3, an imaginary number.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Four):
Identify the purely real part of the expression (4 + 5i)(6 - 7i):
A) 24
B) -35i
C) 4 + 5i
D) 6 - 7i
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To find the purely real part, multiply the real parts of the binomials: (4 * 6) = 24.

Explanation:
When multiplying complex numbers, the imaginary parts combine to form another imaginary term.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing C or D, which are not purely real parts.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (Five):
Which of the following is a purely imaginary number raised to an integer power?
A) (2i)^3
B) (5 + 3i)^2
C) (4 - 6i)
D) (sqrt(2)i)^0
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(2i)^3 = 8i^3 = 8(-1) = -8i, which is a purely imaginary number.

Explanation:
When raising an imaginary number to an integer power, the result remains imaginary if the exponent is odd.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing D, which simplifies to 1 (a real number).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (Six):
What is the purely real part of the expression (3 + 4i)/(5 - 6i)?
A) (27/61) + (24/61)i
B) (27/61)
C) (24/61)
D) (-27/61)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To find the purely real part, multiply both numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator: (3 + 4i)(5 + 6i) = 27 + 24i. The real part is 27.

Explanation:
When dividing complex numbers, multiplying by the conjugate eliminates imaginary denominators.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing C or D, which are not purely real parts.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Seven):
Identify the purely imaginary part of the expression (2 + 3i)^4 - (1 - i)^5:
A) -i
B) 27i
C) 64 + 81i
D) 64
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
(2 + 3i)^4 = 625 + 1000i, (1 - i)^5 = -31 + 31i. The imaginary part of their difference is 1000i - 31i = 969i ≈ 27i.

Explanation:
When subtracting complex numbers, the real parts are subtracted from the real parts and the imaginary parts from the imaginary parts.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing C or D, which include real parts.
