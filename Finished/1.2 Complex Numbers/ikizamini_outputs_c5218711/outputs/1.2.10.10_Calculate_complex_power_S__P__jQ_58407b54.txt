Problem 1 (:1):
What is the complex power if the active power (P) is 100 W and the reactive power (Q) is 50 VAR?
A) 50 + j100
B) 100 - j50
C) 150 + j50
D) 100 + j50
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The formula for complex power is S = P + jQ. Given P = 100 W and Q = 50 VAR, we substitute these values into the formula: S = 100 + j(50) = 100 + j50.

Explanation:
Complex power combines active (real) power with reactive power using the imaginary unit j. The correct answer uses the given values for P and Q in the formula.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to reverse the sign of the reactive power term, which results in choice B. This error occurs when混淆了active and reactive power terms.

Problem 2 (:2):

A) 0 + j75
B) 75 + j0
C) 75 - j0
D) 0 - j75
Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
When there's no active power (P), the complex power is purely reactive and has a positive imaginary part. Choice A correctly represents this scenario.

Problem 4 (:4):
An AC circuit has a complex power of 120 + j60. What is the reactive power (Q) in VAR?
A) 60
B) 120
C) 180
D) 75
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The complex power is given as S = 120 + j60. To find the reactive power (Q), we look at the imaginary part of the complex number: Q = Im(S) = 60.

Explanation:
To find reactive power from complex power, we only consider the imaginary part of the complex number. Choice A correctly extracts the reactive power from the given complex power.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to choose choice B, which represents the active power (P) instead of reactive power. This error occurs when混淆了active and reactive power terms.
