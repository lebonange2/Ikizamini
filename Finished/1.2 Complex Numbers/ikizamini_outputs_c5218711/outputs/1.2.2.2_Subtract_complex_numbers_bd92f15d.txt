Problem 1 (:1):
Find the difference of (3 + 4i) and (-1 - 2i):
A) (2 + 6i)
B) (4 + 2i)
C) (4 - 2i)
D) (2 - 2i)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(3 + 4i) - (-1 - 2i) = 3 + 4i + 1 + 2i = (3 + 1) + (4i + 2i) = 4 + 6i

Explanation:
When subtracting complex numbers, distribute the negative sign and combine like terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to incorrectly distribute the negative sign or not combining like terms.

Problem 2 (2):
Subtract (-4 - 3i) from (7 + 5i):
A) (11 + 8i)
B) (11 - 2i)
C) (3 + 2i)
D) (3 - 8i)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
(7 + 5i) - (-4 - 3i) = 7 + 5i + 4 + 3i = (7 + 4) + (5i + 3i) = 11 + 8i

Explanation:
When subtracting complex numbers, distribute the negative sign and combine like terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to change the sign of the second complex number when subtracting.

Problem 3 (3):
Find the difference between (2 - 7i) and (5 + 9i):
A) (3 - 16i)
B) (7 + 2i)
C) (7 - 2i)
D) (3 + 16i)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(2 - 7i) - (5 + 9i) = 2 - 7i - 5 - 9i = (2 - 5) + (-7i - 9i) = -3 - 16i

Explanation:
When subtracting complex numbers, distribute the negative sign and combine like terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to change the signs of both parts of the second complex number when subtracting.

Problem 4 (4):
Subtract (6 + 3i) from (-8 - 5i):
A) (14 - 2i)
B) (-14 + 8i)
C) (-2 - 8i)
D) (-2 + 8i)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
(-8 - 5i) - (6 + 3i) = -8 - 5i - 6 - 3i = (-8 - 6) + (-5i - 3i) = -14 - 8i

Explanation:
When subtracting complex numbers, distribute the negative sign and combine like terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to change the signs of both parts of the first complex number when subtracting.

Problem 5 (5):
Find the difference between (1 - 2i) and (-3 + 4i):
A) (4 - 6i)
B) (4 + 6i)
C) (2 - 6i)
D) (2 + 6i)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(1 - 2i) - (-3 + 4i) = 1 - 2i + 3 - 4i = (1 + 3) + (-2i - 4i) = 4 - 6i

Explanation:
When subtracting complex numbers, distribute the negative sign and combine like terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to add instead of subtracting the second complex number.

Problem 6 (6):
Subtract (2i) from (5 + 3i):
A) (5 + i)
B) (7 + 5i)
C) (3 + 5i)
D) (3 + i)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
(5 + 3i) - (2i) = 5 + 3i - 2i = 5 + (3i - 2i) = 5 + i

Explanation:
When subtracting complex numbers, distribute the negative sign and combine like terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that when subtracting a pure imaginary number from another complex number, you only need to subtract the imaginary part.

Problem 7 (7):
Find the difference between (-4 + 6i) and (3 - 8i):
A) (-7 + 14i)
B) (7 - 14i)
C) (-1 - 2i)
D) (1 + 2i)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(-4 + 6i) - (3 - 8i) = -4 + 6i - 3 + 8i = (-4 - 3) + (6i + 8i) = -7 + 14i

Explanation:
When subtracting complex numbers, distribute the negative sign and combine like terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to change the signs of both parts of the first complex number when subtracting.
