Problem 1 (:1):
(4/9) / (3/8)
A) (32/27)
B) (8/27)
C) (9/32)
D) (27/32)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To simplify the complex fraction, multiply the numerator by the reciprocal of the denominator:
(4/9) * (8/3) = 4*8 / 9*3 = 32/27
So, the simplified form is 32/27.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by multiplying the numerators and denominators as described in the solution.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to simply divide the numerators or denominators, which would lead to an incorrect result.
