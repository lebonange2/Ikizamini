Problem 1 (x1):
What is the conjugate of the binomial (3x - 4)?
A) (3x + 4)
B) (-3x - 4)
C) (-3x + 4)
D) (4x - 3)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The conjugate of a binomial (a - b) is obtained by changing the sign between the two terms, so the conjugate of (3x - 4) is (-3x + 4).

Explanation:
Conjugates are pairs of binomials that differ only in the sign between their terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to reverse both signs, which results in choice A, but this is not the correct conjugate.

Problem 2 (x2):
Find the conjugate pair of the binomial (2 - √5).
A) (2 + √5)
B) (-2 - √5)
C) (-2 + √5)
D) (5 - 2)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The conjugate of a binomial with a radical (a - b√c) is (a + b√c), so the conjugate pair is (2 - √5) and (2 + √5).

Explanation:
When dealing with radicals, only the term inside the square root changes sign.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to reverse both terms, which results in choice B, but this does not form a conjugate pair.

Problem 3 (x3):
Identify the conjugate of (x + 3i).
A) (x - 3i)
B) (-x - 3i)
C) (-x + 3i)
D) (3i - x)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
In complex numbers, the conjugate switches the sign of the imaginary part. So, the conjugate of (x + 3i) is (x - 3i).

Explanation:
Complex conjugates involve swapping the sign of the imaginary component.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to reverse both parts, which results in choice B, but this does not form a complex conjugate.
