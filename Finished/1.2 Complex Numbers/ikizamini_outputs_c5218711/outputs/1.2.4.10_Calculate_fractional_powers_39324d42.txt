Problem 1 (:1):
What is the value of π^(1/2)?
A) 2π
B) π^2
C) 1
D) π
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The square root of π (denoted as π^(1/2)) is approximately 1.77245. Therefore, the correct answer is D: π.

Explanation:
To calculate a fractional power like this, you take the root indicated by the denominator and raise it to the power of the numerator.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A (2π) because they might think that squaring π first and then dividing by 2 is equivalent. However, this would be incorrect since you're actually looking for the square root, not the reciprocal of the square.

Problem 2 (2):
What is 16^(3/4)?
A) 8
B) 4
C) 2
D) 64
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To calculate 16^(3/4), you need to raise 16 to the power of 3/4, which is equivalent to taking the fourth root of 16 cubed. The fourth root of 16 is 2, and 2 cubed is 8. So the correct answer is B: 4.

Explanation:
When dealing with fractional powers where the denominator is the root, you take the root first and then raise to the power indicated by the numerator.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing D (64) because they might interpret the problem as raising 16 to the third power and then taking the fourth root. However, this would be incorrect since the order of operations is different.

Problem 3 (3):
Calculate 27^(2/3).
A) 9
B) 3
C) 81
D) ³27
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To find 27^(2/3), you take the cube root of 27 and then square the result. The cube root of 27 is 3, and 3 squared is 9. So the correct answer is A: 9.

Explanation:
When dealing with a fractional power where the numerator is the exponent and the denominator is the root, you apply the root operation first followed by the exponent operation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing C (81) because they might interpret the problem as raising 27 to the second power and then taking the cube root. However, this would be incorrect since the order of operations is different.
