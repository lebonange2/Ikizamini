Problem 1 (What is the modulus of the complex number 3 + 4i?):
What is the modulus of the complex number 3 + 4i?
A) math:2
B) math:5
C) math:7
D) math:10
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The modulus (absolute value) of a complex number (a + bi) is given by √(a^2 + b^2). For the complex number 3 + 4i, we have a = 3 and b = 4. So, the modulus is √(3^2 + 4^2) = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5.

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by applying the formula for the modulus of a complex number.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to add the real and imaginary parts instead of squaring them, which would result in choice A (2) or choice C (7).

Problem 2 (What is the modulus of the complex number -4 + 3i?):
What is the modulus of the complex number -4 + 3i?
A) math:1
B) math:5
C) math:7
D) math:25
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The modulus (absolute value) of a complex number (-a + bi) is still given by √(a^2 + b^2). For the complex number -4 + 3i, we have a = -4 and b = 3. So, the modulus is √((-4)^2 + 3^2) = √(16 + 9) = √25 = 5.

Explanation:
The negative sign in front of the real part does not affect the modulus calculation.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to consider the negative sign as part of the square root, which would result in choice A (1).

Problem 3 (What is the modulus of the complex number 0 + 2i?):
What is the modulus of the complex number 0 + 2i?
A) math:0
B) math:1
C) math:2
D) math:4
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
For a complex number with no real part (0 + bi), the modulus is simply the absolute value of the imaginary part. So, for the complex number 0 + 2i, the modulus is |2| = 2.

Explanation:
The modulus of a complex number with zero real part is just the absolute value of the imaginary part.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to think that the modulus is always zero when there's no real part, which would result in choice A (0).

Problem 4 (What is the modulus of the complex number 2 - 2i?):
What is the modulus of the complex number 2 - 2i?
A) math:1
B) math:2
C) math:4
D) math:√8
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The modulus (absolute value) of a complex number (a - bi) is still given by √(a^2 + b^2). For the complex number 2 - 2i, we have a = 2 and b = -2. So, the modulus is √(2^2 + (-2)^2) = √(4 + 4) = √8 = 2√2.

Explanation:
The negative sign in front of the imaginary part does not affect the modulus calculation, but it does change the value under the square root.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget that the imaginary part is negative and calculate the modulus as if it were positive (choice D), which would result in √8.

Problem 5 (What is the modulus of the complex number 1 + i√3?):
What is the modulus of the complex number 1 + i√3?
A) math:2
B) math:√4
C) math:√7
D) math:2√3
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The modulus (absolute value) of a complex number (a + bi) is given by √(a^2 + b^2). For the complex number 1 + i√3, we have a = 1 and b = √3. So, the modulus is √(1^2 + (√3)^2) = √(1 + 3) = √4 = 2.

Explanation:
The square root of 3 does not affect the overall modulus calculation; it's just part of the imaginary part.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to think that the square root of 3 affects the modulus directly, which would result in choice D (2√3).

Problem 6 (What is the modulus of the complex number -1 - i?):
What is the modulus of the complex number -1 - i?
A) math:1
B) math:√2
C) math:2
D) math:4
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The modulus (absolute value) of a complex number (-a - bi) is given by √(a^2 + b^2). For the complex number -1 - i, we have a = -1 and b = -1. So, the modulus is √((-1)^2 + (-1)^2) = √(1 + 1) = √2.

Explanation:
The negative signs in both the real and imaginary parts do not affect the modulus calculation; they just change the values under the square root.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to think that the negative signs make the modulus zero or less than 1, which would result in choice A (1).

Problem 7 (What is the modulus of the complex number -2√3 + 2i?):
What is the modulus of the complex number -2√3 + 2i?
A) math:2
B) math:4
C) math:2√7
D) math:4√3
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The modulus (absolute value) of a complex number (-a + bi) is given by √(a^2 + b^2). For the complex number -2√3 + 2i, we have a = -2√3 and b = 2. So, the modulus is √((-2√3)^2 + 2^2) = √(12 + 4) = √16 = 4.

Explanation:
The square root of 3 does not affect the overall modulus calculation; it's just part of the real part.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to think that the square root of 3 affects the modulus directly, which would result in choice D (4√3).
