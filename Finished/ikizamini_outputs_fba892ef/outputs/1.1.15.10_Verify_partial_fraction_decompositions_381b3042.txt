Problem 1 (One):
Given the function f(x) = (2x + 3)/(x^2 - 4), find the correct partial fraction decomposition.
A) (1/(x+2)) + (1/(x-2))
B) (1/(x+2))^2 + (1/(x-2))
C) (2/(x+2)) + (3/(x-2))
D) (x/(x+2)) + (x/(x-2))
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The denominator factors into (x-2)(x+2), suggesting the decomposition should be A/(x-2) + B/(x+2). Solving for A and B gives A=1, B=1.

Explanation:
To verify a partial fraction decomposition, equate it to the original function and solve for constants. Here: f(x) = (A/(x-2)) + (B/(x+2)). Multiply both sides by x^2 - 4: 2x + 3 = A(x+2) + B(x-2). Expand and equate coefficients: A=1, B=1.

Common mistake note:
Forget to multiply decomposition by original denominator or incorrect expansion.

Problem 2 (Two):
Decompose the function g(x) = (4x + 1)/(x^2 + 3x + 2) into partial fractions.
A) (2/(x+1)) + (2/(x+2))
B) (4/(x+1))^2 + (1/(x+2))
C) (4/(x+1)) + (1/(x+2))
D) (x/(x+1)) + (x/(x+2))
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The denominator factors into (x+1)(x+2), so the decomposition is A/(x+1) + B/(x+2). Solving for A and B gives A=4, B=1.

Explanation:
Find partial fraction decomposition by setting up a system: 4x + 1 = A(x+2) + B(x+1). Expand and solve: A=4, B=1.

Common mistake note:
Misidentify factors or incorrect expansion.

Problem 4 (Four):
Verify the partial fraction decomposition of f(x) = (5x - 7)/(x^2 - 4x + 3): A/(x-1) + B/(x-3).
A) True
B) False, constant terms don't match
C) False, linear terms don't match
D) False, incorrect decomposition form
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Multiply both sides by x^2 - 4x + 3: 5x - 7 = A(x-3) + B(x-1). Expand and solve for A, B: A=2, B=3. The decomposition is correct.

Explanation:
Multiply decomposition by original denominator and compare to function. Here: A=2, B=3 matches the original function.

Common mistake note:
Forget to multiply decomposition or incorrect expansion.

Problem 6 (Six):
The function h(x) = (3x^2 + 6x - 5)/(x(x-1)(x+2)) has a constant term in its partial fraction decomposition. True or False?
A) True
B) False
C) Cannot determine without solving
D) Depends on the values of x
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
No, since the numerator is quadratic and the denominator has no repeated linear factors, there will be no constant term in the decomposition.

Explanation:
A constant term requires a linear factor in the numerator not dividing the denominator. Here, it doesn't occur.

Common mistake note:
Assume quadratic numerator always leads to a constant term.

Problem 7 (Seven):
The partial fraction decomposition of f(x) = (5x^3 + 10x^2 - 7x + 4)/(x(x+1)^2(x-2)) has how many terms?
A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 4
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The decomposition will have one term for each unique factor: x, (x+1)^2, and (x-2). So, 4 terms.

Explanation:
Count distinct factors in the denominator: x, (x+1)^2, (x-2), giving 4 terms.

Common mistake note:
Miscount or overlook repeated factors.
