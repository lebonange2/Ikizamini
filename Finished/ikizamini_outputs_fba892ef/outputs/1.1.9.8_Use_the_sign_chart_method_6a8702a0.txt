Problem 1 (Asdf):
Find the intervals where the function f(x) = x^3 - 4x^2 + 5x is positive using the sign chart method.
A) (0, 1)
B) (1, +∞)
C) (-∞, 0) ∪ (1, +∞)
D) (-∞, +∞)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The sign chart for f(x) is as follows:

x | -∞ | 0 | 1 | +∞
f'(x) | - | 0 | + | +

Since the function changes from negative to positive at x = 0 and again at x = 1, it will be positive in the intervals between these roots. Therefore, f(x) is positive on the intervals (-∞, 0) and (1, +∞).

Explanation:
The sign chart method helps identify where a polynomial function changes signs by analyzing its derivative's zeros.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that the function changes sign at the roots of its derivative. Choosing A or B would be incorrect because they don't include the entire positive interval.
