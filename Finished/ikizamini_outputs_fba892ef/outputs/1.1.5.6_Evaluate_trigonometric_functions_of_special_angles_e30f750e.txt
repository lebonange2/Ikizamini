Problem 1 (?):
Find the value of sin(30°).
A) 0.25
B) 0.5
C) 0.75
D) 1
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The sine of 30 degrees is a well-known value in trigonometry: sin(30°) = 1/2 = 0.5.

Explanation:
In a right-angled triangle with angles 30°, 60°, and 90°, the side opposite the 30° angle is half the length of the hypotenuse. This leads to sin(30°) being equal to 1/2.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be choosing A (0.25), which might come from a misunderstanding or forgetting the actual value.

Problem 2 (?):
Calculate cos(45°).
A) 0.707
B) 0.866
C) 0.951
D) 1
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The cosine of 45 degrees is another well-known value: cos(45°) = √2/2 ≈ 0.707.

Explanation:
In a right-angled isosceles triangle with two 45° angles, the adjacent side to one of these angles is equal to the hypotenuse divided by √2. This gives cos(45°) as √2/2.

Problem 3 (?):
Determine tan(60°).
A) 1
B) √2
C) √3
D) 2
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The tangent of 60 degrees is well-known: tan(60°) = √3.

Explanation:
In a right-angled triangle with angles 30°, 60°, and 90°, the side opposite the 60° angle (opposite to tan(60°)) is √3 times the side adjacent to it. This gives tan(60°) as √3.

Problem 4 (?):
Find the value of cot(30°).
A) 1
B) √2
C) √3
D) 3
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The cotangent is the reciprocal of tangent: cot(30°) = 1/tan(30°) = 1/√3.

Explanation:
Since tan(30°) = √3, its reciprocal, cot(30°), would be 1/√3. However, in decimal form, it is commonly expressed as √3 for simplicity.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be choosing A (1), which might come from混淆 with the values of sin(30°) or cos(60°).

Explanation:
Since cos(45°) = √2/2, its reciprocal, sec(45°), would be 2/√2, which simplifies to √2.

Explanation:
