Problem 1 (One-Variable Linear System):
Solve the system of equations by graphing:

y = 2x + 3
y = x + 4

Which point represents the solution to the system?
A) (1, 5)
B) (2, 7)
C) (3, 8)
D) (0, 3)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The graphs of both lines intersect at the point (1, 5). Therefore, this is the solution to the system.

Explanation:
When graphing systems, find the intersection point(s) of the lines. The coordinates of the intersection are the solution.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to misinterpret one line as a parallel line and not finding the intersection. Both lines must intersect for a solution.

Problem 2 (Two-Variable Linear System):
Solve the system of equations by graphing:

y = -2x + 5
3x + y = 7

Which point represents the solution to the system?
A) (1, 3)
B) (2, 1)
C) (0, 5)
D) (-1, 7)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The graphs of both lines intersect at the point (1, 3). Therefore, this is the solution to the system.

Explanation:
When graphing systems, find the intersection point(s) of the lines. The coordinates of the intersection are the solution.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to mix up the equations or misinterpret one line as a vertical line, which would not intersect with the other line.

Problem 3 (Quadratic and Linear System):
Solve the system of equations by graphing:

y = x^2 - 3x + 2
y = x - 1

Which point represents the solution to the system?
A) (0, -1)
B) (1, 0)
C) (2, 1)
D) (3, 4)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The graphs of the quadratic and linear equations intersect at the point (1, 0). Therefore, this is the solution to the system.

Explanation:
When graphing systems, find the intersection point(s) of the curves and lines. The coordinates of the intersection are the solution.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to misinterpret the quadratic equation as a straight line or not recognizing that it may intersect with the linear equation at multiple points.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (System with No Solution):
Solve the system of equations by graphing:

y = 2x + 1
y = -2x + 3

Does this system have a solution?
A) Yes, at (0, 1)
B) Yes, at (1, 3)
C) No Solution
D) Depends on the graphing method
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The graphs of both lines are parallel and do not intersect. Therefore, this system has no solution.

Explanation:
If two lines are parallel, they will never intersect, indicating a system with no solution.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to think that the intersection point is at infinity, which is not a valid solution for a system of equations.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (System with Infinite Solutions):
Solve the system of equations by graphing:

y = 2x + 3
4x + 2y = 12

Does this system have a solution?
A) Yes, at (0, 3)
B) Yes, at (3, 9)
C) No Solution
D) Infinite Solutions
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The second equation is a multiple of the first equation, meaning they are the same line. Therefore, this system has infinite solutions.

Explanation:
If two lines are identical, they will overlap completely, indicating a system with infinite solutions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to think that there is only one solution because the lines appear to be the same but not recognizing that it represents an infinite number of intersections.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (System with One Solution and a Circle):
Solve the system of equations by graphing:

x^2 + y^2 = 5
y = x - 1

Which point represents the solution to the system?
A) (0, 1)
B) (1, 0)
C) (2, 1)
D) (-1, 2)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The circle and line intersect at the point (1, 0). Therefore, this is the solution to the system.

Explanation:
When graphing systems involving a circle and a line, find the intersection point(s) between them. The coordinates of the intersection are the solution.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to misinterpret the circle as a straight line or not recognizing that it may intersect with the linear equation at only one point.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (System with Ellipse and Line):
Solve the system of equations by graphing:

4x^2 + y^2 = 16
y = x - 3

Which point represents the solution to the system?
A) (0, -3)
B) (1, -2)
C) (2, -1)
D) (-1, -4)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The ellipse and line intersect at the point (1, -2). Therefore, this is the solution to the system.

Explanation:
When graphing systems involving an ellipse and a line, find the intersection point(s) between them. The coordinates of the intersection are the solution.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to misinterpret the ellipse as a circle or not recognizing that it may intersect with the linear equation at only one or two points.
