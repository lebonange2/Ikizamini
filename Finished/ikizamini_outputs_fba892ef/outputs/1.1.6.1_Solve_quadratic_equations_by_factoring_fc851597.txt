Problem 1 (Asdf):
What are the solutions to the equation x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0?
A) x = 1, x = 3
B) x = -1, x = -3
C) x = 2, x = -3
D) x = -2, x = 3
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation factors as (x - 2)(x - 3) = 0. Setting each factor equal to zero gives us the solutions x = 1 and x = 3.

Explanation:
Factoring quadratic equations involves finding two binomials whose product equals the given quadratic equation. Setting each factor to zero gives us the solutions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to confuse the roots of the factored form with the roots of the original equation. Remember, when factoring, we find values that make the entire expression equal to zero.

Problem 2 (Asdf2):
Solve the quadratic equation 3x^2 + 10x - 8 = 0 by factoring.
A) (3x - 4)(x + 2) = 0
B) (x - 4)(3x + 2) = 0
C) (x + 4)(3x - 2) = 0
D) (3x + 4)(x - 2) = 0
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
First, we need to factor the quadratic expression. We look for two numbers that multiply to give 3 	imes (-8) = -24 and add up to give 10. These numbers are 6 and -4. Now we can rewrite the equation as 3x^2 + 6x - 4x - 8 = 0, then group terms: (3x(x + 2) - 4(x + 2)) = 0. Factoring out (x + 2) gives us (3x - 4)(x + 2) = 0.

Explanation:
To factor a quadratic equation, we need to find two binomials whose product equals the given expression. We do this by finding numbers that multiply to give the product of the leading coefficient and constant term, and add up to give the middle coefficient.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to distribute the leading coefficient (3 in this case) when factoring. Make sure to distribute correctly before grouping terms.

Problem 3 (Asdf3):
What are the solutions to the equation 2x^2 + 7x - 15 = 0?
A) x = -5, x = 3/2
B) x = 5, x = -3/2
C) x = 5, x = 3/2
D) x = -5, x = -3/2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation factors as (2x - 3)(x + 5) = 0. Setting each factor equal to zero gives us the solutions x = 3/2 and x = -5.

Explanation:
To factor a quadratic equation, we need to find two binomials whose product equals the given expression. We do this by finding numbers that multiply to give the product of the leading coefficient and constant term, and add up to give the middle coefficient.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to misidentify the factors or forget to divide one factor by the leading coefficient when simplifying. Double-check your calculations.

Problem 4 (Asdf4):
Solve the quadratic equation x^2 - 12x + 36 = 0 by factoring.
A) (x - 6)^2 = 0
B) (x + 6)^2 = 0
C) (2x - 6)^2 = 0
D) (2x + 6)^2 = 0
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation is already a perfect square trinomial, which factors as (x - 6)(x - 6) or (x - 6)^2. Setting this equal to zero gives us the solution x = 6.

Explanation:
Perfect square trinomials can be factored directly into a binomial squared. In this case, we recognize that x^2 - 12x + 36 is a perfect square because it's (x - 6)^2.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to try to factor the expression as two separate linear terms, which would lead to incorrect solutions. Recognize perfect square trinomials and factor them accordingly.
