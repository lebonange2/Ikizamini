Problem 1 (?):
Solve for x: 3x + 2 = 14
A) x = 5
B) x = 4
C) x = 6
D) x = 3
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
First, subtract 2 from both sides: 3x + 2 - 2 = 14 - 2
This gives us: 3x = 12
Now, divide by 3 to solve for x: x = 12 / 3
So, x = 4

Explanation:
We used the cover-up method (subtracting a constant from both sides) to isolate the variable term. Then we divided by the coefficient of x to find its value.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to divide both sides by 2 instead of subtracting it, which would lead to an incorrect solution.

Problem 2 (?):
Solve for y: 5y - 7 = 18
A) y = 5
B) y = 6
C) y = 7
D) y = 4
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
First, add 7 to both sides: 5y - 7 + 7 = 18 + 7
This gives us: 5y = 25
Now, divide by 5 to solve for y: y = 25 / 5
So, y = 5

Explanation:
We used the cover-up method (adding a constant to both sides) to isolate the variable term. Then we divided by the coefficient of y to find its value.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to divide both sides by 7 instead of adding it, which would lead to an incorrect solution.

Problem 3 (?):
Solve for z: (z + 3)(z - 2) = 0
A) z = -3 or z = 2
B) z = 3 or z = -2
C) z = 0
D) No real solution
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Using the zero product property, if a * b = 0, then either a = 0 or b = 0. So we set each factor equal to zero:
z + 3 = 0 → z = -3
z - 2 = 0 → z = 2

Explanation:
We applied the cover-up method by setting each linear expression within the product equal to zero and solving for z.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to multiply both sides by (z + 3)(z - 2) to eliminate the parentheses, which is not necessary when applying the zero product property.

Problem 4 (?):
Solve for x: 2(x + 1) = 10
A) x = 4
B) x = 3
C) x = -3
D) x = -4
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
First, distribute the 2: 2x + 2 = 10
Now, subtract 2 from both sides: 2x + 2 - 2 = 10 - 2
This gives us: 2x = 8
Finally, divide by 2 to solve for x: x = 8 / 2
So, x = 3

Explanation:
We used the cover-up method (distributing and then subtracting a constant) to isolate the variable term. Then we divided by the coefficient of x to find its value.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to divide both sides by 10 instead of distributing, which would lead to an incorrect solution.

Problem 5 (?):
Solve for y: (y - 4)^2 = 9
A) y = 7 or y = 1
B) y = 5 or y = -3
C) y = 0 or y = 8
D) No real solution
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Taking the square root of both sides, we get: y - 4 = ±3
This gives us two solutions:
y - 4 = 3 → y = 7
y - 4 = -3 → y = -3

Explanation:
We applied the cover-up method by taking the square root of both sides and solving for y.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to assume that only the positive square root is valid, neglecting the negative solution.

Problem 6 (?):
Solve for x: 3/x + 2 = 5
A) x = 1
B) x = 2
C) x = 3/2
D) x = 6
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
First, subtract 2 from both sides: 3/x = 5 - 2
This gives us: 3/x = 3
Now, multiply both sides by x to solve for x: 3 = 3x
Finally, divide by 3 to solve for x: x = 3 / 3
So, x = 1

Explanation:
We used the cover-up method (subtracting a constant from both sides) to isolate the variable term. Then we multiplied and divided by x to find its value.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to divide both sides by 3 directly, which would lead to an incorrect solution.

Problem 7 (?):
Solve for y: 4y / (y + 1) = 2
A) y = 1
B) y = -1
C) y = 0
D) No real solution
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
First, multiply both sides by (y + 1) to eliminate the fraction: 4y = 2(y + 1)
Expanding and simplifying gives us: 4y = 2y + 2
Subtracting 2y from both sides: 2y = 2
Finally, divide by 2 to solve for y: y = 2 / 2
So, y = 1

Explanation:
We used the cover-up method (multiplying both sides by the denominator) to eliminate the fraction and then solved for y.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to multiply both sides by 4 instead of multiplying by (y + 1), which would lead to an incorrect solution.
