A) 686

Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The formula for wavelength is λ = v/f, where v is the velocity and f is the frequency. Plugging in the given values gives us λ = 343 m/s / 500 Hz = 0.686 meters.

Problem 5 (:5):
A ship travels at a speed of 20 knots (nautical miles per hour) due east. The wind blows at a speed of 10 knots from the north. What is the ship's actual velocity vector? Use trigonometry to find the magnitude and direction.
A) 20
cos(45)
B) 20
/ 10
C) 20
* 10
D) √(20^2 + 10^2), 45 degrees north of east
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
To find the ship's actual velocity, we can use vector addition. The eastward component is 20 knots and the northward component due to wind is 10 knots. The magnitude of the total velocity (v) is √(20^2 + 10^2) = √400 = 20 knots. The direction (θ) can be found using the arctangent function: θ = arctan(10/20) = arctan(0.5) ≈ 26.57 degrees north of east. However, since the ship is moving due east and the wind blows from the north, the actual direction is 45 degrees north of east (90 - 26.57).

Explanation:
The problem involves adding two vectors: one for the ship's speed and one for the wind's effect. The magnitude is found using Pythagoras' theorem, and the direction is adjusted to account for the actual movement.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A because it uses cosine instead of arctangent. Arctangent should be used to find the angle between the two components.

The formula for the period of a satellite in orbit around Earth is T^2 = (4	au^2 / G * M) * r^3, where T is the period, G is the gravitational constant (6.674 × 10^-11 m^3 kg^-1 s^-2), and M is the mass of Earth (5.972 × 10^24 kg).
