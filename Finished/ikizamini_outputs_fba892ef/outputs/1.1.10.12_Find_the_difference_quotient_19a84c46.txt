Problem 1 (IncrementalChange):
Find the difference quotient of f(x) = x^2 at x=3 with a small change h.
A) (3+h)^2 - 9 / h
B) 6h + 9
C) 6
D) 18/h
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The difference quotient is given by (f(x+h) - f(x)) / h. For f(x) = x^2 at x=3, substitute x with 3 and then add h to both x terms inside the square.

Explanation:
(3+h)^2 - 9 / h

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include forgetting to square (3+h), resulting in B. Another mistake is considering only the derivative at x=3, which gives C.

Problem 2 (NegativeChange):
Find the difference quotient of f(x) = 2x - 1 at x=4 with a negative change -2.
A) (2(4-2) - 1) - (7 - 1) / -2
B) -6
C) 3
D) 8
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The difference quotient is given by (f(x+h) - f(x)) / h. For f(x) = 2x - 1 at x=4 and h=-2, substitute x with 4 and then add -2.

Explanation:
(2(4-2) - 1) - (7 - 1) / -2

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include forgetting that h is negative, leading to B. Another mistake is calculating the derivative directly, which gives C.

Problem 3 (FractionalChange):
Find the difference quotient of f(x) = x^3 at x=2 with a fractional change 1/2.
A) (2^(3/2) - 8) / (1/2)
B) 6
C) 4.5
D) 16
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The difference quotient is given by (f(x+h) - f(x)) / h. For f(x) = x^3 at x=2 and h=1/2, substitute x with 2 and then add 1/2 to the power.

Explanation:
(2^(3/2) - 8) / (1/2)

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include raising 2 directly to the power of 1/2 instead of using the fractional exponent, which gives B. Another mistake is forgetting to divide by h, resulting in C.

Problem 4 (QuadraticFunction):
Find the difference quotient of f(x) = x^2 - 4x + 3 at x=5 with a change 1.
A) (6 - 11) / 1
B) 10
C) 9
D) 2
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The difference quotient is given by (f(x+h) - f(x)) / h. For f(x) = x^2 - 4x + 3 at x=5 and h=1, substitute x with 5 and then add 1.

Explanation:
(6 - 11) / 1

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include forgetting the coefficient of x when expanding (x+1)^2, which gives A. Another mistake is subtracting f(x+h) from f(x) instead of taking their difference, resulting in C.

Problem 7 (ConstantFunction):
Find the difference quotient of f(x) = 7 at x=3 with a change h.
A) (7 - 7) / h
B) 0
C) 14/h
D) h
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The difference quotient is given by (f(x+h) - f(x)) / h. For a constant function like f(x) = 7, the value does not change with x.

Explanation:
(7 - 7) / h

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include thinking that there's some change in the function, leading to C or D. Another mistake is dividing by zero (h), which gives A.
