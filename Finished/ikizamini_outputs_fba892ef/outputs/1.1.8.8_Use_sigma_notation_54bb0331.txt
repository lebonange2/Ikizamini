Problem 1 (Asdf):
What is the sum of the first 5 terms of the sequence: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10?
A) 10
B) 20
C) 30
D) 40
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The sum of the first 5 terms can be found using sigma notation: Σμ=1^5 (2μ). This represents the sum as μ goes from 1 to 5, with each term being 2 times the value of μ. The sum is 30.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by evaluating the sigma notation for this arithmetic sequence.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to simply add up the terms without realizing that sigma notation represents a more general way of summing a series.

Problem 2 (Asdf):
What does the following sigma notation represent? Σμ=1^5 (2μ)
A) The sum of the first 5 even numbers.
B) The product of the first 5 odd numbers.
C) The difference between consecutive odd numbers from 1 to 9.
D) The average of the first 5 prime numbers.
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The sigma notation Σμ=1^5 (2μ) represents the sum as μ goes from 1 to 5, with each term being 2 times the value of μ. This is equivalent to the sum of the first 5 even numbers.

Explanation:
The sigma notation indicates a sum where each term is twice the index value, resulting in an arithmetic sequence of even numbers.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to interpret '2μ' as multiplication instead of repeated addition.

Problem 7 (Asdf):
What is the value of the following sigma notation? Σμ=1^8 ((-1)^μ * μ)
A) -36
B) 0
C) 36
D) 72
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The sigma notation Σμ=1^8 ((-1)^μ * μ) represents the sum as μ goes from 1 to 8, with each term being the product of the value of μ and (-1) raised to the power of μ. The sum is -36.

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by evaluating the sigma notation for this sequence with alternating signs.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to overlook the alternating signs caused by (-1)^μ, leading to a calculation that treats all terms as positive or negative.
