Problem 1 (Asd123):
What is the rectangular coordinate of a point located 3 units to the right and 4 units up from the origin?
A) (4, 3)
B) (3, 4)
C) (7, 0)
D) (-3, -4)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Rectangular coordinates are represented as (x, y), where x is the horizontal distance from the origin and y is the vertical distance. The point at 3 units right and 4 units up has coordinates (3, 4).

Explanation:
The order of coordinates matters: first x, then y.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is reversing the order of the coordinates. Remember that x comes before y.

Problem 2 (Xyz789):
What are the rectangular coordinates of a point located 2 units to the left and 1 unit down from the origin?
A) (1, -2)
B) (-2, 1)
C) (-1, 2)
D) (2, -1)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Rectangular coordinates are represented as (x, y), where x is the horizontal distance from the origin and y is the vertical distance. A point 2 units left and 1 unit down has coordinates (-2, -1).

Explanation:

Common mistake note:

Problem 3 (Qwe456):
What are the rectangular coordinates of a point on the x-axis, 7 units away from the origin?
A) (0, 7)
B) (7, 0)
C) (-7, 0)
D) (0, -7)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Points on the x-axis have a y-coordinate of 0. A point 7 units along the x-axis has coordinates (7, 0).

Explanation:
On the x-axis, all points have a y-coordinate of 0.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is assuming the point would be on the positive or negative y-axis instead of the x-axis.

Problem 5 (Uij123):
What is the distance between the points (2, 5) and (-4, -3)?
A) 9
B) 10
C) 11
D) 12
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The distance between two points in a rectangular coordinate system is calculated using the distance formula: d = sqrt((x2 - x1)^2 + (y2 - y1)^2). For points (2, 5) and (-4, -3), d = sqrt((-4 - 2)^2 + (-3 - 5)^2) = sqrt(36 + 64) = sqrt(100) = 10.

Explanation:
The distance formula gives the straight-line distance between two points in a coordinate plane.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is forgetting to square the differences and take the square root.

Problem 6 (Kol789):
What is the slope of the line passing through the points (3, 1) and (-2, -4)?
A) -1
B) 1/3
C) -5/3
D) 3
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The slope of a line passing through two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) is calculated as m = (y2 - y1) / (x2 - x1). For points (3, 1) and (-2, -4), m = (-4 - 1) / (-2 - 3) = -5 / -5 = 1. The slope is positive if the line rises to the right, negative if it falls to the right.

Explanation:
The slope of a line describes its steepness and direction.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is reversing the order of coordinates when calculating the slope.

Problem 7 (Mno456):
What is the equation of the line passing through the points (1, 2) and (3, 6)?
A) y = x + 1
B) y = 2x - 1
C) y = 2x + 1
D) y = 2x
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The equation of a line in slope-intercept form is y = mx + b, where m is the slope and b is the y- intercept. First, find the slope: m = (6 - 2) / (3 - 1) = 4 / 2 = 2. Then, substitute one point into the equation to solve for b: using (1, 2), 2 = 2(1) + b => b = 0. The equation is y = 2x.

Explanation:
The slope-intercept form gives the slope and y-intercept directly.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is using the wrong point to find the y-intercept.
