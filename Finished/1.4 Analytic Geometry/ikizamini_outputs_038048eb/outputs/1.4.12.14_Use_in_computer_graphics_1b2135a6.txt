Problem 1 (Andromeda Galaxy Rendering):
Which technique is commonly used in computer graphics for rendering detailed images of the Andromeda Galaxy?
A) Ray tracing
B) Rasterization
C) Shadow mapping
D) Normal mapping
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Ray tracing simulates light interactions, making it suitable for complex scenes like galaxies. Rasterization is faster but less detailed.

Explanation:
Rasterization is used for real-time graphics, shadow mapping creates shadows, and normal mapping adds surface detail without extra polygons.

Common mistake note:
Choosing B (Rasterization) as it's faster, but ray tracing is more accurate for detailed scenes.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Realistic Water Simulation):
Which computer graphics method simulates the appearance of realistic water in a scene?
A) Particle systems
B) Fog volume
C) Normal mapping
D) Bump mapping
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Particle systems create fluid motion, essential for simulating water. Other techniques enhance texture or lighting.

Explanation:
Fog volumes add atmospheric effects, normal mapping and bump mapping improve surface detail without changing geometry.

Common mistake note:
Choosing C (Normal mapping) or D (Bump mapping) as they enhance texture, but not for fluid motion simulation.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Real-Time Terrain Rendering):
What technique is used in real-time computer graphics to efficiently render large outdoor terrains?
A) Level of Detail (LOD)
B) Ray marching
C) Screen space reflections
D) SSAO (Screen Space Ambient Occlusion)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
LOD adjusts model complexity based on distance, optimizing real-time rendering. Ray marching and SSAO are for other effects.

Explanation:
Ray marching is for volumetric rendering, screen space reflections add realism to reflective surfaces, and SSAO enhances ambient lighting.

Common mistake note:
Choosing B (Ray marching) as it's related to rendering, but LOD is more suitable for real-time terrains.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Anti-Aliasing Technique):
Which method reduces jagged edges, or aliasing, in rendered computer graphics images?
A) Temporal anti-aliasing (TAA)
B) Mipmapping
C) Bloom post-processing effect
D) Depth of Field
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
TAA averages frames to smooth edges, improving image quality. Mipmapping affects texture detail.

Explanation:
Mipmapping enhances texture resolution, bloom creates an over-exposed effect, and depth of field adjusts focus.

Common mistake note:
Choosing B (Mipmapping) as it impacts textures, but TAA is for aliasing reduction.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (Physically Based Shading):
What approach in computer graphics produces realistic lighting and materials?
A) Gouraud shading
B) Phong shading
C) Vertex lighting
D) Physically Based Rendering (PBR)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
PBR models light interactions based on physics, resulting in lifelike appearances. Older techniques are less accurate.

Explanation:
Gouraud and Phong shading are outdated methods, vertex lighting affects only mesh vertices.

Common mistake note:
Choosing B (Phong shading) as an older technique, but PBR is more realistic.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (Realistic Fire Simulation):
What computer graphics method simulates fire behavior and appearance?
A) Fluid dynamics simulation
B) Procedural texture generation
C) Shadow mapping
D) Normal mapping
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Fluid dynamics simulates complex behaviors like fire by modeling particle movement. Other techniques enhance visuals.

Explanation:
Procedural texture generation creates textures, shadow mapping adds shadows, and normal mapping enhances surface detail.

Common mistake note:
Choosing B (Procedural texture generation) as it generates textures, but not for simulating fire behavior.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Real-Time Character Animation):
Which technique is used in real-time applications like games to animate characters?
A) Skinning with skeletal animation
B) Lattice deformation
C) Vertex displacement mapping
D) Particle systems
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Skinning with skeletal animation allows for realistic character movements by attaching a mesh to bones. Other techniques are suited for other tasks.

Explanation:
Lattice deformation changes shape, vertex displacement mapping adds detail, and particle systems simulate fluids or particles.

Common mistake note:
Choosing B (Lattice deformation) as it can change shape, but skinning with skeletal animation is better for character animations.
