Problem 1 (Asdf):
Two circles with radii 3 and 5 units have their centers at (2, 3) and (-4, -1), respectively. How many points of intersection do the circles have?
A) 0
B) 1
C) 2
D) 3
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The distance between the centers is sqrt((2-(-4))^2 + (3-(-1))^2) = sqrt(52). Since this distance is less than the sum of their radii (3+5=8), the circles intersect at exactly 2 points.

Explanation:
To find intersection points, check if center distance < sum of radii. If so, 2 intersections; else, no intersections. Used distance formula: sqrt((x1-x2)^2 + (y1-y2)^2).

Common mistake note:
Assuming distance ≥ sum of radii means no intersections, but they can touch (1 intersection) if equal.

Problem 2 (Asdf):
Circle A has a radius of 7 units and is centered at (5, 2). Circle B has a radius of 4 units and is centered at (-3, -6). How many points of intersection do the circles have?
A) 0
B) 1
C) 2
D) 3
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The distance between centers is sqrt((5-(-3))^2 + (2-(-6))^2) = 8 units. Since this equals the difference of radii (7-4=3), the circles touch at one point, no further intersection.

Explanation:
If center distance = difference in radii, circles are tangent with 1 intersection point. Used distance formula: sqrt((x1-x2)^2 + (y1-y2)^2).

Common mistake note:
Assuming sum of radii > distance means no intersections, but they can touch at one point.

Problem 3 (Asdf):
Circle C has a radius of 9 units and is centered at (0, 7). Circle D has a radius of 6 units and is centered at (8, -3). How many points of intersection do the circles have?
A) 0
B) 1
C) 2
D) 3
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The distance between centers is sqrt((0-8)^2 + (7-(-3))^2) = 14 units. Greater than sum of radii (9+6=15), no intersection points.

Explanation:
If center distance > sum of radii, circles do not intersect. Used distance formula: sqrt((x1-x2)^2 + (y1-y2)^2).

Common mistake note:
Assuming larger radius guarantees intersection; it only means potential intersection.

Problem 4 (Asdf):
Circle E has a radius of 4 units and is centered at (-1, -2). Circle F has a radius of 6 units and is centered at (3, 4). How many points of intersection do the circles have?
A) 0
B) 1
C) 2
D) 3
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The distance between centers is sqrt((-1-3)^2 + (-2-4))^2 = 8 units. Less than sum of radii (4+6=10), 2 intersection points.

Explanation:
If center distance < sum of radii, circles intersect at 2 points. Used distance formula: sqrt((x1-x2)^2 + (y1-y2)^2).

Common mistake note:
Comparing radii directly is incorrect; consider centers' positions and use distance formula.
