Problem 1 (Q1):
Find the arc length of a circle with radius 5 units and central angle of 60 degrees.
A) 2.5π units
B) π units
C) 1.5π units
D) 0.5π units
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The formula for arc length is L = rθ, where L is the arc length, r is the radius of the circle, and θ is the central angle in radians. First, convert the angle from degrees to radians: 60° * (π/180) = π/3 radians. Then plug into the formula: L = 5 * (π/3) = 5π/3 units. Simplify by multiplying by 3/3: L = 1.5π units.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by using the arc length formula and converting the angle to radians.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is not converting the central angle from degrees to radians before plugging it into the formula.

Problem 2 (Q2):
What is the arc length of a sector with radius 7 units and a central angle of 240°?
A) 14π units
B) 8π units
C) 16π/3 units
D) 7π/3 units
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
First, convert the angle from degrees to radians: 240° * (π/180) = 4π/3 radians. The formula for arc length is L = rθ. Plugging in the values gives us L = 7 * (4π/3) = 28π/3 units. Simplify by dividing by 3: L = 16π/3 units.

Explanation:
The correct answer uses the arc length formula and converts the angle to radians.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is forgetting to convert the central angle from degrees to radians.

Problem 5 (Q5):
Find the arc length of a circle with radius 12 units subtended by an angle of π/6 radians.
A) 4π units
B) 2π units
C) 6π units
D) 3π units
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The formula for arc length is L = rθ. Plugging in the values gives us L = 12 * (π/6) = 2π units.

Explanation:
The correct answer uses the arc length formula without needing to convert the angle, as it's already in radians.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is converting the angle to degrees even though it's already given in radians.
