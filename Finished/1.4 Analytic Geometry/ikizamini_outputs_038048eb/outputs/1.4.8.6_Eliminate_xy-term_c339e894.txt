Problem 1 (Asdf):
Simplify the expression: x^2 + 2xy + y^2
A) (x+y)^2
B) x(x+2y)
C) y(x^2+2)
D) (x-y)^2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The expression is a perfect square trinomial, which can be factored as (x + y)^2. Thus, the correct answer is A.

Explanation:
Perfect square trinomials have the form (a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose B, which represents x multiplied by the linear term (x+2y), but this does not eliminate the xy-term.

Problem 2 (Bghj):

A) 3(x+y)^2

C) 3y(x^2+2)

Correct Answer: A

Solution:

Explanation:
