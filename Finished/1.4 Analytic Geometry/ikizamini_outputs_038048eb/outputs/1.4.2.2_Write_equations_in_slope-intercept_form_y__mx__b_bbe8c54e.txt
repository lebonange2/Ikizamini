Problem 1 (:1):
What is the slope-intercept form of the equation that passes through the points (2, 5) and (-3, -7)?
A) y = 2x + 1
B) y = -2x + 4
C) y = x + 3
D) y = -½x - 1
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The slope (m) can be found using the formula m = (y2 - y1) / (x2 - x1). Using points (2, 5) and (-3, -7), we get m = (-7 - 5) / (-3 - 2) = -12 / -5 = 2.4. Rounding to the nearest integer, the slope is 2. The y-intercept (b) can be found by plugging one of the points into the equation y = mx + b and solving for b. Using point (2, 5): 5 = 2 × 2 + b => b = -1. So the equation is y = 2x - 1, which simplifies to y = -2x + 4.

Explanation:
The correct answer has a slope of 2 and a y-intercept that makes the equation true when plugged in with one of the given points.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to calculate the wrong slope or misplace the sign when simplifying the equation.

Problem 2 (:2):
What is the slope-intercept form of the line passing through (1, 3) with a slope of -4?
A) y = -4x + 7
B) y = 4x - 1
C) y = -½x + ¼
D) y = 2x - 5
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Given a point (1, 3) and a slope of -4, we can plug these values into the slope-intercept form y = mx + b. Substituting m = -4 and (x, y) = (1, 3): 3 = -4 × 1 + b => b = 3 + 4 => b = 7. So the equation is y = -4x + 7.

Explanation:
The correct answer has a slope of -4 and passes through the point (1, 3).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to add the y-intercept after calculating the slope.

Problem 3 (:3):
Write the equation in slope-intercept form for a line with a slope of 0 and passing through the point (5, 2).
A) y = 0x + 2
B) y = x + 5
C) y = 2x + 5
D) y = -½x + ¼
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
A line with a slope of 0 is a horizontal line. The equation for a horizontal line passing through any point (x, y) is y = c, where c is the constant value of y. In this case, c = 2, so the equation is y = 0x + 2 or simply y = 2.

Explanation:
The correct answer represents a horizontal line with a slope of 0 and passing through the given point.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to assume that the x- intercept is (5, 0) instead of recognizing that a horizontal line has no x-intercept.

Problem 4 (:4):

A) y = 3x + 6
B) y = -½x + ¼
C) y = 3x - 6
D) y = -3x + 12
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The equation y = 3x - 6 is already in slope-intercept form, where m = 3 and b = -6.

Explanation:
The correct answer is the given equation itself, as it is already in slope-intercept form.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to try to simplify or manipulate an equation that is already in the correct form.

Problem 5 (:5):
Find the slope-intercept form of the line passing through (4, -1) and parallel to y = 2x + 3.
A) y = 2x - 7
B) y = 2x + 5
C) y = x - 3
D) y = -½x + ¼
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Lines that are parallel have the same slope. The given line has a slope of 2, so our new line will also have a slope of 2. We can use the point (4, -1) and the slope to find the y-intercept (b). Substituting m = 2 and (x, y) = (4, -1): -1 = 2 × 4 + b => b = -1 - 8 => b = -9. So the equation is y = 2x - 9, which simplifies to y = 2x - 7.

Explanation:
The correct answer has a slope of 2 and passes through the point (4, -1).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to assume that parallel lines have different slopes.

Problem 7 (:7):
Find the slope-intercept form of the line passing through (0, 7) and perpendicular to y = ½x - 3.
A) y = 2x + 14
B) y = -2x + 7
C) y = x - 3
D) y = -½x + ¼
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Lines that are perpendicular have slopes that are negative reciprocals of each other. The given line has a slope of 1/2, so our new line will have a slope of -2. As the line passes through (0, 7), this is the y-intercept (b). So the equation is y = -2x + 7.

Explanation:
The correct answer has a slope that is perpendicular to the given line and passes through the point (0, 7).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to use the wrong formula for finding the slope of a perpendicular line.
