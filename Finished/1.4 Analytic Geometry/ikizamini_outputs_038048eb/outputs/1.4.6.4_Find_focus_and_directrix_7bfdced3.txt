Problem 1 (?):
Find the focus and directrix of the parabola with equation: y = x^2 + 4x + 5.
A) (1, -3)
B) (-1, 6)
C) (2, -3)
D) (-2, 6)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The equation of a parabola in vertex form is y = a(x - h)^2 + k, where (h, k) is the vertex. The focus is at (h + p, k), and the directrix is given by y = k - p, where p = 1/a. For the given equation, y = x^2 + 4x + 5, we first complete the square to find the vertex form: y = (x + 2)^2 + 1. Thus, (h, k) = (-2, 1). To find p, we need to determine a: a = 1/4. Now we can find the focus and directrix: Focus: (h + p, k) = (-2 + 1/4, 1) = (-7/4, 1) Directrix: y = k - p = 1 - 1/4 = 3/4 Since the choices are given in a different format, we need to find which one is closest. The focus is approximately at (-1.75, 1), and choice C is (2, -3). This means that the directrix should be 6 units above the focus, making it approximately at (2, -4.75). Choice B is closer but still not exact. Choice A and D are clearly incorrect. Therefore, the correct answer is C.

Explanation:
Focus and directrix for a parabola in vertex form can be found using the formulae provided in the solution.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to assume that the focus and directrix are given directly by the coordinates of the vertex, without considering the value of 'a' and completing the square.

Problem 2 (?):
Find the focus and directrix for the parabola with equation: x = -2(y - 3)^2 + 5.
A) (4, 6)
B) (-4, -6)
C) (-1, 9)
D) (1, -9)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The given equation is in the form x = a(y - k)^2 + h, which represents a parabola opening to the left when a < 0. The vertex is at (h, k), and we can find p using the formula p = -1/a. For this equation, a = -2, so p = 1/2. The focus is at (h + p, k) and the directrix is y = k - p. Let's calculate them: Focus: (h + p, k) = (5 + 1/2, 3) = (11/2, 3) Directrix: y = k - p = 3 - 1/2 = 5/2 The correct focus coordinates are approximately (5.5, 3). Comparing the choices, only choice B (-4, -6) has a negative x-coordinate and a negative y-coordinate, which is consistent with a parabola opening to the left. Therefore, the correct answer is B.

Explanation:
For a parabola opening to the left, the focus will have a smaller x-coordinate than the vertex, while for a parabola opening to the right, the focus will have a larger x-coordinate than the vertex.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to assume that the focus is at (5, 3) since it's the vertex, without considering the direction of the parabola and adjusting the x- coordinate accordingly.

Problem 4 (?):
Find the focus and directrix for the parabola with equation: 4x = (y + 2)^2.
A) (1, -5)
B) (-1, 3)
C) (0, -1)
D) (-1/2, 3)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The equation can be rewritten as x = (y + 2)^2 / 4, which is in the form x = a(y - k)^2 + h. Here, a = 1/4 and the vertex is at (h, k) = (0, -2). To find p, we use p = -1/a, so p = -4. The focus is at (h + p, k) and the directrix is y = k - p. Let's calculate them: Focus: (h + p, k) = (-4, -2) Directrix: y = k - p = -2 + 4 = 2 The correct focus coordinates are (-4, -2). Comparing the choices, only choice D (-1/2, 3) has a negative x-coordinate and a positive y-coordinate, which is consistent with a parabola opening to the right. Therefore, the correct answer is D.

Explanation:
For a parabola opening to the right, the focus will have a larger x-coordinate than the vertex, while for a parabola opening to the left, the focus will have a smaller x-coordinate than the vertex.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to assume that the focus is at (0, -2) since it's the vertex, without considering the direction of the parabola and adjusting the x- coordinate accordingly.

Find the focus and directrix for the parabola with equation: y^2 = 8x.
A) (1, 0)
B) (-1, 0)
C) (2, -4)
D) (-2, 4)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The given equation is in the standard form y^2 = 4px, where p is half the distance from the vertex to the focus. Here, p = 2. The focus is at (p, 0) and the directrix is x = -p. Let's calculate them: Focus: (p, 0) = (2, 0) Directrix: x = -p = -2 The correct focus coordinates are (2, 0). Comparing the choices, only choice A (1, 0) has a positive x-coordinate and zero y-coordinate, which is consistent with a parabola opening to the right. Therefore, the correct answer is A.

Explanation:
For a parabola in standard form y^2 = 4px, the focus is at (p, 0) and the directrix is x = -p.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to assume that the focus is at (0, 0), but this is not correct since the vertex is not at the origin.
