Problem 1 (Incremental Translation):
Given a figure translated 3 units right and 2 units up, which transformation would return it to its standard position?
A) Translated 3 units left and 2 units down
B) Translated 2 units right and 3 units up
C) Translated 3 units left and 2 units up
D) Translated 2 units right and 3 units down
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To return the figure to its standard position, we need to undo the translations. Since it was translated 3 units right and 2 units up, translate it back by those amounts in the opposite direction: 3 units left and 2 units down. Choice A correctly reverses the original translation.

Explanation:
Undoing translations involves applying the negative of the original translation values to each coordinate.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to apply only one direction (left or up) instead of both, which wouldn't return the figure to its standard position.

Problem 2 (Incremental Rotation):
Given a figure rotated 90 degrees clockwise, which transformation would return it to its standard position?
A) Rotated 90 degrees counterclockwise
B) Rotated 180 degrees clockwise
C) Rotated 270 degrees clockwise
D) Rotated 360 degrees clockwise
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To return the figure to its standard position, undo the rotation. Since it was rotated 90 degrees clockwise, rotate it back by 90 degrees counterclockwise. Choice A correctly reverses the original rotation.

Explanation:
Undoing rotations involves applying the negative of the original angle or a multiple of 360 degrees that brings the figure back to its initial orientation.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to apply an incorrect angle, such as 180 degrees, which would result in a different orientation rather than returning the figure to its standard position.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Incremental Reflection):
Given a figure reflected across the y-axis, which transformation would return it to its standard position?
A) Reflected across the x-axis
B) Reflected across the line y = x
C) Reflected across the line y = -x
D) Not transformed at all
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Reflecting a figure across the y-axis and then reflecting it back across the same axis returns it to its original position. Choice D correctly represents this action.

Explanation:
Reflecting a figure twice across the same line results in no net change, as if the figure was never reflected in the first place.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to choose a reflection across another line, which wouldn't return the figure to its standard position.

Problem 4 (Scaling with Center):
Given a figure scaled by a factor of 2 with center at (0, 0), which transformation would return it to its standard position?
A) Scaled by a factor of 1/2 with the center at (0, 0)
B) Scaled by a factor of 1/2 with the center at (2, 2)
C) Scaled by a factor of -2 with the center at (0, 0)
D) Not transformed at all
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To return the figure to its standard position after scaling, scale it back by the inverse of the original scaling factor. Since it was scaled by 2, scale it back by 1/2. Choice A correctly reverses the original scaling with the same center.

Explanation:
Undoing scaling involves applying the reciprocal of the original scaling factor, provided the center of scaling remains the same.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to use a different center of scaling or an incorrect scaling factor, which wouldn't return the figure to its standard position.

Given a figure translated 4 units right, rotated 180 degrees counterclockwise, and reflected across the x-axis, which sequence of transformations would return it to its standard position?
A) Reflected across the x-axis, rotated 180 degrees clockwise, translated 4 units left
B) Translated 4 units left, rotated 180 degrees counterclockwise, reflected across the x-axi... [truncated]...e standard position.
C) Translated 5 units up, reflected across the y-axis
D) Translated 5 units up, reflected across the y-axis
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
To return the point (3, 4) to its original position after translation, undo the translations. Since it was translated 2 units left and 1 unit down, translate it back by those amounts in the opposite direction: 2 units right and 1 unit up. The new coordinates would be (5, 3), which is choice B.

Explanation:
Undoing translations involves applying the negative of the original translation values to each coordinate.
