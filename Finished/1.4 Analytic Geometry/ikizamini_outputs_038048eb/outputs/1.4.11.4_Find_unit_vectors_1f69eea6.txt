Problem 1 (Asdf):
What is the unit vector in the direction of the vector <3, 4>?
A) <0.6, 0.8>
B) <3/5, 4/5>
C) <9, 12>
D) <-0.6, -0.8>
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The unit vector in the direction of a given vector is found by dividing the vector by its magnitude. The magnitude of <3, 4> is sqrt(3^2 + 4^2) = 5. So, the unit vector is <3/5, 4/5>. Common mistake notes: Some students may forget to divide by the magnitude and instead use the original vector (choice B).

Explanation:
Unit vectors have a magnitude of 1 and represent direction only.

Common mistake note:
Some students may forget to divide by the magnitude and instead use the original vector (choice B).
