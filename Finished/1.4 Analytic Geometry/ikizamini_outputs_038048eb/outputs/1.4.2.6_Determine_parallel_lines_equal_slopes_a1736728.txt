Problem 1 (Asdf):
Which pair of lines have equal slopes?
A) [0, 5], [4, 9]
B) [2, -1], [6, -3]
C) [3, 2], [9, 6]
D) [1, 0], [7, 7]
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Parallel lines have equal slopes. To find the slope of a line given two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2), use the formula: slope = (y2 - y1) / (x2 - x1). Let's calculate the slopes for each pair of lines:

A: Slope = (9 - 5) / (4 - 0) = 1
B: Slope = (-3 - (-1)) / (6 - 2) = -0.5
C: Slope = (6 - 2) / (9 - 3) = 2/3
D: Slope = (7 - 0) / (7 - 1) = 7/6

The only pair with equal slopes is C, which has a slope of 2/3.

Explanation:
Parallel lines have the same slope. In this case, line C has a slope of 2/3, while all other options have different slopes.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose option A or D because they both have an increase in y values, but it's important to remember that parallel lines must have the exact same slope, not just increasing values.

Problem 2 (Asdf2):
Are the lines with equations 3x - 6y = 12 and 6x - 12y = 24 parallel?
A) Yes
B) No
C) Cannot be determined without rearranging the equations
D) Cannot be determined without graphing
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To determine if two lines are parallel, we need to compare their slopes. First, let's rewrite the equations in slope-intercept form (y = mx + b), where m is the slope and b is the y-intercept.

3x - 6y = 12 becomes y = (1/2)x - 2
6x - 12y = 24 becomes y = (1/2)x - 3

Now we can see that both lines have the same slope, m = 1/2. Since they have equal slopes, they are parallel.

Explanation:
Parallel lines have the same slope. By rearranging the equations into slope-intercept form, we found that both lines have a slope of 1/2, making them parallel.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to think that since the y-intercepts are different (y = -2 and y = -3), the lines must not be parallel. However, it's the slopes that determine parallelism, not the y-intercepts.

Problem 3 (Asdf3):
Which of the following equations represents a line with the same slope as y = 4x + 1?
A) y = 2x - 3
B) y = 4x + 5
C) y = -4x + 7
D) y = 8x + 9
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The line y = 4x + 1 has a slope of 4. Parallel lines have the same slope, so we need to find an equation with a slope of 4. Let's check each option:

A: Slope = 2 (not equal to 4)
B: Slope = 4 (equal to 4)
C: Slope = -4 (not equal to 4)
D: Slope = 8 (not equal to 4)

Option B has the same slope as y = 4x + 1, making it parallel.

Explanation:
Parallel lines have the same slope. By comparing the slopes of each option with the given line, we found that only option B has a slope of 4, which is equal to the slope of the given line.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose an equation with a different slope (e.g., A or C), thinking it represents parallel lines. It's crucial to remember that parallel lines have identical slopes.

Problem 4 (Asdf4):
Are the lines passing through points (-2, 3) and (1, 7) parallel to those passing through points (0, 5) and (3, 9)?
A) Yes
B) No
C) Cannot be determined without calculating slopes
D) Cannot be determined without graphing
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
First, let's find the slope of both pairs of lines. For the first pair: (-2, 3) and (1, 7), the slope is (7 - 3) / (1 - (-2)) = 4/3. For the second pair: (0, 5) and (3, 9), the slope is (9 - 5) / (3 - 0) = 4/3. Since both pairs have the same slope of 4/3, they are not parallel.

Explanation:
Parallel lines have the same slope. By calculating the slopes for each pair of points, we found that both pairs have a slope of 4/3, making them not parallel but coincident (they overlap).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose 'Yes' because the slopes are the same, but it's important to remember that if two lines have the same slope and at least one point in common, they are not parallel but rather coincident.

Problem 5 (Asdf5):
Which of the following pairs of equations do NOT have the same slope?
A) (x - 1)/2 = y - 3
B) y = 2x + 4
C) 6x - 3y = 9
D) y = 2(x - 1) + 3
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
First, let's rewrite the equations in slope-intercept form (y = mx + b):

A: y = (x - 1)/2 + 3 becomes y = x/2 + 7/2
B: Already in slope-intercept form with m = 2
C: 6x - 3y = 9 becomes y = 2x - 3
D: y = 2(x - 1) + 3 becomes y = 2x + 1

Now let's compare the slopes:

A and B have different slopes (1/2 and 2)
B and C have different slopes (2 and 2)
C and D have different slopes (2 and 2)
D and A have different slopes (2 and 1/2)

The pair with different slopes is A and C.

Explanation:
Parallel lines have the same slope. By comparing the slopes of each pair, we found that only the pairs A and C have different slopes, making them not parallel.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose a pair with the same slope (e.g., B and C), thinking they are not parallel. It's important to remember that lines with different slopes can never be parallel.

Problem 6 (Asdf6):
Are the lines passing through points (1, 2) and (3, 4) parallel to those passing through points (-2, -4) and (0, -2)?
A) Yes
B) No
C) Cannot be determined without calculating slopes
D) Cannot be determined without graphing
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
First, let's find the slope of both pairs of lines. For the first pair: (1, 2) and (3, 4), the slope is (4 - 2) / (3 - 1) = 1. For the second pair: (-2, -4) and (0, -2), the slope is (-2 - (-4)) / (0 - (-2)) = 1. Since both pairs have the same slope of 1, they are parallel.

Explanation:
Parallel lines have the same slope. By calculating the slopes for each pair of points, we found that both pairs have a slope of 1, making them parallel.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose 'No' because the points seem unrelated, but it's important to remember that parallelism is determined by the slopes, not the points themselves.

Problem 7 (Asdf7):
Which of the following equations represents a line with a different slope than y = -3x + 5?
A) y = -3x - 2
B) y = 3x - 10
C) y = -3/2x + 7
D) y = -6x + 15
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The line y = -3x + 5 has a slope of -3. Non-parallel lines have different slopes, so we need to find an equation with a different slope. Let's check each option:

A: Slope = -3 (same as given line)
B: Slope = 3 (different from given line)
C: Slope = -3/2 (different from given line)
D: Slope = -6 (different from given line)

Option B has a different slope than y = -3x + 5, making it not parallel.

Explanation:
Non-parallel lines have different slopes. By comparing the slopes of each option with the given line, we found that only option B has a slope of 3, which is different from the slope of the given line (-3), making them non-parallel.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose an equation with the same slope as the given line (e.g., A), thinking it represents a non-parallel line. It's crucial to remember that lines with different slopes are not parallel.
