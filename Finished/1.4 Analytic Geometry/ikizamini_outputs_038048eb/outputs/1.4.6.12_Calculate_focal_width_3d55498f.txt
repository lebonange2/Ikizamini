Problem 1 (Asdf):
What is the focal width of a lens with a focal length of 50 mm and an aperture of f/4?
A) 25 mm
B) 12.5 mm
C) 50 mm
D) 100 mm
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The formula to calculate the focal width is: focal width = (focal length) x (aperture value). Given a focal length of 50 mm and an aperture of f/4, we can plug these values into the formula: focal width = 50 mm x 4 = 200 mm. Dividing by 2 to get the actual focal width gives us 100 mm / 2 = 50 mm. However, since option B is half of this value (12.5 mm), it must be a typo or mistake. The correct answer should be twice that amount, which is 25 mm.

Explanation:
The focal width calculation involves multiplying the focal length by the aperture value and then dividing by 2. In this case, the provided choices don't match the calculated result directly due to a potential typo in one of the options.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to not divide by 2 after calculating the product of focal length and aperture value.
