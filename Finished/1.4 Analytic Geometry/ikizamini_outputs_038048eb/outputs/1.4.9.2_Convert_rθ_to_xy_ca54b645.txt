Problem 1 (Asdf):
Convert the polar coordinates (5, π/3) to rectangular coordinates (x, y).
A) (2.5, 4.33)
B) (4.33, 2.5)
C) (5
cos(π/3), 5
sin(π/3))
D) (5
cos(π), 5
cos(π))
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
(x, y) = (r 
cos(θ), r 
sin(θ)). For (5, π/3): x = 5 
cos(π/3) = 2.5, y = 5 
sin(π/3) = 4.33.

Explanation:
The conversion formula from polar to rectangular coordinates is (x, y) = (r 
cos(θ), r 
sin(θ)). Make sure to use the correct angle measure.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Using the wrong angle (π instead of π/3). Remember to use the given θ value in the conversion formula.
