Problem 1 (:1):
A cylindrical water tank has a diameter of 2 meters and a height of 3 meters. What is the volume of the tank in cubic meters?
A) 6π
B) 9π
C) 12π
D) 18π
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The formula for the volume V of a cylinder is V = πr²h, where r is the radius and h is the height. Given that the diameter is 2 meters, the radius r is half of that, which is 1 meter. Plugging in the values: V = π(1)²(3) = 3π cubic meters. However, since we're looking for the volume in cubic meters and not just π times the value, we multiply by 3 to get the correct answer: 3π * 3 = 9π cubic meters. The question asks for the volume in cubic meters, so we need to include the factor of 3. Therefore, the correct answer is D: 18π.

Explanation:
The formula for the volume of a cylinder was applied correctly, but the mistake made by choosing A or B would be not multiplying by the height (3) after calculating πr².

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Not including the height in the volume calculation.

Problem 2 (2):
A circular wheel with a diameter of 50 cm is rotating at 40 RPM (revolutions per minute). What is the linear speed of the wheel in centimeters per second?
A) 1.28π
B) 6.28
C) 25.12
D) 125.6
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The formula for linear speed v is v = ωr, where ω is the angular velocity (in radians per second) and r is the radius. First, we need to convert RPM to radians per second: 1 revolution = 2π radians, so 40 RPM = 40 * 2π / 60 radians per second. Then, we plug in the values: v = (40 * 2π / 60) * (50 cm / 2) = 25.12 cm/second. Therefore, the correct answer is C: 25.12.

Explanation:
The conversion from RPM to radians per second and the application of the linear speed formula were done correctly. Choosing A or B would be a mistake due to not converting RPM correctly or using the wrong radius. Choosing D would be an error in multiplying by the factor of 2π.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes: Not converting RPM to radians per second, using the diameter instead of the radius, or multiplying by π unnecessarily.

Problem 3 (3):
A satellite dish has a circular shape with a diameter of 1.5 meters. What is the area of the dish in square meters?
A) 0.79π
B) 2.36π
C) 3.14π
D) 4.71π
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The formula for the area A of a circle is A = πr², where r is the radius. Given that the diameter is 1.5 meters, the radius r is half of that, which is 0.75 meters. Plugging in the values: A = π(0.75)² = 0.5625π square meters. However, since we're looking for the area in square meters and not just π times the value, we multiply by 0.5625 to get the correct answer: 0.5625π * π = 3.1416π square meters. The question asks for the area in square meters, so we need to include the factor of 0.5625. Therefore, the correct answer is C: 3.14π.

Explanation:
The formula for the area of a circle was applied correctly, but the mistake made by choosing A or B would be not squaring the radius or not multiplying by π. Choosing D would be an error in using the wrong radius value.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Not squaring the radius or including π in the final calculation.

Problem 4 (4):
A circular swimming pool has a circumference of 31.4 meters. What is the diameter of the pool in meters?
A) 5
B) 10
C) 15
D) 20
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The formula for the circumference C of a circle is C = 2πr, where r is the radius. Given that the circumference is 31.4 meters, we can solve for the radius: 31.4 = 2πr. Solving for r: r = 31.4 / (2π) ≈ 5 meters. The diameter d is twice the radius, so d = 2 * 5 = 10 meters. Therefore, the correct answer is B: 10.

Explanation:
The circumference formula was used to find the radius, and then the diameter was calculated correctly by multiplying the radius by 2. Choosing A or C would be a mistake due to underestimating or overestimating the radius based on the circumference value. Choosing D would be an error in not doubling the radius to get the diameter.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes: Not using the circumference formula correctly, dividing instead of multiplying by 2 for the diameter, or rounding errors.

Problem 7 (7):
A circular pizza has a diameter of 30 cm. If it is cut into 8 equal slices, what is the area of one slice in square centimeters?
A) 11.78π
B) 23.56π
C) 47.12π
D) 94.25π
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The area of one slice is 1/8th of the total area, since there are 8 slices. First, we find the total area A using the formula A = πr², where r is the radius (half the diameter): A = π(15)² = 225π square centimeters. Then, we divide by 8: (225π / 8) ≈ 11.78π square centimeters. Therefore, the correct answer is A: 11.78π.

Explanation:
The area of one slice was calculated correctly by dividing the total area by 8 after finding it using the circle area formula. Choosing B or C would be a mistake due to not dividing by 8 or using an incorrect radius value. Choosing D would be an error in overestimating the area of one slice.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes: Not dividing the total area by 8, using the wrong radius, or multiplying by π unnecessarily.
