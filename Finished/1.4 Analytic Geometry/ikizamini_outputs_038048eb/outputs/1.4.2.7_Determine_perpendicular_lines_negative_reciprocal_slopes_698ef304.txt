Problem 1 (:1):
Which line is perpendicular to the line with equation y = 3x + 2?
A) y = -1/3x + 4
B) y = 3x - 5
C) y = -3x + 2
D) y = x + 3
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The slope of the given line is 3. The negative reciprocal of 3 is -1/3, which means the perpendicular line will have a slope of -1/3. Therefore, option A is the correct answer.

Explanation:
Perpendicular lines have slopes that are negative reciprocals of each other.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose an option with the same slope or no slope (a vertical line), which are not perpendicular to the given line.

Problem 2 (:2):
Find the equation of a line that is perpendicular to y = -4x + 1 and passes through the point (3, 5).
A) y = 1/4x - 7
B) y = -1/4x + 9
C) y = 4x - 7
D) y = -4x + 13
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The slope of the given line is -4. The negative reciprocal of -4 is 1/4, which will be the slope of the perpendicular line. Using the point-slope form (y - y1 = m(x - x1)), we can find the equation with the point (3, 5) and slope 1/4: y - 5 = 1/4(x - 3). Solving for y gives us option B.

Explanation:
To find the perpendicular line passing through a specific point, use the negative reciprocal of the original slope and plug in the coordinates of the point into the point-slope form.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to apply the negative reciprocal when finding the slope of the perpendicular line.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (:3):
Which pair of lines are perpendicular?
A) (y = 2x + 1) and (y = -1/2x - 3)
B) (y = x - 5) and (y = x + 7)
C) (y = -3x + 4) and (y = 3x - 8)
D) (y = 5x - 2) and (y = 1/5x + 6)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The slopes of the lines in option A are 2 and -1/2, which are negative reciprocals. Therefore, they are perpendicular. The other options do not have negative reciprocal slopes.

Explanation:
Two lines are perpendicular if their slopes are negative reciprocals of each other.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to overlook the requirement that both lines must have negative reciprocal slopes.

Problem 4 (:4):
Find the slope of a line perpendicular to the line passing through the points (2, 3) and (5, -1).
A) -2/3
B) 2/3
C) -3/2
D) 3/2
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
First, find the slope of the given line using the formula (y2 - y1) / (x2 - x1): (-1 - 3) / (5 - 2) = -4 / 3. The negative reciprocal of this slope is -3/2, which is option C.

Explanation:
To find the slope of a line perpendicular to another line, take the negative reciprocal of the original line's slope.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to take the negative reciprocal when finding the slope of the perpendicular line.

Problem 5 (:5):
Which equation represents a line that is perpendicular to y = -x + 7 and has the same y-intercept?
A) y = x - 7
B) y = -1/2x + 7
C) y = -x + 7
D) y = 1/2x + 7
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The slope of the given line is -1. The negative reciprocal of -1 is 1/2, which will be the slope of the perpendicular line. Since both lines have the same y-intercept (7), option D is correct.

Explanation:
To find a line perpendicular to another with the same y-intercept, use the negative reciprocal of the original slope and keep the y-intercept unchanged.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose an option with a different y-intercept, which would not result in two lines with the same starting point.

Explanation:
To find the perpendicular line passing through a specific point, use the negative reciprocal of the original slope and plug in the coordinates of the point into the point-slope form.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to apply the negative reciprocal when finding the slope of the perpendicular line.
