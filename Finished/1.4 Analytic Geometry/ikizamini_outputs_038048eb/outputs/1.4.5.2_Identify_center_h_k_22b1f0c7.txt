Problem 1 (Asdf):
What is the center of the circle represented by the equation πr^2 + 6πr + 9 = 0?
A) (3, -1)
B) (-3, 1)
C) (-3, -1)
D) (3, 1)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The equation is a perfect square trinomial: (ar + b)^2 = 0. Expanding gives a^2r^2 + 2abr + b^2 = 0. Solving for a and b, we get a = 3 and b = -1. The center is (-b, a), or (1, 3). However, the question asks for (h, k), so the correct answer is (-3, -1).

Explanation:
Mistake: Directly interpreting coefficients without recognizing perfect square trinomial. Incorrect values for 'a' and 'b' lead to an incorrect center.

Common mistake note:
Misinterpreting equation as standard form instead of a perfect square trinomial.

Problem 2 (Asdf2):
Find the center of the circle with equation x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 8y = 0.
A) (3, -4)
B) (-3, 4)
C) (-3, -4)
D) (3, 4)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Complete the square for both x and y: (x^2 - 6x + 9) + (y^2 + 8y + 16) = 9 + 16, or (x - 3)^2 + (y + 4)^2 = 25. The center is (-b, a), so for x - 3, it's (3, 0); for y + 4, it's (0, -4). Combining gives (3, -4).

Explanation:
Common mistakes: Forgetting to complete the square for both variables or neglecting to adjust the constant term.

Common mistake note:
Forgetting to complete the square for both x and y or not adjusting the constant term.

Problem 3 (Asdf3):
What is the center of the circle with equation (x - 5)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 9?
A) (5, -2)
B) (-5, 2)
C) (-5, -2)
D) (5, 2)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation is in standard form: (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2. Here, h = 5 and k = -2, so the center is (5, -2).

Explanation:
Common mistake: Assuming additional calculations are needed when the equation is already in standard form.

Common mistake note:
Assuming extra steps are required when equation is in standard form.

Problem 4 (Asdf4):
Find the center of the circle with equation 9x^2 + 9y^2 - 18x + 36y = -32.
A) (2, -4)
B) (-2, 4)
C) (-2, -4)
D) (2, 4)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Divide by 9: x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 4y = -π. Complete the square: (x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 5. The center is (-b, a), so for x - 1, it's (1, 0); for y + 2, it's (0, -2). Combining gives (1, -2) or (2, -4) simplified.

Explanation:
Common mistakes: Forgetting to divide by 9, errors in simplifying, not recognizing constant term adjustment.

Common mistake note:
Forgetting to simplify before completing the square, simplification errors, or neglecting to adjust constant term.
