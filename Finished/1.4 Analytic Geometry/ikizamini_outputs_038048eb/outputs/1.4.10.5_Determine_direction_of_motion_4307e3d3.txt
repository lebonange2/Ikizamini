Problem 1 (:1):
An object is moving from left to right on a screen. What is its direction of motion?
A) Up
B) Down
C) Left
D) Right
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The object's direction of motion is to the right.

Explanation:
Direction of motion refers to the path an object takes. In this case, the object moves from left to right.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be choosing 'Left', which is opposite to the actual direction.

Problem 6 (:6):
A satellite orbits around Earth in an elliptical path. What is its average direction of motion?
A) Towards Earth's center
B) Away from Earth's center
C) Random
D) Tangential to the orbit
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The satellite's average direction of motion is tangential to the orbit.

Explanation:
Direction of motion in this context refers to the satellite's path around Earth. The satellite follows a tangential path at all points on its orbit.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be choosing 'Towards Earth's center' or 'Away from Earth's center', which is not consistent with the satellite's elliptical orbit.

Problem 7 (:7):
A rollercoaster car goes up a steep incline and then down a drop. What are the two main directions of motion?
A) Up, Down
B) Left, Right
C) Forward, Backward
D) Diagonal, Vertical
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The rollercoaster car's main directions of motion are up and down.

Explanation:
Direction of motion in this context refers to changes in elevation. The car moves upwards on the incline and then downwards during the drop.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be choosing 'Left, Right' or 'Forward, Backward', which are not the primary directions of motion in this scenario.
