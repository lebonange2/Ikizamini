Problem 1 (Asdf):
A cycloid is the path traced by a point on the rim of a rolling wheel. Which equation models the vertical position y of a point on a cycloid with radius 3 units as it rolls along the x-axis?
A) y = 2πx - 4x^2
B) y = 3πωx - x^2
C) y = 3πωx - 2x^2
D) y = 3πx - 3x^2
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The correct equation for the vertical position y of a point on a cycloid with radius r as it rolls along the x-axis is given by y = r(πx - x^2/2). In this case, r = 3. Substituting r into the formula gives us y = 3πx - (3x^2)/2, which simplifies to y = 3πx - 3x^2.

Explanation:
The cycloid equation models the path traced by a point on the rim of a wheel. The formula provided in choice D correctly represents this path for a given radius.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget the factor of π or to use the wrong variable for the radius.

Problem 2 (Asdf2):
What is the period T of the cycloid function y = 4πωx - 2x^2, where r = 4?
A) π
B) 2π
C) 4π
D) 8π
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The period of a cycloid function y = r(πx - x^2/2) is given by the length of one complete cycle along the x-axis. For a cycloid with radius r, the period T is equal to 2r. In this case, r = 4, so the period T = 2 * 4 = 8. Therefore, the period of the function y = 4πωx - 2x^2 is 8 units.

Explanation:
The period of a cycloid is determined by its radius. Since r = 4 in this case, the period is twice the radius.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to confuse the period with the coefficient of π or to misunderstand how the radius affects the period.

Problem 3 (Asdf3):
A cycloid is generated by a wheel with radius 5 units rolling along the x-axis. At what value of x does the point on the rim of the wheel first reach its maximum vertical height above the x-axis?
A) 0
B) 5
C) π
D) 2π
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The maximum vertical height of a point on the cycloid occurs when the wheel has rolled forward just enough for the point to be directly above its starting position. At this point, the horizontal distance traveled by the wheel is equal to its circumference, which is 2πr, where r is the radius. For a wheel with radius 5 units, the circumference is 10π. Therefore, at x = 10π, the point on the rim reaches its maximum height.

Explanation:
The maximum vertical height of a cycloid occurs when the horizontal distance traveled by the wheel equals its circumference. This can be calculated using the radius and the value of π.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that the point reaches its maximum height at the circumference or to use the wrong formula for calculating the circumference.

Problem 4 (Asdf4):
The equation y = 6πx - 3x^2 models a cycloid with radius r. What is the value of r?
A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 6
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The equation for a cycloid with radius r is y = r(πx - x^2/2). Comparing this to the given equation y = 6πx - 3x^2, we can see that r must be equal to the coefficient of π, which is 6 in this case. However, since the term inside the parentheses is multiplied by 1/2, we need to divide 6 by 2 to get the actual radius: r = 6 / 2 = 3.

Explanation:
The radius of a cycloid can be determined by comparing the equation given for the cycloid with the general form y = r(πx - x^2/2).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to overlook the factor of 1/2 inside the parentheses or to misinterpret the coefficient of π as the radius directly.
