Problem 1 (Q1):
What is the capital of France?
A) London
B) Paris
C) Berlin
D) Rome
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The capital of France is Paris.

Explanation:
Paris is the capital city of France, known for its iconic landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower and Louvre Museum.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting London (the capital of England) or Berlin (the capital of Germany).

Problem 2 (Q2):
What is photosynthesis?
A) The process by which plants convert sunlight into energy
B) The movement of animals during seasonal changes
C) The breakdown of complex molecules into simpler ones
D) The growth and development of an organism from birth to maturity
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Photosynthesis is the process by which plants, algae, and some bacteria convert sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide into glucose and oxygen.

Explanation:
During photosynthesis, plants use chlorophyll to capture light energy, which is then used to produce glucose for growth and release oxygen as a byproduct.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting processes like migration (B) or cellular respiration (C), which are not related to plant energy production.

Problem 3 (Q3):
What is the boiling point of water in degrees Celsius?
A) 100
B) -100
C) 212
D) 0
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The boiling point of water at standard atmospheric pressure (1 atmosphere) is 100 degrees Celsius or 212 degrees Fahrenheit.

Explanation:
Water boils when its temperature reaches 100°C, causing the liquid to transform into vapor. This temperature may vary with changes in air pressure.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting the freezing point of water (D) or temperatures associated with boiling water at higher elevations where atmospheric pressure is lower (C).

Problem 4 (Q4):
What is the largest organ in the human body?
A) Liver
B) Skin
C) Brain
D) Lungs
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The skin is the largest organ in the human body, covering and protecting our internal organs.

Explanation:
The skin serves multiple functions, including acting as a barrier against pathogens, regulating body temperature, and sensing touch and other sensations.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting organs like the liver (A), brain (C), or lungs (D), which are important but not as large in terms of surface area.

Problem 5 (Q5):
What is the formula for calculating the area of a circle?
A) Area = πr²
B) Area = 2πrh
C) Area = (1/2)bh
D) Area = lw
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The formula for calculating the area of a circle is Area = π times the radius squared (Area = πr²).

Explanation:
π (pi) is a mathematical constant approximately equal to 3.14, and r is the radius of the circle. The other choices are formulas for different shapes like cylinders (B), triangles (C), or rectangles (D).

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting formulas associated with other geometric shapes.

Problem 6 (Q6):
What is the process by which Earth's atmosphere regulates temperature?
A) Photosynthesis
B) Nuclear fusion
C) Greenhouse effect
D) Radiation
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The greenhouse effect is the process by which Earth's atmosphere traps heat, regulating the planet's temperature and making it habitable.

Explanation:
Greenhouse gases like carbon dioxide and water vapor absorb and re-emit infrared radiation, creating a warming effect on the surface. This balance of incoming solar radiation and outgoing heat keeps Earth's temperature within a livable range.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting processes not directly related to atmospheric temperature regulation (A, B, D).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Q7):
What is the smallest unit of an element that retains its chemical properties?
A) Atom
B) Molecule
C) Ion
D) Compound
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
An atom is the smallest unit of an element that retains its chemical properties. Atoms combine to form molecules, ions, and compounds.

Explanation:
Atoms are composed of protons, neutrons, and electrons, which determine their chemical behavior. Molecules, ions, and compounds consist of two or more atoms bonded together.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting larger structures like molecules (B), ions (C), or compounds (D) that result from the combination of atoms.
