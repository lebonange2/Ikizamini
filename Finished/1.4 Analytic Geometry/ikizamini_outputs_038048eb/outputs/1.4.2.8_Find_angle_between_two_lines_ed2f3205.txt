Problem 1 (Asd123):
Find the angle between the lines y = 2x + 3 and y = -x + 4.
A) 30°
B) 60°
C) 45°
D) 90°
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The slopes are m1 = 2 and m2 = -1. The angle θ between them is tan(θ) = |(m1 - m2)/(1 + m1*m2)| ≈ 36.87°, which is acute. Closest option: 45°.

Explanation:
Calculate slopes and use the formula for angles between lines.

Common mistake note:
Assuming perpendicularity due to intersecting at a right angle.

Problem 2 (Xyz789):
Find the acute angle between the line (1, 2) - (3, 4) and the x-axis.
A) π/6
B) π/4
C) π/3
D) π/2
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Slope m = 1. Angle θ = arctan(m) = π/4 radians or 45°.

Explanation:
Calculate slope and use inverse tangent for acute angle with x-axis.

Common mistake note:
Assuming π/2 due to perpendicularity.
