Problem 1 (:1):
What is the general form of a circle's equation in the xy-plane?
A) x  + y  + D  = 0
B) x²  + y²  + Dx  + Ey  + F  = 0
C) x²  + y²  - 2xy  = 0
D) ax²  + by²  + cx  + dy  + e  = 0
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The general form of a circle's equation in the xy-plane is x²  + y²  + Dx  + Ey  + F  = 0, where D, E, and F are constants. This form represents the set of all points (x, y) that are equidistant from the center of the circle.

Explanation:
The general form given includes the squared terms for x and y, which is necessary to represent a circle. The other choices either lack the squared terms or have different structures.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A, which represents a linear equation rather than a circle.

Problem 2 (2):
What term in the general form x²  + y²  + Dx  + Ey  + F  = 0 represents the horizontal translation of the circle's center?
A) x²
B) y²
C) D
D) F
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The term 'D' in the general form x²  + y²  + Dx  + Ey  + F  = 0 represents the horizontal translation of the circle's center from the origin (0, 0). It shifts the circle along the x-axis.

Explanation:
The coefficient D multiplies x, which affects the horizontal position of the circle. A and B represent the radii of the circle, C represents the vertical translation, and D represents the horizontal translation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or B, which are related to the radius rather than the center's position.

Problem 4 (4):
What term in the general form x²  + y²  + Dx  + Ey  + F  = 0 represents the sum of the squares of the radii?
A) x²
B) y²
C) D
D) F
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The term 'y²' in the general form x²  + y²  + Dx  + Ey  + F  = 0 represents the sum of the squares of the radii, which determines the circle's size. The total is (x-h)²  + (y-k)², where h and k are the coordinates of the center.

Explanation:
A and B represent the radii, C and D represent translations, and F represents the sum of the squares of the radii after translation. The term 'y²' alone does not give the actual radius; it's part of the equation that determines the circle's size.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or D, which are related to translations or the center rather than the circle's size.

B) E = 0

D) No specific value for E
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
When E = 0 in the general form x²  + y²  + Dx  + Ey  + F  = 0, the circle is centered at the origin (0, 0). This occurs because there is no vertical translation.

Explanation:
For a circle to be centered at the origin, there should be no vertical shift. In the general form, E represents the vertical translation; setting E = 0 eliminates any vertical movement, placing the center at the origin.
