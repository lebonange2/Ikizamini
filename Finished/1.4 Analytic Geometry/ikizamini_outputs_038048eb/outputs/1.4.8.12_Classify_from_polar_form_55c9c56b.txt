Problem 1 (Asdf):
Convert the complex number 3cis(60°) to rectangular form.
A) (3, -√3)
B) (√3, 3)
C) (1.5, 1.5√3)

Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The polar form of a complex number is given by $r	ext{cis}(	heta)$ where $r$ is the modulus and $	heta$ is the argument in radians. To convert from polar to rectangular form, we use Euler's formula: $z = r(	ext{cos}	heta + i	ext{sin}	heta)$. For $3	ext{cis}(60°)$, we have $r=3$ and $	heta=rac{	ext{π}}{3}$ radians. Substituting these values into Euler's formula gives us the rectangular form: $z = 3(	ext{cos}(rac{	ext{π}}{3}) + i	ext{sin}(rac{	ext{π}}{3}))$. Evaluating this expression, we get $z = 3(rac{1}{2} + rac{	ext{i}	ext{√}3}{2}) = 1.5 + 1.5	ext{i}	ext{√}3$, which is equivalent to $(1.5, 1.5	ext{√}3)$.

Explanation:
The correct answer uses the correct values for $r$ and $	heta$ and applies Euler's formula correctly.

Problem 2 (Asdf):
Identify the type of the complex number -2cis(270°).
A) Real
B) Purely imaginary
C) Complex conjugate
D) Zero
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The polar form $z = r	ext{cis}(	heta)$ gives us the modulus $r$ and argument $	heta$. For -2cis(270°), we have $r = -2$ and $	heta = rac{3	ext{π}}{2}$ radians. Explanation:

Common mistake note:
