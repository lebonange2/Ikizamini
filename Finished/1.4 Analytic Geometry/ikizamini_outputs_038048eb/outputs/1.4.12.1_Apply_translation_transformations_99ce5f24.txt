Problem 1 (One-dimensional Translation):
Given a point P(3, 0) that is translated by 5 units to the right, what are the coordinates of the new point?
A) (8, 0)
B) (3, 5)
C) (3, -5)
D) (0, 5)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The translation moves the point along the x-axis by 5 units to the right. So, we add 5 to the x-coordinate of P: (3 + 5, 0) = (8, 0).

Explanation:
Translation transformations change the position of a shape without changing its size or orientation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake is to add the translation value to both coordinates. Remember, translations only affect the coordinate in the direction of the movement.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Two-dimensional Translation):
A triangle with vertices A(1, 2), B(3, 4), and C(5, 6) is translated by the vector (2, -3). What are the new coordinates of vertex A?
A) (3, -1)
B) (1, 5)
C) (5, 3)
D) (3, 6)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Translate each coordinate by adding the vector values: A'(1 + 2, 2 - 3) = (3, -1).

Explanation:
In two-dimensional translations, both x- and y-coordinates are changed according to the given vector.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake is to apply the translation separately to each coordinate. The entire vector should be applied to both coordinates simultaneously.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Translation along a line):
A parallelogram with vertices D(-2, 1), E(-4, -3), F(-6, -1), and G(-4, 3) is translated by the point (3, 2). What are the new coordinates of vertex D?
A) (1, 3)
B) (-5, -1)
C) (0, 0)
D) (-5, 5)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Translate each coordinate by adding the corresponding values from the point (3, 2): D'(-2 + 3, 1 + 2) = (1, 3).

Explanation:
Translation moves every point in a shape by the same distance and direction.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake is to mix up the addition order. Always add the x-value from the translation point to the original x-coordinate, and do the same for the y-values.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Translation with negative values):
A rectangle with vertices W(-3, 4), X(-1, 2), Y(1, 4), and Z(3, 2) is translated by the vector (-5, -2). What are the new coordinates of vertex W?
A) (2, 6)
B) (-8, 2)
C) (-8, 0)
D) (2, 0)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Translate each coordinate by adding the vector values: W'(-3 - 5, 4 - 2) = (-8, 2).

Explanation:
Negative translation values move points in the opposite direction of the positive values.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake is to interpret negative translations as subtraction instead of addition. Remember, you're still adding the vector value.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (Translation across axes):
Point P(-5, 7) is translated by 8 units along the x-axis and 3 units along the y-axis. What are the new coordinates of point P?
A) (3, 10)
B) (-13, 4)
C) (-13, 10)
D) (3, 4)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Translate along each axis separately: P'(-5 + 8, 7 + 3) = (3, 10).

Explanation:
When translating across both axes, apply the x-translation to the x-coordinate and the y-translation to the y-coordinate.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake is to mix up the translations for different axes. Apply each translation separately.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (Translation of a circle):
A circle with center C(2, 3) and radius 4 is translated by the vector (1, -2). What are the new coordinates of the center of the circle?
A) (3, 5)
B) (1, 1)
C) (3, 1)
D) (1, 5)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Translate the center of the circle: C'(2 + 1, 3 - 2) = (3, 1). The radius remains unchanged.

Explanation:
Translation affects only the position of a shape's center or vertices; it does not change its size or shape.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake is to think that the radius changes. Remember, translation doesn't affect the size of the circle.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Translation and reflection):
A square with vertices A(0, 0), B(2, 0), C(2, 2), and D(0, 2) is translated by 3 units to the right and then reflected across the y-axis. What are the new coordinates of vertex A?
A) (3, 0)
B) (-3, 0)
C) (6, 2)
D) (-3, 2)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Translate first: A'(3, 0). Then reflect across the y-axis: A''(-3, 0).

Explanation:
When applying multiple transformations, perform them in the correct order (translation then reflection).

Common mistake note:
Common mistake is to mix up the order of transformations. Always apply translations before reflections.
