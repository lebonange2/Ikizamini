Problem 1 (AslQz):
Find the arc length of a circle with radius 5 units and central angle of 60 degrees.
A) 2.5π units
B) 5π/3 units
C) π units
D) 10π/3 units
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The formula for arc length is L = rθ, where L is the arc length, r is the radius, and θ is the central angle in radians. First, convert the 60-degree angle to radians: θ = (60° * π) / 180° = π/3 radians. Then calculate the arc length: L = 5 * (π/3) = 5π/3 units.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by applying the formula for arc length and converting the angle to radians.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is not converting the degree measure of the central angle to radians before plugging it into the formula.
