Problem 1 (:1):
Convert the quadratic equation to general form: y = 2(x - 3)^2 + 4.
A) y = 2x^2 - 12x + 22
B) y = 2x^2 + 12x + 10
C) y = 2x^2 - 6x + 13
D) y = 2x^2 + 6x + 7
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To convert the given equation to general form, we expand and simplify. The general form is y = ax^2 + bx + c.

Explanation:
y = 2(x - 3)^2 + 4
y = 2(x^2 - 6x + 9) + 4
y = 2x^2 - 12x + 18 + 4
y = 2x^2 - 12x + 22

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Not expanding the squared term correctly. Some students may forget to multiply the coefficient of x by 2 when expanding (x - 3)^2.

Problem 2 (:2):
Convert the quadratic equation to general form: y = -(x + 5)(x - 1).
A) y = -x^2 - 4x + 5
B) y = x^2 + 6x - 5
C) y = -x^2 - 6x + 5
D) y = x^2 - 4x - 5
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To convert the given equation to general form, we multiply the binomials and simplify.

Explanation:
y = -(x + 5)(x - 1)
y = -(x^2 - x + 5x - 5)
y = -(x^2 + 4x - 5)
y = -x^2 - 6x + 5

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Incorrectly distributing the negative sign. Some students may distribute the negative sign to only one term instead of both terms in each binomial.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (:3):
Convert the quadratic equation to general form: y = (x - 2)(x + 3) - 4.
A) y = x^2 + x - 10
B) y = x^2 + x - 6
C) y = x^2 - x - 10
D) y = x^2 - x - 6
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To convert the given equation to general form, we multiply the binomials and simplify.

Explanation:
y = (x - 2)(x + 3) - 4
y = (x^2 + 3x - 2x - 6) - 4
y = x^2 + x - 10

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Forgetting to multiply the constant term in the second binomial. Some students may forget to multiply -4 by (x - 2).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (:4):
Convert the quadratic equation to general form: y = 3(x + 1)^2 - 2(x + 1) - 1.
A) y = 3x^2 + 5x - 2
B) y = 3x^2 + x - 6
C) y = 3x^2 + 4x - 4
D) y = 3x^2 + 2x - 1
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To convert the given equation to general form, we expand and simplify.

Explanation:
y = 3(x + 1)^2 - 2(x + 1) - 1
y = 3(x^2 + 2x + 1) - 2x - 2 - 1
y = 3x^2 + 6x + 3 - 2x - 3
y = 3x^2 + 4x

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Not distributing the coefficient of x correctly in the expanded term. Some students may distribute 3 to only one term instead of two terms.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (:5):
Convert the quadratic equation to general form: y = -2(x - 4)(x + 2) + 1.
A) y = -2x^2 + 4x - 7
B) y = -2x^2 + 8x - 9
C) y = -2x^2 + 6x - 3
D) y = -2x^2 + 10x - 9
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
To convert the given equation to general form, we multiply the binomials and simplify.

Explanation:
y = -2(x - 4)(x + 2) + 1
y = -2(x^2 + 2x - 4x - 8) + 1
y = -2(x^2 - 2x - 8) + 1
y = -2x^2 + 4x + 16 + 1
y = -2x^2 + 4x + 17

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Forgetting to distribute the negative sign in front of the binomial product. Some students may not multiply -2 by (x - 4)(x + 2).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (:6):
Convert the quadratic equation to general form: y = 4(x - 1/2)^2 - 3.
A) y = 4x^2 - 4x - 1
B) y = 4x^2 - 2x - 7/4
C) y = 4x^2 - 2x - 5/4
D) y = 4x^2 - 4x - 9/4
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
To convert the given equation to general form, we expand and simplify.

Explanation:
y = 4(x - 1/2)^2 - 3
y = 4(x^2 - x + 1/4) - 3
y = 4x^2 - 4x + 1 - 3
y = 4x^2 - 4x - 2

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Incorrectly squaring the binomial. Some students may square only one term instead of both terms in (x - 1/2).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (:7):
Convert the quadratic equation to general form: y = -(x + 3/2)(x - 5/4).
A) y = -x^2 - x + 19/8
B) y = -x^2 - x + 7/8
C) y = -x^2 + 17/8x - 15/8
D) y = -x^2 + 17/8x - 23/8
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To convert the given equation to general form, we multiply the binomials and simplify.

Explanation:
y = -(x + 3/2)(x - 5/4)
y = -(x^2 - (5/4)x + (3/2)x - (3/2)(5/4))
y = -(x^2 - (5/4)x + (3/2)x - 15/8)
y = -x^2 + (5/4)x - (3/2)x + 15/8
y = -x^2 + (5/4 - 3/2)x + 15/8
y = -x^2 + 17/8x - 15/8

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Incorrectly multiplying the fractions. Some students may not correctly multiply the numerators and denominators when multiplying the binomials.
