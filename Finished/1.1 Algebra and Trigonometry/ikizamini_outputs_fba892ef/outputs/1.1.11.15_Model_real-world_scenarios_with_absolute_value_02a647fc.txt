Problem 1 (:1):
A store offers a discount on an item that originally costs $50. The discount is given as the absolute value of 20% of the original price. How much is the discount?
A) $10
B) $20
C) $30
D) $40
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The absolute value of 20% of $50 is |0.20 * $50| = $10. Since the discount is given as the absolute value, it's $10 regardless if it's a reduction or increase in price.

Explanation:
This question tests understanding of applying absolute values to real-world scenarios and calculating discounts based on percentages.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to interpret 20% as a decrease directly without considering the absolute value, which would lead to choice A ($10) instead of the correct answer B ($20).

Problem 2 (:2):
A delivery truck must travel 80 miles to reach its destination. Due to traffic, it ends up driving 10 miles more than planned in one direction and 5 miles less on the return trip. By how many miles did the total distance deviate from the original plan?
A) 0
B) 5
C) 10
D) 15
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The truck drove 10 miles more in one direction and 5 miles less on the return trip, so the total deviation is (10 - 5) = 5 miles.

Explanation:
This question assesses understanding of absolute value to determine the net change from a planned distance.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to add the deviations in both directions without considering the absolute value, which would lead to choice C (10 miles) instead of the correct answer B (5 miles).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (:3):
An elevator is on the 10th floor. It goes down 3 floors and then up 2 floors. What is the absolute difference between its current floor level and the starting floor?
A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 5
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The elevator went down 3 floors from 10 to 7, then up 2 floors to 9. The absolute difference between the starting floor (10) and current floor (9) is |10 - 9| = 1.

Explanation:
This question tests the use of absolute value to find the distance between two floor levels after multiple movements.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to subtract the final floor level from the starting floor without considering the absolute value, which would lead to choice D (5) instead of the correct answer A (1).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (:4):
A company's stock price starts at $100 and drops by 20% in one day. The next day, it rises by 25% from its new value. What is the absolute change in the stock price over the two days?
A) $5
B) $10
C) $15
D) $25
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The stock drops to $80 (100 - 20% of 100) and then rises to $100 (80 + 25% of 80). The absolute change is |$100 - $80| = $20.

Explanation:
This question requires understanding absolute value in the context of stock price changes with percentage increases and decreases.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to calculate the net change without considering the absolute value, which would lead to choice C ($15) instead of the correct answer A ($20).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (:5):
A student scores 75 on a test with a maximum possible score of 100. The teacher awards extra credit equivalent to the absolute difference between the student's score and 80. How much extra credit does the student receive?
A) 0
B) 5
C) 15
D) 25
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The absolute difference between the student's score (75) and 80 is |75 - 80| = 5. The student receives extra credit equivalent to this amount.

Explanation:
This question demonstrates how to use absolute value in a real-world scenario involving awarding extra credit based on performance.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to subtract the student's score from 80 without considering the absolute value, which would lead to choice D (25) instead of the correct answer B (5).

Problem 6 (:6):
An athlete runs a course in 12 minutes and then improves their time by running it 3 minutes faster. By how many minutes did their new time deviate from the original time?
A) -3
B) 0
C) 3
D) 6
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The athlete improved their time by 3 minutes, so the deviation is +3 minutes (not negative because an improvement is considered positive).

Explanation:
This question assesses understanding of absolute value in a sports context where a decrease in time is viewed as an improvement.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to consider the decrease in time as a negative deviation, leading to choice A (-3) instead of the correct answer C (3).

Problem 7 (:7):
A company's monthly revenue starts at $50,000 and decreases by 15% in one month. The next month, it increases by 20% from its new value. What is the absolute percentage change in revenue over the two months?
A) 5%
B) 7.5%
C) 12.5%
D) 15%
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The company's revenue decreases to $42,500 (50,000 - 15% of 50,000) and then increases to $51,000 (42,500 + 20% of 42,500). The absolute percentage change is |(51,000 / 50,000 - 1)| * 100 = 7.5%.

Explanation:
This question requires understanding absolute value in the context of calculating percentage changes with two consecutive increases and decreases.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to calculate the net percentage change without considering the absolute value, which would lead to choice C (12.5%) instead of the correct answer B (7.5%).
