Problem 1 (:1):
Find the sum of the infinite geometric series with first term a = 3 and common ratio r = 0.5.
A) a
B) b
C) c
D) d
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The formula for an infinite geometric series is S = a / (1 - r), where 'a' is the first term and 'r' is the common ratio. Plugging in a = 3 and r = 0.5, we get S = 3 / (1 - 0.5) = 6.

Explanation:
The sum of an infinite geometric series converges if |r| < 1. Since |0.5| < 1, the series converges to 6.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to think that because the terms decrease, the sum must also decrease. However, in this case, the sum approaches a finite value due to the convergence condition.

Problem 2 (2):
Find the sum of the first 5 terms of the geometric series with first term a = 1 and common ratio r = 4.
A) a
B) b
C) c
D) d
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The formula for the sum of 'n' terms of a geometric series is S_n = a * (1 - r^n) / (1 - r). Plugging in a = 1, r = 4, and n = 5, we get S_5 = 1 * (1 - 4^5) / (1 - 4) = 63.

Explanation:
The sum of the first 'n' terms is calculated differently from the sum of an infinite series. Here, we use the formula for a finite geometric series.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to use the formula for an infinite series instead of the one for a finite number of terms.

Problem 3 (3):
Find the sum of the first 10 terms of the geometric series with first term a = 2 and common ratio r = -1/2.
A) a
B) b
C) c
D) d
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Using the formula for the sum of 'n' terms, S_n = a * (1 - r^n) / (1 - r), with a = 2, r = -1/2, and n = 10, we get S_10 = 2 * (1 - (-1/2)^10) / (1 - (-1/2)) = -61.

Explanation:
The sum of the first 'n' terms is calculated differently from the sum of an infinite series. Here, we use the formula for a finite geometric series with a negative common ratio.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that when dealing with negative common ratios, the sign of each term alternates.
