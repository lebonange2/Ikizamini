Problem 1 (Asd123):
Solve the equation for y: 3x + 2y = 8
A) y = (8 - 3x) / 2
B) y = 3x + 4
C) y = (8 + 3x) / 2
D) y = 8 - 3x
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To eliminate the parameter x, we need to solve for y. We start by subtracting 3x from both sides of the equation: 3x + 2y - 3x = 8 - 3x. This simplifies to 2y = 8 - 3x. Next, divide both sides by 2 to solve for y: (2y) / 2 = (8 - 3x) / 2. So, y = (8 - 3x) / 2.

Explanation:
Choice A is the correct solution because it correctly isolates y on one side of the equation after simplifying. Choices B, C, and D either do not isolate y or have incorrect arithmetic.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to divide both sides by 3 instead of subtracting 3x from both sides, leading to an incorrect solution.

Problem 2 (Xcv123):
Simplify the expression: (5a + 7b) / a
A) 5 + 7b/a
B) 5 + 7b
C) 5a + 7b
D) 7b/a
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To eliminate the parameter 'a', we can divide both terms in the numerator by 'a'. This gives us (5a / a) + (7b / a), which simplifies to 5 + 7b/a.

Explanation:
Choice A correctly simplifies the expression by dividing each term by 'a'. Choices B and C do not eliminate 'a', while Choice D only divides one term, resulting in an incomplete simplification.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to assume that 'a' can be cancelled out entirely, which is incorrect because it might be zero. In this case, we must keep the fraction form.

Problem 3 (Zxc123):
Factor out the greatest common factor from 6x^2 + 9xy:
A) 3x(2x + 3y)
B) 2x(3x + 9y)
C) 3y(x + 3x)
D) 6y(x + 3)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The greatest common factor of 6x^2 and 9xy is 3x. Factoring out 3x gives us 3x(2x + 3y).

Explanation:
Choice A correctly factors out the GCF, resulting in a simplified expression. Choices B, C, and D either have an incorrect factor or do not fully factor out the GCF.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to factor out only one of the variables (e.g., x or y) instead of finding the GCF that includes both.

Problem 4 (Qwe123):
Solve for z in terms of w: 4z = 8w + 16
A) z = 2w + 4
B) z = 2w - 4
C) z = 4w + 4
D) z = 4w - 4
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To eliminate the parameter 'w', we need to solve for 'z'. First, divide both sides by 4: (4z) / 4 = (8w + 16) / 4. This simplifies to z = 2w + 4.

Explanation:
Choice A correctly isolates 'z' after dividing by 4. Choices B, C, and D have incorrect arithmetic or do not fully isolate 'z'.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to divide only one side of the equation, leading to an imbalance and an incorrect solution.

Problem 5 (Rty123):
Simplify the expression: 4(2m + 5) - 8m
A) 8m + 20 - 8m
B) 16m + 20 - 8m
C) 8m + 20 - 16m
D) 8m + 40
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
First, distribute the 4 to both terms inside the parentheses: 8m + 20. Then, combine like terms with the -8m: (8m - 8m) + 20 = 20. So, the simplified expression is 20.

Explanation:
Choice C correctly simplifies the expression by distributing and combining like terms. Choices A, B, and D either have incorrect arithmetic or do not fully simplify the expression.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to distribute the 4 to both terms inside the parentheses, resulting in an incomplete simplification.

Problem 6 (Uij123):
Find the value of k when x = 2 and y = 3 for the equation 5x + 7y = k
A) 29
B) 41
C) 31
D) 16
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Substitute x = 2 and y = 3 into the equation: 5(2) + 7(3) = k. This simplifies to 10 + 21 = k, or k = 31.

Explanation:
Choice A correctly calculates the value of 'k' by substituting the given values for 'x' and 'y'. Choices B, C, and D have incorrect arithmetic or use incorrect values for 'x' and 'y'.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to substitute the wrong values for 'x' and 'y', leading to an incorrect result.

Problem 7 (Kol123):
Factor the quadratic expression: x^2 + 5x + 6
A) (x + 2)(x + 3)
B) (x - 2)(x - 3)
C) (x + 2)(x - 3)
D) (x - 2)(x + 3)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To factor the quadratic expression, find two numbers that multiply to give 6 (the constant term) and add up to give 5 (the coefficient of x). These numbers are 2 and 3. So, the factored form is (x + 2)(x + 3).

Explanation:
Choice A correctly factors the quadratic expression using the sum and product method. Choices B, C, and D have incorrect factor pairs that do not satisfy the given conditions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose factor pairs that multiply to give the coefficient of x instead of the constant term.
