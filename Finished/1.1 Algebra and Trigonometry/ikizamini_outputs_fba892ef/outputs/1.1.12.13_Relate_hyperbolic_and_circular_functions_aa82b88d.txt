Problem 1 (One):

A) sinh(x) = (1/2)(	an(x) + 	anh(x))

C) sinh(x) = (1/2)(	an(x)	anh(x))
D) sinh(x) = 	an(x)	anh(x)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The correct relation is sinh(x) = (1/2)(	an(x) - 	anh(x)). This relationship comes from the Taylor series expansion and the correspondence between circular and hyperbolic functions. The other choices are incorrect variations.

Explanation:
The connection between sinh(x) and sin(x) can be seen through their Taylor series expansions, where sinh(x) is expressed as a combination of tan(x) and tanh(x).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to confuse the subtraction in the correct formula with addition. This might happen if students are not familiar with the specific form of the relationship between hyperbolic and circular functions.
