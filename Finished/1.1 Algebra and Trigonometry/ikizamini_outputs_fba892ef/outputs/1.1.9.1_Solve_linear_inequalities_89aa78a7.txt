Problem 1 (:1):
Solve the inequality: 3x - 5 < 7
A) -4 < x < 4
B) 2 < x < 4
C) x > 4
D) x < 2
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To solve the inequality, we first add 5 to both sides and then divide by 3:
3x - 5 + 5 < 7 + 5
3x < 12
x < 4
Next, we divide by 3 without changing the direction of the inequality because 3 is positive.
The correct solution is x < 4, which corresponds to option B.

Explanation:
In this problem, we simplified the inequality step by step. The common mistake would be to change the direction of the inequality when dividing by a negative number, but since 3 is positive, the direction remains unchanged.

Common mistake note:
Dividing by a negative number would reverse the inequality direction, but here it's not applicable because we're dividing by a positive number.

Problem 2 (:2):
Solve the inequality: -2(x + 3) ≥ 8
A) x ≤ -7
B) x ≥ -7
C) x ≤ -5
D) x ≥ -5
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
First, distribute the -2:
-2x - 6 ≥ 8
Next, add 6 to both sides:
-2x ≥ 14
Finally, divide by -2 and remember to reverse the inequality direction since we're dividing by a negative number:
x ≤ -7

Explanation:
Here, we distributed the -2 across the parentheses and then simplified the inequality step by step. A common mistake would be forgetting to change the direction of the inequality when dividing by a negative number.

Common mistake note:
Dividing by a negative number reverses the inequality direction; in this case, it's crucial to remember that.

Problem 4 (:4):
Solve the inequality: x/3 + 2 > 5
A) x > 9
B) x < 9
C) x > 12
D) x < 12
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
First, subtract 2 from both sides:
x/3 > 3
Next, multiply by 3 (since 3 is positive, the direction of the inequality remains unchanged):
x > 9

Explanation:
We simplified the inequality step by step, considering the effect of multiplying or dividing by a positive number. A common mistake would be to forget that multiplying or dividing by a positive number does not change the direction of the inequality.

Common mistake note:
Multiplying or dividing by a positive number does not change the inequality direction; it's crucial to remember this.
