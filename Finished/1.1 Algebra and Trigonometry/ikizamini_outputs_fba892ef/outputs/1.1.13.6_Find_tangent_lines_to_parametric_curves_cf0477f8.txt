Problem 1 (1.1.13.6-1):
Given the parametric curve defined by x(t) = t^2 and y(t) = 2t, find the slope of the tangent line at t=1.
A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 4
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The derivative of x with respect to t is dx/dt = 2t, and the derivative of y with respect to t is dy/dt = 2. The slope of the tangent line at a particular value of t is given by dy/dx, which can be found using the derivatives and the original function as follows: dy/dx = (dy/dt) / (dx/dt). At t=1, dx/dt = 2(1) = 2 and dy/dt = 2. So, dy/dx = 2/2 = 1. Therefore, the slope of the tangent line at t=1 is 1.

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by calculating the derivative of y with respect to x using the parametric equations and evaluating it at t=1.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to directly use the value of y(t) (which is 2t) instead of finding the derivative dy/dx. This would lead to an incorrect slope of 2t, which is not constant and thus does not represent a tangent line.

Problem 2 (1.1.13.6-2):
Find the equation of the tangent line to the curve defined by x(t) = cos(t) and y(t) = sin(t) at t=π/4.
A) y = x - 1/√2
B) y = x + 1/√2
C) y = √2x - 1
D) y = √2x + 1
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The derivative of x with respect to t is dx/dt = -sin(t), and the derivative of y with respect to t is dy/dt = cos(t). At t=π/4, dx/dt = -sin(π/4) = -1/√2 and dy/dt = cos(π/4) = 1/√2. The slope of the tangent line at this point is m = dy/dx = (1/√2) / (-1/√2) = -1. At t=π/4, x(t) = cos(π/4) = √2/2 and y(t) = sin(π/4) = √2/2. Using the point-slope form of a line (y - y1 = m(x - x1)), we get y - √2/2 = -1(x - √2/2), which simplifies to y = x - 1/√2.

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by calculating the derivative of y with respect to x, evaluating it at t=π/4, and then using the point-slope form of a line to find the equation of the tangent line.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to use the coordinates (x(t), y(t)) directly as the point on the line without first finding the slope. This would lead to an incorrect equation that does not have the correct slope.

Problem 3 (1.1.13.6-3):
Find the equation of the tangent line to the curve defined by x(t) = 3t^2 and y(t) = t^3 at t=1.
A) y = 6x - 3
B) y = 6x + 3
C) y = 3x - 1
D) y = 3x + 1
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The derivative of x with respect to t is dx/dt = 6t, and the derivative of y with respect to t is dy/dt = 3t^2. At t=1, dx/dt = 6(1) = 6 and dy/dt = 3(1)^2 = 3. The slope of the tangent line at this point is m = dy/dx = (3) / (6) = 0.5. At t=1, x(t) = 3(1)^2 = 3 and y(t) = (1)^3 = 1. Using the point-slope form of a line (y - y1 = m(x - x1)), we get y - 1 = 0.5(x - 3), which simplifies to y = 6x - 3.

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by calculating the derivative of y with respect to x, evaluating it at t=1, and then using the point-slope form of a line to find the equation of the tangent line.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to mix up the derivatives or use incorrect values for x(t) and y(t) when plugging them into the point-slope form. This would result in an incorrect equation for the tangent line.

Problem 4 (1.1.13.6-4):
Given the parametric curve defined by x(t) = e^t and y(t) = t^2, find the slope of the tangent line at t=0.
A) 1
B) 2
C) e
D) 2e
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The derivative of x with respect to t is dx/dt = e^t, and the derivative of y with respect to t is dy/dt = 2t. The slope of the tangent line at a particular value of t is given by dy/dx, which can be found using the derivatives and the original function as follows: dy/dx = (dy/dt) / (dx/dt). At t=0, dx/dt = e^0 = 1 and dy/dt = 2(0) = 0. Since division by zero is undefined, we cannot find the slope of the tangent line at t=0. This means there is no tangent line at this point on the curve.

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by recognizing that the derivative of y with respect to x is undefined at t=0 due to a division by zero error. This indicates that there is no tangent line at this point.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to assume that the slope can be calculated as usual, leading to an incorrect value for dy/dx. This would result in an incorrect conclusion about the existence or equation of the tangent line.

Problem 5 (1.1.13.6-5):
Find the equation of the tangent line to the curve defined by x(t) = t^3 - 2t and y(t) = t^2 + 1 at t=-1.
A) y = 4x + 5
B) y = 4x - 5
C) y = -4x + 5
D) y = -4x - 5
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The derivative of x with respect to t is dx/dt = 3t^2 - 2, and the derivative of y with respect to t is dy/dt = 2t. At t=-1, dx/dt = 3(-1)^2 - 2 = 1 and dy/dt = 2(-1) = -2. The slope of the tangent line at this point is m = dy/dx = (-2) / (1) = -2. At t=-1, x(t) = (-1)^3 - 2(-1) = -1 + 2 = 1 and y(t) = (-1)^2 + 1 = 1 + 1 = 2. Using the point-slope form of a line (y - y1 = m(x - x1)), we get y - 2 = -2(x - 1), which simplifies to y = 4x + 5.

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by calculating the derivative of y with respect to x, evaluating it at t=-1, and then using the point-slope form of a line to find the equation of the tangent line.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to use an incorrect value for x(t) or y(t) when plugging them into the point-slope form. This would result in an incorrect equation for the tangent line.

Problem 7 (1.1.13.6-7):
Find the equation of the tangent line to the curve defined by x(t) = 2t^3 - t and y(t) = t^4 + 1 at t=1.
A) y = 6x + 5
B) y = 6x - 5
C) y = -6x + 5
D) y = -6x - 5
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The derivative of x with respect to t is dx/dt = 6t^2 - 1, and the derivative of y with respect to t is dy/dt = 4t^3. At t=1, dx/dt = 6(1)^2 - 1 = 5 and dy/dt = 4(1)^3 = 4. The slope of the tangent line at this point is m = dy/dx = (4) / (5) = 0.8. At t=1, x(t) = 2(1)^3 - 1 = 2 - 1 = 1 and y(t) = (1)^4 + 1 = 1 + 1 = 2. Using the point-slope form of a line (y - y1 = m(x - x1)), we get y - 2 = 0.8(x - 1), which simplifies to y = 6x + 5.

Explanation:
The correct answer is found by calculating the derivative of y with respect to x, evaluating it at t=1, and then using the point-slope form of a line to find the equation of the tangent line.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to use an incorrect value for x(t) or y(t) when plugging them into the point-slope form. This would result in an incorrect equation for the tangent line.
