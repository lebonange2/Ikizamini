Problem 1 (AssembleRegionEasy01):
Solve the inequality: 2x + 3 < 7
A) -2 < x < 2
B) x < 2
C) x > -2
D) No solution
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Subtract 3 from both sides: 2x < 4
Divide by 2: x < 2

Explanation:
The correct solution region is where x is less than 2.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A, which includes an equal sign. The inequality does not include 'or equals to' so x cannot be equal to 2.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (AssembleRegionEasy02):
Solve the inequality: -4 < 3x + 1
A) -5 < x
B) x > -5/3
C) x < -5/3
D) No solution
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Subtract 1 from both sides: -5 < 3x
Divide by 3: x > -5/3

Explanation:
The correct solution region is where x is greater than -5/3.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing C, which reverses the direction of the inequality. When dividing or multiplying by a negative number, the inequality sign must be flipped.

Problem 3 (AssembleRegionMedium01):
Solve the system of inequalities:
2x + y < 6
x - y > 1
A) x > 1 and y < 4
B) x < 1 and y > 4
C) x > 1 and y < 1
D) x < 1 and y > 1
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Solve for x in the second inequality: x > y + 1
Substitute into first inequality: 2(y + 1) + y < 6
3y + 2 < 6
y < 4
Combine with x > y + 1 to get x > 1

Explanation:
The correct solution region is where x is greater than 1 and y is less than 4.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B, which reverses the direction of the inequalities. The system does not indicate that x or y should be less than their respective values.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (AssembleRegionMedium02):
Solve the system of inequalities:
-3x + 2y ≤ 6
x - 4y < -8
A) x ≥ -2 and y > 1
B) x < -2 and y ≤ 1
C) x > -2 and y < 1
D) x ≤ -2 and y > 1
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Solve for x in the second inequality: x < 4y - 8
Substitute into first inequality: -3(4y - 8) + 2y ≤ 6
-12y + 24 + 2y ≤ 6
-10y ≤ -18
y ≥ 1.8
Combine with x < 4y - 8 to get x ≥ -2

Explanation:
The correct solution region is where x is greater than or equal to -2 and y is greater than or equal to 1.8.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing C, which does not account for the inequality sign being less than or equal to in the first equation.

Problem 5 (AssembleRegionMedium03):
Solve the system of inequalities:
|x - 1| < 2
|y + 3| > 4
A) -5 < y < -7 and 3 < x < 5
B) -7 < y < -3 and -1 < x < 3
C) y < -7 or y > -3 and x < -1 or x > 3
D) y < -5 or y > -3 and x < 3 or x > 5
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Solve for x: -2 < x - 1 < 2
-1 < x < 3
Solve for y: y + 3 > 4 or y + 3 < -4
y > 1 or y < -7

Explanation:
The correct solution region is where x is between -1 and 3, and y is less than -7 or greater than 1.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A, which uses the wrong inequality signs for y. The absolute value of y + 3 being greater than 4 means y can be either greater than 1 or less than -7.

Problem 6 (AssembleRegionMedium04):
Solve the system of inequalities:
|x - 2| ≤ 3
-2y + 5 < 9
A) -1 ≤ x ≤ 5 and y > -2
B) x < -1 or x > 5 and y < -2
C) x < -1 or x > 5 and y > -2
D) -1 ≤ x ≤ 5 and y < -2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Solve for x: -3 ≤ x - 2 ≤ 3
-1 ≤ x ≤ 5
Solve for y: -2y + 5 < 9
-2y < 4
y > -2

Explanation:
The correct solution region is where x is between -1 and 5, and y is greater than -2.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing C or D, which do not account for the inequality sign being less than or equal to in the first equation.

Problem 7 (AssembleRegionMedium05):
Solve the system of inequalities:
|x - 4| < |2x + 1|
3y - 7 > 8
A) -9 < x < -5 and y > 5
B) x < -9 or x > -5 and y > 5
C) x < -9 or x > -5 and y < 5
D) -9 < x < -5 and y < 5
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Solve for x: |x - 4| < |2x + 1|
(x - 4)^2 < (2x + 1)^2
x^2 - 8x + 16 < 4x^2 + 4x + 1
3x^2 + 12x - 15 > 0
(3x - 3)(x + 5) > 0
x < -5 or x > 1
Solve for y: 3y - 7 > 8
3y > 15
y > 5

Explanation:
The correct solution region is where x is less than -5 or greater than 1, and y is greater than 5.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B or C, which do not account for the inequality being strict (less than) in the first equation.
