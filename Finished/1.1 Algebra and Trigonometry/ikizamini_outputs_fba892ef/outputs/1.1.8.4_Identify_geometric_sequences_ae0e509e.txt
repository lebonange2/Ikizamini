Problem 1 (Asdf):
Which geometric sequence has a common ratio of 3?
A) [1, 2, 3, 4]
B) [3, 6, 9, 12]
C) [1, 3, 9, 27]
D) [5, 10, 15, 20]
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The sequence [1, 3, 9, 27] is a geometric sequence with a common ratio of 3 because each term is obtained by multiplying the previous term by 3.

Explanation:
A geometric sequence is characterized by a constant ratio between consecutive terms. In this case, C has a ratio of 3 between each term.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or B because they are arithmetic sequences (the difference between consecutive terms is constant), not geometric sequences.

Problem 2 (Bghj):
Find the next term in the geometric sequence 2, 6, ___, 54.
A) 8
B) 10
C) 18
D) 24
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The next term in the sequence is 18 because it's found by multiplying the previous term (6) by the common ratio (3). So, 6 * 3 = 18.

Explanation:
In a geometric sequence, each term after the first is obtained by multiplying the previous term by a fixed non-zero number called the common ratio. Here, the ratio is 3.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or B because they are multiples of 2 but do not follow the pattern established by multiplying by 3.

Problem 4 (Yuiop):
What is the common ratio in the geometric sequence 4, 16, 64, ___?
A) 2
B) 3
C) 4
D) 5
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The common ratio is 2 because it's found by dividing any term by the previous term. In this case, 16 / 4 = 4 / 16 = 2.

Explanation:
To find the common ratio in a geometric sequence, divide any term by the one before it. Here, A has a ratio of 2 between each term.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B or C because they are factors of the terms but not the actual ratio between consecutive terms.
