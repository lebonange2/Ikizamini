Problem 1 (:num1):
Simplify the expression: (x^3 + 2x^2 - x) / (x^2 + 3x)
A) (x + 2) / (x + 3)
B) x(x + 2) / (x + 3)
C) x(x - 1)(x + 2) / (x + 3)
D) x^2(x - 1) / (x + 3)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To simplify the expression, factor out an x from the numerator and cancel out common factors with the denominator. The correct simplified form is x(x - 1)(x + 2) / (x + 3).

Explanation:
Factoring out an x simplifies both the numerator and the denominator, allowing for proper cancellation of terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to incorrectly factor or not cancel out the common term x, resulting in choice A or D.

Problem 2 (:num2):
Simplify the expression: (4x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x) / (2x^2 - 3x)
A) 2x(x - 1)(x + 3) / (x - 1)
B) 2x(x - 3) / (x - 1)
C) 2x(x + 1)(x - 3) / (x - 1)
D) 4x^2(x - 3) / (x - 1)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Factor out the greatest common factor of 2x from the numerator and cancel out the common term (x - 1). The correct simplified form is 2x(x + 1)(x - 3) / (x - 1).

Explanation:
Factoring out the GCF simplifies both parts of the expression, allowing for proper cancellation.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to not factor out the GCF or incorrectly cancel terms, leading to choices A, B, or D.
