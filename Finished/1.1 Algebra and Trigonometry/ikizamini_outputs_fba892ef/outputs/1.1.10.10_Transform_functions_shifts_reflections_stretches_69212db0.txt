Problem 1 (AsdFgHjK):
The function f(x) = x^2 is transformed by the equation g(x) = -(x + 3)^2 + 4. What are the effects of this transformation on the graph of f(x)?
A) Shifted left by 3 units, stretched by a factor of -1, and shifted up by 4 units.
B) Shifted right by 3 units, reflected across the x-axis, and shifted down by 4 units.
C) Shifted left by 3 units, reflected across the y-axis, and shifted up by 4 units.
D) Shifted right by 3 units, reflected across both axes, and shifted up by 4 units.
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The transformation is as follows: g(x) = -(x + 3)^2 + 4. This means the function is shifted left by 3 units (because of x + 3), reflected across the x-axis (due to the negative sign outside the parentheses), and then shifted up by 4 units (because of the +4).

Explanation:
The key to understanding this transformation is recognizing that adding a positive value inside the parentheses shifts the function left, multiplying the entire expression by -1 reflects it across the x-axis, and adding a constant at the end shifts it vertically.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to confuse the direction of the shift based on the sign inside the parentheses. Remember that a positive value inside the parentheses shifts left, not right.

Problem 2 (XyzWvuT):
The function h(x) = 2|x| is transformed by the equation j(x) = |(x - 1)| / 2. What are the effects of this transformation on the graph of h(x)?
A) Stretched by a factor of 2, reflected across the y-axis, and shifted right by 1 unit.
B) Compressed by a factor of 2, reflected across the x-axis, and shifted left by 1 unit.
C) Compressed by a factor of 2, reflected across both axes, and shifted right by 1 unit.
D) Stretched by a factor of 2, reflected across the y-axis, and shifted left by 1 unit.
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The transformation is as follows: j(x) = |(x - 1)| / 2. This means the function is compressed by a factor of 2 (because of dividing by 2), reflected across the y-axis (due to the absolute value), and shifted left by 1 unit (because of x - 1).

Explanation:
The absolute value function reflects the graph across the y-axis, while dividing by a positive constant compresses it. Shifting the input variable inside the absolute value changes the horizontal position.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget that the reflection across the y-axis occurs due to the absolute value.

Problem 3 (BnmLkjI):
The function p(x) = 3sin(x) is transformed by the equation q(x) = sin(2x - π/2) + 1. What are the effects of this transformation on the graph of p(x)?
A) Compressed by a factor of 2, shifted right by π/4 units, and shifted up by 1 unit.
B) Stretched by a factor of 2, shifted left by π/4 units, and shifted down by 1 unit.
C) Stretched by a factor of 2, shifted right by π/4 units, and shifted up by 1 unit.
D) Compressed by a factor of 2, shifted left by π/4 units, and shifted up by 1 unit.
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The transformation is as follows: q(x) = sin(2x - π/2) + 1. This means the function is compressed by a factor of 2 (because of 2x), shifted left by π/4 units (due to the phase shift inside the sine function), and shifted up by 1 unit (because of the +1).

Explanation:
The coefficient in front of x inside the trigonometric function determines the stretch or compression, while a phase shift inside the argument changes the horizontal position. The addition outside the sine function shifts the graph vertically.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to confuse the direction of the shift based on the sign of the phase shift.
