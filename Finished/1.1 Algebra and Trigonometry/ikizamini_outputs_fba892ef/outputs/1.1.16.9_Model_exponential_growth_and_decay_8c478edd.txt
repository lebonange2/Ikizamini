Problem 1 (Asdf):
The population of a city is growing exponentially at a rate of 5% per year. If the current population is 10,000, what will be the population after 8 years? Round to the nearest whole number.
A) 14,129
B) 16,384
C) 21,544
D) 27,945
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The formula for exponential growth is P = P_0 * (1 + r)^t, where P is the final population, P_0 is the initial population, r is the growth rate, and t is the time in years. Plugging in the given values: P = 10,000 * (1 + 0.05)^8 = 21,544. Rounding to the nearest whole number gives 21,544.

Explanation:
This question tests understanding of exponential growth and using the formula to calculate future population values.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is forgetting to convert the percentage rate to a decimal (0.05 instead of 5%). Another mistake might be not rounding the final result to the nearest whole number.

Problem 2 (Asdf2):
The half-life of a radioactive substance is 10 years. If there are initially 200 grams, how much will remain after 30 years? Round to the nearest gram.
A) 50 g
B) 75 g
C) 125 g
D) 150 g
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The formula for exponential decay is P = P_0 * (1/2)^(t/h), where P is the remaining quantity, P_0 is the initial quantity, t is the time in years, and h is the half-life. Plugging in the given values: P = 200 * (1/2)^(30/10) = 50 g.

Explanation:
This question tests understanding of exponential decay and using the formula to calculate remaining quantity after a certain time.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is not dividing the time by the half-life in the exponent (30 years instead of 3 half-lives). Another mistake might be not rounding the final result to the nearest gram.

Problem 4 (Asdf4):
The population of a bacteria culture decreases by 25% every hour. If there are initially 1,000 bacteria, how many will remain after 3 hours? Round to the nearest whole number.
A) 750
B) 563
C) 469
D) 375
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The formula for exponential decay is P = P_0 * (1 - r)^t, where P is the remaining quantity, P_0 is the initial quantity, t is the time in hours, and r is the decay rate. Plugging in the given values: P = 1,000 * (1 - 0.25)^3 = 374.6875, which rounds to 375.

Explanation:
This question tests understanding of exponential decay and using the formula to calculate remaining bacteria population after a certain time.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is not subtracting the percentage rate from 1 (0.25 instead of 1 - 0.25). Another mistake might be not rounding the final result to the nearest whole number.
