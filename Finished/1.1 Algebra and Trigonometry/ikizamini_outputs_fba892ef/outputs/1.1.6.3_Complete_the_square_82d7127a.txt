Problem 1 (What is the value of x in the equation (x - 2)^2 = 9?):
(x - 2)^2 = 9
A) 1
B) 3
C) 4
D) 5
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To solve the equation (x - 2)^2 = 9, we take the square root of both sides. The square roots of 9 are +3 and -3. So, x - 2 can be either 3 or -3. Solving for x in each case gives us x = 5 or x = -1. However, since only one correct answer is required, we choose the positive value: x = 3.

Explanation:
The correct choice is (B) because it represents the positive root of the equation when completing the square.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to select (A) or (D), which are the results of only taking one square root (+3) and not considering both possibilities (±3).

Problem 4 (What is the vertex of the parabola defined by the equation y = x^2 - 6x + 8?):
y = x^2 - 6x + 8
A) (3, -1)
B) (2, -1)
C) (3, 1)
D) (2, 1)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To find the vertex of a parabola in the form y = ax^2 + bx + c, we use the formula for the x-coordinate of the vertex: h = -b/(2a). For the equation y = x^2 - 6x + 8, a = 1, b = -6. Plugging these values into the formula gives us h = 3. To find the corresponding y-value, we substitute h back into the original equation: y = (3)^2 - 6(3) + 8 = -1. So, the vertex is at (3, -1).

Explanation:
The correct choice is (A) because it represents the coordinates of the vertex when completing the square for the given parabola.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to select (B), which is the result of not correctly applying the formula for the x-coordinate of the vertex or making an arithmetic error in calculating h.

Problem 6 (What is the standard form equation of a parabola with vertex (1, 2) and opens downward?):
Vertex: (1, 2), Opens Downward
A) y = -(x - 1)^2 + 2
B) y = (x - 1)^2 + 2
C) y = -(x + 1)^2 + 2
D) y = (x + 1)^2 + 2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The standard form of a parabola with vertex (h, k) and opening downward is y = -a(x - h)^2 + k. Given the vertex (1, 2), we can plug in h = 1 and k = 2: y = -(x - 1)^2 + 2. The negative sign before the squared term indicates that the parabola opens downward.

Explanation:
The correct choice is (A) because it represents the standard form equation of a parabola with the given vertex and opening direction.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to select (B), which represents a parabola opening upward instead of downward.
