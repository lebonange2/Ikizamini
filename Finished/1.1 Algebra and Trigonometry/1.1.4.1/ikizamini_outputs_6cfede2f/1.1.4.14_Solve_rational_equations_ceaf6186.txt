Problem 1 (Q1):
What is the capital of France?
A) London
B) Paris
C) Berlin
D) Rome
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The capital of France is Paris.

Explanation:
Paris has been the capital city of France since the 16th century and serves as an important cultural, political, and economic center.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting London (the capital of England) or Berlin (the capital of Germany).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Q2):
What is photosynthesis?
A) The process by which plants convert sunlight into energy.
B) The process by which animals digest food.
C) The movement of water through plant roots.
D) The reproduction of plants.
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Photosynthesis is the process by which plants, algae, and some bacteria convert sunlight, carbon dioxide, and water into glucose and oxygen.

Explanation:
This process is essential for the survival of these organisms and plays a critical role in Earth's atmosphere and ecosystems.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting options related to animal processes or plant reproduction.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Q3):
What is the boiling point of water in degrees Celsius?
A) 100
B) 212
C) 0
D) 80
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The boiling point of water at standard atmospheric pressure is 100 degrees Celsius or 212 degrees Fahrenheit.

Explanation:
This temperature varies with altitude and air pressure, but the given value is for average conditions on Earth.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting the Fahrenheit equivalent (212) or temperatures associated with other processes like freezing.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Q4):
What is the largest organ in the human body?
A) Brain
B) Liver
C) Skin
D) Heart
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The skin is the largest organ in the human body, covering an average surface area of about 1.5 to 2 square meters.

Explanation:
It serves as a protective barrier, regulates temperature, and aids in sensory perception.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting organs with smaller surface areas like the brain or heart.

Problem 5 (Q5):
What is the formula for calculating the area of a circle?
A) Area = πr²
B) Area = 2πr
C) Area = πd
D) Area = (1/4)πd²
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The formula for calculating the area of a circle is Area = π times the radius squared (Area = πr²).

Explanation:
The radius is half the diameter, and π (pi) is a mathematical constant approximately equal to 3.14.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting formulas for other shapes or using the wrong variable.

Problem 6 (Q6):
What is the smallest planet in our solar system?
A) Mercury
B) Mars
C) Venus
D) Earth
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The smallest planet in our solar system is Mercury, with a diameter of about 4,879 kilometers.

Explanation:
Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and has a relatively small size compared to other planets like Earth or Jupiter.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting larger planets like Mars, Venus, or Earth.

Problem 7 (Q7):
What is the primary function of white blood cells?
A) Transport oxygen throughout the body.
B) Protect the body against infections.
C) Produce hormones and enzymes.
D) Regulate blood pressure.
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The primary function of white blood cells, also known as leukocytes, is to defend the body against infections and foreign substances.

Explanation:
They are an essential part of the immune system and help fight off pathogens like bacteria, viruses, and parasites.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting functions associated with red blood cells or other cell types.
