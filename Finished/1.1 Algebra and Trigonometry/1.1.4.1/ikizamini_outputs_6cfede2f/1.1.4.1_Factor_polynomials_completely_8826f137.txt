Problem 1 (:1):
Factor the polynomial: x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x
A) (x - 3)^3
B) x(x - 3)(x - 9)
C) x(x - 3)^2
D) x^2(x - 9)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Factor out x and recognize the perfect square trinomial (3x)^2 = 9x^2 within the remaining expression. The factorization is x(3x - 3)(3x - 3), which simplifies to x(x - 3)^2.

Explanation:
Recognize the greatest common factor and factor it out, followed by identifying a perfect square trinomial for complete factorization.

Common mistake note:
Not factoring out the greatest common factor first can lead to incorrect factorizations like B or D.

Problem 2 (2):
Factor the polynomial: 16x^2 - 49
A) (4x + 7)(4x - 7)
B) (8x + 7)(8x - 7)
C) (2x + 7)^2
D) (2x - 7)^2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Apply the difference of squares formula: (a^2 - b^2) = (a + b)(a - b). Here, a = 4x and b = 7.

Explanation:
Use the difference of squares formula when given a square of one term subtracted by another square.

Common mistake note:
Trying to factor as a perfect square trinomial is incorrect since it's not in the form (ax)^2 + b^2 or (ax)^2 - b^2.

Problem 3 (3):
Factor the polynomial: 6x^2 + 18xy + 9y^2
A) 3(2x + y)^2
B) 6(x + y)(x + 3y)
C) 3(2x + 3y)^2
D) 2(3x + y)^2
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Group the first two terms and factor out the greatest common factor. Then, recognize the perfect square trinomial: 6x^2 + 18xy = 6x(x + 3y), so the factorization is 3(2x + 3y)^2.

Explanation:
Group terms, factor out the greatest common factor from each group, and identify a perfect square trinomial for complete factorization.

Common mistake note:
Not grouping the first two terms correctly or forgetting to factor out the greatest common factor of 3 can lead to errors.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (4):
Factor the polynomial: x^3 - 27
A) (x - 3)^3
B) x(x + 3)(x - 9)
C) x(x - 3)(x + 3)
D) (x + 3)^3
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Apply the sum of cubes formula: (a^3 - b^3) = (a - b)(a^2 + ab + b^2). Here, a = x and b = 3.

Explanation:
Use the sum of cubes formula when given a cube of one term subtracted by another cube.

Common mistake note:
Not recognizing it as a sum of cubes can lead to incorrect factorizations like A, B, or D.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (5):
Factor the polynomial: 27x^3 - 12x
A) 3x(3x - 4)^2
B) 9x(3x + 4)(3x - 4)
C) 3x(3x + 4)(3x - 4)
D) 9x(3x - 4)^2
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Factor out the greatest common factor of 3x, then recognize and factor a difference of squares: 9x^2 - 4 = (3x + 2)(3x - 2).

Explanation:
Factor out the greatest common factor first, followed by recognizing and factoring a difference of squares.

Common mistake note:
Not factoring out the greatest common factor before attempting to factor the remaining expression can lead to errors.
