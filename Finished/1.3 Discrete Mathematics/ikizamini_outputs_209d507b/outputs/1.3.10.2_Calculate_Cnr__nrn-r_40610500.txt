Problem 1 (:1):
What is the value of C(8, 3)?
A) Assemble the correct combination
B) 42
C) 56
D) 70
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
C(8, 3) = 8! / (3!(8-3)!) = (8 * 7 * 6) / (3 * 2 * 1) = 56

Explanation:
The combination formula C(n, r) calculates the number of ways to choose r items from a set of n items.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to divide by r! and (n-r)!, which leads to an incorrect result.
