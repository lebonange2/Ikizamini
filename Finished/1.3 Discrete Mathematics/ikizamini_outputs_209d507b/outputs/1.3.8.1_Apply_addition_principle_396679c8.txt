Problem 1 (OnePlusOneEasy):
What is the sum of 2 and 3?
A) 4
B) 5
C) 6
D) 7
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The addition principle states that when you add two numbers, you combine their values. So, 2 + 3 equals 5.

Explanation:
This is a basic addition problem where the sum of two single-digit numbers is asked for.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose A (4), which is the result of adding 2 and 2 instead of 2 and 3.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (TwoPlusThreeMedium):
What is the sum of 23 and 47?
A) 60
B) 69
C) 70
D) 71
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To find the sum, align the numbers by place value and add each column: (20 + 40) + (3 + 7) = 60 + 10 = 70.

Explanation:
This problem involves two-digit numbers but still uses the addition principle.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake could be to choose A (60), which is the result of adding only the tens place values and forgetting to add the units.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (ThreePlusFiveMedium):
What is the sum of 345 and 567?
A) 812
B) 910
C) 912
D) 920
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Again, align by place value: (300 + 500) + (40 + 60) + (5 + 7) = 800 + 100 + 12 = 912.

Explanation:
This problem involves larger two-digit numbers but still adheres to the addition principle.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake might be to choose A (812), which is the result of adding only the hundreds and tens place values without considering the units.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (NegativeOnePlusPositiveOneDifficult):
What is the sum of -1 and 1?
A) -2
B) 0
C) 1
D) 2
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
When adding a negative number to a positive number with the same magnitude, their sum is zero.

Explanation:
This problem tests understanding of addition with opposite numbers.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake could be to choose A (-2), which would result from subtracting 1 from -1 instead of adding.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (NegativeTwoPlusPositiveThreeDifficult):
What is the sum of -2 and 3?
A) -5
B) -1
C) 1
D) 5
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
When adding a smaller positive number to a larger negative number, the result will be the difference between their magnitudes with the sign of the larger number.

Explanation:
This problem tests understanding of addition involving numbers of different signs.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake might be to choose B (-1), which would result from subtracting 2 from 3 instead of adding.
