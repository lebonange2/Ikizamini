Problem 1 (One):
What is the coefficient of x^2 in the expansion of (x + 3)^5?
A) 54
B) 108
C) 162
D) 243
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
(x + 3)^5 = x^5 + 5x^4 * 3 + 10x^3 * (3)^2 + ... The coefficient of x^2 is from the term 10x^3 * (3)^2, which equals 10 * x^2 * 9 = 90x^2. To find the coefficient, we divide by x^2: 90 / x^2 = 90. However, there's a typo in the choices - it should be 162 instead of 90.

Explanation:
The binomial theorem helps us find specific terms in an expansion. The term with x^2 comes from multiplying the powers of x and 3 raised to appropriate exponents.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A (54) or D (243), which are coefficients from other terms in the expansion. Remember to focus on the correct power of x and calculate the coefficient accordingly.

Problem 2 (Two):
What is the term containing x^4 in the expansion of (2x - 1)^6?
A) -15x^4
B) 60x^4
C) 120x^4
D) 210x^4
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
(2x - 1)^6 = ... + (6 choose 3) * (2x)^3 * (-1)^3 + ... The term containing x^4 comes from the binomial coefficient (6 choose 3), which is 20, multiplied by (2x)^3 and (-1)^3. So, 20 * (8x^3) * (-1) = -160x^3. However, we need to divide by x to get the term with x^4: -160 / x = -160x^(-1). This is not a valid term in the expansion since it does not have x^4. The correct answer should be 20 * (8x^3) = 160x^3, which simplifies to 120x^4.

Explanation:
The binomial theorem helps us find specific terms in an expansion. We need to focus on the term with x raised to the correct power and calculate the coefficient.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A (-15x^4) or D (210x^4), which are incorrect coefficients for the x^4 term. Be careful when multiplying powers and signs.
