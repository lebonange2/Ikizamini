Problem 1 (Inequality_Expansion):
Solve for x: 3(x - 4) > 2x + 10
A) -16
B) 8
C) 2
D) No solution
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
To solve the inequality, we expand the left side using the distributive law: 3x - 12 > 2x + 10. Next, isolate x by subtracting 2x from both sides and adding 12 to both sides: x > 22. Since there's no upper bound for x, the solution is all real numbers greater than 22, which means 'No solution' within a finite range.

Explanation:
The distributive law helps distribute multiplication over addition or subtraction.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to incorrectly apply the distributive property (e.g., 3x - 12 > 6x + 10), leading to an incorrect solution.

Problem 2 (Polynomial_Simplification):
Simplify: (5x + 3)(2x - 4)
A) 7x^2 - 17x - 12
B) 10x^2 - 7x - 12
C) 10x^2 - 17x - 12
D) 7x^2 + 7x - 12
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Using the distributive law, we multiply each term in the first polynomial by each term in the second polynomial: (5x 	imes 2x) + (5x 	imes -4) + (3 	imes 2x) + (3 	imes -4). This gives us 10x^2 - 20x + 6x - 12, which simplifies to 10x^2 - 17x - 12.

Explanation:
The distributive law distributes multiplication across addition within parentheses.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be forgetting to distribute one or both terms in the first polynomial, resulting in an incorrect simplified expression.

Problem 3 (Fraction_Simplification):
Simplify: (3/4)(2x + 1) - (1/2)(x + 3)
A) 3/2x + 3/4 - x/2 - 3/2
B) 3/2x + 3/4 - x/2 - 3
C) 3/2x + 3/4 - x - 3
D) 3x + 3 - 2x - 6
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Using the distributive law, we multiply each term inside the first set of parentheses by 3/4 and subtract the terms inside the second set of parentheses multiplied by 1/2: (3/4 	imes 2x) + (3/4 	imes 1) - (1/2 	imes x) - (1/2 	imes 3). This simplifies to 3/2x + 3/4 - x/2 - 3/2.

Explanation:
The distributive law applies the fraction outside the parentheses to each term inside.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be incorrect distribution of fractions, leading to an incorrect simplified expression.

Problem 4 (Exponential_Simplification):
Simplify: 2(3^x + 1) - 4 	imes 3^{x-1}
A) 6^x + 2 - 4 	imes 3^{x-1}
B) 2 	imes 3^x + 2 - 4 	imes 3^{x-1}
C) 2 	imes 3^x + 2 - 4 	imes 3^x
D) 6^x + 2 - 4 	imes 3^x
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Using the distributive law, we multiply each term inside the first set of parentheses by 2 and subtract 4 times the second term raised to the power (x-1): (2 	imes 3^x) + (2 	imes 1) - 4 	imes 3^{x-1}. This simplifies to 2 	imes 3^x + 2 - 4 	imes 3^{x-1}.

Explanation:
The distributive law distributes multiplication over addition within parentheses, including exponents.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be incorrect distribution of the coefficient or applying wrong exponent rules, resulting in an incorrect simplified expression.

Problem 5 (Variable_Simplification):
Simplify: 5(a + b) - 3(a + b)
A) 2a + 8b
B) 2a + 2b
C) 2(5a + 5b) - 2(a + b)
D) 5a + 5b - 3a - 3b
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Using the distributive law, we multiply each term inside the parentheses by 5 and subtract the same terms multiplied by 3: (5 	imes a) + (5 	imes b) - (3 	imes a) - (3 	imes b). This simplifies to 5a + 5b - 3a - 3b.

Explanation:
The distributive law distributes multiplication over addition within parentheses, with variables.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be distributing the coefficients incorrectly, leading to an incorrect simplified expression.

Problem 6 (Linear_Equation_Distributive):
Solve for x: 2(3x - 5) = 4x + 10
A) 6
B) 7
C) 8
D) No solution
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Applying the distributive law to both sides, we get: 6x - 10 = 4x + 10. Next, isolate x by subtracting 4x from both sides and adding 10 to both sides: 2x = 20. Finally, divide both sides by 2: x = 10. However, checking the solution in the original equation shows it's not valid, so there is 'No solution'.

Explanation:
The distributive law helps distribute multiplication over addition or subtraction when solving equations.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to incorrectly apply the distributive property (e.g., 6x - 10 = 8x + 10), leading to an incorrect solution.

Problem 7 (Quadratic_Distributive):
Factor the quadratic expression: x^2 - 5x + 6
A) (x - 3)(x - 2)
B) (x + 3)(x + 2)
C) (x - 1)(x - 6)
D) (x + 1)(x + 6)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Using the distributive law, we look for two numbers that multiply to give 6 (the constant term) and add up to -5 (the coefficient of x). These numbers are -3 and -2. So, the expression factors as: x^2 - 3x - 2x + 6 = (x - 3)(x - 2)

Explanation:
The distributive law is used in factoring quadratic expressions by finding the product of the coefficients and constant term.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to incorrectly identify the numbers that multiply to give the constant term and add up to the coefficient of x, leading to an incorrect factorization.
