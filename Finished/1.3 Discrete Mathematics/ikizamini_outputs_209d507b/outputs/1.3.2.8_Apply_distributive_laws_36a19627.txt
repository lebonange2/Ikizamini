Problem 1 (In):
Simplify the expression: 5(2x + 3)
A) 7x + 15
B) 10x + 15
C) 10x + 18
D) 7x + 20
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The distributive law states that a * (b + c) = ab + ac. Applying this to the expression, we get: 5(2x) + 5(3) = 10x + 15.

Explanation:
The correct application of the distributive law multiplies each term inside the parentheses by the number outside.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to multiply the 5 by both terms inside the parentheses, resulting in answer A (7x + 15).

Problem 2 (Out):
Simplify the expression: 3(x - 4)
A) 3x - 8
B) 3x - 12
C) 6x - 16
D) 6x - 12
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The distributive law states that a * (b - c) = ab - ac. Applying this to the expression, we get: 3x - 3(4) = 3x - 12.

Explanation:
The correct application of the distributive law multiplies both terms inside the parentheses by the number outside, including the negative sign.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to multiply the -4 by 3, resulting in answer A (3x - 8).

Problem 3 (Mixed):
Simplify the expression: (-2)(4y + 6z)
A) -8y - 12z
B) -8y + 12z
C) 8y - 12z
D) 8y + 12z
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The distributive law states that a * (b + c) = ab + ac. Applying this to the expression, we get: (-2)(4y) + (-2)(6z) = -8y - 12z.

Explanation:
The correct application of the distributive law multiplies both terms inside the parentheses by the number outside, including the negative sign.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that multiplying a negative number with another term results in a negative product, leading to answers B, C, or D.

Problem 4 (Neg):
Simplify the expression: -3(2x - 5)
A) -6x + 15
B) -6x - 15
C) 6x + 15
D) 6x - 15
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The distributive law states that a * (b - c) = ab - ac. Applying this to the expression, we get: (-3)(2x) - (-3)(5) = -6x + 15.

Explanation:
The correct application of the distributive law multiplies both terms inside the parentheses by the number outside, including the negative sign.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that multiplying a negative number with another term results in a negative product, leading to answers B or C.

Problem 5 (Diff):
Simplify the expression: 4(3x - 2y) + 2(5x + 3y)
A) 17x - 2y
B) 17x + 2y
C) 22x - 2y
D) 22x + 2y
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
First, apply the distributive law to each term: 4(3x) - 4(2y) + 2(5x) + 2(3y). Then simplify: 12x - 8y + 10x + 6y = 22x - 2y.

Explanation:
The correct application of the distributive law multiplies each term inside its respective parentheses by the number outside, and then combines like terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to combine the x and y terms before distributing, which would lead to an incorrect result.

Problem 6 (Combine):
Simplify the expression: 6(2x + 3) - 4(2x + 3)
A) 8x + 18
B) 10x + 6
C) 8x + 6
D) 10x + 18
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
First, apply the distributive law to each term: 6(2x) + 6(3) - 4(2x) - 4(3). Then simplify: 12x + 18 - 8x - 12 = 4x + 6.

Explanation:
The correct application of the distributive law multiplies each term inside its respective parentheses by the number outside, and then combines like terms. The common factor (2x + 3) can be factored out to simplify further: 4(2x + 3) - 4(2x + 3) = 0.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to combine the simplified expressions after distributing, leading to answers A or D.

Problem 7 (Fraction):
Simplify the expression: (1/2)(5x - 3)
A) 5/2x + 3/2
B) 5/2x - 3/2
C) 10x - 6
D) 10x + 6
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The distributive law states that a * (b - c) = ab - ac. Applying this to the expression, we get: (1/2)(5x) - (1/2)(3) = 5/2x - 3/2.

Explanation:
The correct application of the distributive law multiplies both terms inside the parentheses by the number outside, including the fraction. Remember to distribute the fraction across each term.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to multiply the fraction with the second term, leading to answer A (5/2x + 3/2).
