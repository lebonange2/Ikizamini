Problem 1 (:1):
What is the domain of discourse for the statement: 'All mammals have hair.'?
A) Vegetables
B) Birds
C) Mammals
D) Fish
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The domain of discourse for this statement is 'mammals' as it discusses a characteristic specific to mammals.

Explanation:
To identify the domain, we consider the subject matter: mammals and their shared trait of having hair.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be selecting an answer outside the scope, like 'Vegetables' or 'Fish', which are unrelated to mammals.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (:2):
Which domain does the phrase 'The periodic table includes elements such as hydrogen and oxygen.' belong to?
A) Chemistry
B) Biology
C) Physics
D) Geology
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The domain is 'Chemistry' as it refers to the periodic table, a fundamental concept in chemistry.

Explanation:
The periodic table and elements like hydrogen and oxygen are part of chemical science.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake might be choosing 'Biology' if associating elements with living organisms, but they're studied in chemistry regardless.
