Problem 1 (:1):
What is the value of 5!?
A) 10
B) 15
C) 24
D) 120
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
5! (5 factorial) is calculated by multiplying all positive integers from 1 to 5. So, 5! = 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 120.

Explanation:
Factorial calculations involve multiplying a number by all the numbers below it down to 1.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to include one or more of the numbers in the multiplication, leading to an incorrect result. For example, choosing A (10) would be a mistake because it only includes two factors (5 and 4), not all five.

Problem 2 (2):
What is the value of 3!?
A) 6
B) 9
C) 18
D) 54
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
3! (3 factorial) is calculated by multiplying all positive integers from 1 to 3. So, 3! = 3 × 2 × 1 = 6.

Explanation:
Remember that factorial calculations start with 1 and multiply up to the given number.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to include numbers larger than the given one, like choosing C (18) which includes 4 and 5 in the multiplication, which are not part of 3!.
