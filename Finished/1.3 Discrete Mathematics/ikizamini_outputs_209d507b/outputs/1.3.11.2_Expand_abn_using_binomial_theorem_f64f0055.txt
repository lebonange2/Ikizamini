Problem 1 (Asdf):
What is the coefficient of the term a^3b^4 in (a+ b)^7?
A) 35
B) 210
C) 280
D) 560
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
(a+b)^7 can be expanded using the binomial theorem. The coefficient of a^3b^4 is C(7, 3) * a^(7-3) * b^(4). C(7, 3) = 7! / (3!(7-3)!) = 35. So the coefficient is 35 * a^3 * b^4. The correct choice is B: 210.

Explanation:
The binomial theorem helps find coefficients in the expansion of (a+b)^n by choosing combinations from 'a' and 'b' terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be using the wrong power for a or b. Remember that the total power is 7, so when you choose 3 for a, the remaining power for a should be (7-3).

Problem 2 (Asdf):
What is the coefficient of the middle term in (a+ b)^9?
A) 126
B) 300
C) 378
D) 450
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The middle term in (a+b)^9 will be the (n/2 + 1)th term, where n is 9. It's the 5th term. The coefficient for this term can be found using C(9, 4). C(9, 4) = 9! / (4!(9-4)!) = 378. So the correct choice is C: 378.

Explanation:
The middle term in a binomial expansion with an odd power is found by taking (n/2 + 1)th term, where n is the total power.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be assuming that the middle term is always the middle of the expanded series. For odd powers, it's the term after the first half.

Problem 5 (Asdf):
What is the coefficient of the last term in (a+ b)^7?
A) 1
B) 7
C) 21
D) 35
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(a+b)^7 can be expanded using the binomial theorem. The coefficient of the last term is C(7, 0) * a^(7-0) * b^(7). C(7, 0) = 7! / (0!(7-0)!) = 1. So the coefficient is 1 * a^0 * b^7. The correct choice is A: 1.

Explanation:
The binomial theorem helps find coefficients in the expansion of (a+b)^n by choosing combinations from 'a' and 'b' terms. For the last term, there are no 'a' terms left, so it's a^0.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be forgetting that a^0 is always 1. Remember that any non-zero number raised to the power of 0 is 1.
