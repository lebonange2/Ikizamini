Problem 1 (:1):
Determine if the following two propositions are logically equivalent: (A ∧ B) → C and ¬A ∨ (B → C)
A) (A ∧ B) → C

| T  | T  | F  |   T     |     F        |
| T  | F  | T  |   F     |     T        |

| F  | F  | F  |   F     |     T        |

| A  | B  | C  | ¬A  | B → C  | ¬A ∨ (B → C)  |
|---|---|---|----|-------|-------------|
