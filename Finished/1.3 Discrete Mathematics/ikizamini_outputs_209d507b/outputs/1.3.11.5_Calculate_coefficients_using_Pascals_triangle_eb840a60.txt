Problem 1 (?):
What is the coefficient of the x^2 term in the expansion of (a + b)^4 using Pascal's Triangle?
A) math_1
B) math_2
C) math_3
D) math_4
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
(a + b)^4 can be expanded using binomial theorem. The coefficient for x^2 term is the fourth entry in the third row of Pascal's Triangle, which is 6.

Explanation:
In Pascal's Triangle, each number is the sum of the two numbers directly above it. The third row starts with 1, then 3 (1+2), and ends with 1 (2+1). So, the coefficient for x^2 in (a + b)^4 is 6.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose 'B' (4) which is the coefficient of x term in (a + b)^3 instead of (a + b)^4.
