Problem 1 (:1):
What is the value of P(8, 4)?
A) 7!
B) 4!
C) 20
D) 35
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
P(8, 4) = 8! / (8 - 4)! = 8! / 4! = (8 × 7 × 6 × 5) / (4 × 3 × 2 × 1) = 20

Explanation:
The formula for P(n, r) is n!/(n- r)!, where n is the total number of items and r is the number chosen. In this case, n=8 and r=4.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to calculate 8! directly without dividing by (8-4)!. This would result in an incorrect answer.

Problem 2 (:2):
What is the probability of selecting 3 red balls from a bag containing 5 red and 7 blue balls, if order does not matter?
A) 0.167
B) 0.286
C) 0.429
D) 0.571
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
P = P(12, 3) / C(12, 3) = (12! / (12 - 3)!)/(12! / (12 - 5)!) = (12 × 11 × 10) / (7 × 6 × 5) ≈ 0.286

Explanation:
We use the combination formula C(n, r) for selecting balls without regard to order, then divide by the total combinations of selecting 3 balls from 12.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to calculate P(5, 3) instead of P(12, 3), assuming only red balls were considered. This would lead to an incorrect probability.

Problem 3 (:3):
How many ways can you choose 4 students from a group of 10 for a committee?
A) 6!
B) 10!
C) 210
D) 360
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
P(10, 4) = 10! / (10 - 4)! = 10 × 9 × 8 × 7 = 210

Explanation:
The number of ways to choose r items from a set of n is given by P(n, r). Here, n=10 and r=4.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to use the combination formula C(10, 4) instead of P(10, 4), which would result in a smaller number.

Problem 7 (:7):
What is the probability of drawing a pair of kings from a standard deck of 52 cards, if order does not matter?
A) 0.0036
B) 0.0144
C) 0.0288
D) 0.0576
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
P = P(4, 2) / C(52, 2) = (4! / (4 - 2)!)/(52! / (52 - 2)!) ≈ 0.0144

Explanation:
We use the permutation formula for selecting kings and the combination formula for selecting any two cards from a deck of 52, then divide to find the probability.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to calculate P(4, 2) without dividing by C(52, 2), which would result in an incorrect probability.
