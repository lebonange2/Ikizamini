Problem 1 (Asdf):
Find the general solution to the recurrence relation: a_n = 2a_{n-1} - a_{n-2}, with initial conditions a_0 = 3 and a_1 = 5.
A) [3(-1)^n + 5(2^n)]
B) [3(2^n) + 5(-1)^n]
C) [6(2^n) - 8(-1)^n]
D) [6(-1)^n + 8(2^n)]
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The characteristic equation for the homogeneous recurrence relation is r^2 = 2r - 1. Solving this quadratic equation gives us r = 1 and r = -1. The general solution for a homogeneous linear recurrence relation with distinct real roots is a combination of e^{rn}. Thus, the general solution is a_n = A(1)^n + B(-1)^n. Using the initial conditions, we find A = 3 and B = 5. So the correct answer is [3(2^n) + 5(-1)^n].

Explanation:
The characteristic equation helps us determine the roots of the recurrence relation, which are used to form the general solution. The initial conditions are then used to find the constants A and B.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to mix up the signs when forming the general solution with complex roots. In this case, since we have real distinct roots, there's no imaginary part involved.

Problem 2 (Asdf2):

A) [1(2^n) + 4(3^n)]

Solution:

The general solution for a homogeneous linear recurrence relation with real roots is a combination of e^{rn}. Thus, the general solution is a_n = A(2^n) + B(1)^n. Using the initial conditions, we find A = 1 and B = 4. So the correct answer is [1(3^n) + 4(-2)^n].

Explanation:
The characteristic equation helps us determine the roots of the recurrence relation, which are used to form the general solution. The initial conditions are then used to find the constants A and B.

Common mistake note:

Problem 3 (Asdf3):
Find the closed-form solution for the recurrence relation: a_n = 4a_{n-1} - 3a_{n-2}, with initial conditions a_0 = 2 and a_1 = 7.
A) [2(4^n) + 7(-3)^n]
B) [2(-4)^n + 7(3)^n]
C) [2(3^n) + 7(-4)^n]
D) [2(3^n) - 7(-4)^n]
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The characteristic equation for the homogeneous recurrence relation is r^2 = 4r - 3. Solving this quadratic equation gives us r = 3 and r = 1. The closed-form solution for a homogeneous linear recurrence relation with real roots is a combination of e^{rn}. Since we have positive roots, we can use the general form a_n = A(3^n) + B(1)^n, then multiply by the initial conditions' ratio to eliminate one term. This gives us a_ n = 2(3^n) + 7(-3)^n. So the correct answer is [2(4^n) + 7(-3)^n].

Explanation:
The characteristic equation helps us determine the roots of the recurrence relation, which are used to form the closed-form solution. The initial conditions are then used to find the constants A and B by multiplying by the ratio of initial values.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to multiply by the ratio of initial values when finding the closed-form solution. This step ensures that only one term remains in the final expression.

The general solution for a homogeneous linear recurrence relation with real roots is a combination of e^{rn}. Thus, the general solution is a_n = A(3^n) + B(2)^n. Using the initial conditions, we find A = 1 and B = 2. So the correct answer is [1(6^n) + 2(-5)^n].

Explanation:
