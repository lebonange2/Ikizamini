Problem 1 (:1):
What is the result of multiplying 5 and 7?
A) 11
B) 35
C) 28
D) 40
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To find the product of 5 and 7, you simply multiply them together: 5 	imes 7 = 35.

Explanation:
The multiplication principle states that when you multiply two numbers, you are essentially adding one number to itself a certain number of times. In this case, you add 5 seven times.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to accidentally add the numbers instead of multiplying them (resulting in 12) or to transpose the numbers (multiplying 7 by 5, which gives 35 but choosing option A).
