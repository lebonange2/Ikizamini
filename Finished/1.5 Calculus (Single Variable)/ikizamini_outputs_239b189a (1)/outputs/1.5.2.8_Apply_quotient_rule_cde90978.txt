Problem 1 (Asdf):
Find the derivative of f(x) = (3x^2 + 5x - 7)/(x^3 - 4x). Which of the following is correct?
A) (6x + 5)(x^3 - 4x) - (3x^2 + 5x - 7)(3x^2 - 4)
B) (6x + 5)/(x^3 - 4x) - (3x^2 + 5x - 7)/(3x^2 - 4)
C) (18x + 10 - 7x)/(x^3 - 4x)^2
D) (6x + 5)(3x^2 - 4) - (3x^2 + 5x - 7)(3x^2 - 4)/(x^3 - 4x)^2
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Using the quotient rule, f'(x) = [u'v - uv']/(v^2), where u = 3x^2 + 5x - 7 and v = x^3 - 4x. Then, u' = 6x + 5 and v' = 3x^2 - 4. Plugging these into the quotient rule formula gives f'(x) = [(6x + 5)(x^3 - 4x) - (3x^2 + 5x - 7)(3x^2 - 4)]/(x^3 - 4x)^2, which simplifies to D.

Explanation:
The quotient rule is used to differentiate a function that is the ratio of two other functions. The formula is f'(x) = [u'v - uv']/(v^2), where u and v are the numerator and denominator functions respectively, and u' and v' are their derivatives.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to square the denominator in the denominator of the quotient rule formula. Answer A forgets to square (x^3 - 4x), while B does not apply the product rule correctly.

Problem 2 (Asdf2):
Find the derivative of g(x) = (e^x + sin(x))/(cos(x)). Which is the correct answer?
A) (e^x - cos(x)) / (cos(x))^2
B) (e^x + cos(x)) / (sin(x))^2
C) (e^x + cos(x) - sin(x)) / (cos(x))^2
D) (e^x + cos(x) - e^x * sin(x) - cos(x) * cos(x)) / (cos(x))^2
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Using the quotient rule, g'(x) = [u'v - uv']/(v^2), where u = e^x + sin(x) and v = cos(x). Then, u' = e^x + cos(x) and v' = -sin(x). Plugging these into the formula gives g'(x) = [(e^x + cos(x))cos(x) - (e^x + sin(x))(-sin(x))]/(cos(x))^2, simplifying to D.

Explanation:
The quotient rule is applied here to differentiate a function with exponential and trigonometric components. The formula remains the same as in the previous question.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that the derivative of sin(x) is cos(x), which leads to Answer A using -cos(x) instead. B混淆了分子和分母的函数，而C没有正确处理u'v和uv'项。
