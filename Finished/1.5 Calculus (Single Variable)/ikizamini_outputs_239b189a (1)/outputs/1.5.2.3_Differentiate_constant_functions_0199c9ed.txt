Problem 1 (OneConstant):
What is the derivative of the function f(x) = 5?
A) 0
B) 1
C) x
D) 5
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The derivative of a constant function is always zero. So, the derivative of f(x) = 5 is 0.

Explanation:
Constants don't change with respect to x, hence their rate of change (derivative) is zero.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be selecting 'B' (1), as some might think that the derivative of any function is 1. However, this is not true for constant functions.

Problem 4 (FourConstant):
What is the slope of the line represented by the equation y = 7?
A) 0
B) 1
C) x + 3
D) 7
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The slope of a horizontal line (which is a constant function) is always zero. So, the slope of y = 7 is 0.

Explanation:
Horizontal lines have no change in 'y' for any change in 'x', making their slope zero.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be selecting 'D' (7), as some might think that the slope retains the constant value. However, for horizontal lines, the slope is always 0.

Problem 5 (FiveConstant):
What is the derivative of the function j(x) = -3x + c, where c is a constant?
A) -3
B) -3x
C) c
D) j'(x)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The derivative of a linear function with no x-intercept term (-3x) is the coefficient of x. So, the derivative of j(x) = -3x + c is -3.

Explanation:
Constants don't affect the rate of change; only the coefficient of x matters in a linear function.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be selecting 'C' (c), as some might think that constants affect the derivative. However, for linear functions, constants do not contribute to the derivative.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (SixConstant):
What is the rate of change of the function k(x) = 4x^2 + 6x + 9 at x = 0?
A) 0
B) 4
C) 6
D) 15
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The rate of change is the derivative. At x = 0, the derivative of k(x) = 4x^2 + 6x + 9 is 0.

Explanation:
Constants don't affect the rate of change; only the coefficients and powers of x matter in a polynomial function. At x = 0, the coefficient of x matters.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be selecting 'D' (15), as some might think that the derivative at x = 0 is the sum of coefficients. However, for polynomial functions, it's the derivative evaluated at x = 0.

Problem 7 (SevenConstant):
What is the derivative of the function l(x) = 2sin(x) + 3cos(x) + 8?
A) 2cos(x)
B) 3sin(x)
C) 2cos(x) + 3sin(x)
D) l'(x)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The derivative of a constant function is always zero. So, the derivative of l(x) = 2sin(x) + 3cos(x) + 8 is 2cos(x) + 3sin(x).

Explanation:
Constants don't change with respect to x, so they do not affect the derivative. The derivatives of sin(x) and cos(x) are cos(x) and -sin(x), respectively.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be selecting 'D' (l'(x)), as some might think that the derivative is a function of x. However, for constant functions within a trigonometric expression, only the non-constant parts contribute to the derivative.
