Problem 1 (AsdFgHjK):
Find the derivative of f(x) = x^3 + 4x^2 - 7x + 10. Which of the following is correct?
A) 3x^2 + 8x - 7
B) x^2 + 8x - 6
C) 3x^2 + 4x - 7
D) x^2 + 4x - 6
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The derivative of f(x) is found by applying the power rule to each term. The derivative of x^n is n 	imes x^(n-1). So, for f(x) = x^3 + 4x^2 - 7x + 10, we get: f'(x) = 3x^2 + 8x - 7.

Explanation:
The power rule states that the derivative of x^n is n 	imes x^(n-1).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to apply the exponent to the coefficient when differentiating, resulting in choice B. Another possible mistake is applying the wrong power rule (e.g., using n+1 instead of n-1), which would lead to choice C.
