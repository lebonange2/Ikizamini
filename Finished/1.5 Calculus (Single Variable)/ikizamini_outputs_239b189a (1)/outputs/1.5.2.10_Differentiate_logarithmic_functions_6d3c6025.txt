Problem 1 (InverseDerivative):
Find the derivative of f(x) = ln(3x).
A) 1/3x
B) 1/(3x)
C) 3/2x
D) 3ln(3x)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The derivative of ln(u) is 1/u, where u is the argument inside the logarithm. In this case, u = 3x. So, the derivative of f(x) = ln(3x) is 1/(3x).

Explanation:
To differentiate a logarithmic function, use the chain rule and remember that the derivative of ln(u) is 1/u.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to apply the chain rule and divide by the argument inside the logarithm. Choice A (1/3x) is incorrect because it lacks the division by 3x.

Problem 2 (LogBaseChange):
Find the derivative of f(x) = log base 2 (5x).
A) 1/(5ln(2)x)
B) 1/5ln(2)
C) ln(5)/ln(2)
D) 5/ln(2)x
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The derivative of log base b (u) is 1/(u ln(b)). In this case, u = 5x. So, the derivative of f(x) = log base 2 (5x) is 1/(5x ln(2)).

Explanation:
To differentiate a logarithm with a different base, use the change-of-base formula and then apply the chain rule.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that the derivative involves both u and the natural logarithm of the base. Choice D (5/ln(2)x) is incorrect because it does not include ln(2).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (LogProduct):
Find the derivative of f(x) = 2ln(x) + ln(3x^2).
A) 2/x + 6/x
B) 2/x + 3/2x
C) 4/x + 6
D) 2 + 3
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The derivative of a sum of logarithms is the sum of their derivatives. So, the derivative of f(x) = 2ln(x) + ln(3x^2) is (2/x) + (1/(3x^2)) * 6 = 2/x + 6/2x.

Explanation:
When differentiating a sum of logarithms, apply the chain rule to each term separately and then add them together.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to multiply by the coefficient inside the second logarithm or to incorrectly distribute the derivative. Choice B (2/x + 3/2x) is incorrect because it does not account for the coefficient 6.

Problem 4 (LogQuotient):
Find the derivative of f(x) = ln(x/4).
A) 1/(x/4)
B) 1/x - 1/4
C) 4/x
D) 1/x
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The derivative of ln(u/v) is (v/u)/(u/v) = v/u. In this case, u = x and v = 4. So, the derivative of f(x) = ln(x/4) is 4/x - 1/4.

Explanation:
To differentiate a logarithm of a quotient, use the quotient rule and then simplify the result.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to subtract the derivative of the denominator from the derivative of the numerator. Choice A (1/(x/4)) is incorrect because it does not properly handle the quotient.

Problem 5 (LogBasee):
Find the derivative of f(x) = ln(e^3x).
A) 3e^3x
B) 3/x
C) e^(3x)
D) 1/e^3x
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The logarithm of e raised to a power is equal to the power itself. So, f(x) = ln(e^3x) simplifies to 3x, and its derivative is 3e^3x.

Explanation:
When the argument inside the logarithm is e raised to a power, you can simplify it before differentiating.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to apply the chain rule to ln(e^3x) directly, which would lead to an incorrect result. Choice D (1/e^3x) is incorrect because it assumes the logarithm has base e.

Problem 6 (LogNatural):
Find the derivative of f(x) = 4ln(x).
A) 4/x
B) 4ln(x)
C) 1/(x ln(10))
D) 1/x
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The derivative of a logarithm with base e (natural logarithm) is 1/x. So, the derivative of f(x) = 4ln(x) is 4/x.

Explanation:
Remember that the derivative of ln(x) is 1/x, regardless of whether it's written as loge(x) or ln(x).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to confuse natural logarithms with other bases. Choice C (1/(x ln(10))) is incorrect because it assumes a base-10 logarithm.

Problem 7 (LogSumOfLogs):
Find the derivative of f(x) = ln(ln(x)) + ln(2).
A) (1/ln(x))/x
B) 1/(ln(x))^2
C) 1/x * 1/ln(x)
D) 1/(ln(x))
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The derivative of ln(ln(x)) is (1/ln(x)) * (1/x) = 1/(x ln(x)). So, the derivative of f(x) = ln(ln(x)) + ln(2) is 1/(x ln(x)), which simplifies to 1/(ln(x))^2.

Explanation:
When differentiating a logarithm of another logarithm, apply the chain rule twice. Remember to simplify the result.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to multiply by the derivative of the inner logarithm or to incorrectly distribute the derivative. Choice A (1/ln(x))/x is incorrect because it does not handle the outer logarithm correctly.
