Problem 1 (Q1):
$
A) 2
B) 4
C) 6
D) 12
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To find the total cost, multiply the number of apples by their individual price: 3 apples * $2/apple = $6.

Explanation:
This question tests multiplication in a real-world context.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is forgetting to multiply.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Q2):
miles
A) 120
B) 150
C) 90
D) 300
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To find the distance traveled, multiply the speed by the time: 60 mph * 2.5 hours = 150 miles.

Explanation:
This question tests the application of distance formula (distance = speed × time).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is using incorrect unit conversions.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Q3):
square feet
A) 2
B) 16
C) 80
D) 100
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To find the area of a rectangle, multiply its length by its width: 10 ft * 8 ft = 80 square feet.

Explanation:
This question tests the application of the area formula for rectangles.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is adding dimensions instead of multiplying.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Q4):
books
A) 15
B) 16
C) 23
D) 24
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To find the number of books, divide the total weight capacity by the weight of each book: 25 kg / 1.5 kg/book = 16.67 ≈ 16 books.

Explanation:
This question tests division to determine items within a weight limit.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is rounding up without considering indivisible items.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (Q5):
% change in temperature
A) -5°C
B) 5°C
C) 10°C
D) 15°C
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To find the net change in temperature, calculate percentage increases and decreases: 20% increase - 15% decrease = 5% + 10% = 15%

Explanation:
This question tests percentage changes in temperature scenarios.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is adding percentages instead of subtracting.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (Q6):
inches
A) 16π
B) 8π
C) 4π
D) 2π
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To find the circumference of a circle, use the formula: circumference = π × diameter. For a diameter of 8 inches, the circumference is 8π inches.

Explanation:
This question tests the application of the circumference formula for circles.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is using radius instead of diameter.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Q7):
square meters
A) 9
B) 18
C) 27
D) 36
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To find the area of a triangle, use the formula: area = 0.5 × base × height. For a base of 6 m and height of 6 m, the area is 0.5 × 6 m × 6 m = 18 square meters.

Explanation:
This question tests the application of the area formula for triangles.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is using base and height of a rectangle instead of a triangle.
