Problem 1 (:1):
Find the surface area of a cylinder with radius 5 cm and height 10 cm using the shell method.
A) 25ﻯπ cm²
B) 78.5π cm²
C) 250π cm²
D) 500π cm²
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The surface area of a cylinder using the shell method is given by 2ﻯπrh, where r is the radius and h is the height. Plugging in the values for this problem: 2ﻯπ × 5 cm × 10 cm = 100π cm². Doubling this to account for both curved surfaces gives us 200π cm², which simplifies to 500π cm².

Explanation:
The surface area formula for a cylinder using the shell method is applied correctly with the given radius and height.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be forgetting to double the result since there are two curved surfaces on a cylinder.

Problem 4 (4):
Calculate the surface area of a rectangular prism with length 8 cm, width 6 cm, and height 3 cm using the shell method (not applicable).
A) 172π cm²
B) 192π cm²
C) 216π cm²
D) 240π cm²
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The surface area of a rectangular prism is given by 2lw + 2lh + 2wh, not the shell method. Plugging in the values for this problem: 2 × 8 cm × 6 cm + 2 × 8 cm × 3 cm + 2 × 6 cm × 3 cm = 96 cm² + 48 cm² + 36 cm² = 180 cm². Since there are no curved surfaces, the shell method is not applicable.

Explanation:
The correct formula for the surface area of a rectangular prism is used, recognizing that the shell method is not applicable.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be attempting to use the shell method, which is meant for cylindrical and conical shapes, instead of the proper formula for a rectangular prism.

Problem 5 (5):
Find the surface area of a right circular cone with radius 9 cm and height 12 cm using the shell method.
A) 486π cm²
B) 567π cm²
C) 648π cm²
D) 729π cm²
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The surface area of a right circular cone using the shell method is given by ×πrl, where r is the radius and l is the slant height. First, we need to find the slant height using Pythagorean theorem: l = √(r² + h²) = √(9 cm² + 12 cm²) = √225 cm² = 15 cm. Now, plugging in the values for this problem: π × 9 cm × 15 cm = 135π cm². Since there's only one curved surface on a cone, we don't need to double the result. Thus, the surface area is 135π cm².

Explanation:
The correct formula for the surface area of a right circular cone using the shell method is applied with the given radius and height, along with finding the slant height using Pythagorean theorem.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be forgetting to find the slant height or applying the wrong formula.
