Problem 1 (Asdf1):
A box weighs 10 kg and is being lifted vertically by a crane at a constant speed. What is the net force acting on the box?
A) 0 N
B) 9.8 N
C) 10 N
D) 20 N
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The box is in equilibrium as it's lifted at a constant speed. In equilibrium, the net force acting on an object is zero. Therefore, the correct answer is 0 N.

Explanation:
Equilibrium occurs when gravitational force (mg) and the upward crane force balance each other out, resulting in no acceleration and a net force of zero.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to choose '9.8 N', which is the box's weight due to gravity alone, without considering the crane's upward force that counteracts it.

Problem 2 (Asdf2):
An apple falls from a tree and reaches the ground in 3 seconds. What is the average velocity of the apple during its fall?
A) 0 m/s
B) 9.8 m/s
C) 14.7 m/s
D) 29.4 m/s
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The average velocity is (final velocity - initial velocity) / time. The apple starts from rest, so its initial velocity is 0 and the final velocity is approximately 9.8 m/s due to free fall. Average velocity ≈ (9.8 m/s * 3 s) / 3 s = 9.8 m/s.

Explanation:
The apple accelerates due to gravity during its fall. Calculating the average velocity accounts for both the increase in speed and the time spent falling.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to choose '0 m/s', assuming that since the apple starts from rest, its average velocity must also be zero, neglecting the acceleration.

Problem 3 (Asdf3):
A block of wood with a mass of 2 kg slides across a frictionless surface at a constant speed of 5 m/s. What is the kinetic energy of the block?
A) 10 J
B) 25 J
C) 50 J
D) 75 J
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Kinetic energy (KE) = 0.5 * m * v². Plugging in the values: KE = 0.5 * 2 kg * (5 m/s)² = 25 J.

Explanation:
The block's kinetic energy depends on its mass and velocity squared. Since it slides at a constant speed, its kinetic energy remains constant.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to multiply only the mass by the velocity (10 J = 2 kg * 5 m/s), neglecting the square of the velocity term.

Problem 4 (Asdf4):
A spring with a spring constant of 100 N/m is stretched from its natural length by 0.2 meters. What is the potential energy stored in the spring?
A) 2 J
B) 4 J
C) 8 J
D) 16 J
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Potential energy (PE) = 0.5 * k * x². Plugging in the values: PE = 0.5 * 100 N/m * (0.2 m)² = 4 J.

Explanation:
The potential energy stored in a spring increases with its displacement from equilibrium, considering both the spring's stiffness and the displacement.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to multiply only the spring constant by the displacement (2 J = 100 N/m * 0.2 m), neglecting the square of the displacement term.

Problem 5 (Asdf5):
A car with a mass of 1,500 kg accelerates from 0 to 60 km/h in 8 seconds. What is the average power output of the engine during this acceleration?
A) 2.2 kW
B) 4.4 kW
C) 8.8 kW
D) 17.6 kW
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Average power (P) = (0.5 * m * (vf² - vi²)) / t. Plugging in the values: P ≈ (0.5 * 1500 kg * ((16.67 m/s)² - (0 m/s)²)) / 8 s = 8.8 kW.

Explanation:
The average power is determined by the change in kinetic energy over time, considering mass and velocity change during acceleration.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to calculate instantaneous power at a specific moment instead of finding the average power over the entire duration.

Problem 6 (Asdf6):
An object with a mass of 4 kg is placed on an inclined plane that makes a 30° angle with the horizontal. If the coefficient of static friction between the object and the plane is 0.5, what is the maximum force parallel to the slope that can be applied without causing the object to slide?
A) 19.6 N
B) 29.4 N
C) 39.2 N
D) 49 N
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Maximum static friction force = μs * mg * cos(θ). Plugging in the values: fs_max ≈ 0.5 * 4 kg * 9.8 m/s² * cos(30°) ≈ 29.4 N.

Explanation:
The maximum force that can be applied without sliding is determined by static friction, considering mass, gravitational force perpendicular to the plane, and the angle of inclination.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is using sine instead of cosine, leading to an incorrect calculation of the maximum friction force.

Problem 7 (Asdf7):
A block of ice with a mass of 1 kg is placed on a frozen lake. If the coefficient of kinetic friction between the ice and the lake is 0.1, how far will the block slide if pushed with a constant force of 5 N for 2 seconds?
A) 0.1 m
B) 0.2 m
C) 0.4 m
D) 0.8 m
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Acceleration (a) = F / (μk * m). Plugging in the values: a ≈ 5 N / (0.1 * 1 kg) = 50 m/s². Distance (d) = 0.5 * a * t². Plugging in the values: d ≈ 0.5 * 50 m/s² * (2 s)² = 0.4 m.

Explanation:
The block slides due to the applied force and experiences friction opposing its motion. Distance traveled depends on acceleration caused by net force and time of action.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is assuming constant velocity without considering deceleration due to friction.
