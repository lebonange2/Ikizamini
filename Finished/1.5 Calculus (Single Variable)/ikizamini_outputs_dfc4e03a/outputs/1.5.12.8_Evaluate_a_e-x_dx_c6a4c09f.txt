Problem 1 (:1):
Calculate the definite integral ∫[2,∞] e^(-x) dx.
A) e^{-2}
B) 1 - e^{-2}
C) e^{-∞} - e^{-2}
D) 0
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
∫[2,∞] e^(-x) dx = [-e^{-x}]_{2}^{∞} = -e^{-∞} + e^{-2} = 1 - e^{-2}

Explanation:
The integral of e^(-x) is -e^{-x}. Evaluate at the limits of integration and subtract.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to evaluate the antiderivative at infinity, which results in -e^{-∞} = 0.

Problem 2 (2):
Evaluate ∫[1,∞] e^(-x) dx.

C) e^{-∞} - e^{-1}
D) 0
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
∫[1,∞] e^(-x) dx = [-e^{-x}]_{1}^{∞} = -e^{-∞} + e^{-1} = 1 - e^{-1}

Explanation:
The integral of e^(-x) is -e^{-x}. Evaluate at the limits of integration and subtract.

Common mistake note:

Problem 3 (3):
Find the value of ∫[0,∞] e^(-x) dx.
A) 1
B) 2
C) e^{-∞}
D) 0
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
∫[0,∞] e^(-x) dx = [-e^{-x}]_{0}^{∞} = -e^{-∞} + e^{0} = 1 - 0 = 1

Explanation:
The integral of e^(-x) is -e^{-x}. Evaluate at the limits of integration and subtract.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to think that ∫[0,∞] e^(-x) dx diverges, but it actually converges to 1.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (4):
Evaluate ∫[-3,∞] e^(-x) dx.
A) e^{3}
B) 1 - e^{-3}
C) e^{-∞} - e^{-3}
D) 0
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
∫[-3,∞] e^(-x) dx = [-e^{-x}]_{-3}^{∞} = -e^{-∞} + e^{-(-3)} = 1 - e^{-3}

Explanation:
The integral of e^(-x) is -e^{-x}. Evaluate at the limits of integration and subtract.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that the limit of integration is negative, which results in e^{-(-3)} = e^{3}.

Problem 5 (5):
Calculate the definite integral ∫[1,∞] 2e^(-x) dx.
A) 2 - 2e^{-1}
B) 2e^{-1}
C) 2(e^{-∞} - e^{-1})
D) 0
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
∫[1,∞] 2e^(-x) dx = [2(-e^{-x})]_{1}^{∞} = -2e^{-∞} + 2e^{-1} = 2(1 - e^{-1})

Explanation:
The integral of 2e^(-x) is -2e^{-x}. Evaluate at the limits of integration and subtract.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to multiply by the coefficient 2 when finding the antiderivative.

Problem 6 (6):
