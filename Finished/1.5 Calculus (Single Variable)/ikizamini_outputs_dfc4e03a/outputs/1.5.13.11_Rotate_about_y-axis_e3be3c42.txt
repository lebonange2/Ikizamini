Problem 1 (AsdFgHjK):
What is the result of rotating the figure 90 degrees counterclockwise about the y-axis?
A) [insert image of rotated figure A]
B) [insert image of rotated figure B]
C) [insert image of rotated figure C]
D) [insert image of rotated figure D]
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
When rotating an object 90 degrees counterclockwise about the y-axis, the x-coordinates change to -y and the y-coordinates remain the same. The correct rotation is shown in option A.

Explanation:
Option A correctly shows the figure rotated as described. Option B does not rotate enough, Option C rotates too much, and Option D rotates around the wrong axis (x-axis instead of y-axis).

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Rotating around the x-axis instead of the y-axis.

Problem 2 (XyzWvUtS):
A cube is rotated 90 degrees about the y-axis. Which face will now be facing directly upward?
A) Front face
B) Back face
C) Top face
D) Bottom face
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Rotating a cube 90 degrees counterclockwise about the y-axis will cause the top face to move upward and become the front face. The correct answer is option C.

Explanation:
Option A remains in its original position, Option B moves to the back, and Option D moves to the bottom. Only Option C faces upward after rotation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Confusing clockwise with counterclockwise rotation.

Problem 3 (BnmLkIjH):
A right rectangular prism is rotated 90 degrees about the y-axis. Which pair of edges will now form a right angle?
A) AB and CD
B) BC and EF
C) DE and GH
D) FG and AB
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Rotating the prism 90 degrees counterclockwise about the y-axis causes edge FG to align with edge AB, forming a right angle. The correct answer is option D.

Explanation:
Options A, B, and C do not form right angles after rotation. Only Option D forms a right angle as a result of the rotation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Not considering how the edges change relative to each other during rotation.

Problem 4 (QweRtYui):
A triangle with vertices at (1, 2), (3, 4), and (5, 6) is rotated 90 degrees counterclockwise about the y-axis. What are the new coordinates of the vertex (5, 6)?
A) (6, 5)
B) (-5, 6)
C) (5, -6)
D) (-6, -5)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Rotating the triangle about the y-axis results in a change of x-coordinates to negative values while the y-coordinate remains the same. The new coordinates for (5, 6) are (-5, 6).

Explanation:
Option A and C have incorrect signs, Option D has both coordinates negated, which is not correct for a rotation about the y-axis.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Forgetting to change only the x-coordinate sign.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (ZxcVbNmL):
A circle with center (3, 4) and radius 2 is rotated 90 degrees counterclockwise about the y-axis. What are the new coordinates of the center?
A) (4, 3)
B) (3, -4)
C) (-3, 4)
D) (-4, -3)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Rotating the circle about the y-axis causes the x-coordinate to change sign while the y-coordinate remains the same. The new center is (-3, 4).

Explanation:
Option A and B have incorrect signs on the x-coordinate, Option D has both coordinates negated, which is not correct for a rotation about the y-axis.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Not accounting for the change in x-coordinate sign.

Problem 6 (KjHgFdfE):
A parallelogram with vertices at (1, 2), (3, 4), (5, 6), and (7, 8) is rotated 90 degrees counterclockwise about the y-axis. Which vertex will be located at the point (6, 5)?
A) (1, 2)
B) (3, 4)
C) (5, 6)
D) (7, 8)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Rotating the parallelogram about the y-axis causes vertex (7, 8) to be located at point (6, 5). The correct answer is option D.

Explanation:
Option A moves to (2, 1), Option B moves to (4, 3), and Option C remains in its original position. Only Option D ends up at the specified point after rotation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Not recognizing how the vertices change positions during rotation.

Problem 7 (GhiJklMn):
A regular hexagon with vertices on the Cartesian plane is rotated 90 degrees counterclockwise about the y-axis. Which vertex will now be at the origin?
A) (1, 0)
B) (0, 1)
C) (-1, 0)
D) (0, -1)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Rotating the hexagon about the y-axis causes vertex (0, 1) to be located at the origin. The correct answer is option B.

Explanation:
Option A moves to (0, -1), Option C remains in its original position, and Option D moves to (-1, 0). Only Option B ends up at the origin after rotation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Not considering how the hexagon's vertices change during rotation.
