Problem 1 (AsdFgHjK):
A rectangular garden has a fixed perimeter of 36 meters. What is the maximum possible area of the garden?
A) 9 square meters
B) 18 square meters
C) 81 square meters
D) 72 square meters
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The perimeter of a rectangle is given by P = 2l + 2w, where l is the length and w is the width. Given that P = 36 meters, we have 2l + 2w = 36. We can rewrite this as l + w = 18. The area A of a rectangle is given by A = lw. To maximize the area, we want to maximize both length and width while keeping their sum constant at 18. This occurs when the rectangle is a square (l = w), so l = w = 9 meters. Now we can calculate the maximum area: A = 9 	imes 9 = 81 square meters.

Explanation:
The problem involves maximizing area with a fixed perimeter, which typically leads to a square shape for maximum efficiency.
