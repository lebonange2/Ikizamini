Problem 1 (Just a number):
What is the angle required to rotate an object around the x-axis by 90 degrees?
A) 45°
B) 90°
C) 180°
D) 270°
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
A rotation of 90 degrees around the x-axis is a quarter turn. Therefore, the correct angle is 90°.

Explanation:
Rotating an object around the x-axis changes its position along the y and z axes while keeping the x-coordinate unchanged.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing 45° or 180° as they are not a quarter turn. The rotation must be exactly 90 degrees to meet the objective.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Negative angle):
What is the equivalent positive angle for rotating an object around the x-axis by -270 degrees?
A) 90°
B) 180°
C) 270°
D) 360°
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
A negative angle can be thought of as a rotation in the opposite direction. To find an equivalent positive angle, add 360 degrees to -270 degrees.

Explanation:
Rotating an object around the x-axis by -270 degrees is equivalent to rotating it clockwise by 90 degrees since adding 360° brings the object back to its original position along the y and z axes.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Subtracting 270° from 360°, which would result in a positive angle that is not equivalent. Remember to add 360° to convert a negative angle to a positive one.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Rotate object):
An object is rotated around the x-axis by an angle of θ. Which of the following statements is true about the object's new position?
A) The y-coordinate remains unchanged.
B) The z-coordinate remains unchanged.
C) Both the y- and z-coordinates remain unchanged.
D) None of the coordinates remain unchanged.
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
When an object is rotated around the x-axis, only the y- and z-coordinates change. The x-coordinate remains constant.

Explanation:
Rotation around the x-axis affects the position along the y and z axes while keeping the x-coordinate intact.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B or C, assuming that both y- and z- coordinates remain unchanged. Only the x-coordinate remains constant during a rotation about the x-axis.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Rotate vector):
A 2D vector (1, 3) is rotated around the x-axis by 180°. What are the new coordinates of the vector?
A) (1, -3)
B) (-1, 3)
C) (-1, -3)
D) (3, 1)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Rotating a vector (1, 3) around the x-axis by 180° will result in a reflection across the x-axis. The y-coordinate changes sign while the x-coordinate remains unchanged.

Explanation:
When rotating a vector around the x-axis, only the component perpendicular to the axis changes sign.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B or D, assuming that both coordinates change signs or remain unchanged. Only the y-coordinate should change sign.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (Rotate matrix):
A 2x2 matrix [[1, 2], [3, 4]] is rotated around the x-axis by 90°. What is the new matrix?
A) [[-2, -1], [-4, -3]]
B) [[-1, 2], [-3, 4]]
C) [[2, 1], [4, 3]]
D) [[1, -2], [3, -4]]
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Rotating a matrix around the x-axis by 90° is equivalent to swapping rows and columns and negating the elements in the new first column.

Explanation:
Rotation of matrices can be performed using matrix multiplication with a rotation matrix. For a 90° rotation, the result will have the original second row as the new first row, and the original first row negated as the new second row.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Swapping rows without negating elements or vice versa. Remember to negate the elements in the new first column.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (Rotate 3D object):
A rectangular prism is rotated around the x-axis by 180°. Which of the following statements is true about its new orientation?
A) The front face remains facing forward.
B) The top face remains facing up.
C) Both the front and top faces remain in their original orientations.
D) None of the faces maintain their original orientations.
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Rotating a 3D object around the x-axis by 180° will cause all its faces to flip, so none of them will maintain their original orientation.

Explanation:
A full rotation around any axis results in a complete change in orientation for a 3D object.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or B, assuming that certain faces remain unchanged. All faces will flip during a 180° rotation.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Rotate coordinate system):
A point P(2, 4, 6) is described in a coordinate system rotated around the x-axis by 45°. What are the new coordinates of point P?
A) (2, φ, 6)
B) (2, 4φ, 6)
C) (2, 4, 6φ)
D) (2, 4φ, 6φ)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
When the coordinate system is rotated around the x-axis by 45°, the new y and z coordinates can be found using trigonometric functions. The x-coordinate remains unchanged.

Explanation:
Rotating a coordinate system affects how points are described in that system. To find the new coordinates of a point after rotation, use appropriate trigonometric relationships.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Applying the 45° angle directly to the original coordinates without using trigonometry. Remember to consider the effect of the rotated coordinate system.
