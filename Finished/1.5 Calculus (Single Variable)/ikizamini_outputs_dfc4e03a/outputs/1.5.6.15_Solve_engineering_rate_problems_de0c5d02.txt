Problem 1 (OneMachineOneProduct):
A machine produces widgets at a constant rate of 10 widgets per minute. How many widgets will it produce in 45 minutes?
A) 400
B) 450
C) 500
D) 550
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To find the total number of widgets produced, multiply the rate by the time: 10 widgets/minute × 45 minutes = 450 widgets.

Explanation:
The solution is obtained directly from multiplying the given rate and time.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to multiply the two values, resulting in an incorrect total.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (TwoMachinesSameRate):
Two identical machines produce widgets at a constant rate of 15 widgets per minute. How many widgets will they produce together in 30 minutes?
A) 400
B) 450
C) 900
D) 1200
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Each machine produces 15 widgets per minute, so together they produce 15 × 2 = 30 widgets/minute. In 30 minutes, they will produce 30 widgets/minute × 30 minutes = 900 widgets.

Explanation:
The solution accounts for the combined rate of both machines and multiplies it by the given time.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to multiply only one machine's rate by the total time, neglecting that there are two machines working simultaneously.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (MachineAndHumanRate):
A machine produces widgets at a constant rate of 20 widgets per minute, while a human operator can produce 5 widgets per minute. If they work together for 25 minutes, how many widgets will they produce in total?
A) 600
B) 750
C) 800
D) 1000
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The machine produces 20 widgets per minute and the human operator produces 5 widgets per minute, so together they produce 20 + 5 = 25 widgets/minute. In 25 minutes, they will produce 25 widgets/minute × 25 minutes = 625 widgets.

Explanation:
The solution combines the rates of both the machine and the human operator and multiplies it by the given time.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to add the total number of widgets produced by each separately, without considering that they are working simultaneously.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (FlowRateAndTime):
Water flows through a pipe at a constant rate of 0.5 gallons per second. How many gallons will flow through the pipe in 4 minutes?
A) 12
B) 18
C) 24
D) 30
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To find the total volume, multiply the rate by the time: 0.5 gallons/second × 60 seconds/minute × 4 minutes = 12 gallons.

Explanation:
The solution converts minutes to seconds and multiplies the flow rate by the total number of seconds in the given time period.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to convert minutes to seconds, leading to an incorrect volume calculation.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (DistanceAndSpeed):
A car travels at a constant speed of 60 miles per hour. How far will it travel in 2 hours and 30 minutes?
A) 150
B) 180
C) 210
D) 240
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To find the distance, multiply the speed by the time: 60 miles/hour × 2.5 hours = 150 miles.

Explanation:
The solution calculates the total distance by multiplying the given speed by the total travel time in hours.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to convert the 30 minutes to hours, resulting in an incorrect distance calculation.

Problem 6 (MultipleRatesAndTime):
A factory has three production lines: Line A produces at a rate of 12 units per minute, Line B produces at a rate of 8 units per minute, and Line C produces at a rate of 6 units per minute. If all lines operate simultaneously for 30 minutes, how many units will be produced in total?
A) 900
B) 1200
C) 1500
D) 1800
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The total production rate is 12 + 8 + 6 = 26 units/minute. In 30 minutes, they will produce 26 units/minute × 30 minutes = 780 units.

Explanation:
The solution combines the rates of all three production lines and multiplies it by the given time.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to add the total number of units produced by each line separately, without considering that they are working simultaneously.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (RateAndEfficiency):
An engineer can assemble a widget in 10 minutes when working alone. If two engineers work together with the same efficiency, how long will it take them to assemble 5 widgets?
A) 20
B) 30
C) 40
D) 50
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
One engineer can assemble a widget in 10 minutes, so two engineers working together can assemble one widget in 5 minutes. To assemble 5 widgets, they will take 5 minutes/widget × 5 widgets = 25 minutes.

Explanation:
The solution accounts for the increased efficiency when two engineers work together and calculates the total time to assemble 5 widgets.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to assume that the time halves with each additional engineer, neglecting that the rate of assembly increases proportionally to the number of engineers working.
