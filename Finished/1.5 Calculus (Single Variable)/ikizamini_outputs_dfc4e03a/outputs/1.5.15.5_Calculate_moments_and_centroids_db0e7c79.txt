Problem 1 (AsdFg67h):
Find the moment of inertia of a solid cylinder with radius 5 cm and height 10 cm about its central axis. Assume uniform density.
A) 2375π g·cm²
B) 2500π g·cm²
C) 2875π g·cm²
D) 3000π g·cm²
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The moment of inertia for a solid cylinder about its central axis is given by the formula I = (1/2)MR², where M is the mass and R is the radius. First, find the volume V: V = πr²h = π(5 cm)²(10 cm) = 250π cm³. The density ρ cancels out when calculating the moment of inertia since M = ρV. Using r = 5 cm and h = 10 cm, I = (1/2)(ρV)(r²) = 625π g·cm². Doubling for the full cylinder gives 1250π g·cm², which simplifies to 2500π g·cm².

Explanation:
Apply the moment of inertia formula and use given dimensions; density cancels out.

Common mistake note:
Forgetting to double the moment of inertia after calculating for half the cylinder leads to choice A (2375π g·cm²).

Problem 2 (ZxvBn91k):
Calculate the centroid of a triangle with vertices at (0, 0), (4, 0), and (0, 6) in the xy-plane.
A) (2, 3)
B) (3, 2)
C) (4, 1)
D) (1, 4)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The centroid is found by averaging x-coordinates and y-coordinates: (0 + 4 + 0)/3 = 2 for x, (0 + 0 + 6)/3 = 2 for y. Centroid is at (2, 3).

Explanation:
Average x and y coordinates of vertices.

Common mistake note:
Incorrectly averaging coordinates leads to choices B, C, or D.

Problem 3 (XcVbN39m):
Find the moment of inertia of a thin rod with length 2 meters and mass 5 kg about an axis perpendicular to the rod at its midpoint.
A) 10/3 kg·m²
B) 20/3 kg·m²
C) 40/3 kg·m²
D) 60/3 kg·m²
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The moment of inertia is I = (1/12)ML². With M = 5 kg and L = 2 m, I = 20/3 kg·m².

Explanation:
Apply the rod's moment of inertia formula with given dimensions.

Common mistake note:
Using wrong formula or forgetting to square length leads to choices A, C, or D.

Problem 4 (PqRsT45u):

A) 9π kg·m²
B) 18π kg·m²
C) 27π kg·m²

Solution:

Explanation:
Apply the hoop's moment of inertia formula with given dimensions; remember it's thin.

Common mistake note:

Problem 5 (KjHgF56t):
Find the centroid of a rectangle with dimensions 8 cm by 12 cm in the xy-plane.
A) (5, 7)
B) (6, 9)
C) (7, 6)
D) (8, 4)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Centroid is at (w/2, h/2): (8/2, 12/2) = (4, 6). Note: use midpoints only.

Explanation:
Average midpoints of rectangle sides for x and y coordinates.

Common mistake note:
Using wrong dimensions or forgetting midpoints leads to choices A, B, or D.

Problem 6 (LmNop78y):
Calculate the moment of inertia of a solid rectangular prism with length 10 cm, width 5 cm, and height 3 cm about an axis passing through its center and parallel to its width.
A) 75π g·cm²
B) 125π g·cm²
C) 175π g·cm²
D) 225π g·cm²
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
I = Mw² + (1/12)Ml²h², where M = ρV. With V = lwh, I ≈ 400.25 g·cm², closest to B (125π g·cm²).

Explanation:
Sum moments of inertia for each face perpendicular to the axis.

Common mistake note:
Using wrong formulas or forgetting to sum moments leads to choices A, C, or D.
