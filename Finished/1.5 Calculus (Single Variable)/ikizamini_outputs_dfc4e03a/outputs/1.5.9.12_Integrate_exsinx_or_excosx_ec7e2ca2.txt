Problem 1 (1):
Evaluate the integral: ∫ e^(3x) sin(2x) dx
A) 1/5 e^(3x) cos(2x)
B) e^(3x) sin(2x) - x/6
C) 1/5 e^(3x) cos(2x) - x/6
D) e^(3x) sin(2x)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To integrate e^(3x) sin(2x), use the substitution method: u = 2x, du/dx = 2, dx = du/2. ∫ (1/6) e^u sin(u) du = (1/6) e^u cos(u) - C. Substituting back: (1/6) e^(3x) cos(2x) - C.

Explanation:
Integrate by substituting u = 2x and using the result from integrating e^x sin(x).

Common mistake note:
Students may forget to divide by 6, resulting in e^(3x) cos(2x) + C instead of 1/5 e^(3x) cos(2x) + C.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (2):
Evaluate the integral: ∫ x^2 e^(4x) sin(4x) dx
A) x^2 e^(4x) cos(4x) - x^2/16
B) x^2 e^(4x) sin(4x) - x^3/16
C) x^2 e^(4x) cos(4x) - x^3/8
D) x^2 e^(4x) sin(4x)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Use integration by parts: u = x^2, dv = e^(4x) sin(4x) dx. du = 2x dx, v = -1/16 e^(4x) cos(4x). ∫ x^2 e^(4x) sin(4x) dx = x^2 (-1/16) e^(4x) cos(4x) + ∫ (-1/16) e^(4x) cos(4x) 2x dx. Repeat integration by parts for the remaining integral.

Explanation:
Apply integration by parts twice, recognizing a second application is needed to solve the original integral.

Common mistake note:
Students may stop after first application and think answer is x^2 e^(4x) cos(4x) - x^2/16 + C; remember to apply integration by parts again.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (3):
Evaluate the integral: ∫ e^(-5x) cos(3x) dx
A) -e^(-5x) sin(3x)
B) e^(-5x) cos(3x) - x/15
C) -e^(-5x) sin(3x) - x/15
D) e^(-5x) cos(3x)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Use integration by parts: u = cos(3x), dv = e^(-5x) dx. du = -3sin(3x) dx, v = -1/5 e^(-5x). ∫ e^(-5x) cos(3x) dx = (-1/5) e^(-5x) cos(3x) + ∫ (3/5) e^(-5x) sin(3x) dx. Repeat integration by parts for the remaining integral.

Explanation:
Apply integration by parts twice with a negative exponent, simplifying the result.

Common mistake note:
Students may forget the negative exponent, resulting in e^(-5x) cos(3x) + C instead of -e^(-5x) sin(3x) - x/15 + C.
