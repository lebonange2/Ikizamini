Problem 1 (Inclined Plane Force):
A box of mass 10 kg is pushed up a frictionless inclined plane at a 30° angle with the horizontal. What force maintains its constant velocity?
A) 50 N
B) 75 N
C) 98 N
D) 150 N
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The required force equals m*g*sin(30°), where m = 10 kg and g ≈ 9.8 m/s². F ≈ 10 kg * 9.8 m/s² * sin(30°) ≈ 50 N.

Explanation:
Balancing forces on an inclined plane; using trigonometry to find the force needed for constant velocity.

Common mistake note:
Forgetting sine function or using wrong angle.

Problem 2 (Spring Potential Energy):
A spring with k = 10 N/m is stretched from equilibrium by 0.2 m. What potential energy does it store?
A) 0.2 J
B) 0.4 J
C) 0.6 J
D) 0.8 J
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Potential energy U = 1/2 * k * x², where k = 10 N/m and x = 0.2 m. U = 1/2 * 10 N/m * (0.2 m)² ≈ 0.4 J.

Explanation:
Spring potential energy formula; relating force to displacement from equilibrium.

Common mistake note:
Forgetting the factor of 1/2 or using wrong units.

Problem 3 (Simple Pendulum Period):
A 0.5 m long simple pendulum oscillates with a 30° amplitude and g = 9.8 m/s². What's its period?
A) 1 s
B) 1.4 s
C) 1.7 s
D) 2 s
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Period T = 2π√(L/g), where L = 0.5 m and g = 9.8 m/s². T ≈ 2π√((0.5 m) / (9.8 m/s²)) ≈ 1.7 s.

Explanation:
Simple pendulum period formula; considering length and gravity's effect.

Common mistake note:
Forgetting π or using wrong units.

Problem 4 (Work Done by Friction):
A 2 kg object slides across a surface at constant velocity. μ = 0.5, speed = 1 m/s, distance = 4 m. What work does friction do?
A) 4 J
B) 8 J
C) 12 J
D) 16 J
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Work W = Ff * d, where Ff = μ*N and N = mg. Ff ≈ 0.5 * 2 kg * 9.8 m/s² = 9.8 N. W ≈ 9.8 N * 4 m ≈ 39.2 J.

Explanation:
Work by friction; constant velocity, force of friction and distance traveled.

Common mistake note:
Forgetting to multiply Ff by d or using wrong units.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (Buoyancy Force):
An object (0.1 m³) floats in water displacing 75% of its volume. ρ_water = 1000 kg/m³. What's the buoyancy force?
A) 750 N
B) 980 N
C) 1225 N
D) 1470 N
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Buoyancy Fb = ρ * V * g, where ρ = 1000 kg/m³, V = 0.1 m³ * 0.75, and g ≈ 9.8 m/s². Fb ≈ 1000 kg/m³ * 0.075 m³ * 9.8 m/s² ≈ 1225 N.

Explanation:
Buoyancy principle; volume displaced, water density, and gravity.

Common mistake note:
Forgetting to multiply ρ, V, and g or using wrong units.

Problem 6 (Power in Electrical Circuit):
A circuit with 12 V and 2 A operates for 5 s. What power does it consume?
A) 6 W
B) 12 W
C) 30 W
D) 60 W
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Power P = V * I, where V = 12 V and I = 2 A. P = 12 V * 2 A = 24 W. Energy W = P * t = 24 W * 5 s = 120 J.

Explanation:
Electrical power formula; calculating energy over a given time.

Common mistake note:
Forgetting to multiply power by time or using wrong units.

Problem 7 (Conservation of Momentum):
Objects A (2 kg, 5 m/s) and B (3 kg, at rest) collide. A ends with 1 m/s. What's B's final velocity?
A) 2 m/s
B) 3 m/‌s
C) 4 m/s
D) 5 m/s
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Conservation of momentum: 10 kg*m/s = 2 kg * 1 m/s + 3 kg * v_B. v_B ≈ (10 - 2) / 3 ≈ 4 m/s.

Explanation:
Momentum conservation; setting up and solving the equation.

Common mistake note:
Forgetting direction or incorrect signs in momentum equation.
