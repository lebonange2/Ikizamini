Problem 1 (Incremental Scaling):
Find the volume of a cylinder with radius 4 cm and height 6 cm.
A) V = π(2)^2 	imes 6
B) V = π(4)^2 	imes 6
C) V = π(8)^2 	imes 6
D) V = π(16)^2 	imes 6
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The formula for the volume of a cylinder is V = πr^2h. Given radius r = 4 cm and height h = 6 cm, we substitute these values into the formula: V = π(4)^2 	imes 6.

Explanation:
This question tests the ability to choose the correct method (cylinder volume formula) and apply it correctly with given dimensions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be using the wrong radius (e.g., A uses r = 2 instead of r = 4).
