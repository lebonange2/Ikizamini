Problem 1 (Asdf):
What is the minimum perimeter of a rectangle with an area of 24 square units?
A) 16 units
B) 20 units
C) 24 units
D) 32 units
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To minimize the perimeter, make length and width close. Factors of 24: 4x6. Perimeter = 2 * (4 + 6) = 20 units. But this choice is incorrect. The correct pair for min perimeter is 4x4, giving a perimeter of 2 * (4 + 4) = 16 units.

Explanation:
Common mistake: choosing B (20 units), not realizing that a smaller perimeter (16 units) is possible by making sides closer in value.

Common mistake note:
Choosing 20 units, not considering a smaller perimeter of 16 units.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Bghj):
A square has an area of 36 square units. What is its minimum perimeter?
A) 24 units
B) 32 units
C) 36 units
D) 48 units
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Side length: √36 = 6 units. Perimeter = 4 * 6 = 24 units.

Explanation:
Common mistake: choosing C (36 units), thinking area equals perimeter for squares. In reality, the perimeter is always four times the side length.

Common mistake note:
Assuming square perimeter equals its area.

Problem 3 (Qwer):
What is the minimum perimeter of a triangle with sides measuring 5 units, 7 units, and an unknown third side?
A) 12 units
B) 16 units
C) 20 units
D) 24 units
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Third side: smallest integer > (7 - 5) = 3. Min perimeter with third side of 3 is 5 + 7 + 3 = 15 units, not available. Next smallest option is C (20 units), incorrect. Correct answer is B (16 units) with a third side of 4 units.

Explanation:
Common mistake: choosing C (20 units), not realizing that a smaller perimeter (16 units) is possible by making the third side closer to the smallest known side.

Common mistake note:
Choosing 20 units, not considering a smaller perimeter of 16 units with a third side of 4.

Problem 4 (Yuiop):
Find the minimum perimeter of a right-angled triangle with an area of 48 square units.
A) 24 units
B) 32 units
C) 40 units
D) 48 units
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Factors: 6x8. Perimeter = 6 + 8 + √(6^2 + 8^2) ≈ 24 units.

Explanation:
Common mistake: choosing C (40 units), not realizing that a smaller perimeter (24 units) is possible by using factors forming a Pythagorean triple with a difference of 1.

Common mistake note:
Choosing 40 units, not considering a smaller perimeter of 24 units.

Problem 5 (Bvnmm):
A rectangle has an area of 72 square units. If its length is twice its width, what is the minimum perimeter?
A) 36 units
B) 40 units
C) 48 units
D) 56 units
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Width: √(72/2) ≈ 6.12 units, length: 12.24 units. Min perimeter with width of 6 is 36 units, not available. Correct answer is B (40 units), as a square with sides of 8.49 units has a smaller perimeter.

Explanation:
Common mistake: choosing A (36 units), not realizing that a smaller perimeter (40 units) is possible by making the rectangle a square.

Common mistake note:
Assuming min perimeter occurs at given area constraint, without considering a square's smaller perimeter.

Problem 7 (Qwerty):
A right-angled triangle has an area of 90 square units. If one leg is 15 units long, what is the minimum possible perimeter?
A) 30 units
B) 45 units
C) 60 units
D) 75 units
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Other leg: 90 / (15/2) = 12 units. Hypotenuse ≈ √(15^2 + 12^2) ≈ 19.21 units. Min perimeter ≈ 15 + 12 + 19.21 ≈ 46.21 units, rounds to C (60 units).

Explanation:
Common mistake: choosing D (75 units), assuming the hypotenuse must be a whole number and not considering the decimal value in calculation.

Common mistake note:
Rounding up the hypotenuse without realizing it leads to an overestimated perimeter.
