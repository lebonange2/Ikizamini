Problem 1 (What is the factored form of x^2 - 4x?):
What is the factored form of x^2 - 4x?
A) (x - 2)^2
B) x(x - 4)
C) (x + 2)(x - 2)
D) (x - 4)^2
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The given expression is a special case of the difference of squares, which can be factored as x(x - a) where a = 4 in this case. So, the correct factorization is x(x - 4).

Explanation:
Factoring by recognizing patterns or common factors helps to simplify expressions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to use the wrong identity (e.g., (x + a)(x - a) for difference of squares instead of x(x - a)), which would lead to choice A.

Problem 2 (What is the factored form of 3x^2 + 9x?):
What is the factored form of 3x^2 + 9x?
A) 3x(x + 3)
B) x(3x + 9)
C) (3x + 9)^2
D) 3(x^2 + 3)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The expression has a common factor of 3x, which can be factored out: 3x(x + 3). So, the correct factorization is 3x(x + 3).

Explanation:
Factoring by taking out the greatest common factor (GCF) simplifies expressions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to factor out the GCF, leading to choice B or not factoring completely, which could result in choice D.

Problem 3 (What is the factored form of 6x^2 - 18?):
What is the factored form of 6x^2 - 18?
A) 3(2x + 3)(x - 3)
B) 6(2x + 3)(x - 3)
C) 3(2x - 3)(x + 3)
D) 2(3x - 9)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The expression has a common factor of 6, which can be factored out: 6(x^2 - 3). Then, we recognize the difference of squares inside the parentheses: (x + √3)(x - √3), leading to 2(3x - 9) as the factored form. So, the correct factorization is D.

Explanation:
Factoring by taking out the GCF and recognizing special patterns like difference of squares is crucial here.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to not recognize the difference of squares inside the parentheses, leading to choices A, B, or C.

Problem 4 (What is the factored form of 2x^3 - 8x?):
What is the factored form of 2x^3 - 8x?
A) 2x(x + 2)(x - 2)
B) x(2x^2 - 4)
C) $(2x + 4)(x - 2)$
D) $2x(x - 2)^2$
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The expression has a common factor of 2x, which can be factored out: 2x(x^2 - 4). Then, we recognize the difference of squares inside the parentheses: (x + 2)(x - 2), leading to 2x(x + 2)(x - 2) as the factored form. So, the correct factorization is A.

Explanation:
Factoring by taking out the GCF and recognizing special patterns like difference of squares is crucial here.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to not recognize the difference of squares inside the parentheses, leading to choices B, C, or D.

Problem 5 (What is the factored form of x^3 - 27?):
What is the factored form of x^3 - 27?
A) (x - 3)^3
B) $(x + 3)(x^2 - 3x + 9)$
C) $(x - 3)(x^2 + 3x + 9)$
D) $(x + 3)^2(x - 3)$
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The given expression is a special case of the sum of cubes, which can be factored as (x - a)(x^2 + ax + a^2) where a = 3 in this case. So, the correct factorization is (x - 3)(x^2 + 3x + 9).

Explanation:
Factoring by recognizing patterns like sum of cubes helps to simplify expressions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to use the wrong identity (e.g., (x + a)^3 for sum of cubes instead of (x - a)(x^2 + ax + a^2)), which would lead to choice A.

Problem 6 (What is the factored form of x^4 - 16?):
What is the factored form of x^4 - 16?
A) $(x - 2)^2(x + 2)^2$
B) $(x + 2)(x - 2)(x^2 + 4)$
C) $(x + 4)(x - 4)(x^2 + 1)$
D) $(x + 2)^3(x - 2)$
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The given expression is a difference of squares twice, which can be factored as (x^2 - a^2)(x^2 - b^2) where a = 4 and b = 1 in this case. So, the correct factorization is $(x - 2)^2(x + 2)^2$.

Explanation:
Factoring by recognizing patterns like difference of squares twice helps to simplify expressions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to not recognize both differences of squares, leading to choices B, C, or D.

Problem 7 (What is the factored form of 4x^3 - 27y^3?):
What is the factored form of 4x^3 - 27y^3?
A) $(2x + 3y)(4x^2 - 6xy + 9y^2)$
B) $(2x - 3y)^3$
C) $(2x - 3y)(4x^2 + 6xy + 9y^2)$
D) $(2x + 3y)^2(4x - 9y)$
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The given expression is a difference of cubes, which can be factored as (a^3 - b^3) = (a - b)(a^2 + ab + b^2) where a = 2x and b = 3y in this case. So, the correct factorization is $(2x + 3y)(4x^2 - 6xy + 9y^2)$.

Explanation:
Factoring by recognizing patterns like difference of cubes helps to simplify expressions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to use the wrong identity (e.g., (a + b)^3 for sum of cubes instead of (a - b)(a^2 + ab + b^2)), which could lead to choice B.
