Problem 1 (What is the definite integral of f(x) = -2x from x=0 to x=1?):
-2
-1
2
1
A) -2
B) -1
C) 2
D) 1
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The integral of f(x) = -2x is -x^2. Evaluating from x=0 to x=1 gives (-1^2) - (0^2) = -1.

Explanation:
When integrating a negative function, the area under the curve is negative. Thus, the definite integral will be negative as well.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that the area is negative when integrating a negative function.

Problem 2 (What is the net signed area between x=0 and x=2 for the function g(x) = -x^2 + 1?):
-3
-4
divide by zero
3
A) -3
B) -4
C) divide by zero
D) 3
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The function has two parts: a parabola with negative area and a constant increase. The area under the curve from x=0 to x=1 is positive (1), but from x=1 to x=2 it's negative (-3). Net signed area = 1 - 3 = -2.

Explanation:
When calculating net signed area, consider both positive and negative areas under the curve.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to only calculate the area between x=0 and x=1, forgetting that the function changes sign at x=1.

Problem 3 (Find the definite integral of h(x) = 3x^2 - 4x from x=-1 to x=1.):
-2
-1
divide by zero
1
A) -2
B) -1
C) divide by zero
D) 1
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The integral of h(x) is x^3 - 2x^2. Evaluating from x=-1 to x=1 gives [(1^3 - 2(1)^2) - ((-1)^3 - 2(-1)^2)] = [1 - 2 + 1 - 2] = 0.

Explanation:
When integrating, don't forget the constant of integration. However, since we're evaluating a definite integral, it will be zero.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to evaluate the antiderivative at both limits and then subtract.
