Problem 1 (Asdf):
Find the surface area of a cylinder with radius 5 cm and height 10 cm.
A) 25ﻯπ cm²
B) 50ﻯπ cm²
C) 75ﻯπ cm²
D) 100ﻯπ cm²
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The surface area of a cylinder is given by the formula SA = 2ﻯπrh, where r is the radius and h is the height. Plugging in the values for this problem: SA = 2ﻯπ × 5 cm × 10 cm = 100ﻯπ cm².

Explanation:
The correct formula was used to calculate the surface area, and the numbers were multiplied correctly.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is forgetting to multiply by 2 for the curved surface area or using the wrong radius or height.

Problem 2 (Asdf):
Calculate the surface area of a rectangular prism with dimensions 3 m, 4 m, and 5 m.
A) 90 m²
B) 120 m²
C) 150 m²
D) 180 m²
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The surface area of a rectangular prism is given by the formula SA = 2lw + 2lh + 2wh. Plugging in the values for this problem: SA = 2 × 3 m × 4 m + 2 × 3 m × 5 m + 2 × 4 m × 5 m = 120 m².

Explanation:
The correct formula was used to calculate the surface area, and the dimensions were multiplied correctly.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is forgetting to add the areas of all six faces or using the wrong dimensions.

Problem 3 (Asdf):
Find the lateral surface area of a cone with radius 6 cm and slant height 10 cm.
A) 72π cm²
B) 96π cm²
C) 144π cm²
D) 192π cm²
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The lateral surface area of a cone is given by the formula LA = πrl, where r is the radius and l is the slant height. Plugging in the values for this problem: LA = π × 6 cm × 10 cm = 60π cm².

Explanation:
The correct formula was used to calculate the lateral surface area, and the numbers were multiplied correctly.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is using the wrong formula (total surface area instead of lateral) or mixing up radius with slant height.

Problem 5 (Asdf):
Find the total surface area of a triangular prism with base 8 cm, height 6 cm, and length 10 cm.
A) 208 cm²
B) 248 cm²
C) 288 cm²
D) 328 cm²
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The total surface area of a triangular prism is given by the formula SA = 2b × h + 2lw, where b is the base, h is the height of the triangle, l is the length, and w is the width of the triangle. The width can be found using Pythagorean theorem: w = √(l² - (h/2)²). Plugging in the values for this problem: w = √(10 cm² - (6 cm / 2)²) = 8 cm, SA = 2 × 8 cm × 6 cm + 2 × 8 cm × 10 cm = 208 cm².

Explanation:
The correct formula was used to calculate the surface area, and the width of the triangle was calculated correctly using Pythagorean theorem.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is forgetting to add the areas of all faces or using the wrong dimensions.

Problem 6 (Asdf):
Calculate the surface area of a rectangular pyramid with base 12 cm x 8 cm and height 15 cm.
A) 336 cm²
B) 432 cm²
C) 576 cm²
D) 672 cm²
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The surface area of a rectangular pyramid is given by the formula SA = B + 1/2 × P × h, where B is the base area, P is the perimeter of the base, and h is the height. Plugging in the values for this problem: B = 12 cm × 8 cm = 96 cm², P = 2 × (12 cm + 8 cm) = 40 cm, SA = 96 cm² + 1/2 × 40 cm × 15 cm = 336 cm².

Explanation:
The correct formula was used to calculate the surface area, and the base area and perimeter were calculated correctly.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is forgetting to add the base area or using the wrong height.

Problem 7 (Asdf):
Find the surface area of a cone with radius 9 cm and slant height 12 cm, including the base.
A) 282.6π cm²
B) 378π cm²
C) 472.5π cm²
D) 567π cm²
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The total surface area of a cone, including the base, is given by the formula SA = πr² + πrl, where r is the radius and l is the slant height. Plugging in the values for this problem: SA = π × (9 cm)² + π × 9 cm × 12 cm = 472.5π cm².

Explanation:
The correct formula was used to calculate the total surface area, and the radius and slant height were used correctly.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is forgetting to add the base area or using the wrong formula (lateral instead of total).
