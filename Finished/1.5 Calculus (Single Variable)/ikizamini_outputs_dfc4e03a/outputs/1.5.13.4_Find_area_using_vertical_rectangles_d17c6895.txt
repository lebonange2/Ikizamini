Problem 1 (OneRectEasy):
Find the area of the rectangle with a base of 6 units and a height of 4 units.
A) 10 square units
B) 20 square units
C) 24 square units
D) 30 square units
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The area of the rectangle is found by multiplying the base and height: 6 units * 4 units = 24 square units.

Explanation:
Area calculations involve multiplying the dimensions of the shape.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to multiply the lengths of two sides that are not the base and height.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (TwoRectsDifficult):
Find the total area of the two rectangles combined. The first rectangle has a base of 8 units and a height of 5 units, while the second rectangle has a base of 4 units and a height of 7 units.
A) 60 square units
B) 62 square units
C) 66 square units
D) 70 square units
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The combined area is found by adding the areas of the individual rectangles: (8 units * 5 units) + (4 units * 7 units) = 40 square units + 28 square units = 68 square units.

Explanation:
The areas of separate rectangles can be added to find the combined area.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to add the bases and heights instead of multiplying each pair, then summing the results.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (RectWithHoleEasy):
Find the area of the rectangle with a missing corner. The outer dimensions are 10 units by 8 units, and there is a small rectangle removed from the center that measures 2 units by 3 units.
A) 64 square units
B) 70 square units
C) 74 square units
D) 80 square units
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The total area is the outer rectangle's area minus the inner rectangle's area: (10 units * 8 units) - (2 units * 3 units) = 80 square units - 6 square units = 74 square units.

Explanation:
When removing a shape from another, subtract its area from the larger shape's area.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to subtract the inner rectangle's area or to subtract it twice.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (RectWithDiagonalEasy):
Find the area of the rectangle with a diagonal. The length is 10 units and the width is 6 units.
A) 30 square units
B) 42 square units
C) 50 square units
D) 60 square units
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The area of a rectangle can be found by multiplying the length and width: 10 units * 6 units = 60 square units, regardless of the presence of a diagonal.

Explanation:
The area of a rectangle does not depend on its diagonals; only its base and height matter.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to try using the Pythagorean theorem instead of multiplying the length and width.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (RectWithUnevenSidesEasy):
Find the area of the rectangle with uneven sides. The longer side is 9 units and the shorter side is 5 units.
A) 14 square units
B) 20 square units
C) 45 square units
D) 75 square units
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The area of a rectangle is found by multiplying the length and width: 9 units * 5 units = 45 square units, regardless of their relative sizes.

Explanation:
Uneven sides do not affect the area calculation method.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to assume that one side must be the base and the other the height.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (RectWithFractionalSidesEasy):
Find the area of the rectangle with fractional sides. The base is 3/2 units and the height is 5/4 units.
A) 7 square units
B) 10 square units
C) 14 square units
D) 15 square units
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To find the area, multiply the base and height: (3/2) * (5/4) = 1.875 square units, which is approximately 1.9 square units when rounded.

Explanation:
Fractions can be multiplied like whole numbers and decimals.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to multiply the numerators and denominators separately.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (RectWithNegativeSidesEasy):
Find the area of the rectangle with negative sides. The base is -6 units and the height is -4 units.
A) 24 square units
B) -24 square units
C) 30 square units
D) -30 square units
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
When multiplying two negative numbers, the result is positive: (-6) * (-4) = 24 square units.

Explanation:
Multiplying negative numbers follows specific rules; in this case, a positive area results.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to assume that the product of two negative numbers is negative.
