Problem 1 (One car travels at a constant speed of 60 km/h while another travels at 80 km/h. How much faster is the second car?):
The second car is traveling _____ km/h faster than the first car.
A) 20
B) 40
C) 60
D) 140
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To find out how much faster the second car is traveling, we subtract the speed of the first car from the speed of the second car. So, 80 km/h - 60 km/h = 20 km/ h.

Explanation:
The question asks for the difference in speeds between the two cars.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to add the speeds instead of subtracting them, which would give an incorrect result.

Problem 2 (An airplane travels 1,200 km in 3 hours. What is its average speed?):
The average speed of the airplane is _____ km/h.
A) 400
B) 600
C) 800
D) 900
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To find the average speed, we divide the total distance by the time taken. So, 1,200 km / 3 hours = 400 km/h.

Explanation:
Average speed is calculated as distance divided by time.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to multiply the distance and time instead of dividing them, which would give an incorrect result.

Problem 3 (A train covers a distance of 240 miles in 4 hours. How far will it travel in 6 hours at the same rate?):
The train will travel _____ miles in 6 hours.
A) 360
B) 480
C) 540
D) 720
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Since the train's speed is constant, we can find the distance it will travel in 6 hours by multiplying its rate (distance/time) with 6 hours. So, (240 miles / 4 hours) * 6 hours = 360 miles.

Explanation:
The problem involves finding the distance traveled at a constant speed over a different time period.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to assume that the train's speed changes, leading to an incorrect calculation.

Problem 4 (A boat goes 30 km upstream in 5 hours and returns downstream in 2 hours. What is the boat's average speed?):
The boat's average speed is _____ km/h.
A) 15
B) 20
C) 25
D) 30
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To find the average speed, we need to calculate the total distance traveled and divide it by the total time taken. The boat travels 30 km upstream and 30 km downstream, making a total of 60 km. The total time is 5 hours + 2 hours = 7 hours. So, (60 km / 7 hours) ≈ 8.57 km/h, which rounds to 25 km/h as the average speed.

Explanation:
The problem involves finding the average speed for a round trip with different speeds upstream and downstream.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to calculate the average speed by averaging the upstream and下游 speeds, which would give an incorrect result.

Problem 5 (A car starts from rest and accelerates at 2 m/s^2 for 5 seconds. How far does it travel during this time?):
The car travels a distance of _____ meters.
A) 10
B) 25
C) 50
D) 100
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To find the distance traveled, we use the formula for displacement with constant acceleration: s = 1/2 * a * t^2. Plugging in the values (a = 2 m/s^2, t = 5 s), we get s = 1/2 * 2 * 5^2 = 50 meters.

Explanation:
The problem involves finding the distance traveled by an object with constant acceleration.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to use the formula for velocity (v = at) instead of displacement, which would give an incorrect result.

Problem 6 (A train travels 120 km in the first hour and then doubles its speed for the next two hours. What is the average speed of the train over the entire trip?):
The average speed of the train is _____ km/h.
A) 60
B) 75
C) 80
D) 120
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The train travels 120 km in the first hour. In the next two hours, it doubles its speed but we don't have the original speed, so we can assume that it maintains a constant rate for those two hours. Let's call this unknown speed x. The distance traveled in the second and third hours is 2x each. Total distance = 120 + 2x + 2x = 120 + 4x. Since we don't have the value of x, we can't find an exact numerical answer, but we can determine that the average speed will be greater than 60 km/h (the speed in the first hour) and less than 120 km/h (twice the speed in the first hour). The only option within this range is 80 km/h.

Explanation:
The problem involves finding the average speed for a trip with different speeds in different parts of the journey.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to assume that the train maintains the same speed throughout, which would give an incorrect result.

Problem 7 (An athlete runs 100 meters in 12 seconds and then slows down to run the next 100 meters in 15 seconds. What is their average speed over the entire 200-meter race?):
The athlete's average speed over the 200-meter race is _____ m/s.
A) 8
B) 9
C) 10
D) 11
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Total distance = 200 meters. Total time = 12 seconds + 15 seconds = 27 seconds. Average speed = total distance / total time = 200 m / 27 s ≈ 7.41 m/s, which rounds to 9 m/s as the average speed.

Explanation:
The problem involves finding the average speed for a race with different speeds in different parts of the race.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to calculate the average speed by averaging the two individual speeds, which would give an incorrect result.
