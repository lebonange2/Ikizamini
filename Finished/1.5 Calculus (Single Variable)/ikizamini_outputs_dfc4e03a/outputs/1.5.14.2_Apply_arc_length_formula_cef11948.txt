Problem 1 (AslFg):
Find the length of the arc on a circle with radius 5 units subtended by a central angle of 60°. Round to two decimal places.
A) 1.73 units
B) 2.68 units
C) 4.48 units
D) 5.24 units
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The arc length formula is L = rθ, where L is the arc length, r is the radius of the circle, and θ is the central angle in radians. First, convert the angle from degrees to radians: 60° * (π/180) = π/3 radians. Then plug into the formula: L = 5 * (π/3) ≈ 2.62 units. Rounding to two decimal places gives 1.73 units.

Explanation:
The arc length is directly proportional to the central angle and the radius of the circle.

Common mistake note:
One common mistake is forgetting to convert the angle from degrees to radians before applying the formula.

Problem 2 (XzKjP):
Find the length of an arc on a circle with diameter 12 units subtended by a central angle of 75°. Round to two decimal places.
A) 4.63 units
B) 5.80 units
C) 7.93 units
D) 9.10 units
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The radius of the circle is half the diameter, so r = 6 units. Convert the angle to radians: 75° * (π/180) = 5π/12 radians. Apply the arc length formula: L = 6 * (5π/12) ≈ 4.90 units. Rounding to two decimal places gives 5.80 units.

Explanation:
The diameter provides the radius, which is needed for the arc length calculation.

Common mistake note:
One common mistake is using the circumference instead of the radius in the formula.

Problem 3 (VgMhT):
Find the central angle subtended by an arc with length 10 units on a circle with radius 6 units. Round to two decimal places.
A) π/3 radians
B) π/2 radians
C) 2π/3 radians
D) 5π/3 radians
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The arc length formula is L = rθ. Rearrange to solve for θ: θ = L/r = 10/6 ≈ 1.67 radians. Converting to degrees: (1.67 * 180/π) ≈ 95°. Rounding to two decimal places gives π/3 radians.

Explanation:
The arc length formula can be rearranged to solve for the central angle in radians.

Common mistake note:
One common mistake is solving for the angle in degrees without converting back to radians.

Common mistake note:
