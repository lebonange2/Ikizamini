Problem 1 (Asdf):
Find the area under the curve of f(x) = x^2 from x=0 to x=2.
A) 4/3
B) 8/3
C) 16/3
D) 32/3
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To find the area under the curve, integrate f(x) = x^2 from 0 to 2 and apply the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus: ∫(x^2)dx from 0 to 2 = [(2^3)/3] - [(0^3)/3] = (8/3).

Explanation:
The area under a curve is given by the definite integral of the function. Integrate and evaluate at the limits.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to apply the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.

Problem 2 (Asdf):
Evaluate ∫[1, 3] (x + 1) dx.
A) 9
B) 12
C) 15
D) 18
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Integrate the function x + 1: ∫(x + 1)dx = (x^2)/2 + x. Apply the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus: [(3^2)/2 + 3] - [(1^2)/2 + 1] = [9/2 + 3 - 1/2 - 1] = 4. Multiply by the constant term: 4.

Explanation:
To evaluate a definite integral, integrate the function and apply the limits of integration.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to multiply the result by the constant term (in this case, 1).

Problem 3 (Asdf):
Find the definite integral of f(x) = 2x - 3 from x=1 to x=4.
A) -8
B) 0
C) 8
D) 16
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Integrate the function: ∫(2x - 3)dx = x^2 - 3x. Apply the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus: [4^2 - 3 	imes 4] - [1^2 - 3 	imes 1] = 8.

Explanation:
To find a definite integral, integrate the function and evaluate at the limits.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to subtract the antiderivative evaluated at the lower limit from the value evaluated at the upper limit.

Problem 4 (Asdf):
Calculate ∫[0, π/2] sin(x) dx.
A) -1
B) 0
C) 1
D) π/2
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The integral of sine is negative cosine: ∫sin(x)dx = -cos(x). Apply the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus: [-cos(π/2) + cos(0)] = [0 + 1] = 1.

Explanation:
Integrate the function and evaluate at the limits using the antiderivative of sine.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget that the sine function is negative on the interval [π/2, π], which would lead to an incorrect evaluation.

Problem 5 (Asdf):
Find the definite integral of f(x) = e^x from x=1 to x=3.
A) e - 1
B) e^2 - 1
C) e^3 - e
D) e^4 - e^2
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The integral of e^x is simply e^x: ∫e^xdx = e^x. Apply the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus: [e^3 - e^1] = [e^3 - e].

Explanation:
Integrate the function and evaluate at the limits using the antiderivative of e^x.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to evaluate the antiderivative at both limits, leading to an incorrect result.

Problem 7 (Asdf):
Find the area under the curve of f(x) = x^3 from x=-1 to x=1.
A) 0
B) 2/5
C) 4/5
D) 6/5
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Integrate f(x) = x^3 from -1 to 1 and apply the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus: ∫(x^3)dx from -1 to 1 = [(1^4)/4] - [(-1)^4]/4 = (1/4) - (1/4) = 0. The area under an odd function over a symmetric interval is zero due to symmetry.

Explanation:
The area under an odd function over a symmetric interval is zero due to symmetry.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget that the function is odd and thus the definite integral over a symmetric interval will be zero.
