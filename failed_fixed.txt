1.1.7.7 Solve nonlinear systems
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Problem 1 (Q1):
Solve the following system of equations for \(x\) and \(y\): \(x^2 + y^2 = 25\) and \(x + y = 7\).
A) (3, 4) and (-3, -4)
B) (3, 4) and (4, 3)
C) (5, 2) and (-5, -2)
D) (6, 1) and (1, 6)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
First, express \(y\) in terms of \(x\) from the second equation: \(y = 7 - x\). Substitute this into the first equation: \(x^2 + (7-x)^2 = 25\). Simplifying gives \(2x^2 - 14x + 24 = 0\), which factors to \((x - 3)(x - 4) = 0\). Thus, \(x = 3\) or \(x = 4\). For \(x = 3\), \(y = 4\); for \(x = 4\), \(y = 3\). Solutions are \((3, 4)\) and \((4, 3)\).

Explanation:
The system involves a nonlinear equation (a circle) and a linear equation (a line). By substituting \(y = 7-x\) into the circle equation, we can simplify it to a quadratic equation which shows the possible intersection points.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to miscalculate the factors of the quadratic equation or incorrectly simplify expressions when substituting \(y = 7-x\).

Explanation:
The solutions are found by setting the equations equal, leading to a quadratic equation. Solving it yields potential x-values which are then used to find corresponding y-values.

Common mistake note:
A mistake could be incorrect application of the quadratic formula, leading to wrong x-values.
