Problem 1 (Q1):
Find the x-intercepts of the quadratic function \( f(x) = x^2 - 4x + 3 \).
A) 1 and 3
B) -1 and 3
C) 2 and 4
D) -2 and -3
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To find the x-intercepts, set \( f(x) = 0 \): \( x^2 - 4x + 3 = 0 \). Factoring gives \((x-1)(x-3) = 0\). Thus, the intercepts are at \( x = 1 \) and \( x = 3 \).

Explanation:
The x-intercepts occur where the graph crosses the x-axis, which is where \( f(x) = 0 \). Factor the quadratic to solve for \( x \).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is incorrectly factoring the quadratic or solving for \( x \).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Q2):
Determine the x-intercepts of \( f(x) = 2x^2 + 3x - 2 \).
A) 1 and -2
B) 2 and -1
C) -2 and 0.5
D) -1.5 and 0.5
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Using the quadratic formula \( x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} \), with \( a = 2, b = 3, c = -2 \), we calculate \( x = \frac{-3 \pm \sqrt{25}}{4} \), so \( x = -2 \) and \( x = 0.5 \).

Explanation:
Applying the quadratic formula gives the roots, which represent the x-intercepts where \( f(x) = 0 \).

Common mistake note:
Missteps often involve calculation errors within the quadratic formula application, especially with square roots.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Q3):
What are the x-intercepts of the function \( f(x) = -x^2 + 5x - 6 \)?
A) -1 and 6
B) 2 and 3
C) 0 and 5
D) 1 and 5
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Rewriting \(-x^2 + 5x - 6 = 0\) as \(-(x^2 - 5x + 6) = 0\) and factoring gives \((x-2)(x-3) = 0\). The intercepts are at \( x = 2 \) and \( x = 3 \).

Explanation:
Factor out the negative to simplify the quadratic function, making standard factorization straightforward.

Common mistake note:
Forgetting to handle the negative sign can lead to incorrect factorization.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Q4):
Identify the x-intercepts of the quadratic \( f(x) = 3x^2 - 12x + 9 \).
A) 1 and 3
B) 1.5
C) 1.5 and 3
D) 0 and 3
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The equation \( 3x^2 - 12x + 9 = 0 \) factors to \( 3(x-1.5)^2 = 0 \). The intercept is at \( x = 1.5 \), a repeated root.

Explanation:
Factoring the quadratic results in a perfect square, indicating a single repeated root where the graph touches the x-axis.

Common mistake note:
Assuming two intercepts without recognizing repeated roots is a common misconception.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (Q5):
Calculate the x-intercepts of \( f(x) = x^2 + x - 6 \).
A) 1 and -6
B) -2 and 3
C) -3 and 2
D) 1 and 6
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Factor the quadratic: \( x^2 + x - 6 = (x+3)(x-2)\). Solving gives intercepts at \( x = -3 \) and \( x = 2 \).

Explanation:
Factoring gives the real roots where the quadratic equals zero, showing the x-intercepts.

Common mistake note:
Errors frequently occur during factorization due to incorrect recognition of number pairs.

Problem 6 (Q6):
Find the x-intercepts of \( f(x) = x^2 - 6x + 8 \).
A) 2 and 4
B) 3 and 4
C) 1 and 8
D) 2 and 3
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Factor the equation: \( x^2 - 6x + 8 = (x-2)(x-4) \). Solving gives intercepts at \( x = 2 \) and \( x = 4 \).

Explanation:
The solution is found by setting the factors to zero, representing the points where the function crosses the x-axis.

Common mistake note:
Misidentifying factor pairs leads to incorrect intercept calculations.

Problem 7 (Q7):
Determine the x-intercepts of \( f(x) = 4x^2 - 4x \).
A) -1 and 4
B) 1 and 4
C) 0 and 1
D) -1 and 0
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Set \( 4x^2 - 4x = 0 \) to \( 4x(x-1) = 0 \), resulting in x-intercepts at \( x = 0 \) and \( x = 1 \).

Explanation:
Factor out the common factor, simplifying into two easily solved expressions indicating intercepts.

Common mistake note:
Neglecting the common factor can result in missing one intercept.
