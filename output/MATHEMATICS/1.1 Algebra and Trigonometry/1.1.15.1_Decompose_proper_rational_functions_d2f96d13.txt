Problem 1 (:1):
Decompose the proper rational function: (4x^2 + 8x) / (2x + 4)
A) (2x + 4)(2x)
B) (2x + 4)(x + 2)
C) (2x)(x + 2)
D) (x + 2)^2
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Factor out the common factor from both the numerator and the denominator. The common factor is (2x + 4), which simplifies to 2(x + 2). So, the decomposition is (2x + 4)(x + 2).

Explanation:
The correct answer decomposes the rational function by factoring out the greatest common factor from both the numerator and the denominator.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to not recognize that 4x^2 + 8x can be factored as 4x(x + 2), or to incorrectly factor 2x + 4. Answer A fails because it doesn't fully factor out the common term, and answer C only factors one of the terms in the numerator. Answer D is incorrect because it squares x + 2 without factoring out the common term.
