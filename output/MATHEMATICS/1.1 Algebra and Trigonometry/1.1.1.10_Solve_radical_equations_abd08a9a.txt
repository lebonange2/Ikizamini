Problem 1 (Q1):
Solve the equation \( \sqrt{x} = 5 \).
A) 25
B) 10
C) 5
D) 0
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Square both sides to get \( x = 25 \).

Explanation:
To eliminate the square root, square both sides of the equation: \( \sqrt{x} = 5 \) becomes \( x = 25 \).

Common mistake note:
Students might choose B, calculating \( \sqrt{5} \cdot 2 \), or D, ignoring squaring.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Q2):
Solve the equation \( \sqrt{2x + 3} = 4 \).
A) 1.5
B) 2
C) 3.5
D) 5.5
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Square both sides to obtain \( 2x + 3 = 16 \), then solve for \( x \) to get \( x = 3.5 \).

Explanation:
Squaring both sides gives \( 2x + 3 = 16 \). Subtract 3 from both sides, then divide by 2 to solve for \( x = 3.5 \).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is subtracting 3 first, leading to an incorrect setup before squaring.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Q3):
Find \( x \) if \( \sqrt{x + 4} + 1 = 6 \).
A) 36
B) 21
C) 25
D) 29
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Subtract 1 from both sides: \( \sqrt{x + 4} = 5 \). Square both sides: \( x + 4 = 25 \). Solve for \( x = 21 \).

Explanation:
Subtract 1 from both sides to isolate the square root: \( \sqrt{x + 4} = 5 \). Square the result and solve \( x + 4 = 25 \) to get \( x = 21 \).

Common mistake note:
Choosing A could result from failing to subtract correctly before squaring.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Q4):
Solve for \( x \) in the equation \( \sqrt{3x - 5} = 2 \).
A) 3
B) 1
C) 4.5
D) 2.5
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Square both sides: \( 3x - 5 = 4 \). Add 5 and divide by 3, so \( x = 3 \).

Explanation:
Square both sides and rearrange to form \( 3x - 5 = 4 \), then solve for \( x = 3 \).

Common mistake note:
Choosing B might occur if incorrectly simplifying post-squaring steps.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (Q5):
Solve \( \sqrt{5x + 1} - 3 = 0 \).
A) 0.4
B) 2.4
C) 1.6
D) 3
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Add 3 to both sides: \( \sqrt{5x + 1} = 3 \). Square to get \( 5x + 1 = 9 \). Solve for \( x: x = 1.6 \).

Explanation:
Add 3 to isolate the square root, square both sides, and solve for \( x = 1.6 \).

Common mistake note:
Choosing D might result from improperly isolating the square root before squaring.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (Q6):
What is \( x \) if \( \sqrt{x - 6} = x - 8 \)?
A) 14
B) 6
C) 10
D) 8
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Square both sides: \( x - 6 = (x - 8)^2 \). Expand to \( x^2 - 17x + 64 = 0 \). Solve for roots \( x = 14 \) and \( x = 5 \). Only \( x = 14 \) makes both sides equal in the original equation.

Explanation:
Square both sides to form the quadratic equation. Solving gives roots \( x = 14 \) and \( x = 5 \). Verify both in the original equation; only \( x = 14 \) is valid.

Common mistake note:
Neglecting to check solutions might lead to accepting the extraneous solution \( x = 5 \).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Q7):
Determine \( x \) for \( \sqrt{7x} - 5 = 2 \).
A) 1
B) 3
C) 4.5
D) 7
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Add 5 to both sides: \( \sqrt{7x} = 7 \). Square both sides: \( 7x = 49 \). Thus, \( x = 7 \).

Explanation:
Add 5 to isolate the square root, square both sides, and solve \( 7x = 49 \) to find \( x = 7 \).

Common mistake note:
Incorrect isolation steps might lead to an incorrect choice such as B, 3.
