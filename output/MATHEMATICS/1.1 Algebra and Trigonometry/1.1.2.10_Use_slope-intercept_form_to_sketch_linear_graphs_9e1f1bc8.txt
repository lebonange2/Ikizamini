Problem 1 (Q1):
What is the y-intercept of the line given by the equation \( y = 3x + 2 \)?
A) -3
B) 2
C) 3
D) 0
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
In the equation \( y = mx + b \), \( b \) is the y-intercept. Here, \( b = 2 \).

Explanation:
The equation is already in slope-intercept form, \( y = mx + b \), where \( b \) is the y-intercept.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is confusing the slope (3) with the y-intercept, leading to choosing option C.

Problem 2 (Q2):
Which line represents the equation \( y = -\frac{5}{2}x + 4 \) on a graph?
A) A line crossing the y-axis at 4 and sloping downward.
B) A line crossing the y-axis at 5 and sloping upward.
C) A line crossing the y-axis at -4 and sloping upward.
D) A line crossing the y-axis at 7 and sloping downward.
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Since \( b = 4 \), the line crosses the y-axis at 4. The slope is negative, so it slopes downward.

Explanation:
The slope-intercept form \( y = mx + b \) shows the slope \( m = -\frac{5}{2} \) and y-intercept \( b = 4 \).

Common mistake note:
Students may overlook the negative slope, mistakenly thinking the line slopes upward.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Q3):
If the line \( y = \frac{1}{2}x - 3 \) were graphed, how would it appear?
A) A line starting at -3 on the y-axis and sloping upwards, rising 1, running 2.
B) A line starting at 3 on the y-axis and sloping downwards, rising 1, running 2.
C) A line starting at -3 on the y-axis and sloping downwards, rising 1, running 2.
D) A line starting at 3 on the y-axis and sloping upwards, rising 2, running 1.
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The y-intercept (where it starts) is -3. The slope \( \frac{1}{2} \) means it goes up 1 unit for every 2 units it goes to the right.

Explanation:
Use the y-intercept and slope from \( y = mx + b \) to determine the graph features.

Common mistake note:
Misidentifying the direction of the slope or intercept is common when not carefully assessing the equation.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Q4):
Identify the correct slope for the line given by the equation \( y = -7x + 1 \).
A) -1
B) 7
C) -7
D) 1
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
In \( y = mx + b \), \( m = -7 \), which is the slope.

Explanation:
The coefficient of \( x \) is the slope, hence \( -7 \).

Common mistake note:
Choosing positive 7 might result from ignoring the sign.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (Q5):
A line is represented by \( y = x - 2 \). Which point does the line pass through?
A) (0, -2)
B) (-2, 0)
C) (2, 0)
D) (1, -3)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The y-intercept \( b \) is -2, so the point \((0, -2)\) is on the line.

Explanation:
The line passes through the y-intercept, \( (0, -2) \), when \( x = 0 \).

Common mistake note:
A common error is neglecting that \( x = 0 \) for the y-intercept point, leading to wrong selections.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (Q6):
Which of the following is the correct graph representation for the equation \( y = 2x + 3 \)?
A) A line with slope 1 and intercept 3.
B) A line with slope -2 and intercept 3.
C) A line with slope 2 and intercept 3.
D) A line with slope 2 and intercept -3.
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
From \( y = mx + b \), \( m = 2 \) and \( b = 3 \).

Explanation:
The slope is the coefficient of \( x \), and the y-intercept is the constant.

Common mistake note:
Mistaking intercept signs is a typical error due to hasty evaluation.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Q7):
If the slope of a line is zero, which equation represents this line?
A) y = 4
B) y = x
C) x = 4
D) y = -3x + 4
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
A line with zero slope is horizontal, so the equation has the form \( y = c \).

Explanation:
For zero slope, \( y = mx + b \) becomes \( y = b \).

Common mistake note:
Confusing zero slope with vertical lines, mistaking \( x = c \) as the form.
