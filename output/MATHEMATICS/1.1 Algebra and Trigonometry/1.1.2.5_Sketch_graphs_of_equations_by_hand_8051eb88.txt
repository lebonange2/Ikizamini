Problem 1 (q1):
Which of the following is the correct sketch of the graph y = x^2?
A) An upward-opening parabola centered at the origin.
B) A downward-opening parabola centered at the origin.
C) A vertical line through the origin.
D) A horizontal line through the origin.
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation y = x^2 is a quadratic function that forms a parabola opening upwards with its vertex at the origin.

Explanation:
Quadratic functions of the form y = x^2 always produce parabolas opening upwards with the vertex at the origin.

Common mistake note:
Choosing B can occur if a student confuses the sign in front of x^2, affecting the direction of the parabola.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (q2):
What is the correct sketch of the graph of y = -2x?
A) A line passing through the origin with a positive slope.
B) A line passing through the origin with a negative slope.
C) A horizontal line through y = -2.
D) A vertical line through x = -2.
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The slope of the line is -2, indicating a negative slope, and it passes through the origin.

Explanation:
Linear equations in the form y = mx will always pass through the origin. Here, m is -2, leading to a downward slope.

Common mistake note:
Choosing A can occur due to misunderstanding the negative sign in front of the slope, resulting in confusion about the slope direction.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (q3):
Select the correct sketch for the equation y = |x|.
A) A V-shaped graph opening upwards with the vertex at the origin.
B) A U-shaped graph opening upwards with the vertex at the origin.
C) A straight line through the origin.
D) A horizontal line through y = 0.
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The absolute value function y = |x| results in a V-shaped graph that opens upwards.

Explanation:
Absolute value functions form V-shaped graphs, with the vertex at the origin reflecting no negative values.

Common mistake note:
B is a common mistake due to misconceptions between absolute value and quadratic functions, leading to confusion in graph shapes.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (q4):
Sketch the graph of y = 3, an equation with no x-term. What does it look like?
A) A horizontal line through y = 3.
B) A vertical line through x = 3.
C) A diagonal line passing through the origin.
D) A line parallel to the x-axis but through y = 0.
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation y = 3 represents a line parallel to the x-axis crossing the y-axis at y = 3.

Explanation:
For equations with a constant y and no x-term, the graph is always a horizontal line at that y-value.

Common mistake note:
Selecting B can happen if a student incorrectly associates the lack of an x-term with a vertical line.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (q5):
Identify the graph of y = x^3.
A) A line passing through the origin.
B) A V-shaped graph opening upwards.
C) An S-shaped curve through the origin.
D) A horizontal line through y = 0.
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The cubic function y = x^3 creates an S-shaped curve passing through the origin.

Explanation:
Cubic functions generate graphs with an S-shape because of the symmetry and inflection point at the origin.

Common mistake note:
Choosing A is a typical error when confusing cubic functions with linear equations, due to misunderstanding the graphical representation.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (q6):
Which of the following is the correct representation of y = sqrt(x)?
A) A parabola opening upwards starting from the origin.
B) A half-parabola opening upwards starting from the origin.
C) A vertical line through x = 0.
D) A horizontal line through y = 0.
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The square root function y = sqrt(x) yields a graph that starts at the origin and forms half of a parabola opening upwards.

Explanation:
Square root functions form a curve starting from the origin, only covering the positive x-region due to the domain constraints of real numbers.

Common mistake note:
Selecting A is a mistake often made by assuming full parabolas, reflecting confusion between quadratic and square root functions.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (q7):
Determine the graph that represents the equation x = 2.
A) A vertical line through x = 2.
B) A horizontal line through y = 2.
C) A line passing through the origin with a slope of 2.
D) A V-shaped graph with vertex at the origin.
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation x = 2 represents a vertical line crossing the x-axis at x = 2.

Explanation:
Equations of the form x = c depict vertical lines passing through x = c, irrespective of the y values.

Common mistake note:
Choosing B is a mistake often made when students confuse vertical and horizontal representations.
