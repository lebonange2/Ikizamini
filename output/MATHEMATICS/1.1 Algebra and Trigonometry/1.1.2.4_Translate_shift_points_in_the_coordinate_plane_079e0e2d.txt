Problem 1 (Q1):
What is the new location of the point (3, -2) after it is translated 4 units right and 3 units up?
A) (7, 1)
B) (3, 1)
C) (7, 5)
D) (-1, 1)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The point (3, -2) is translated 4 units right and 3 units up. Right translation increases the x-coordinate by 4: 3 + 4 = 7. Up translation increases the y-coordinate by 3: -2 + 3 = 1. So, the new point is (7, 1).

Explanation:
In translation, adjust the x-coordinate for left/right shifts and the y-coordinate for up/down shifts.

Common mistake note:
A common error is to confuse the direction of translation, such as moving left instead of right.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Q2):
Translate the point (-1, 5) left by 6 units and down by 2 units. What is the new point?
A) (5, 3)
B) (-7, 3)
C) (-7, 7)
D) (5, 7)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To translate left by 6 units, subtract 6 from the x-coordinate: -1 - 6 = -7. To translate down by 2 units, subtract 2 from the y-coordinate: 5 - 2 = 3. The new point is (-7, 3).

Explanation:
Subtract from x when moving left and from y when moving down.

Common mistake note:
Confusing left with right or up with down can lead to incorrect translations.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Q3):
If the point (0, 0) is shifted 8 units up, what are the new coordinates?
A) (8, 0)
B) (0, 8)
C) (8, 8)
D) (0, -8)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
A translation up by 8 units changes the y-coordinate from 0 to 8: (0, 8).

Explanation:
Only the y-coordinate changes when translating up.

Common mistake note:
Students may incorrectly adjust the x-coordinate or move in the opposite direction.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Q4):
Point (4, 7) is translated to (9, 12). What is the translation vector?
A) (5, 5)
B) (4, 7)
C) (9, 9)
D) (5, -5)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The translation requires moving from (4, 7) to (9, 12). Calculate the change: x: 9 - 4 = 5, y: 12 - 7 = 5. The vector is (5, 5).

Explanation:
Find the difference between coordinates to determine the translation vector.

Common mistake note:
Calculating changes incorrectly or reversing them (e.g., -5 instead of 5) is a typical error.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (Q5):
The point (-3, -4) is moved 10 units to the right. What is the resulting coordinate?
A) (7, -4)
B) (-13, -4)
C) (7, 6)
D) (-3, 6)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Moving right by 10 units adds 10 to the x-coordinate: -3 + 10 = 7. The y-coordinate remains the same: (-4). Result is (7, -4).

Explanation:
Right translation affects only the x-coordinate, increasing its value.

Common mistake note:
Forgetting which axis to adjust or leaving other coordinates unchanged can cause errors.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (Q6):
Translate the point (2, -3) so that it becomes (-1, 1). How many units was it translated?
A) 3 left, 4 up
B) 3 left, 4 down
C) 3 right, 4 down
D) 3 right, 4 up
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The point moved from (2, -3) to (-1, 1). This requires moving 3 units left: 2 - 3 = -1, and 4 units up: -3 + 4 = 1. Therefore, the translation is 3 units left and 4 units up.

Explanation:
Translation involves calculating differences in x and y to determine the movement.

Common mistake note:
Students often mix up directions, such as swapping left with right and down with up.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Q7):
Translate the point (5, 6) 3 units left and 5 units down. What is the new point?
A) (8, 1)
B) (2, 11)
C) (2, 1)
D) (8, 11)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To translate the point 3 units left, subtract 3 from x: 5 - 3 = 2. To translate 5 units down, subtract 5 from y: 6 - 5 = 1. The new point is (2, 1).

Explanation:
Apply negative shifts for left and down moves.

Common mistake note:
Confusing left with right or mixing up x and y directions are common errors.
