Problem 1 (Q1):
A square garden has an area of 64 square meters. What is the length of one side of the garden?
A) 6 meters
B) 8 meters
C) 10 meters
D) 12 meters
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The side length of the square is the square root of the area: \( \sqrt{64} = 8 \) meters.

Explanation:
A square with an area of 64 square meters would have a side length equal to the square root of 64.

Common mistake note:
Choosing 10 or 12 might result from incorrectly estimating the square root or miscalculating.

Problem 2 (Q2):
A ladder leans against a wall, touching the wall at 9 feet high. If the ladder is 15 feet long, how far is the base of the ladder from the wall?
A) 6 feet
B) 7 feet
C) 12 feet
D) 13 feet
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Use the Pythagorean theorem: \( x^2 + 9^2 = 15^2 \). \( x^2 + 81 = 225 \). \( x^2 = 144 \). \( x = \sqrt{144} = 12 \) feet.

Explanation:
Using the Pythagorean theorem, \( x = \sqrt{15^2 - 9^2} \) which calculates to \( 12 \) feet.

Common mistake note:
Mixing the hypotenuse with one of the other sides often causes errors.

Problem 3 (Q3):
A right triangle has one side of length 5 cm and a hypotenuse of 13 cm. What is the length of the remaining side?
A) 10 cm
B) 11 cm
C) 12 cm
D) 13 cm
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Using the Pythagorean theorem: \( 5^2 + x^2 = 13^2 \), meaning \( 25 + x^2 = 169 \). Solving gives \( x^2 = 144 \) or \( x = 12 \) cm.

Explanation:
The other side of the triangle is found by solving \( 5^2 + x^2 = 13^2 \).

Common mistake note:
Choosing incorrect squares, such as 11 or 10, due to mathematical errors, often leads to wrong answers.

Problem 4 (Q4):
A cube has a volume of 343 cubic centimeters. What is the length of one edge of the cube?
A) 5 cm
B) 6 cm
C) 7 cm
D) 8 cm
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Cube root of volume gives edge length: \( \sqrt[3]{343} = 7 \) cm.

Explanation:
The edge length of a cube is the cube root of its volume.

Common mistake note:
Mistakenly rooting through arithmetic or confusing cube roots with squares might lead to errors.

Problem 5 (Q5):
You pour 125 cubic meters of water into a new cylindrical tank with radius 5 meters. What is the height of the water in the tank?
A) 1 meter
B) 2 meters
C) 3 meters
D) 4 meters
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Volume of a cylinder is given by \( \pi r^2 h = 125 \). Solving for \( h \), \( h = \frac{125}{\pi \times 25} = 2 \) meters.

Explanation:
Applying the volume formula for cylinders, solve for height using the given volume and radius.

Common mistake note:
Confusing radius with diameter or forgetting \( \pi \) can lead to incorrect calculations.

Problem 6 (Q6):
A city's park is rectangular, and its diagonal measures 50 meters. If the park's length is 40 meters, what is its width?
A) 20 meters
B) 30 meters
C) 35 meters
D) 40 meters
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Use \( a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \). \( 40^2 + b^2 = 50^2 \). Solve to find \( b = 30 \) meters.

Explanation:
Using the Pythagorean theorem, the width is found when the length and diagonal are known.

Common mistake note:
Misapplying the diagonal theorem by incorrectly substituting values might result in wrong widths.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Q7):
Jane wants to tile a square floor. Each tile covers 1 square foot, and she needs 144 tiles. What is the length of one side of the floor?
A) 10 feet
B) 11 feet
C) 12 feet
D) 13 feet
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The side length of the floor is the square root of the number of tiles: \( \sqrt{144} = 12 \) feet.

Explanation:
To cover a square floor, the side length can be found by taking the square root of the total area in square feet.

Common mistake note:
Choosing a value that's too small or large, often from underestimating or overestimating the square root.
