Problem 1 (Q1):
Find the slope of the line passing through the points (3, 4) and (7, 10).
A) 1.5
B) 2
C) 3
D) 4
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The slope \( m \) is calculated by \( m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{10 - 4}{7 - 3} = \frac{6}{4} = 1.5 \).

Explanation:
Subtract the y-values and the x-values of the two points and divide the differences to find the slope.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is incorrectly swapping x and y differences, leading to an incorrect slope calculation.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Q2):
Determine the slope of the line that passes through the points (-2, 1) and (4, -5).
A) -1
B) 1
C) -1.5
D) 0
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The slope \( m = \frac{-5 - 1}{4 - (-2)} = \frac{-6}{6} = -1 \).

Explanation:
Subtract the y-values and the x-values in the correct order to apply the slope formula accurately.

Common mistake note:
Ignoring negative signs or swapping points incorrectly may result in wrong calculations.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Q3):
What is the slope of the line through (0, 0) and (3, 9)?
A) 0
B) 3
C) 9
D) 1
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Using the formula \( m = \frac{9 - 0}{3 - 0} = \frac{9}{3} = 3 \).

Explanation:
Divide the change in y by the change in x based on given points to determine the slope.

Common mistake note:
Miscalculating either change may lead to an incorrect slope.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Q4):
Calculate the slope of the line through (-1, -1) and (2, 2).
A) 1
B) -1
C) -2
D) 2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The slope \( m = \frac{2 - (-1)}{2 - (-1)} = \frac{3}{3} = 1 \).

Explanation:
Both x and y differences are the same; thus the slope is 1.

Common mistake note:
Errors often occur when subtracting negative numbers.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (Q5):
Find the slope of the vertical line through (5, -2) and (5, 3).
A) 0
B) Undefined
C) 5
D) 3
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The x-values are the same, thus the slope is \( \frac{3 - (-2)}{5 - 5} \), which is undefined.

Explanation:
Vertical lines have undefined slopes as division by zero is not possible.

Common mistake note:
Confusing vertical for horizontal lines can mislead one to assume zero slope.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (Q6):
Determine the slope of the line passing through (8, 3) and (8, 8).
A) 0
B) Undefined
C) 1
D) 5
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Both points have the same x-value, making the division \( \frac{8 - 3}{8 - 8} \) undefined.

Explanation:
Vertical lines do not have a defined slope due to division by zero in the slope formula.

Common mistake note:
Forgetting to check for zero in the denominator leads to incorrect assumptions of zero slope.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Q7):
If a line passes through the points (1, -3) and (4, -3), what is the slope?
A) 0
B) 1
C) -1
D) 3
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The slope \( m = \frac{-3 - (-3)}{4 - 1} = \frac{0}{3} = 0 \).

Explanation:
Horizontal lines have a slope of 0, represented by zero change in y-values across different x-values.

Common mistake note:
Mistakenly considering a non-zero change in y can lead to incorrect slope determination.
