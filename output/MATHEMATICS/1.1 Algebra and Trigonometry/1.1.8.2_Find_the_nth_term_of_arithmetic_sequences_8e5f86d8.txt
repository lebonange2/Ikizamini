Problem 1 (:1):
What is the 5th term of an arithmetic sequence with a common difference of 3 and a first term of 2?
A) 7
B) 9
C) 11
D) 14
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The formula for the nth term of an arithmetic sequence is: a_n = a_1 + (n-1)d, where a_1 is the first term and d is the common difference. Given a_1 = 2 and d = 3, we find the 5th term as follows: a_5 = 2 + (5-1) * 3 = 2 + 4 * 3 = 2 + 12 = 14.
