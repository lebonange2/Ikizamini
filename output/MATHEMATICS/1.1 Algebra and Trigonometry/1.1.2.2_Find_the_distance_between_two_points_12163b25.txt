Problem 1 (1):
Find the distance between the points (3, 4) and (7, 1).
A) 5
B) 6
C) 7
D) √25
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Distance = √((7-3)² + (1-4)²) = √(16 + 9) = √25 = 5.

Explanation:
Use the distance formula: √((x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)²).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is mistaking the calculated distance as the non-rooted value, leading to choosing choices like 6.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (2):
What is the distance between the points (0, 0) and (5, 12)?
A) 12
B) 13
C) 17
D) 11
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Distance = √((5-0)² + (12-0)²) = √(25 + 144) = √169 = 13.

Explanation:
Apply the distance formula from (0, 0) to (5, 12) to find √169.

Common mistake note:
Some might forget to apply the square root, resulting in a choice of 169 or another incorrect value such as 12.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (3):
Determine the distance between the points (-5, 2) and (-1, -3).
A) √26
B) 7
C) 5
D) √41
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Distance = √((-1+5)² + (-3-2)²) = √(16 + 25) = √41.

Explanation:
Plug into the distance formula: √((x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)²).

Common mistake note:
Not applying negative signs properly might lead to √26 or other errors.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (4):
Find the distance between points (-3, -4) and (3, 4).
A) 10
B) 14
C) 2√13
D) 8
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Distance = √((3+3)² + (4+4)²) = √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10.

Explanation:
Use the distance formula: √((x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)²).

Common mistake note:
Mistaking the simplification of √100 as 14 or 2√13 is a common error.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (5):
Calculate the distance between (9, -6) and (2, -8).
A) √53
B) 9
C) 7
D) √45
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Distance = √((2-9)² + (-8+6)²) = √(49 + 4) = √53.

Explanation:
Substitute into the distance formula to find the correct value: √53.

Common mistake note:
Errors can occur by miscalculating subtraction or failing the squaring process, resulting in √45 or similar errors.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (6):
What is the distance between (1, 1) and (-2, 5)?
A) 4
B) √25
C) 5
D) 6
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Distance = √((-2-1)² + (5-1)²) = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5.

Explanation:
Distance formula gives √25, translating to 5 by computation.

Common mistake note:
Some may calculate directly to 25 without recognizing the need for taking the square root.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (7):
Find the distance between the points (4, -3) and (-4, -3).
A) 8
B) √64
C) 4
D) 0
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Distance = √((-4-4)² + (-3+3)²) = √(64 + 0) = √64 = 8.

Explanation:
The x-coordinates are 8 units apart, while y-coordinates are the same.

Common mistake note:
Ignoring the y-difference or incorrect simplification could result in 0 or other errors.
