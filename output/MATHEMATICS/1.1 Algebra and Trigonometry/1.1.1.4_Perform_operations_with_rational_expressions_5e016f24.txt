Problem 1 (1):
Simplify the expression \( \frac{8x^2}{4x} \).
A) 8x
B) 2x
C) 2x^2
D) 4x^2
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
\( \frac{8x^2}{4x} = \frac{8}{4} \cdot \frac{x^2}{x} = 2x \).

Explanation:
Cancel the common factor of 4 in the numerator and denominator and simplify the variable terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to simplify incorrectly as \( 8x \), failing to divide both coefficients and the variables.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (2):
Add the rational expressions \( \frac{3}{x} + \frac{5}{x} \).
A) \frac{8}{x^2}
B) \frac{8}{x}
C) \frac{15}{x}
D) \frac{8}{2x}
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
When adding \( \frac{3}{x} + \frac{5}{x} \), use the common denominator \( x \): \( \frac{3+5}{x} = \frac{8}{x} \).

Explanation:
Add the numerators and keep the denominator the same.

Common mistake note:
One might incorrectly multiply the denominators, leading to \( \frac{8}{x^2} \).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (3):
Subtract \( \frac{2}{x} - \frac{4}{x} \).
A) \frac{6}{x}
B) \frac{-2}{x}
C) \frac{2}{x}
D) 0
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Subtract \( \frac{2}{x} - \frac{4}{x} \) by subtracting the numerators: \( \frac{2-4}{x} = \frac{-2}{x} \).

Explanation:
Subtract numerators \( 2 - 4 \) over the common denominator \( x \).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to add instead of subtract, leading to \( \frac{6}{x} \).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (4):
Multiply the rational expressions \( \frac{3}{2x} \cdot \frac{4x}{5} \).
A) \frac{12x}{10x}
B) \frac{12}{10}
C) \frac{6}{5}
D) \frac{6}{10}
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
\( \frac{3}{2x} \cdot \frac{4x}{5} = \frac{3 \cdot 4x}{2x \cdot 5} = \frac{12x}{10x} = \frac{6}{5} \).

Explanation:
Cancel \( x \) from numerator and denominator and simplify \( \frac{12}{10} \) to \( \frac{6}{5} \).

Common mistake note:
Failing to cancel \( x \) results in \( \frac{12}{10} \) or missing simplification.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (5):
Divide \( \frac{7}{x} \) by \( \frac{2}{3} \).
A) \frac{7}{2}
B) \frac{7x}{2}
C) \frac{21}{2x}
D) \frac{21}{x}
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
\( \frac{7}{x} \div \frac{2}{3} = \frac{7}{x} \cdot \frac{3}{2} = \frac{21}{2x} = \frac{21}{x} \).

Explanation:
Multiply by the reciprocal \( \frac{3}{2} \) and simplify.

Common mistake note:
A common error is to multiply instead of dividing, resulting in \( \frac{21}{2x} \).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (6):
Simplify the rational expression \( \frac{6x^2 + 12x}{3x} \).
A) 2x+4
B) x+4
C) 2x+3
D) 2x+2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Factor the numerator: \( \frac{6x(x + 2)}{3x} = 2(x+2) \). Simplify to get \( 2x + 4 \).

Explanation:
Cancel the common \( x \) from numerator and denominator after factoring.

Common mistake note:
A mistake is to divide only coefficients, leading to \( 2x + 2 \).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (7):
If \( \frac{a}{b} \cdot \frac{b}{c} \), what is the simplified result?
A) \frac{a}{c}
B) ab
C) \frac{c}{a}
D) \frac{a}{b}
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
\( \frac{a}{b} \cdot \frac{b}{c} = \frac{a \cdot b}{b \cdot c} = \frac{a}{c} \).

Explanation:
Cancel \( b \) from numerator and denominator, leaving \( \frac{a}{c} \).

Common mistake note:
Forgetting to cancel \( b \) might result in incorrect \( ab \).
