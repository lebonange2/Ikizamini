Problem 1 (Q1):
Calculate the partial sum of the first 5 terms of the arithmetic series given by: a_n = 3n - 2.
A) 35
B) 40
C) 25
D) 30
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The series is given by a_n = 3n - 2. The first five terms are: 1, 4, 7, 10, 13.
The partial sum S_5 = 1 + 4 + 7 + 10 + 13 = 35.

Explanation:
Calculate each term and sum them to find the partial sum of the series.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to miscalculate one or more terms, leading to an incorrect sum.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Q2):
Find the sum of the first 4 terms of the geometric series where a = 2 and r = 3.
A) 80
B) 98
C) 124
D) 24
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The first term a_1 = 2, a_2 = 2*3 = 6, a_3 = 6*3 = 18, a_4 = 18*3 = 54.
Sum = 2 + 6 + 18 + 54 = 80.

Explanation:
Each term is obtained by multiplying the previous term by the common ratio r.

Common mistake note:
Students may forget to multiply by the right number of terms, mistakenly multiplying fewer or more times.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Q3):
Calculate the partial sum S_7 of the arithmetic series where a_1 = 5 and d = 2.
A) 56
B) 77
C) 49
D) 63
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The series has a_1 = 5 and d = 2, so terms are 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17.
S_7 = (7/2)(5 + 17) = 63.

Explanation:
Use the formula for the sum of an arithmetic series: S_n = n/2 * (2a + (n-1)d).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to miscalculate the nth term, leading to an incorrect sum.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Q4):
Find the sum of the first 6 terms of the sequence defined recursively by a_1 = 3, a_n = a_{n-1} + 4.
A) 78
B) 90
C) 81
D) 87
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Terms are 3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23. Sum = 3 + 7 + 11 + 15 + 19 + 23 = 78.

Explanation:
Find each term using the recursive formula and then sum them.

Common mistake note:
Students may misapply the recurrence relation, giving incorrect terms.

Problem 5 (Q5):
For the series defined by a_n = 2^n, find the partial sum of the first 3 terms.
A) 15
B) 14
C) 12
D) 8
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
a_1 = 2, a_2 = 4, a_3 = 8. Sum = 2 + 4 + 8 = 14.

Explanation:
List and add up the first 3 terms of the series.

Common mistake note:
Students may calculate an additional term, leading them to an incorrect total sum.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (Q6):
Calculate the partial sum of the series where the nth term is given by \( a_n = n^2 + n \) for n = 1 to 4.
A) 35
B) 30
C) 50
D) 40
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Terms are \( n(n+1) = 2, 6, 12, 20 \). Sum = 2 + 6 + 12 + 20 = 40.

Explanation:
Compute each term and then calculate the total sum.

Common mistake note:
Miscomputing individual terms by mishandling the functional expression can lead to the wrong sum.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Q7):
Find the sum of the first 3 terms of a series where each term is i times its position \( a_i = 3i \).
A) 9
B) 6
C) 18
D) 24
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Terms are 3(1), 3(2), 3(3). So, sum = 3 + 6 + 9 = 18.

Explanation:
Multiply the index by 3 for each term, then sum the terms.

Common mistake note:
Multiplying the term incorrectly by not maintaining index integrity often results in wrong sums.
