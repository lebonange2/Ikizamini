Problem 1 (Asdf):
Factor the expression: x^2 + 4x
A) (x - 2)^2
B) (x + 2)(x - 2)
C) (x + 4)(x + 1)
D) (x + 4)(x - 1)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The expression can be factored by grouping. Group the terms as (x^2 + 2x) + (2x), then factor out x from each group: x(x + 2) + 2(x). Combine the x and 2 outside the parentheses: (x + 2)(x).

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by factoring the expression using distinct linear factors. A common mistake would be to try to factor it as a perfect square, which doesn't apply here.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Factoring as a perfect square (A), which would only work if the expression was x^2 + 4x + 4.

Problem 2 (Asdf):
Factor the expression: 12x^2 - 36x
A) 12(x - 3)^2
B) 12(x - 3)(x + 3)
C) 3(4x - 9)(x + 3)
D) 4(3x - 9)(x + 3)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Factor out the greatest common factor (GCF) of 12x from both terms: 12x(x - 3). Since the GCF is a multiple of x, we can further simplify by factoring out an additional x: 12(x)(x - 3) = 12(x - 3)^2.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by first factoring out the GCF and then recognizing that the remaining binomial is a difference of squares, which can be further factored. A common mistake would be to not factor out the GCF first.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Not factoring out the GCF 12x (B, C, D), leading to an incorrect or more complex factorization.

Problem 3 (Asdf):
Factor the expression: 6x^2 + 18x - 24
A) 3(2x + 8)(x - 1)
B) 6( x + 4)(x - 1)
C) 2(3x + 6)(x - 2)
D) 3(3x + 4)(x - 2)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Factor out the GCF of 6: 6(x^2 + 3x - 4). Then factor the quadratic expression by finding two numbers that multiply to -4 and add to 3 (these are 4 and -1): 6(x + 4)(x - 1).

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by factoring out the GCF and then using the AC method to factor the quadratic. A common mistake would be to not factor out the GCF first.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Not factoring out the GCF 6 (A, B, C), leading to an incorrect or more complex factorization.

Problem 4 (Asdf):
Factor the expression: 27x^3 - 18x^2 + 3x
A) 9x(3x - 1)^2
B) 3x(9x - 1)(x + 1)
C) 3x(3x - 1)^2
D) 9x(3x - 1)(x - 1)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Factor out the GCF of 3x: 3x(9x^2 - 6x + 1). The remaining quadratic expression is a perfect square, which factors to (3x - 1)^2. So, the final factorization is 3x(3x - 1)^2.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by factoring out the GCF and recognizing that the remaining quadratic is a perfect square. A common mistake would be to not factor out the GCF first or misidentify the perfect square.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Not factoring out the GCF 3x (A, B, D) or misidentifying the perfect square (B).
