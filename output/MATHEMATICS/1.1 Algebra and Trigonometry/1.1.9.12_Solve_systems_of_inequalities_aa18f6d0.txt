Problem 1 (Q1):
Solve the system of inequalities: \( y > 2x + 1 \) and \( y < -x + 3 \). Which region do these inequalities represent in the coordinate plane?
A) Region above both lines
B) Region below both lines
C) Region between the two lines
D) Region to the left of both lines
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The solution to the system of inequalities is the set of points where the region of \( y > 2x + 1 \) and \( y < -x + 3 \) overlap. Graphically, this is the area between the two lines.

Explanation:
To find the solution region, graph both lines on the coordinate plane. The correct region is where \( y \) is greater than \( 2x + 1 \) and less than \( -x + 3 \), creating a band between these lines.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is choosing option A, assuming the region is above both lines. This misses the constraint that y must also be less than \(-x + 3\).

Problem 2 (Q2):
Identify the solution set for the system of inequalities \( x + y \leq 4 \) and \( x - y > 1 \).
A) Points right of the line \( y = x - 1 \) but below \( y = -x + 4 \)
B) Points where \( x > y - 1 \) and \( x + y < 4 \)
C) Points left of the line \( y = x + 1 \) and above \( x + y = 4 \)
D) Points above the line \( y = x - 1 \) and below \( y = -x + 4 \)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The inequalities \( x + y \leq 4 \) and \( x - y > 1 \) reveal that the solution is the region that satisfies both conditions. This is translated to points above the line \( y = x - 1 \) and below the line \( y = -x + 4 \).

Explanation:
Graph both inequalities on a coordinate plane. Shade below \( x + y = 4 \) and above \( x - y = 1 \). The overlap is the solution region.

Common mistake note:
A typical error is selecting option A, which misplaces the inequality signs for the position of the line \( x - y = 1 \).

Problem 3 (Q3):
Which point satisfies the system of inequalities \( 2x - y < 4 \) and \( x + 3y \geq 9 \)?
A) (2, 3)
B) (1, 4)
C) (3, 1)
D) (4, 2)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Plug point (1, 4) into both inequalities: \( 2(1) - 4 = -2 < 4 \) and \( 1 + 3(4) = 13 \), which satisfies both conditions.

Explanation:
To find the valid point, each choice must be tested in both inequalities. Only (1, 4) satisfies \( 2x - y < 4 \) and \( x + 3y \geq 9 \) simultaneously.

Common mistake note:
Choosing option A might result from calculating only one inequality, not realizing that it must satisfy both.

Problem 4 (Q4):
Determine the inequality system represented by the shaded region below the line \( y = 4x - 5 \) and above the line \( y = -x + 7 \).
A) \( y \leq 4x - 5 \) and \( y > -x + 7 \)
B) \( y < 4x - 5 \) and \( y \geq -x + 7 \)
C) \( y > 4x - 5 \) and \( y \leq -x + 7 \)
D) \( y \geq 4x - 5 \) and \( y < -x + 7 \)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The region is under the line \( y = 4x - 5 \), thus \( y \leq 4x - 5 \), and above the line \( y = -x + 7 \), thus \( y > -x + 7 \).

Explanation:
Analyze the shading direction relative to each line: below means \( \leq \), and above means \( > \).

Common mistake note:
A potential mistake is flipping inequality symbols by misunderstanding which side forms 'above' and 'below.'

Problem 5 (Q5):
Which set of inequalities is represented by a region to the right of the line \( x = 3 \) and above the line \( y = 2 \)?
A) \( x > 3 \) and \( y < 2 \)
B) \( x \leq 3 \) and \( y \geq 2 \)
C) \( x > 3 \) and \( y > 2 \)
D) \( x \geq 3 \) and \( y \leq 2 \)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The region described includes points with \( x > 3 \) and \( y > 2 \).

Explanation:
To be to the right means \( x > 3 \), and above means \( y > 2 \). The inequalities stem from these directional terms related to the axes.

Common mistake note:
Mistakes arise from reversing the inequality symbols, resulting from misinterpreting 'right' and 'above.'

Problem 6 (Q6):
Find the feasible region for the inequalities \( y \geq 2x - 3 \) and \( y < -3x + 5 \). Which description accurately represents the overlap?
A) Below both lines
B) Above both lines
C) Between the lines
D) None overlap
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Graphically, the solution is the region between \( y \geq 2x - 3 \) and \( y < -3x + 5 \) where both conditions meet.

Explanation:
To determine overlap, graph both lines: one inequality indicates above, the other below, forming a region in between.

Common mistake note:
Choosing D might stem from misunderstanding the sufficient overlap in continuous regions.

Problem 7 (Q7):
A system of inequalities is defined by \( -x + 2y < 8 \) and \( x + y \leq 5 \). Which point is in the solution set?
A) (2, 1)
B) (4, 2)
C) (0, 3)
D) (1, 5)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Test each point: for (0, 3), \(-0 + 2(3) = 6 < 8\) and \(0 + 3 = 3 \leq 5\). Thus (0, 3) satisfies both conditions.

Explanation:
By evaluating each point, we find that only (0, 3) satisfies both inequalities, making it part of the solution set.

Common mistake note:
Beware of switching between strict and non-strict inequality mismatches.
