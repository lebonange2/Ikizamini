Problem 1 (q1):
What is the square root of 49?
A) 5
B) 6
C) 7
D) 8
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The square root of 49 is 7 because 7 * 7 = 49.

Explanation:
Since 7 multiplied by itself equals 49, the square root of 49 is 7.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is choosing 8, thinking that it is closer to 49 when squared. This fails because 8 * 8 equals 64, not 49.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (q2):
What is the cube root of 8?
A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 4
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The cube root of 8 is 2 because 2 * 2 * 2 = 8.

Explanation:
Cube root means finding a number which when cubed equals the given number. Here, 2 cubed is 8.

Common mistake note:
A likely mistake is selecting 4 because 4 seems like a possible option. However, 4 cubed is 64, not 8.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (q3):
Evaluate the fourth root of 16.
A) 2
B) 3
C) 4
D) 5
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The fourth root of 16 is 2 because 2 * 2 * 2 * 2 = 16.

Explanation:
A fourth root means we need a number that, when raised to the power of 4, gives us 16. That number is 2.

Common mistake note:
Some might choose 4, thinking that any root of 16 is 4. But 4 raised to the fourth power is 256.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (q4):
What is the fifth root of 32?
A) 2
B) 4
C) 6
D) 8
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The fifth root of 32 is 2 because 2 * 2 * 2 * 2 * 2 = 32.

Explanation:
To find the fifth root, we need a number that, raised to the power of 5, equals 32. This number is 2.

Common mistake note:
Choosing 4 can be a mistake if not accurately calculating powers, as 4 to the power of 5 is 1024.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (q5):
Calculate the square root of 121.
A) 9
B) 10
C) 11
D) 12
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The square root of 121 is 11 because 11 * 11 = 121.

Explanation:
You find the square root by determining which number squared equals 121. That number is 11.

Common mistake note:
Choosing 10 might be tempting because it is close, but 10 * 10 is 100, not 121.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (q6):
What is the cube root of 27?
A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 9
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The cube root of 27 is 3 because 3 * 3 * 3 = 27.

Explanation:
The cube root of a number is a value that, when cubed, gives the original number. For 27, that value is 3.

Common mistake note:
The mistake may be to consider 9 as it’s a factor of 27, but 9 cubed is 729.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (q7):
Evaluate the fourth root of 81.
A) 2
B) 3
C) 4
D) 9
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The fourth root of 81 is 3 because 3 * 3 * 3 * 3 = 81.

Explanation:
The fourth root requires finding a number that, when raised to the fourth power, equals 81. This number is 3.

Common mistake note:
A likely mistake is choosing 9, as it is the square root of 81, but not the fourth root.
