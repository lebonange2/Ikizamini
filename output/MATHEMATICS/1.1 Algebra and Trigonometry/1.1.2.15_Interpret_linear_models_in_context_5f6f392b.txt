Problem 1 (Q1):
A company analyzes its profit based on the equation \( P = 5x - 100 \), where \( P \) is the profit and \( x \) is the number of units sold. What does the number -100 represent in this context?
A) The fixed costs incurred by the company.
B) The profit made by selling 1 unit.
C) The revenue from each unit sold.
D) The profit made from selling 20 units.
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
In the equation \( P = 5x - 100 \), the term -100 is the constant term. This represents the fixed costs, as it is subtracted from the total revenue to determine profit.

Explanation:
-100 is the constant added to the function, indicating fixed costs regardless of units sold.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to interpret -100 as the revenue of selling units, confusing it with variable costs.

Problem 2 (Q2):
The equation \( y = 3x + 7 \) models the temperature \( y \) in a room based on the number of minutes \( x \) since a heater was turned on. What does the number 3 indicate?
A) The initial temperature of the room.
B) The rate of temperature increase per minute.
C) The temperature after 7 minutes.
D) The maximum temperature.
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
In the equation \( y = 3x + 7 \), 3 is the coefficient of \( x \). It represents the rate at which the temperature increases with each minute that passes.

Explanation:
The coefficient of \( x \) in a linear model represents the rate of change.

Common mistake note:
Students may mistake 3 for the initial temperature, confusing initial value with rate of change.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Q3):
For the linear model \( C = 2.5n + 50 \), where \( C \) is the total cost and \( n \) is the number of items produced, what does the number 2.5 represent?
A) The total cost for 10 items.
B) The cost per item.
C) The total fixed cost.
D) The profit margin per item.
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
In \( C = 2.5n + 50 \), 2.5 is the coefficient of \( n \), meaning it represents the variable cost or cost per item.

Explanation:
The coefficient of the variable represents a per-unit rate in a linear equation.

Common mistake note:
A mistake would be interpreting 2.5 as fixed costs, misplacing role of coefficients vs. constants.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Q4):
The equation \( D = 40 - 0.5t \) describes the distance \( D \) in meters from a starting point after \( t \) seconds. What does the number -0.5 indicate?
A) The initial distance from the starting point.
B) The distance after 1 second.
C) The rate of moving towards the start.
D) The total time taken to reach the start.
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
In \( D = 40 - 0.5t \), the term -0.5 is the coefficient of \( t \) and represents the rate at which the person is moving towards the start point, 0.5 meters per second.

Explanation:
The negative coefficient indicates a decrease in distance, showing movement towards the origin.

Common mistake note:
Students may confuse -0.5 with initial or maximum distance due to misunderstanding signs.

Problem 5 (Q5):
A scientist models plant growth with \( G = 0.8d + 2 \), where \( G \) is growth in cm and \( d \) is days. What does the number 2 mean in this context?
A) Growth per day.
B) Initial growth at day zero.
C) Total growth after 1 day.
D) Maximum growth achieved.
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
In \( G = 0.8d + 2 \), 2 is the constant term, representing the initial growth height at the start (day zero) before any days have passed.

Explanation:
The constant term in a linear equation often denotes the initial value at zero.

Common mistake note:
Misinterpreting 2 as growth per day, confusing constant values with rates.

Problem 6 (Q6):
A rental company uses \( R = 20d + 10 \) to determine cost \( R \) in dollars based on \( d \) days rented. What does the number 10 denote?
A) Daily rental rate.
B) Total cost for 10 days.
C) Fixed initial fee.
D) Discount for early returns.
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
In the equation \( R = 20d + 10 \), 10 is the constant, a fixed initial fee that applies regardless of the number of days rented.

Explanation:
Constants in linear models typically represent fixed amounts or initial values.

Common mistake note:
Assuming it is a daily rate or total cost, misleading the role of constants vs. coefficients.

Problem 7 (Q7):
The linear relationship between distance \( d \) and time \( t \) is given by \( d = 60t \). What concept does this equation illustrate?
A) Distance increases by 60 meters every second.
B) The initial distance is 60 meters.
C) Travel speed is 60 meters per hour.
D) Total distance traveled over 60 seconds.
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
In \( d = 60t \), the coefficient 60 indicates that distance increases by 60 meters for each second that passes.

Explanation:
In a linear model, the coefficient of the variable signifies the rate of change—in this case, speed.

Common mistake note:
Interpreting 60 as an initial distance rather than a rate, misunderstanding the structure of linear equations.
