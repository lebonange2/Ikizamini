Problem 1 (:1):
Sketch the graph of the polar equation: r = 2sin(θ). Which of the following best describes the graph?
A) A straight line passing through the origin.
B) A circle centered at the origin with radius 2.
C) A cardioid with a loop touching the pole.
D) A circle centered at (1,0) with radius 2.
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The graph of r = 2sin(θ) is a circle centered at the origin with radius 2. This occurs because as θ varies from 0 to 2π, sin(θ) takes values between -1 and 1, and multiplying by 2 gives a range of r values from -2 to 2, but since r cannot be negative for a distance, it is only positive, resulting in a circle.

Explanation:
The correct answer is B because the equation represents a circle with radius 2 centered at the origin. The other options are incorrect because: A represents a straight line which is not formed by this polar equation; C represents a cardioid shape, not a circle; and D describes a circle centered at (1,0) which is not correct for the given equation.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to confuse the polar equation with a rectangular one. Remember that r in polar coordinates represents the distance from the origin, while x and y represent horizontal and vertical distances respectively.

Problem 2 (:2):
Sketch the graph of the polar equation: r = 4 - 3cos(θ). Which of the following best describes the graph?
A) A cardioid with a loop touching the pole.
B) An ellipse centered at the origin.
C) A circle centered at (3,0) with radius 1.
D) A straight line passing through the pole.
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The graph of r = 4 - 3cos(θ) is an ellipse centered at the origin. As θ varies from 0 to 2π, cos(θ) ranges from -1 to 1, so (4 - 3cos(θ)) can be between 1 and 7. Since r cannot be negative, the graph forms an ellipse with its major axis along the polar radius.

Explanation:
The correct answer is B because the equation represents an ellipse centered at the origin. The other options are incorrect because: A represents a cardioid shape which is not formed by this polar equation; C describes a circle centered at (3,0) which is not correct for the given equation; and D represents a straight line which does not form an ellipse.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to think that because cos(θ) appears in the equation, it must be related to a cardioid or a straight line. However, the subtraction of 3cos(θ) from 4 creates an offset, resulting in an ellipse.

Problem 3 (:3):
Sketch the graph of the polar equation: r = 5sin(2θ). Which of the following best describes the graph?
A) A circle centered at (5,0) with radius 1.
B) A rose curve with 4 petals.
C) A cardioid with a loop touching the pole.
D) An Archimedes' spiral starting from the origin.
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The graph of r = 5sin(2θ) is a rose curve with 4 petals. The factor of 2 in front of θ causes the curve to repeat itself twice as θ goes from 0 to 2π, resulting in 4 petals.

Explanation:
The correct answer is B because the equation represents a rose curve with 4 petals. The other options are incorrect because: A describes a circle centered at (5,0) which is not correct for the given equation; C represents a cardioid shape, not a rose curve; and D represents an Archimedes' spiral, which is not formed by this polar equation.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to overlook the effect of multiplying θ by 2, which changes the number of petals in the rose curve. Remember that the number of petals is determined by the coefficient of θ.

Problem 4 (:4):
Sketch the graph of the polar equation: r = 3e^(θ/2). Which of the following best describes the graph?
A) An Archimedes' spiral starting from the origin.
B) A cardioid with a loop touching the pole.
C) A circle centered at (3,0) with radius 1.
D) A straight line passing through the origin.
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The graph of r = 3e^(θ/2) is an Archimedes' spiral starting from the origin. The exponential function e^(θ/2) causes the curve to continuously increase in radius as θ increases, forming a spiral.

Explanation:
The correct answer is A because the equation represents an Archimedes' spiral starting at the origin. The other options are incorrect because: B represents a cardioid shape which is not formed by this polar equation; C describes a circle centered at (3,0) which is not correct for the given equation; and D represents a straight line which does not form a spiral.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to confuse the exponential function with a simple linear or trigonometric expression. The exponential growth in this case creates a spiral rather than a closed curve.
