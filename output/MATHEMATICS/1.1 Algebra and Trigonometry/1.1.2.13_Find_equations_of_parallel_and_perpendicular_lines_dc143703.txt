Problem 1 (Q1):
What is the equation of a line parallel to the line y = 3x + 2 and passing through the point (1, 4)?
A) y = 3x + 1
B) y = 3x + 4
C) y = 3x - 1
D) y = 3x + 5
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Parallel lines have the same slope. The given line has a slope of 3. Using the point (1, 4), apply the point-slope form: y - 4 = 3(x - 1). Simplifying, this becomes y = 3x + 1.

Explanation:
Parallel lines share the same slope. Keeping the slope consistent and solving with the point-slope formula gives the correct equation.

Common mistake note:
Mistaking parallel lines as having different slopes (choosing B, C, or D) ignores the defining property of parallelism, which is equal slopes.

Problem 2 (Q2):
Find the equation of the line perpendicular to y = -1/2x + 3 and passing through (4, -2).
A) y = 2x - 10
B) y = -2x + 6
C) y = 2x - 4
D) y = -1/2x - 10
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Perpendicular lines have slopes that are negative reciprocals of each other. The slope of the given line is -1/2, so the perpendicular slope is 2. Use point-slope form: y + 2 = 2(x - 4), which simplifies to y = 2x - 10.

Explanation:
To find a perpendicular line's slope, calculate the negative reciprocal of the given line's slope.

Common mistake note:
Misidentifying the slope as the same (choice D) shows confusion over perpendicularity, which requires slopes to multiply to -1.

Problem 3 (Q3):
A line passes through (3, 5) and is perpendicular to another line with equation 4x + y = 7. What is the equation of the line?
A) y = -1/4x + 5.75
B) y = 4x - 7
C) y = -4x + 17
D) y = 1/4x + 4.25
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Rewriting 4x + y = 7 gives y = -4x + 7, hence the slope is -4. The perpendicular slope is 1/4. Using point-slope form: y - 5 = 1/4(x - 3) simplifies to y = 1/4x + 4.25.

Explanation:
Convert to slope-intercept form to find the slope and use the negative reciprocal for perpendicularity.

Common mistake note:
Failing to convert correctly or calculate the reciprocal (picking A or B) leads to slope errors.

Problem 4 (Q4):
Determine the equation of a line parallel to y = 6x - 3 and passing through (0, -5).
A) y = 6x - 5
B) y = 6x + 10
C) y = -6x - 5
D) y = -6x + 3
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Parallel lines have the same slope, so the slope is 6. Use the point (0, -5) in y = mx + c: -5 = 6(0) + c, so c = -5, giving y = 6x - 5.

Explanation:
For parallelism, maintain the slope. Solve using y-intercept derived from given point.

Common mistake note:
Errors come from misunderstanding parallel slope (like picking C or D, which incorrectly change slope).

Problem 5 (Q5):
What is the equation of a line perpendicular to x = 3?
A) y = 3
B) x = -3
C) y = 0
D) y = 5
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
x = 3 represents a vertical line. A line perpendicular to it is horizontal (y = constant). The choice y = 5 shows such a line.

Explanation:
Vertical lines have undefined slopes, so perpendicular lines are horizontal with y-intercept as the only variable.

Common mistake note:
Choosing non-horizontal lines (choices B, C) shows confusion of line orientation.

Problem 6 (Q6):
Find the equation of a line parallel to 2y - 4x = 8 through point (-2, 1).
A) y = 2x + 5
B) y = 2x - 3
C) y = x + 3
D) y = 2x + 1
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Convert 2y - 4x = 8 to y = 2x + 4, identifying slope as 2. A parallel line maintains this slope. Using point-slope form with (-2, 1): y - 1 = 2(x + 2), simplifies to y = 2x + 1.

Explanation:
Keep the slope constant for parallel lines and solve using point-slope form with the given point.

Common mistake note:
Misapplying the point into slope formula leads to choices like A or C, showing a mix-up in method.

Problem 7 (Q7):
A line is perpendicular to y = x - 1 and passes through (2, 2). What is its equation?
A) y = x - 2
B) y = -x + 4
C) y = x + 2
D) y = -x + 5
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The slope for y = x - 1 is 1, so the perpendicular slope is -1. Using point-slope form with (2, 2): y - 2 = -1(x - 2). Simplifying, y = -x + 4.

Explanation:
Finding perpendicular slope by negative reciprocal and using point-slope form.

Common mistake note:
Choosing A or C indicates misunderstanding perpendicular slopes as similar or equal to original.
