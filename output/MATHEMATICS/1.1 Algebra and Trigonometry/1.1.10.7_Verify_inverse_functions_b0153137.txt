Problem 1 (Q1):
If \( f(x) = 3x + 2 \), which of the following functions is the inverse of \( f(x) \)?
A) \( f^{-1}(x) = \frac{x - 2}{3} \)
B) \( f^{-1}(x) = 3x - 2 \)
C) \( f^{-1}(x) = \frac{x + 2}{3} \)
D) \( f^{-1}(x) = \frac{3x-2}{x} \)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To find the inverse, swap \( x \) and \( y \) in \( y = 3x + 2 \) to get \( x = 3y + 2 \). Solving for \( y \), we obtain \( y = \frac{x - 2}{3} \).

Explanation:
The inverse of a function \( f \) must satisfy \( f(f^{-1}(x)) = x \) and \( f^{-1}(f(x)) = x \). \( f^{-1}(x) = \frac{x - 2}{3} \) meets this condition.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is choosing option B, confusing function reversal with operations inversion.

Problem 2 (Q2):
Given \( f(x) = \frac{x - 1}{x + 1} \), which function is verified as the inverse of \( f(x) \)?
A) \( f^{-1}(x) = \frac{x + 1}{x - 1} \)
B) \( f^{-1}(x) = \frac{1 - x}{1 + x} \)
C) \( f^{-1}(x) = \frac{1 + x}{1 - x} \)
D) \( f^{-1}(x) = \frac{x - 1}{x + 1} \)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Swapping \( x \) and \( y \) in \( y = \frac{x - 1}{x + 1} \), solve \( x = \frac{y - 1}{y + 1} \). Cross-multiply to get \( x(y + 1) = y - 1 \), which simplifies to \( y = \frac{1 + x}{1 - x} \).

Explanation:
To verify, calculate \( f(f^{-1}(x)) = x \) and \( f^{-1}(f(x)) = x \). \( f^{-1}(x) = \frac{1 + x}{1 - x} \) satisfies both.

Common mistake note:
A mistake may arise from choosing option A due to incorrect variable treatment during inversion.

Problem 3 (Q3):
For \( f(x) = x^2 \) restricted to \( x \geq 0 \), which of the following is its inverse?
A) \( f^{-1}(x) = x^2 \)
B) \( f^{-1}(x) = \sqrt{x} \)
C) \( f^{-1}(x) = -\sqrt{x} \)
D) \( f^{-1}(x) = x^{\frac{1}{2}} \)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The function is restricted to non-negative \( x \). So the inverse is \( f^{-1}(x) = \sqrt{x} \), ensuring \( f(f^{-1}(x)) = x \) and \( f^{-1}(f(x)) = x \).

Explanation:
The restriction to \( x \geq 0 \) ensures that the inverse is the positive square root.

Common mistake note:
Option D may mislead if students forget that square roots imply non-negative values only.

Problem 4 (Q4):
If \( g(x) = 2x^3 + 1 \), verify the inverse function among the following options.
A) \( g^{-1}(x) = \sqrt[3]{x - 1} \)
B) \( g^{-1}(x) = \sqrt[3]{\frac{x-1}{2}} \)
C) \( g^{-1}(x) = \sqrt[3]{\frac{x+1}{2}} \)
D) \( g^{-1}(x) = \left(\frac{x-1}{2}\right)^{3} \)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Set \( y = 2x^3 + 1 \). Swap \( x \) and \( y \) to solve \( x = 2y^3 + 1 \). Solving for \( y \), we get \( y = \sqrt[3]{\frac{x-1}{2}} \).

Explanation:
After swapping and solving, the function \( g^{-1}(x) = \sqrt[3]{\frac{x-1}{2}} \) correctly undoes \( g(x) \).

Common mistake note:
The mistake of choosing option A is common by overlooking the division by 2.

Problem 5 (Q5):
Consider \( h(x) = \log_3(x) \). What is the inverse of \( h(x) \)?
A) \( h^{-1}(x) = 10^x \)
B) \( h^{-1}(x) = e^x \)
C) \( h^{-1}(x) = x \ln 3 \)
D) \( h^{-1}(x) = 3^x \)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The inverse of \( h(x) = \log_3(x) \) is \( h^{-1}(x) = 3^x \) because logarithms and exponentials with the same base cancel each other out.

Explanation:
The operations of logarithm base 3 and exponentiation with base 3 are inverses.

Common mistake note:
Choosing A or B could arise from incorrect assumptions about the base of the exponential or logarithm.

Problem 6 (Q6):
Find the inverse of \( k(x) = \frac{1}{x} \).
A) \( k^{-1}(x) = x \)
B) \( k^{-1}(x) = -x \)
C) \( k^{-1}(x) = \frac{1}{x} \)
D) \( k^{-1}(x) = -\frac{1}{x} \)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
For \( k(x) = \frac{1}{x} \), swapping \( x \) and \( y \) gives \( x = \frac{1}{y} \), leading to \( y = \frac{1}{x} \). Thus, the inverse is \( k^{-1}(x) = \frac{1}{x} \).

Explanation:
The reciprocal function is self-inverse, meaning \( k(k^{-1}(x)) = x \).

Common mistake note:
Confusing reciprocal with its negative (option D) is a typical error.

Problem 7 (Q7):
If \( m(x) = 5 - x^4 \) for \( 0 \leq x \leq \sqrt[4]{5} \), what is the inverse of \( m(x) \)?
A) \( m^{-1}(x) = (5-x)^{\frac{1}{4}} \)
B) \( m^{-1}(x) = \sqrt[4]{5-x} \)
C) None: further restrictions needed
D) None
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
By setting \( y = 5 - x^4 \), solve for \( x \) to find \( x = \sqrt[4]{5-y} \). This solution is valid within the given range.

Explanation:
The function is decreasing, and the range is correctly determined as \( m^{-1}(x) = \sqrt[4]{5-x} \), accommodating the domain \( 0 \leq x \leq \sqrt[4]{5} \).

Common mistake note:
Mistakes may involve ignoring the domain restrictions leading to wrong choice C or D.
