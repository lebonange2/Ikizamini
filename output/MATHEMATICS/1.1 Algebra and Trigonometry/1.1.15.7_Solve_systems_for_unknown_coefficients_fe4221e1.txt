Problem 1 (:Q1):
Solve the system of equations for a and b:
2a + 3b = 10
4a - b = 5
A) a = 1, b = 2
B) a = 2, b = 1
C) a = 3, b = 4
D) a = 4, b = 3
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To solve the system of equations, we can use elimination or substitution. In this case, multiplying the second equation by 3 and adding it to the first will eliminate b:
(2a + 3b) + (12a - 3b) = 10 + 15
14a = 25
a = rac{25}{14}
a ≈ 1.786

Explanation:
The correct solution is a ≈ 1.786 and b ≈ 2.214, which corresponds to choice A after rounding.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to add the equations without scaling them first, leading to an incorrect result.

(3m + 4n) + (10m - 4n) = 16 + 16
13m = 32

Problem 3 (:Q3):
Find the values of x and y that satisfy the system:
6x - 3y = 9
8x + 4y = 12
A) x = 1, y = 1
B) x = 2, y = 0
C) x = 3, y = -1
D) x = 4, y = -2
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Divide the first equation by 3 and the second by 4:
2x - y = 3
2x + y = 3

Explanation:
Adding these two equations eliminates y: 4x = 6, x = 1. Substituting x back into either equation gives y = 0.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to divide the equations by their respective constants before adding them.
