Problem 1 (InvestmentReturnEasy):
An investor invested $500 in a stock and after one year, the value of the investment increased by 20%. What is the new value of the investment?
A) $400
B) $500
C) $600
D) $700
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The increase in value is calculated as 20% of $500, which is $100. Adding this to the initial investment of $500 gives a new value of $600.

Explanation:
This question assesses interpretation of a simple percentage increase within the context of an investment return.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or B, which represent the original amount before and after no change. The correct answer is C because it takes into account the 20% increase.

Problem 2 (DistanceTravelledMedium):
A car travels at a constant speed of 60 miles per hour for 3 hours. How far does the car travel?
A) 90 miles
B) 120 miles
C) 180 miles
D) 240 miles
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To find the distance traveled, multiply the speed by the time: 60 mph * 3 hours = 180 miles.

Explanation:
This question evaluates understanding of distance calculation using speed and time in a real-world context.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Dividing instead of multiplying. The correct answer is C because it represents the total distance traveled, not the rate at which the distance was covered.

Problem 3 (TemperatureConversionEasy):
If the temperature is 32 degrees Fahrenheit, what is it in Celsius?
A) -0°C
B) 0°C
C) 32°C
D) 100°C
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The formula to convert from Fahrenheit to Celsius is (°F - 32) * 5/9. Applying this, we get (32°F - 32) * 5/9 = 0°C.

Explanation:
This question tests the interpretation of temperature conversion in a real-world context.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Not subtracting 32 before multiplying. The correct answer is A because it correctly converts 32°F to Celsius.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (PopulationGrowthMedium):
A city's population was 100,000 in 2010 and grew by an average of 2% per year until 2020. What is the approximate population in 2020?
A) 104,000
B) 120,000
C) 121,000
D) 144,000
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The population after 10 years with a 2% annual growth rate can be calculated using the formula: P = P0 * (1 + r)^t. With P0=100,000, r=0.02, and t=10, we get approximately 121,000.

Explanation:
This question assesses understanding of population growth in a real-world context.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Not accounting for the compounding effect of annual growth. The correct answer is C because it takes into account the exponential growth.

Problem 5 (FuelEfficiencyEasy):
A car has a fuel efficiency of 30 miles per gallon (mpg). If the car travels 180 miles, how many gallons of gas does it consume?
A) 4 gallons
B) 6 gallons
C) 9 gallons
D) 12 gallons
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To find the number of gallons used, divide the total distance by the fuel efficiency: 180 miles / 30 mpg = 6 gallons. Since we're looking for how much gas was consumed, we use the entire 6 gallons, not just 4.

Explanation:
This question evaluates understanding of fuel consumption in a real-world context.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Dividing by 180 instead of multiplying. The correct answer is C because it represents the actual amount of gas used.

Problem 6 (PercentageDiscountMedium):
A shirt originally priced at $50 has a 25% discount. What is the sale price of the shirt?
A) $37.50
B) $45.00
C) $47.50
D) $52.50
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To find the sale price, multiply the original price by the discount percentage: $50 * 0.25 = $12.50. Then subtract the discount from the original price: $50 - $12.50 = $37.50.

Explanation:
This question tests understanding of discounts in a real-world context.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Subtracting 25% directly from $50. The correct answer is A because it correctly calculates the discount and subtracts it from the original price.

Problem 7 (WeightConversionEasy):
A person weighs 70 kilograms. How much do they weigh in pounds?
A) 154 lbs
B) 160 lbs
C) 176 lbs
D) 220 lbs
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The formula to convert from kilograms to pounds is weight (kg) * 2.20462. Applying this, we get 70 kg * 2.20462 ≈ 154.32 lbs. Rounding to the nearest whole pound gives 154 lbs.

Explanation:
This question assesses understanding of weight conversion in a real-world context.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Not multiplying by the correct conversion factor. The correct answer is A because it correctly converts 70 kg to pounds.
