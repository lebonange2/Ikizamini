Problem 1 (Q1):
What is \( \sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{8} \)?
A) 2\sqrt{4}
B) 4
C) 2\sqrt{2}
D) 4\sqrt{2}
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
\( \sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{8} = \sqrt{16} = 4\sqrt{2} \).

Explanation:
Multiply inside the radicals: \(2 \times 8 = 16\), leading to \(\sqrt{16} = 4\), but correctly simplifying as \(4\sqrt{2}\).

Common mistake note:
The confusion is between simplifying \(16\) directly and realizing \(4\sqrt{2}\) as part-product.

Problem 2 (Q2):
Calculate \( \frac{\sqrt{50}}{\sqrt{2}} \).
A) 5
B) 5\sqrt{2}
C) \sqrt{25}
D) \sqrt{25} \sqrt{2}
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
\( \frac{\sqrt{50}}{\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{\frac{50}{2}} = \sqrt{25} = 5 \).

Explanation:
Simplify within the radical: \(50 \div 2 = 25\), thus \(\sqrt{25} = 5\).

Common mistake note:
Not simplifying inside the radical can lead to a complication or wrong answers.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Q3):
Simplify \( (\sqrt{3})^2 \times \sqrt{12} \).
A) 18
B) 6\sqrt{3}
C) 12\sqrt{3}
D) 6
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
\( (\sqrt{3})^2 = 3 \), so \( 3 \times \sqrt{12} = 3 \times 2\sqrt{3} = 6\sqrt{3} \).

Explanation:
Square \(\sqrt{3}\) gives 3. Simplify \(\sqrt{12}\) to \(2\sqrt{3}\) and multiply.

Common mistake note:
Misinterpreting the simplification or squaring process results in errors.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Q4):
What is \( \frac{6\sqrt{18}}{\sqrt{2}} \)?
A) 6\sqrt{9}
B) 6\sqrt{3}
C) 18\sqrt{2}
D) 18
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
\( \frac{6\sqrt{18}}{\sqrt{2}} = 6\sqrt{\frac{18}{2}} = 6\sqrt{9} = 6 \times 3 = 18 \).

Explanation:
Divide inside the radical \(18 \div 2 = 9\), then \(\sqrt{9} = 3\). Multiply to get \(6 \times 3 = 18\).

Common mistake note:
Ignoring simplification of radicals before multiplication can lead to errors.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (Q5):
Evaluate \( \frac{\sqrt{72}}{\sqrt{8}} \).
A) \sqrt{9}
B) 3\sqrt{2}
C) 9
D) 3
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
\( \frac{\sqrt{72}}{\sqrt{8}} = \sqrt{\frac{72}{8}} = \sqrt{9} = 3 \).

Explanation:
Simplify inside the radical: \(72 \div 8 = 9\), so \(\sqrt{9} = 3\).

Common mistake note:
Missing simplification steps within radicals can result in miscalculation.

Problem 6 (Q6):
Find the product of \( \sqrt{7} \times \sqrt{14} \).
A) 7\sqrt{2}
B) 14\sqrt{2}
C) 14
D) 7
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
\( \sqrt{7} \times \sqrt{14} = \sqrt{98} = \sqrt{49 \times 2} = 7\sqrt{2} \).

Explanation:
Combine under a single radical \(7 \times 14 = 98\), recognizing \(\sqrt{49} = 7\).

Common mistake note:
Overlooking perfect squares within the radicand leads to incorrect simplification.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Q7):
Simplify \( \sqrt{48} \div \sqrt{3} \).
A) 4\sqrt{2}
B) 8
C) 4
D) 2\sqrt{3}
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
\( \sqrt{48} \div \sqrt{3} = \sqrt{\frac{48}{3}} = \sqrt{16} = 4 \).

Explanation:
Dividing within the radical \(48 \div 3 = 16\), gives \(\sqrt{16} = 4\).

Common mistake note:
Commonly, incorrect division under a radical might alter the result.
