Problem 1 (Q1):
Sarah has 3 times the number of apples as Tom. If together they have 48 apples, how many apples does Tom have?
A) 12
B) 16
C) 18
D) 24
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Let Tom have x apples. Sarah has 3x apples. The equation is x + 3x = 48. Solving, 4x = 48, so x = 12. Tom has 12 apples.

Explanation:
Create the equation x + 3x = 48 to find x, Tom's apples.

Common mistake note:
Misunderstanding '3 times' as an additional 3 might lead to errors.

Problem 2 (Q2):
A car rental company charges a flat fee of $50 plus $0.15 per mile driven. If the total cost billed was $77.25, how many miles were driven?
A) 168.33
B) 182.5
C) 175
D) 185
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Let x be the number of miles. The total cost equation is 50 + 0.15x = 77.25. Solving, 0.15x = 27.25, hence x = 182.5 miles.

Explanation:
Set up the equation based on cost components and solve for x, the mileage.

Common mistake note:
Arithmetic errors may occur when isolating x.

Problem 3 (Q3):
The perimeter of a rectangle is 64 meters. If the length is twice the width, what is the width of the rectangle?
A) 8
B) 10
C) 12
D) 16
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Let the width be x meters. The length is 2x meters. The perimeter equation is 2x + 2(2x) = 64. Solving, 6x = 64, so x = 10 meters.

Explanation:
Express perimeter as 2x + 2(2x) = 64 and solve for x, the width.

Common mistake note:
Mistaking 'twice' can lead to incorrect setup.

Problem 4 (Q4):
A cellphone company offers a monthly plan of $30 plus $0.10 per text message. If a user pays $48 in one month, how many text messages were sent?
A) 180
B) 77
C) 160
D) 140
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Let x be the number of text messages. Cost equation: 30 + 0.10x = 48. Solving gives 0.10x = 18, so x = 180 messages.

Explanation:
Formulate and solve the cost equation to find x, the number of messages.

Common mistake note:
Rounding errors in arithmetic can cause mistakes.

Problem 5 (Q5):
A farmer sells eggs at $2.50 per dozen and sold a total of $200 worth. How many dozens were sold?
A) 80
B) 78
C) 82
D) 81
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Let x be the dozens sold. Revenue equation: 2.5x = 200. Solving gives x = 80 dozens.

Explanation:
Divide total revenue by the price per dozen to find dozens sold.

Common mistake note:
Confusing units of dozens with individual eggs might lead to miscalculations.

Problem 6 (Q6):
Emma has twice as many dimes as nickels. If the total value of her coins is $3.00, how many nickels does she have?
A) 12
B) 20
C) 10
D) 15
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Let x be the number of nickels. Emma has 2x dimes. Value equation: 0.05x + 0.10(2x) = 3.00. Solving, 0.25x = 3.00, so x = 12 nickels.

Explanation:
Calculate by converting coin counts to value and solve for x, nickels.

Common mistake note:
Errors in translating to monetary value may occur if not careful.

Problem 7 (Q7):
A cyclist travels 100 miles, with a portion riding at 12 mph and the rest at 10 mph. If the entire trip took 9 hours, how many miles were covered at 12 mph?
A) 40
B) 60
C) 80
D) 50
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Let x be the miles at 12 mph. The remaining miles (100-x) are at 10 mph. Time equation: x/12 + (100-x)/10 = 9. Solving, x = 60 miles.

Explanation:
Formulate a time equation linking distance and speed to solve for x, the 12 mph distance.

Common mistake note:
Errors occur if time-distance relationships are misunderstood.
