Problem 1 (Asdf1):
Which equation represents a rose curve with 6 petals?
A) (x^2 + y^2)^3 - (x^2 - y^2)^3 = 0
B) x^4 - y^4 = 1
C) x^2 - xy + y^2 = 1
D) (x^2 + y^2)^2 = x^2 - y^2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation for a rose curve with an even number of petals is given by (x^2 + y^2)^n ± (x^2 - y^2)^n = 0, where n is the number of petals. For 6 petals, n = 3.

Explanation:
A rose curve has an even number of petals determined by the exponent n in the equation. The given choices represent different types of curves: A is a rose with 6 petals, B is a lemniscate, C is a limaçon, and D is a cardioid.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake is selecting choice C, which represents a limaçon rather than a rose curve.

Problem 2 (Asdf2):
What is the equation of a cardioid with its cusp at the origin?
A) (x^2 + y^2 - 1)^2 = x^2 - y^2
B) x^2 + y^2 = (x - 1)^2 + (y - 1)^2
C) x^2 - xy + y^2 = 1
D) (x^2 + y^2)^3 = x^2 + y^2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation of a cardioid with its cusp at the origin is given by (x^2 + y^2 - r^2)^2 = 4r^2(x^2 - y^2), where r is the distance from the cusp to the origin. For a cusp at the origin, r = 1.

Explanation:
A cardioid has its cusp at the origin and can be represented by the given equation with r = 1. Choice A satisfies this condition. Choices B and C represent different curves (a circle and a limaçon respectively), while choice D represents a rose curve.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake is selecting choice C, which represents a limaçon rather than a cardioid.

Problem 3 (Asdf3):
Which equation represents a limaçon with an inner loop?
A) (x^2 + y^2 - 1)^2 = x^2 + y^2
B) x^4 - y^4 = 1
C) x^2 - xy + y^2 = 1
D) (x^2 + y^2)^3 = x^2 + y^2
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
A limaçon with an inner loop is represented by the polar equation r = a + b cos(θ) or r = a + b sin(θ), where b ≠ 0. The rectangular form of this curve can be transformed from the polar form, and choice C represents such a curve.

Explanation:
Choice C represents a limaçon with an inner loop when transformed to polar coordinates. Choices A and D represent cardioids and rose curves respectively, while choice B is a lemniscate without an inner loop.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake is selecting choice A, which represents a cardioid rather than a limaçon.

Problem 4 (Asdf4):
What is the number of petals for the rose curve defined by the equation (x^2 + y^2)^3 - 8(x^2 - y^2)^3 = 0?
A) 4
B) 6
C) 12
D) 8
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The equation for a rose curve with an even number of petals is given by (x^2 + y^2)^n ± (x^2 - y^2)^n = 0, where n is the number of petals. For the given equation, n = 3 * 2 = 6.

Explanation:
The number of petals for a rose curve can be determined by the exponent n in the equation. In this case, n = 3 * 2 because the exponents are multiplied by 3 and -8. Thus, there are 12 petals.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake is dividing the exponents (3 and -8) instead of multiplying them.

Problem 6 (Asdf6):
What is the equation of a rose curve with 8 petals and its center at (1, 1)?
A) (x^2 + y^2 - 1)^3 = x^2 - y^2
B) x^4 - y^4 = (x - 1)^2 + (y - 1)^2
C) (x - 1)^2 + (y - 1)^2 = (x^2 + y^2)^2
D) (x^2 + y^2 - 1)^3 = x^2 + y^2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation for a rose curve with an even number of petals centered at (h, k) is given by [(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2]^n ± [(x - h)^2 - (y - k)^2]^n = 0. For 8 petals and center at (1, 1), n = 4.

Explanation:
The equation for a centered rose curve can be derived by adding the square of the distance from the origin to the point (x, y) and subtracting it. Choice A satisfies these conditions with n = 4. Choices B and C represent different curves, while choice D represents a cardioid.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake is selecting choice B, which represents a lemniscate rather than a rose curve.

Problem 7 (Asdf7):
Which equation represents a cardioid with its cusp at the point (1, 0)?
A) (x^2 + y^2 - 1)^2 = x^2 + y^2
B) x^4 - y^4 = (x - 1)^2 + (y - 1)^2
C) (x - 1)^2 + y^2 = (x^2 + y^2)^2
D) (x^2 + y^2 - 1)^3 = x^2 + y^2
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The equation of a cardioid with its cusp at the point (h, k) is given by [(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2]^n ± [(x - h)^2 - (y - k)^2]^n = 0, where n = 1. For a cusp at (1, 0), choice C satisfies this condition.

Explanation:
Choice C represents a cardioid with its cusp at the point (1, 0) when transformed to polar coordinates. Choices A and D represent centered rose curves, while choice B is a lemniscate without an inner loop but not a cardioid.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake is selecting choice A, which represents a centered rose curve rather than a cardioid.
