Problem 1 (:1):
Divide the polynomial 6x^3 - 9x^2 + 3x by x - 1 and write the result in descending order.
A) 6x^2 + 3x + 6
B) 6x^2 + 3x + 3
C) 6x^2 - 3x + 3
D) 6x^2 - 3x + 6
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Using long division, we find that the quotient is 6x^2 - 3x + 3 and there is no remainder. Therefore, the correct answer is C.

Explanation:
Long division of polynomials involves dividing one polynomial by another to obtain a quotient and possibly a remainder. In this case, x divides into 6x^3 six times (6x^2), then -9x^2 becomes -54x^2 which divided by x is -54x, and so on.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to subtract the correct multiple of the divisor from the dividend in each step, leading to an incorrect quotient.

Problem 2 (2):
Divide the polynomial 4x^3 + 8x^2 - 12x by 2x and write the result in standard form.
A) 2x^2 + 4x - 6
B) 2x^2 + 4x - 12
C) x^2 + 2x - 3
D) x^2 + 2x - 6
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Dividing 4x^3 by 2x gives 2x^2, dividing 8x^2 by 2x gives 4x, and dividing -12x by 2x gives -6. The result is 2x^2 + 4x - 6. So the correct answer is A.

Explanation:
When dividing polynomials by a monomial, each term of the polynomial is divided by the monomial. In this case, every term in the dividend is divided by 2x.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to divide all terms in the dividend by the divisor, resulting in an incorrect quotient.

Problem 3 (3):
Divide the polynomial x^4 - 5x^3 + 7x^2 - 5x + 1 by x - 2 and write the result as a sum of a polynomial and a fraction.
A) (x^3 - 3x^2 + 4x - 2) + (1/2)
B) (x^3 - 3x^2 + 4x - 1) + (1/2)
C) (x^3 - 3x^2 + 5x - 2) + (1/2)
D) (x^3 - 3x^2 + 5x - 1) + (1/2)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The quotient is x^3 - 3x^2 + 4x - 2 and the remainder is 1, which can be written as 1/2 when divided by the divisor x - 2. So the correct answer is A.

Explanation:
Long division of polynomials may result in a quotient and a remainder that needs to be expressed as a fraction with the divisor in the denominator. In this case, we have a polynomial quotient and a constant remainder.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to write the remainder as a fraction when there is one, leading to an incomplete or incorrect representation of the division result.

Problem 4 (4):
Divide the polynomial 3x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x by x + 1 and express the quotient in factored form.
A) 3(x - 1)^2
B) 3(x + 1)^2
C) 3(x - 1)(x + 1)
D) 3(x - 1)(x^2 + x + 1)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The quotient is 3(x - 1)^2, as the polynomial can be factored to (x - 1) multiplied by itself. So the correct answer is A.

Explanation:
When dividing polynomials, it's important to recognize common factors that can be factored out of both the dividend and divisor. In this case, we have a perfect square trinomial in the quotient.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to overlook the possibility of factoring the quotient into a perfect square, resulting in an incorrect factorization.

Problem 5 (5):
Divide the polynomial 2x^4 - x^3 + 2x^2 - x + 1 by x - 1 and write the result as a sum of a polynomial and a remainder.
A) (2x^3 + x^2 + x) + (2/1)
B) (2x^3 + x^2 + x) + (0)
C) (2x^3 + 2x^2 + x) + (2/1)
D) (2x^3 + 2x^2 + x) + (0)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The quotient is 2x^3 + x^2 + x and there is no remainder, as the division is exact. So the correct answer is B.

Explanation:
When dividing polynomials, if the division is exact (no remainder), the result can be written simply as a polynomial without any fraction. In this case, we have an exact division.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to include a non-existent remainder when the division is exact, leading to an incorrect representation of the division result.

Problem 6 (6):
Divide the polynomial 5x^3 - 10x^2 + 15x by x(x - 3) and express the quotient as a sum of simpler terms.
A) 5x^2 - 5x + 5
B) 5x^2 - 10x + 15
C) 5x - 5
D) 5(x - 3)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The quotient is 5x^2 - 5x + 5, as we can divide each term of the dividend by x(x - 3). So the correct answer is A.

Explanation:
When dividing polynomials by a binomial, it's helpful to factor out the common terms in the divisor and then perform the division. In this case, we have x(x - 3) which can be factored out.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to distribute the quotient over the factors of the divisor, leading to an incorrect result.

Problem 7 (7):
Divide the polynomial 8x^4 - 16x^3 + 24x^2 by 4x^2 and express the result in standard form.
A) 2x^2 - 4x + 6
B) 2x^2 - 4x + 3
C) 8x^2 - 16x + 24
D) x^2 - 2x + 3
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Dividing each term of the dividend by 4x^2 gives 2x^2 - 4x + 6. So the correct answer is A.

Explanation:
When dividing polynomials by a monomial that's a power of x, we can divide each term's coefficient and exponent separately. In this case, we have a constant factor in the divisor.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to adjust the exponents when dividing by powers of x, resulting in an incorrect quotient.
