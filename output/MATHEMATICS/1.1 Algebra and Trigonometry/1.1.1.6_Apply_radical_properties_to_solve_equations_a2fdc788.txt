Problem 1 (q1):
Solve for \( x \) in the equation \( \sqrt{x+5} = 4 \).
A) 11
B) 21
C) -11
D) 9
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
First, square both sides to eliminate the square root: \((\sqrt{x+5})^2 = 4^2\) which simplifies to \(x + 5 = 16\). Solve for \(x\) by subtracting 5: \(x = 11\).

Explanation:
Squaring both sides removes the square root, allowing you to solve for \(x\) algebraically.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is not squaring both sides correctly, leading to incorrect simplification.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (q2):
If \( \sqrt{2x - 3} = 5 \), find the value of \( x \).
A) 14
B) 13
C) 11
D) 17
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Square both sides: \((\sqrt{2x - 3})^2 = 5^2\) giving \(2x - 3 = 25\). Add 3 to both sides: \(2x = 28\). Divide by 2: \(x = 14\).

Explanation:
Squaring correctly isolates the variable, ensuring accurate solution.

Common mistake note:
Forgetting to divide by 2 at the end can lead to incorrect results.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (q3):
Determine \( x \) in the equation \( 3\sqrt{x} = 12 \).
A) 36
B) 16
C) 4
D) 9
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
First, divide both sides by 3: \(\sqrt{x} = 4\). Square both sides: \((\sqrt{x})^2 = 4^2\), which simplifies to \(x = 16\).

Explanation:
Isolating the square root term before squaring is crucial to solving correctly.

Common mistake note:
A mistake might be squaring both sides too early without isolating \(\sqrt{x}\).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (q4):
Solve the equation \( \sqrt{x - 4} + 3 = 7 \).
A) 12
B) 16
C) 36
D) 20
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Subtract 3 from both sides: \(\sqrt{x - 4} = 4\). Square both sides: \((\sqrt{x - 4})^2 = 4^2\) which simplifies to \(x - 4 = 16\). Add 4 to both sides: \(x = 20\).

Explanation:
Correctly subtracting 3 first helps isolate the radical term, simplifying the solution process.

Common mistake note:
Not isolating the radical first can lead to errors when squaring.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (q5):
For the equation \( \sqrt{3x + 1} = x - 1 \), find \( x \).
A) 0
B) 2
C) 5
D) 1
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Square both sides: \(3x + 1 = (x - 1)^2\), which expands to \(3x + 1 = x^2 - 2x + 1\). Rearrange terms: \(x^2 - 5x = 0\). Factor: \(x(x - 5) = 0\). Solutions are \(x = 0\) or \(x = 5\). Check both: only \(x = 5\) satisfies the original equation.

Explanation:
Ensuring solutions satisfy the original equation is necessary after squaring.

Common mistake note:
Not checking both solutions can lead to trusting an invalid solution.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (q6):
Find \( x \) when \( \sqrt{4x + 25} = x + 5 \).
A) 0
B) 3
C) 4
D) 1
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Square both sides: \(4x + 25 = (x + 5)^2\), which expands to \(4x + 25 = x^2 + 10x + 25\). Simplify: \(0 = x^2 + 6x\). Factor: \(x(x + 6) = 0\). Checking reveals \(x = 1\) satisfies the equation.

Explanation:
Verify each solution with the original equation to avoid extraneous solutions.

Common mistake note:
Relying on unverified solutions can result in incorrect answers.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (q7):
Solve \( \sqrt{x+3} = x - 2 \).
A) 1
B) -1
C) 2
D) 3
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Square both sides: \(x + 3 = (x - 2)^2\), resulting in \(x + 3 = x^2 - 4x + 4\). Rearrange: \(x^2 - 5x + 1 = 0\). Use the quadratic formula: \(x = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{17}}{2}\). Only \(x = 1\) satisfies the original equation.

Explanation:
Using the quadratic formula and verifying solutions ensures accuracy.

Common mistake note:
Failing to check each potential solution can lead to trusting extraneous solutions.
