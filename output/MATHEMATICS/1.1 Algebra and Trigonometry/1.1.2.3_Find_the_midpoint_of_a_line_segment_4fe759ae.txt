Problem 1 (Q1):
Find the midpoint of the line segment with endpoints (2, 3) and (4, 7).
A) (3, 5)
B) (3, 6)
C) (2, 5)
D) (4, 5)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The formula for the midpoint is ((x1 + x2)/2, (y1 + y2)/2). Applying this: ((2 + 4)/2, (3 + 7)/2) = (3, 5).

Explanation:
Add the x-coordinates and y-coordinates of the endpoints separately, divide each sum by 2 to obtain the midpoint.

Common mistake note:
A mistake might involve using the average of coordinates incorrectly; for example, averaging 3 and 7 incorrectly as part of the midpoint formula.

Problem 2 (Q2):
Calculate the midpoint of the line segment connecting (-1, 4) and (3, 10).
A) (1, 7)
B) (0, 7)
C) (1, 6)
D) (2, 8)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The midpoint is ((-1 + 3)/2, (4 + 10)/2) = (2/2, 14/2) = (0, 7).

Explanation:
Using the midpoint formula, combine coordinate pairs and calculate their averages separately.

Common mistake note:
Students might incorrectly combine coordinates before averaging or miscalculate a negative coordinate's impact.

Problem 3 (Q3):
What is the midpoint of the line segment from (5, -2) to (9, 6)?
A) (7, 2)
B) (6, 4)
C) (8, 3)
D) (7, 3)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Midpoint = ((5 + 9)/2, (-2 + 6)/2) = (14/2, 4/2) = (7, 2).

Explanation:
By averaging x-coordinates and y-coordinates, we find the midpoint.

Common mistake note:
Mixing incorrect operations, such as subtracting instead of adding before averaging.

Problem 4 (Q4):
Find the midpoint of the segment joining (0, 0) and (8, 8).
A) (4, 8)
B) (4, 4)
C) (8, 4)
D) (8, 0)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Midpoint = ((0 + 8)/2, (0 + 8)/2) = (8/2, 8/2) = (4, 4).

Explanation:
Midpoint calculation involves averaging the coordinates which, in symmetric points, are equal and centered.

Common mistake note:
Miscalculations often involve misapplying the midpoint formula leading to incorrect coordinate pairing.

Problem 5 (Q5):
Determine the midpoint of a line segment with points (-3, 5) and (7, -9).
A) (2, -4)
B) (2, -2)
C) (1, -2)
D) (0, -4)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Midpoint = ((-3 + 7)/2, (5 + (-9))/2) = (4/2, -4/2) = (2, -4).

Explanation:
Using the midpoint method, compute separately averaging each coordinate's sum.

Common mistake note:
A common error might come from incorrectly handling negative signs during the addition of coordinates.

Problem 6 (Q6):
If a line segment has endpoints (10, 12) and (4, -4), what is its midpoint?
A) (7, 4)
B) (6, 8)
C) (8, 4)
D) (7, 8)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Midpoint = ((10 + 4)/2, (12 + (-4))/2) = (14/2, 8/2) = (7, 4).

Explanation:
Add each pair of coordinates and divide by 2 to find the midpoint location.

Common mistake note:
Errors often stem from overlooking negative values or miscalculating averages.

Problem 7 (Q7):
The endpoints of a line segment are (5, 3) and (-5, -3). What is the midpoint?
A) (0, 0)
B) (5, 0)
C) (0, 3)
D) (-5, -3)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Midpoint = ((5 + (-5))/2, (3 + (-3))/2) = (0, 0).

Explanation:
Symmetrical points around the origin result in the origin itself being the midpoint.

Common mistake note:
The use of subtraction instead of addition, fundamentally altering the resulting calculation.
