Problem 1 (One degree to radians):
What is 1 degree in radians?
A) 0.0174533
B) 0.174533
C) 1.74533
D) 17.4533
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
1 degree is equal to π/180 radians. So, 1 degree = (π/180) radians ≈ 0.0174533 radians.

Explanation:
The conversion factor from degrees to radians is π/180 or 0.0174533 per degree.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is not using the correct conversion factor, which results in an incorrect value.

Problem 2 (60 degrees to radians):
What is 60 degrees in radians?
A) 1.0472
B) 3.14159
C) 6.28319
D) 0.314159
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
60 degrees is equal to π/3 radians. So, 60 degrees = (π/3) radians ≈ 1.0472 radians.

Explanation:
The conversion from degrees to radians can be done by multiplying the number of degrees by π/180.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is forgetting to multiply by π or using the wrong value for π.

Problem 3 (Radians to degrees):
What is 3.14159 radians in degrees?
A) 180°
B) 90°
C) 60°
D) 360°
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To convert from radians to degrees, multiply the number of radians by 180/π. So, 3.14159 radians = (3.14159 × 180) / π ≈ 180°.

Explanation:
The conversion factor from radians to degrees is 180/π or approximately 57.296 degrees per radian.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is using the wrong conversion factor, which results in an incorrect value.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (π/4 radians to degrees):
What is π/4 radians in degrees?
A) 30°
B) 45°
C) 60°
D) 90°
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
π/4 radians is equal to 45 degrees. So, π/4 radians = (π/4 × 180) / π = 45°.

Explanation:
π/4 radians corresponds to a quarter of a full rotation, which is 90° divided by 2.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is not dividing the result by π when converting from radians to degrees.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (π radians to degrees):
What is π radians in degrees?
A) 180°
B) 90°
C) 360°
D) 270°
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
π radians is equal to 180 degrees. So, π radians = (π × 180) / π = 180°.

Explanation:
π radians corresponds to half a full rotation, which is 180°.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is not recognizing that π radians represents half a circle.

2π radians is equal to 360 degrees. So, 2π radians = (2π × 180) / π = 360°.

Explanation:
2π radians corresponds to a full rotation around a circle, which is 360°.

Common mistake note:

What is 5π/3 radians in degrees?
A) 300°
B) 180°
C) 270°
D) 90°
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
5π/3 radians is equal to 300 degrees. So, 5π/3 radians = (5π/3 × 180) / π = 300°.
