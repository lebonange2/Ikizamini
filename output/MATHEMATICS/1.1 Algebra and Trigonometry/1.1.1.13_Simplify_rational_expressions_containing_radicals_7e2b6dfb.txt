Problem 1 (Q1):
Simplify the expression \( \frac{\sqrt{18}}{\sqrt{2}} \).
A) \sqrt{9}
B) 3\sqrt{3}
C) \sqrt{18}
D) 3
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
\( \frac{\sqrt{18}}{\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{\frac{18}{2}} = \sqrt{9} = 3 \).

Explanation:
Combine the radicals under a single square root: \( \sqrt{\frac{18}{2}} = \sqrt{9} \). The square root of 9 is 3.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to multiply the radicals instead of simplifying, resulting in an incorrect expression like \( \sqrt{36} \).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Q2):
Simplify \( \frac{5\sqrt{8}}{\sqrt{2}} \).
A) \sqrt{16}
B) 10
C) 10\sqrt{2}
D) 5\sqrt{4}
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
\( \frac{5\sqrt{8}}{\sqrt{2}} = 5\sqrt{\frac{8}{2}} = 5\sqrt{4} = 5 \times 2 = 10 \).

Explanation:
Simplify under the square root first to get \( \sqrt{4} = 2 \), then multiply by 5.

Common mistake note:
A mistake occurs if simplifying \( \sqrt{8} \) instead of finding \( \sqrt{8/2} \), leading to \( 5\sqrt{2} \).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Q3):
Simplify \( \frac{\sqrt{50}}{\sqrt{5}} \).
A) 5
B) \sqrt{10}
C) 10
D) \sqrt{5}
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
\( \frac{\sqrt{50}}{\sqrt{5}} = \sqrt{\frac{50}{5}} = \sqrt{10} \).

Explanation:
Simplifying under the radical gives \( \sqrt{\frac{50}{5}} = \sqrt{10} \).

Common mistake note:
Ignoring to simplify under the radical and leaving as \( \sqrt{50/5} \) leads to an incorrect result.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Q4):
What is the simplified form of \( \frac{7\sqrt{12}}{\sqrt{3}} \)?
A) 14
B) 7\sqrt{4}
C) 7\sqrt{3}
D) 14\sqrt{3}
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Simplifying gives \( \frac{7\sqrt{12}}{\sqrt{3}} = 7 \sqrt{\frac{12}{3}} = 7\sqrt{4} = 7 \times 2 = 14 \).

Explanation:
Simplify inside the radicals: \( \sqrt{12} = \sqrt{4} \). Multiply \( 7 \times 2 = 14 \).

Common mistake note:
Failing to simplify \( \sqrt{12} \) in the form \( \sqrt{4} \) results in incorrect multiplication.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (Q5):
Simplify \( \frac{\sqrt{45}}{\sqrt{5}} \).
A) \sqrt{9}
B) 3
C) \sqrt{45}
D) \sqrt{36}
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
\( \frac{\sqrt{45}}{\sqrt{5}} = \sqrt{\frac{45}{5}} = \sqrt{9} = 3 \).

Explanation:
\( 45 \div 5 = 9 \), and \( \sqrt{9} = 3 \). Factor and simplify within the root first.

Common mistake note:
An error occurs by omitting the denominator, leading directly to \( \sqrt{45} \).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (Q6):
Simplify \( \frac{\sqrt{48}}{\sqrt{3}} \).
A) 4\sqrt{4}
B) 4\sqrt{3}
C) 4
D) \sqrt{16}
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
\( \frac{\sqrt{48}}{\sqrt{3}} = \sqrt{\frac{48}{3}} = \sqrt{16} = 4 \).

Explanation:
Divide under the radical and simplify: \( 48 \div 3 = 16 \), then find \( \sqrt{16} = 4 \).

Common mistake note:
Mistaking \( \sqrt{48} \) as \( \sqrt{4} \), leading erroneously to \( 2\sqrt{3} \).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Q7):
Simplify \( \frac{8\sqrt{6}}{4\sqrt{2}} \).
A) 2\sqrt{3}
B) 4
C) 4\sqrt{6}
D) 2\sqrt{2}
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
\( \frac{8\sqrt{6}}{4\sqrt{2}} = \frac{8}{4} \times \sqrt{\frac{6}{2}} = 2\sqrt{3} \).

Explanation:
Divide coefficients separately from the radicals and simplify \( \sqrt{\frac{6}{2}} \).

Common mistake note:
Mistakes happen when forgetting to simplify the coefficient and radical separately.
