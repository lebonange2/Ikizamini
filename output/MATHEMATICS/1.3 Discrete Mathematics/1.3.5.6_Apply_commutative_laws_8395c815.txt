Problem 1 (:1):
What is the result of adding 7 and 4?
A) 3
B) 11
C) 18
D) 21
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The commutative law states that the order of addition does not matter. So, 7 + 4 is the same as 4 + 7. The correct answer is 11.

Explanation:
This question tests the understanding of basic arithmetic and the commutative property of addition.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose 'A' (3), which is the result of subtracting 4 from 7, not adding them. This demonstrates a lack of understanding of the commutative law.

Problem 3 (3):
What is the sum of 2 + 5 + 3?
A) 8
B) 10
C) 13
D) 15
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The commutative law allows us to rearrange the terms. So, (2 + 5) + 3 is the same as 2 + (5 + 3). The correct answer is 13.

Explanation:
This question tests the understanding of applying the commutative law when adding multiple numbers.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose 'B' (10), which is the result of adding only the first two numbers, not considering the third. This demonstrates a lack of understanding of the commutative law.

Problem 5 (5):
What is the result of adding 9 and 4 and then adding 3?
A) 12
B) 16
C) 17
D) 20
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
First, we add 9 and 4 (which is 13) and then add 3. So, (9 + 4) + 3 = 13 + 3 = 16. The correct answer is 17.

Explanation:
This question tests the understanding of applying the commutative law in a multi-step calculation.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose 'D' (20), which is the result of adding all three numbers together without considering the order. This demonstrates a lack of understanding of the commutative law and proper arithmetic operations.
