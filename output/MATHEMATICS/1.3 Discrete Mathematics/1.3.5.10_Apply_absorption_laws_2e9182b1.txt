Problem 1 (:1):
Simplify the expression: (a + b)(a + b)
A) (a + b)^2
B) a^2 + b^2
C) 2a + 2b
D) a^2 + ab + ba + b^2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The expression simplifies using the absorption law, which states that (x + y)(x + y) = x^2 + 2xy + y^2. In this case, x = a and y = b. So, (a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2. The correct simplified form is (a + b)^2.

Explanation:
Absorption law helps combine like terms in the product of binomials where both terms are the same.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose B, which is the sum of squares, instead of using the absorption law. This fails because it doesn't account for the middle term (2ab).

Problem 2 (:2):
Simplify: (x - 3)(x + 3)
A) x^2 - 9
B) x^2 + 6x - 9
C) x^2 - 6x - 9
D) x^2 + 9
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The expression simplifies using the difference of squares formula, which states that (a - b)(a + b) = a^2 - b^2. Here, a = x and b = 3. So, (x - 3)(x + 3) = x^2 - 3^2 = x^2 - 9.

Explanation:
The difference of squares formula is an absorption law that simplifies products where one term is the negative of the other.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose D, which is the sum of squares. This fails because it doesn't account for the fact that one term is subtracted from the other.

Problem 3 (:3):
Simplify: (2x + 5)(2x - 5)
A) 4x^2 - 10x - 25
B) 4x^2 - 25
C) 4x^2 + 25
D) 4x^2 + 10x - 25
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The expression simplifies using the difference of squares formula: (a - b)(a + b) = a^2 - b^2. Here, a = 2x and b = 5. So, (2x + 5)(2x - 5) = (2x)^2 - 5^2 = 4x^2 - 25.

Explanation:
The difference of squares formula helps simplify products where one term is the negative of the other, regardless of the coefficient.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose A or D, which include terms that don't match the correct simplification. This happens when not applying the formula correctly.
