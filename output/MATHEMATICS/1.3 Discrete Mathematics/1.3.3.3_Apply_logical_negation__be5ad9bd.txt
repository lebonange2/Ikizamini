Problem 1 (Asdf):
What is the result of ¬(true)?
A) false
B) true
C) unknown
D) not applicable
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The logical negation of true is false.

Explanation:
Logical negation inverts the truth value of a statement. When applied to true, it becomes false.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B (true). This fails because negating a true statement results in false.

Problem 3 (Zxcv):
What is the result of ¬(unknown)?
A) known
B) unknown
C) true
D) false
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The logical negation of unknown does not change its value.

Explanation:
Logical operations do not alter the truth value of 'unknown' or indeterminate values.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A (known). This fails because negating an unknown value does not make it known.

Problem 5 (Ghjk):
Which of the following represents the negation of 'If it's raining, then I'll stay home.'?
A) It's raining and I'll stay home.
B) It's not raining or I'll stay home.
C) It's not raining and I won't stay home.
D) It's raining or I won't stay home.
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The negation of a conditional statement switches the antecedent and consequent and inverts their truth values.

Explanation:
The contrapositive form of the given conditional is 'If it's not raining, then I won't stay home.'

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B (It's not raining or I'll stay home). This fails because it's the inclusive disjunction, not the negation.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (Uyti):
What is the result of ¬(p ∧ q)?
A) ¬p ∧ q
B) p ∧ ¬q
C) ¬p ∨ ¬q
D) p ∨ q
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The negation of a conjunction (AND) is equivalent to the disjunction (OR) of the negations.

Explanation:
De Morgan's law states that the negation of a conjunction is equal to the disjunction of the negated terms.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B (p ∧ ¬q). This fails because it only negates one term, not both.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Rtfg):
What is the result of ¬(p ∨ q)?
A) ¬p ∨ q
B) p ∨ ¬q
C) ¬p ∧ ¬q
D) p ∧ q
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The negation of a disjunction (OR) is equivalent to the conjunction (AND) of the negations.

Explanation:
De Morgan's law states that the negation of a disjunction is equal to the conjunction of the negated terms.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A (¬p ∨ q). This fails because it only negates one term, not both.
