Problem 1 (:1):
Simplify the expression: 5 + 0
A) 5
B) 0
C) -5
D) Cannot be simplified
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The identity law for addition states that any number added to zero equals the original number. So, 5 + 0 = 5.

Explanation:
Identity laws preserve the value of an expression when a specific operation is applied. In this case, applying the addition identity with 0 keeps the value at 5.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose 'B', which is incorrect because it does not apply the identity law correctly.

Problem 7 (:7):
Simplify the expression: 2 + (-2) × 1
A) 0
B) 2
C) -2
D) 4
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The order of operations (PEMDAS/BODMAS) requires multiplication before addition. So, 2 + (-2) × 1 = 2 + (-2) = 0.

Explanation:
This question tests the understanding of applying identity laws in the context of proper order of operations.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose 'B' or 'C', which are incorrect because they do not follow the correct order of operations.
