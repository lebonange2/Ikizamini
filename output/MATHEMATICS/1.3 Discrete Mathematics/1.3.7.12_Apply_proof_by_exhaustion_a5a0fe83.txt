Problem 1 (Asdf):
Prove that the sum of two even numbers is always an even number using proof by exhaustion. Which of the following represents a valid case to consider in the proof?
A) (2, 4)
B) (3, 5)
C) (0, 0)
D) (1, 1)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
In proof by exhaustion, we consider all possible cases. For two even numbers, the smallest case is when both numbers are the smallest even numbers, which are 2 and 4. Thus, considering (2, 4) as a case ensures that we cover all possibilities since any other even number can be expressed as a multiple of 2. Options B, C, and D involve at least one odd number or zero, which is not representative of the general case for two even numbers.

Explanation:
Proof by exhaustion requires considering every possible case within the given domain to establish a result. In this case, we are looking at sums of two even numbers.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose an odd number or zero as one of the numbers, which does not represent the general case for even numbers.

Explanation:

Explanation:
