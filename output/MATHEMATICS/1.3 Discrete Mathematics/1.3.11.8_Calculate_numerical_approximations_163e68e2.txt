Problem 1 (Asdf):
What is the approximate value of √2, rounded to two decimal places?
A) 1.40
B) 1.41
C) 1.42
D) 1.43
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The exact value of √2 is approximately 1.41421356237. Rounding this to two decimal places gives 1.41.

Explanation:
To find the numerical approximation, we calculate the square root and round it to the desired number of decimal places.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to round the result to two decimal places.

Problem 7 (Bnm):
What is the approximate value of 10! / 8!, rounded to seven decimal places?
A) 45.00000
B) 46.00000
C) 47.00000
D) 48.00000
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The exact value of 10! / 8! is approximately 45. The result is an integer, so when rounded to seven decimal places, it remains 45.

Explanation:
To find the numerical approximation, we calculate the ratio of factorials and round it to the desired number of decimal places. Since the result is an integer, there's no need to round beyond that.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that the division results in an integer or to attempt rounding unnecessarily.
