Problem 1 (:1):
What is the result of dividing 27 by 3?
A) 6
B) 9
C) 18
D) 54
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
27 ÷ 3 = 9

Explanation:
To find the quotient, we divide the dividend (27) by the divisor (3). The result is 9.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to multiply instead of dividing, which would give an incorrect answer of 81 (27 × 3).
