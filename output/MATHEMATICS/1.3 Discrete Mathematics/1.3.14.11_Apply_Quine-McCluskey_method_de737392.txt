Problem 1 (:1):
Simplify the boolean expression: A'BC + ABC'D + AB'C'D.
A) A'C'
B) ABC'D'
C) A'B'C'D'
D) AB'C'
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Using Quine-McCluskey method, we group minterms and eliminate overlapping terms. The given expression has three minterms: 1 (A'BC), 5 (ABC'D), and 6 (AB'C'D). Grouping them by their first differing bit, we have two groups: {1, 5} and {6}. For the group {1, 5}, we form a 'don't care' term (X) for the overlapping bit. The simplified expression is A'B'C'D'.

Explanation:
The Quine-McCluskey method helps simplify boolean expressions by grouping minterms and eliminating overlapping terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to not group the minterms correctly or to incorrectly form 'don't care' terms.

Problem 2 (:2):
Simplify the boolean expression: X + YZ.
A) X
B) Y
C) Z
D) XYZ
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The term XYZ can be simplified to X since YZ is a 'don't care' term when combined with X. The simplified expression is X.

Explanation:
In Quine-McCluskey, if a minterm is covered by another term, it becomes a 'don't care' term and can be eliminated.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to think that YZ contributes something to the simplified expression, but in this case, it does not.

Problem 3 (:3):
Simplify the boolean expression: A'B'C'D + ABCD' + AB'C'D.
A) A'C'
B) ABC'D'
C) A'B'C'D'
D) AB'C'
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Grouping minterms by their first differing bit, we have two groups: {A'B'C'D} and {ABCD', AB'C'D}. The second group forms a 'don't care' term (X) for the overlapping bit. The simplified expression is A'B'C'D'

Explanation:
Quine-McCluskey helps identify overlapping terms and eliminate them to simplify boolean expressions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to not recognize that ABCD' and AB'C'D overlap, leading to an incorrect grouping.

Problem 4 (:4):
Simplify the boolean expression: X + Y + Z.
A) X
B) Y
C) Z
D) XYZ
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The terms X, Y, and Z are all distinct and cannot be simplified further. The simplified expression is XYZ.

Explanation:
When no overlapping terms exist, the boolean expression remains unchanged.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to attempt to simplify this expression by grouping terms, but there are no overlapping bits.
