Problem 1 (:1):
Express the boolean expression A'BC + ABC' + AB'C in SOP form.
A) A'B'C'
B) ABC'
C) AB'C'
D) A'B+C'
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The SOP (Sum of Products) form is a way to express a boolean expression as a sum of products. To convert the given expression A'BC + ABC' + AB'C into SOP, we can group the terms based on their common variables and find the product of those variables for each term. The common terms are A'B'C', which covers all three terms.

Explanation:
The correct answer is D because it represents the sum of products where A', B, and C' appear together in one term (A'B+C') and cover all possible combinations of the original expression.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or B. These choices are incorrect because they don't include all the variable combinations present in the original expression.

Problem 2 (2):
Express the boolean expression (X + Y)(Δ + Z) in POS form.
A) XYZ'
B) XY'Z'
C) XΔY'ΔZ'
D) XΔYZ'
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The POS (Product of Sums) form is a way to express a boolean expression as a product of sums. To convert the given expression (X + Y)(Δ + Z) into POS, we distribute each variable from one set across all variables in the other set.

Explanation:
The correct answer is C because it represents the product of sums where X and Δ appear together in one term (XΔ), Y' and Δ appear together in another term (Y'Δ), and Z appears alone, covering all possible combinations of the original expression.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or B. These choices are incorrect because they don't include all the variable combinations present in the original expression.
