Problem 1 (OnePlusOneCommutative):
What is the result of adding 1 and 1?
A) 0
B) 2
C) 1
D) 3
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The commutative law states that changing the order of addition does not change the sum. So, 1 + 1 is equal to 1 + 1, which equals 2.

Explanation:
Commutativity applies to addition: a + b = b + a.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be selecting A (0) or C (1), not understanding that adding two 1s results in 2.

Problem 2 (TwoPlusThreeCommutative):
What is the result of adding 2 and 3?

Solution:

Problem 3 (MultiplicationFiveByTwoCommutative):
What is the result of multiplying 5 by 2?

Solution:

Problem 4 (MultiplicationAndAdditionMixed):
What is the result of adding 3 multiplied by 2 and 1?
A) 7
B) 8
C) 5
D) 6
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
First, multiply 3 by 2 (3 	imes 2 = 6), then add 1: 6 + 1 = 7. The commutative law applies to addition but not multiplication.

Explanation:
Commutativity applies to addition: a + b = b + a, but not to multiplication: a 	imes b ≠ b 	imes a.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be selecting D (6), assuming the commutative law applies to both addition and multiplication.

Problem 5 (MultiplicationAndSubtractionMixed):
What is the result of multiplying 4 by 3 and then subtracting 2?
A) 10
B) 12
C) 8
D) 14
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
First, multiply 4 by 3 (4 	imes 3 = 12), then subtract 2: 12 - 2 = 10. The commutative law does not apply to subtraction.

Explanation:
Commutativity applies to addition but not to subtraction or multiplication: a + b = b + a, and a 	imes b ≠ b 	imes a.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be selecting B (12), assuming the commutative law applies to both multiplication and subtraction.
