Problem 1 (Asdf):
Convert the expression 2x + 3y + 4z to POS form.
A) (x + y + z)(2 + 3 + 4)
B) (2x)(3y)(4z)
C) (2 + 3 + 4)(x + y + z)
D) (x + y)(2 + 3)(x + z)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To convert the expression to POS form, we distribute the coefficients among the variables. The correct POS form for 2x + 3y + 4z is (2 + 3 + 4)(x + y + z).

Explanation:
POS form involves distributing the coefficients of each variable across the sum of all variables.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to multiply the individual terms, as in choice A or B, which is not the correct POS conversion.
