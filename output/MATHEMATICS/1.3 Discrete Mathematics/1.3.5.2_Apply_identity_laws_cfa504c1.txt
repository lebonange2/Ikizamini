Problem 1 (:Q1):
Simplify the expression: 5 + 0
A) 5
B) 0
C) -5
D) Cannot be simplified
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The identity law for addition states that any number added to zero remains unchanged. So, 5 + 0 = 5.

Explanation:
Identity laws preserve the value of an expression when a specific operation is applied. In this case, applying the addition identity with 0 results in the original number.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B (0) or C (-5). This happens when not recognizing that adding zero to any number does not change its value.

Problem 3 (:Q3):
Simplify the expression: (-2)^0
A) 1
B) -2
C) 0
D) Cannot be simplified
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The identity law for exponentiation with 0 states that any non-zero number raised to the power of zero is equal to one. So, (-2)^0 = 1.

Explanation:
Identity laws apply to exponents as well; a non-zero number raised to the power of zero equals one.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B (-2) or C (0). This happens when not remembering that any non-zero number raised to the power of zero is 1.

Problem 5 (:Q5):
Simplify the expression: 4 ÷ (-1/4)
A) 1
B) -1
C) 0
D) Cannot be simplified
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The identity law for division states that any number divided by its reciprocal is equal to 1. So, 4 ÷ (-1/4) = -1.

Explanation:
When a number is divided by its reciprocal (the fraction with flipped numerator and denominator), the result is always 1, unless the number is zero.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A (1). This happens when not recognizing that dividing by the reciprocal results in -1 for non-zero numbers.

B) 5
C) 25
D) Cannot be simplified
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The identity law for squaring states that any number squared is a positive value. So, (-5)^2 = 25.

Explanation:
When a negative number is squared, the result is always positive because squaring removes the negative sign.

Common mistake note:
