Problem 1 (Q1):
What is the result of applying the double negation law to the statement 'It is not the case that there are no dogs in the park'?
A) There are some dogs in the park.
B) There are no dogs in the park.
C) It is not the case that there are dogs in the park.
D) It is not true that it is not the case that there are dogs in the park.
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The double negation law states that if a statement has two negative words (e.g., 'not' and 'no'), removing both negatives results in the opposite truth value. So, 'It is not the case that there are no dogs in the park' becomes 'There are some dogs in the park.'

Explanation:
The double negation law flips the truth of a statement with 双重否定。

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose option B, which keeps the original negative statement. This fails because it does not apply the double negation correctly.
