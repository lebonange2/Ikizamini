Problem 1 (Asdf):
What is the sum of the binomial coefficients for (x+y)^4?
A) a
B) b
C) c
D) d
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The sum of the binomial coefficients in a row of Pascal's triangle is equal to 2^n, where n is the row number. For (x+y)^4, we are looking at row 4 of the triangle. So, the sum is 2^4 = 16. However, there is an extra coefficient of 1 at both ends of the row, making the total sum 1 + 4 + 6 + 4 + 1 = 16 + 10 = 26. The closest answer choice is C: 24.

Explanation:
The correct formula for summing binomial coefficients in a row is used, but common mistake is not accounting for the extra coefficients at the ends of the row.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Not including the first and last coefficients (1) in the sum.

Problem 3 (Asdf3):
What is the sum of the binomial coefficients for the fourth term in the expansion of (x+y)^7?
A) a
B) b
C) c
D) d
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The fourth term in the expansion of (x+y)^7 will have coefficients based on row 7, but we only need to consider the coefficients for the term that has x^3y^4. The binomial coefficient for this term is C(7, 3) = 7! / (3!(7-3)!) = 35. Since we're asked for the sum of these coefficients, which is just one term in this case, the answer is 35 itself. The closest answer choice is C: 42.

Explanation:
The question asks for the sum of coefficients for a specific term, not the entire row.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Summing all coefficients in row 7 instead of just those for x^3y^4.
