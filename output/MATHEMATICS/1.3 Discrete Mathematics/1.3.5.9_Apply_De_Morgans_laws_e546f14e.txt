Problem 1 (What is the negation of (A AND B)?):
(A) NOT A OR NOT B
(B) NOT A AND NOT B
(C) NOT A OR B
(D) A AND NOT B
A) NOT A OR NOT B
B) NOT A AND NOT B
C) NOT A OR B
D) A AND NOT B
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
De Morgan's law states that the negation of a conjunction (AND) is equivalent to the disjunction (OR) of the negations. So, the negation of (A AND B) is (NOT A OR NOT B).

Explanation:
De Morgan's laws help simplify logical expressions by negating both the operands and the operator.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing (C) which is the negation of only one operand. Remember, De Morgan's law applies to both operands.

Problem 2 (What is the negation of (A OR B AND C)?):
(A) NOT A AND NOT (B AND C)
(B) NOT A OR NOT B AND NOT C
(C) NOT A AND NOT B OR NOT C
(D) NOT (A OR B) AND NOT C
A) NOT A AND NOT (B AND C)
B) NOT A OR NOT B AND NOT C
C) NOT A AND NOT B OR NOT C
D) NOT (A OR B) AND NOT C
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
De Morgan's law can be applied to multiple operands as well. The negation of (A OR B AND C) is (NOT A AND NOT B) AND (NOT A AND NOT C), which simplifies to NOT (A OR B) AND NOT C.

Explanation:
When applying De Morgan's laws, consider the grouping and apply the law to each group separately.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing (B) or (C) by incorrectly distributing the negation. Remember to apply De Morgan's law to the entire expression within parentheses.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (What is the negation of ((NOT A) OR B)?):
(A) A AND NOT B
(B) A OR NOT B
(C) NOT (NOT A) AND B
(D) NOT (NOT A OR B)
A) A AND NOT B
B) A OR NOT B
C) NOT (NOT A) AND B
D) NOT (NOT A OR B)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The negation of ((NOT A) OR B) is NOT (NOT A OR B), which simplifies to (A AND NOT B).

Explanation:
When applying De Morgan's laws, remember that NOT distributes over OR and AND.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing (C) by reversing the operands without negating them. Remember to negate both the operands and the operator.
