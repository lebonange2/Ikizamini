Problem 1 (AsdFgHjKlMnO):
Simplify the expression: 4(x + 3)
A) 12x + 12
B) 4x + 12
C) 16x + 12
D) 4x + 9
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To apply the constant multiple rule, multiply each term inside the parentheses by the constant outside: 4 * x = 4x and 4 * 3 = 12. The simplified expression is 4x + 12.

Explanation:
The constant multiple rule states that when a constant multiplies a polynomial, distribute the constant to each term in the parentheses.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to multiply only one term (e.g., 4 * x) and forget the other (4 * 3), resulting in an incorrect expression like 12x + 3.

Problem 2 (BvnCxmDyEzF):
Simplify the expression: -5(2y - 7)
A) -10y + 35
B) 10y - 35
C) -10y - 35
D) 10y + 35
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Apply the constant multiple rule: -5 * 2y = -10y and -5 * (-7) = 35. The simplified expression is -10y + 35.

Explanation:
The constant multiple rule applies to negative constants, distributing the negative sign to each term in the parentheses.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to distribute only the negative sign to one term (e.g., -10y) and forget the other (-35), resulting in an incorrect expression like -20y + 7.

Problem 3 (CwoDxpEzFyG):
Simplify the expression: 6(4a^2 - 2ab + 1)
A) 24a^2 - 12ab + 6
B) 24a^2 + 12ab + 6
C) 18a^2 - 12ab + 6
D) 18a^2 + 12ab + 6
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Use the constant multiple rule: 6 * 4a^2 = 24a^2, 6 * (-2ab) = -12ab, and 6 * 1 = 6. The simplified expression is 24a^2 - 12ab + 6.

Explanation:
The constant multiple rule works with polynomials involving variables, distributing the constant to each term including variable parts.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to multiply only one term (e.g., 6 * 4a^2) and forget others (-12ab and 6), resulting in an incorrect expression like 24a^2 - 12ab.

Common mistake note:

Problem 7 (GhiJklMNpoQ):
Simplify the expression: 2(9y^3 - 6y^2 + 4y)
A) 18y^3 - 12y^2 + 8y
B) 18y^3 + 12y^2 + 8y
C) 12y^3 - 12y^2 + 8y
D) 12y^3 + 12y^2 + 8y
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Use the constant multiple rule: 2 * 9y^3 = 18y^3, 2 * (-6y^2) = -12y^2, and 2 * 4y = 8y. The simplified expression is 18y^3 - 12y^2 + 8y.

Explanation:
The constant multiple rule works with polynomials involving higher powers of variables, distributing the constant to each term.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to multiply only one term (e.g., 2 * 9y^3) and forget others (-12y^2 and 8y), resulting in an incorrect expression like 18y^3 - 12y^2.
