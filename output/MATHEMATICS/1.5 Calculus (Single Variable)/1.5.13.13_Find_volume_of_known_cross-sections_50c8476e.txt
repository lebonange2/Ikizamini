Problem 1 (:1):
A cylinder has a height of 8 cm and a radius of 4 cm. What is the volume of the cylinder in cubic centimeters?
A) 256π
B) 128π
C) 64π
D) 32π
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The formula for the volume of a cylinder is V = πr²h, where r is the radius and h is the height. Given a height of 8 cm and a radius of 4 cm, we can substitute these values into the formula: V = π(4)²(8) = 128π cubic centimeters.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by correctly applying the volume formula with the given dimensions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to square the radius or multiply by π, resulting in an incorrect volume.

Problem 2 (:2):
Find the volume of a rectangular prism with length 10 cm, width 6 cm, and height 3 cm.
A) 90π
B) 180
C) 540
D) 180π
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The formula for the volume of a rectangular prism is V = lwh, where l is the length, w is the width, and h is the height. Given a length of 10 cm, a width of 6 cm, and a height of 3 cm, we can substitute these values into the formula: V = 10 × 6 × 3 = 180 cubic centimeters.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by correctly applying the volume formula with the given dimensions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to multiply the dimensions incorrectly or forget to multiply altogether, resulting in an incorrect volume.

Problem 3 (:3):
What is the volume of a cone with a radius of 5 cm and a height of 12 cm?
A) 60π
B) 150π
C) 75π
D) 300π
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The formula for the volume of a cone is V = (1/3)πr²h, where r is the radius and h is the height. Given a radius of 5 cm and a height of 12 cm, we can substitute these values into the formula: V = (1/3)π(5)²(12) = 75π cubic centimeters.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by correctly applying the volume formula with the given dimensions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to cube the radius or divide by 3, resulting in an incorrect volume.

Problem 4 (:4):
Find the volume of a triangular prism with a base area of 20 square cm and a height of 15 cm.
A) 300
B) 600
C) 900
D) 1800
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The formula for the volume of a triangular prism is V = B * h, where B is the base area and h is the height. Given a base area of 20 square cm and a height of 15 cm, we can substitute these values into the formula: V = 20 × 15 = 300 cubic centimeters.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by correctly applying the volume formula with the given dimensions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to multiply the base area and height incorrectly, resulting in an incorrect volume.

Problem 5 (:5):
What is the volume of a sphere with a diameter of 10 cm?
A) 250π
B) 500π
C) 750π
D) 1000π
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The formula for the volume of a sphere is V = (4/3)πr³, where r is the radius. Given a diameter of 10 cm, the radius is half of that, so r = 5 cm. We can substitute this value into the formula: V = (4/3)π(5)³ = 500π cubic centimeters.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by correctly applying the volume formula with the given dimensions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to use the diameter instead of radius in the formula or forget to cube the radius, resulting in an incorrect volume.

Problem 6 (:6):
Find the volume of a rectangular pyramid with a base area of 30 square cm and a height of 8 cm.
A) 240
B) 120
C) 960
D) 720
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The formula for the volume of a rectangular pyramid is V = (1/3)B * h, where B is the base area and h is the height. Given a base area of 30 square cm and a height of 8 cm, we can substitute these values into the formula: V = (1/3) × 30 × 8 = 240 cubic centimeters.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by correctly applying the volume formula with the given dimensions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to multiply the base area and height by (1/3), resulting in an incorrect volume.

Problem 7 (:7):
Find the volume of a prism with a base area of 48 square cm, a height of 6 cm, and 5 identical rectangular faces.
A) 240
B) 1200
C) 960
D) 720
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The volume of a prism with multiple identical rectangular faces is found by multiplying the base area by the height and then multiplying by the number of faces. Given a base area of 48 square cm, a height of 6 cm, and 5 faces, we can calculate the volume: V = 48 × 6 × 5 = 960 cubic centimeters.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by correctly applying the volume formula with the given dimensions and multiplying by the number of faces.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to multiply by the number of identical rectangular faces, resulting in an incorrect volume.
