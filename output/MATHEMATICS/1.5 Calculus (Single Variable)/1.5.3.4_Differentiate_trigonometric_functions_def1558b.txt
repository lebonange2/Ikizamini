Problem 1 (:Q1):
Find the derivative of f(x) = 3sin(x). Which of the following is correct?
A) 3cos(x)
B) 6sin(x)
C) 9cos(x)
D) 0
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The derivative of sin(x) is cos(x). Since there's a constant multiplier (3), the derivative will be 3cos(x). So, the correct answer is A.

Explanation:
When differentiating a function with a constant coefficient, multiply the coefficient to the derivative of the function.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to differentiate the constant (3) as well, leading to choice B. However, constants do not affect the differentiation process.

Problem 2 (:Q2):
Find the derivative of g(x) = 5cos(2x). Which one is correct?
A) -10sin(2x)
B) 10sin(2x)
C) 10cos(2x)
D) 20cos(2x)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The derivative of cos(u) is -sin(u). Here, u = 2x. So, the derivative will be -sin(2x) multiplied by the derivative of 2x (which is 2). Thus, the correct answer is A.

Explanation:
When differentiating a composite function like cos(2x), use the chain rule: differentiate the outer function and multiply it by the derivative of the inner function.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget the negative sign in front of sin(2x) or to not multiply by the derivative of 2x (2), leading to choices B, C, or D.

Problem 3 (:Q3):
Find the derivative of h(x) = tan(x). Which one is correct?
A) sec^2(x)
B) csc^2(x)
C) cot^2(x)
D) 1
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The derivative of tan(x) is sec^2(x). So, the correct answer is A.

Explanation:
The differentiation rule for tangent is straightforward: d/dx(tan(x)) = sec^2(x).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to confuse the derivatives of tan(x), cot(x), or sec(x) with each other, leading to choices B, C, or D.

Problem 4 (:Q4):
Find the derivative of j(x) = 2csc(3x). Which one is correct?
A) -6cot(3x)csc(3x)
B) 6cot(3x)csc^2(3x)
C) 6csc(3x)
D) 12csc^2(3x)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The derivative of csc(u) is -csc(u)cot(u). Here, u = 3x. So, the derivative will be -csc(3x)cot(3x) multiplied by the derivative of 3x (which is 3). Thus, the correct answer is B.

Explanation:
When differentiating a composite function like csc(3x), use the chain rule: differentiate the outer function and multiply it by the derivative of the inner function. Also, remember to apply the product rule for -csc(u)cot(u).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget the negative sign or to not multiply by the derivative of 3x (3), leading to choices A, C, or D.

Problem 5 (:Q5):
Find the derivative of k(x) = sec(4x). Which one is correct?
A) 4sec^2(4x)
B) 4tan(4x)csc(4x)
C) 4csc^2(4x)
D) 16sec^3(4x)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The derivative of sec(u) is sec(u)tan(u). Here, u = 4x. So, the derivative will be sec(4x)tan(4x) multiplied by the derivative of 4x (which is 4). Thus, the correct answer is D.

Explanation:
When differentiating a composite function like sec(4x), use the chain rule: differentiate the outer function and multiply it by the derivative of the inner function. Also, remember to apply the product rule for sec(u)tan(u).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget the power rule (sec^3(4x)) or to not multiply by the derivative of 4x (4), leading to choices A, B, or C.

Problem 7 (:Q7):
Find the derivative of m(x) = sin(5x)^3. Which one is correct?
A) 15sin^2(5x)
B) 15cos(5x)^2
C) 15sin(5x)^2cos(5x)
D) 3sin(5x)^2
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To differentiate sin(u)^n, use the chain rule and power rule: d/dx(sin(u)^n) = n*sin^(n-1)(u)*cos(u). Here, u = 5x. So, the derivative will be 3*sin^2(5x)*cos(5x), multiplied by the derivative of 5x (which is 5). Thus, the correct answer is C.

Explanation:
When differentiating a composite function like sin(5x)^3, use both the chain rule and power rule. First, differentiate sin(u)^n, then multiply by the derivative of the inner function (5x).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget the power rule (sin^2(5x)) or to not multiply by the derivative of 5x (5), leading to choices A, B, or D.
