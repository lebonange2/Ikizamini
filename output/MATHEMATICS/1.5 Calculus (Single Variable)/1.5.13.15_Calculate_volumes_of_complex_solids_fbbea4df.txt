Problem 1 (Asdf):
Find the volume of a cylinder with radius 5 cm and height 10 cm.
A) 25π cm³
B) 125π cm³
C) 250π cm³
D) 500π cm³
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The volume of a cylinder is V = πr²h. Given r = 5 cm and h = 10 cm, we substitute these values: V = π(5)²(10) = 25π(10) = 250π cm³.

Explanation:
The volume of a cylinder is the product of its radius squared (πr²) and height (h).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to square the radius or multiply by the height, resulting in answers A or B.

Problem 2 (Bghj):
Calculate the volume of a cone with radius 3 m and height 6 m.
A) 9π m³
B) 27π m³
C) 54π m³
D) 108π m³
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The volume of a cone is V = (1/3)πr²h. Given r = 3 m and h = 6 m, we substitute these values: V = (1/3)π(3)²(6) = π(9)(2) = 18π m³.

Explanation:
The volume of a cone is one-third the volume of a cylinder with the same base and height. Remember to multiply by (1/3).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget the factor (1/3), resulting in answer C or D.

Problem 3 (Qwer):
Find the volume of a rectangular prism with dimensions 4 cm x 6 cm x 8 cm.
A) 96 cm³
B) 128 cm³
C) 192 cm³
D) 256 cm³
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The volume of a rectangular prism is V = lwh. Given l = 4 cm, w = 6 cm, and h = 8 cm, we substitute these values: V = 4(6)(8) = 24(8) = 192 cm³.

Explanation:
The volume of a rectangular prism is the product of its length (l), width (w), and height (h).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to transpose or forget one of the dimensions, resulting in answers B, C, or D.

Problem 4 (Yuiop):
Calculate the volume of a sphere with radius 7 cm.
A) 153.94 cm³
B) 307.88 cm³
C) 461.82 cm³
D) 615.76 cm³
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The volume of a sphere is V = (4/3)πr³. Given r = 7 cm, we substitute this value: V = (4/3)π(7)³ = (4/3)π(343) ≈ 461.82 cm³.

Explanation:
The volume of a sphere is four-thirds the product of pi and the cube of its radius.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget the factor (4/3), resulting in answer D.

Problem 5 (Kolm):
Find the volume of a triangular prism with base area 12 cm², height 8 cm, and length 6 cm.
A) 48 cm³
B) 72 cm³
C) 96 cm³
D) 144 cm³
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The volume of a triangular prism is V = base area × height. Given base area = 12 cm² and height = 8 cm, we multiply these values: V = 12(8) = 96 cm³.

Explanation:
The volume of a triangular prism is the product of its base area and length.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to transpose or forget one of the dimensions, resulting in answers A, B, or D.

Problem 6 (Jkl;):
Calculate the volume of a frustrum of a cone with small radius 3 cm, large radius 6 cm, and height 5 cm.
A) 45π cm³
B) 90π cm³
C) 135π cm³
D) 180π cm³
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The volume of a frustrum of a cone is V = (1/3)πh(r₁² + r₂² + r₁r₂), where r₁ and r₂ are the radii of the small and large bases, respectively. Given r₁ = 3 cm, r₂ = 6 cm, and h = 5 cm, we substitute these values: V = (1/3)π(5)(9 + 36 + 18) = (1/3)π(5)(63) = 105π cm³.

Explanation:
The volume of a frustrum of a cone is the product of one-third the product of pi, height, and the sum of the square of the radii and the product of the two radii.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget the (1/3)π factor or to use the wrong formula for a regular cone, resulting in answers A, C, or D.

Problem 7 (Mnbvc):
Find the volume of a composite solid consisting of a cylinder with radius 4 cm and height 6 cm, surmounted by a hemisphere with radius 4 cm.
A) 201.06 cm³
B) 301.59 cm³
C) 402.12 cm³
D) 502.65 cm³
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The volume of the cylinder is Vcylinder = πr²h = π(4)²(6) = 96π cm³. The volume of the hemisphere is Vhemisphere = (2/3)πr³ = (2/3)π(4)³ = 128π/3 cm³. Adding both volumes, we get: Vtotal = 96π + 128π/3 ≈ 301.59 cm³.

Explanation:
The volume of a composite solid is the sum of the volumes of its individual components.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to divide the hemisphere's volume by 3 or to use the wrong radius, resulting in answers A, C, or D.
