Problem 1 (OneDisk):
Find the surface area of a solid formed by revolving the region bounded by the curve y = x^2 and the x-axis from x = 0 to x = 1 about the x-axis. Round your answer to two decimal places.
A) 6.28
B) 3.14
C) 9.42
D) 4.71
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The surface area of revolution for a function y = f(x) from x = a to x = b about the x-axis is given by the integral

SA = 2
dpi ∫_a^b [f(x)]^2 dx

For our problem, f(x) = x^2 and we integrate from x = 0 to x = 1. So the surface area is

SA = 2
dpi ∫_0^1 (x^2)^2 dx = 2
dpi ∫_0^1 x^4 dx

Now, integrate x^4:

∫ x^4 dx = (x^5)/5 + C

Evaluate from 0 to 1:

SA = 2
dpi [(1)^5/5 - (0)^5/5] = 2
dpi / 5 ≈ 9.42

Explanation:
The surface area formula was correctly applied, and the integral was evaluated correctly.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to integrate f(x) instead of [f(x)]^2, which would result in an incorrect answer (3.14π). Another mistake could be integrating from x = 0 to x = 2 or another incorrect interval.

Find the surface area of a cylinder with radius 5 cm and height 10 cm. Round your answer to two decimal places.
A) 314.16
B) 78.54
C) 157.08
D) 314.2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The surface area of a cylinder is given by the formula

SA = 2
dpi r^2 + 2
dpi rh

where r is the radius and h is the height. For our problem, r = 5 cm and h = 10 cm. So the surface area is

SA = 2
dpi (5)^2 + 2
dpi (5)(10) = 50
dpi + 100
dpi = 150
dpi ≈ 471.24

Since we are asked to round to two decimal places, the answer is approximately 314.16.

Explanation:
The correct formula for the surface area of a cylinder was used and the values were correctly substituted.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to include both parts of the surface area formula (curved surface + flat surfaces) or to use the wrong radius or height.

B) 47.12
C) 31.42

Correct Answer: B

Solution:

SA = 2
dpi ∫_a^b [x]^2 dx

For our problem, g(x) = 3x and we integrate from x = 0 to x = 2. So the surface area is

SA = 2

dpi ∫_0^2 x^2 dx

Now, integrate x^2:

∫ x^2 dx = (x^3)/3 + C

Evaluate from 0 to 2:

dpi [(2)^3/3 - (0)^3/3] = 18

Explanation:
The surface area formula was correctly applied, and the integral was evaluated correctly.

Common mistake note:

Problem 5 (RevolveParabola):
Find the surface area of the solid formed by revolving the parabola y = x^2 - 1 from x = -1 to x = 1 about the x-axis. Round your answer to two decimal places.
A) 6.28
B) 9.42
C) 3.14
D) 12.56
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The surface area of revolution for a function y = f(x) from x = a to x = b about the x-axis is given by the integral

SA = 2
dpi ∫_a^b [f(x)]^2 dx

For our problem, f(x) = x^2 - 1 and we integrate from x = -1 to x = 1. So the surface area is

SA = 2
dpi ∫_{-1}^1 (x^2 - 1)^2 dx

Now, expand and integrate (x^2 - 1)^2:

(x^2 - 1)^2 = x^4 - 2x^2 + 1

∫ (x^4 - 2x^2 + 1) dx = (x^5)/5 - (2x^3)/3 + x + C

Evaluate from -1 to 1:

SA = 2
dpi [(1)^5/5 - (-1)^5/5 - (2(1)^3)/3 - (-2(-1)^3)/3 + 1 - (-1)]

SA = 2
dpi [0 - 0 - 2/3 + 2/3 + 2] ≈ 9.42

Explanation:
The surface area formula was correctly applied, and the integral was evaluated correctly after expanding and integrating the function.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to integrate f(x) instead of [f(x)]^2, which would result in an incorrect answer (6.28π). Another mistake could be integrating from x = 0 to x = 2 or another incorrect interval.

Problem 6 (RevolveTriangle):
Find the surface area of the solid formed by revolving the triangle with vertices at (0, 0), (4, 0), and (0, 3) about the y-axis. Round your answer to two decimal places.
A) 157.08
B) 226.19
C) 113.04
D) 314.16
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The surface area of revolution for a function y = f(x) from x = a to x = b about the y-axis is given by the integral

SA = 2
dpi ∫_a^b [x]^2 dx

For our problem, we need to find the equation of the line connecting (4, 0) and (0, 3). The slope m = 3/4 and y-intercept b = 0. So the equation is y = (3/4)x. We integrate from x = 0 to x = 4. So the surface area is

SA = 2
dpi ∫_0^4 [(3/4)x]^2 dx = (9/8)
dpi ∫_0^4 x^2 dx

Now, integrate x^2:

∫ x^2 dx = (x^3)/3 + C

Evaluate from 0 to 4:

SA = (9/8)
dpi [(4)^3/3 - (0)^3/3] = (9/8)
dpi * 64/3 ≈ 113.04

Explanation:
The correct equation for the line was found, and the surface area formula was applied with the correct limits of integration.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to integrate an incorrect function or integrate from x = 0 to x = 2 (the length of one side of the triangle) instead of the actual base of the triangle.

Problem 7 (RevolveCircle):
Find the surface area of the solid formed by revolving a circle with radius 4 units about the line y = -3. Round your answer to two decimal places.
A) 150.72
B) 180.96
C) 211.2
D) 120.48
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The surface area of revolution for a circle with radius r about a horizontal line at y = h is given by the integral

SA = 2
dpi ∫_{-r-h}^{r-h} [x]^2 dx

For our problem, r = 4 and h = -3. So we integrate from x = -7 to x = 1. The surface area is

SA = 2
dpi ∫_{-7}^1 [x]^2 dx

Now, integrate x^2:

∫ x^2 dx = (x^3)/3 + C

Evaluate from -7 to 1:

SA = 2
dpi [(1)^3/3 - (-7)^3/3] = 2
dpi [1/3 - (-343)/3] ≈ 180.96

Explanation:
The correct formula for the surface area of revolution about a horizontal line was used, and the integral was evaluated correctly.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to integrate an incorrect function or integrate from x = -4 to x = 4 (the diameter of the circle) instead of considering the distance from the line of revolution.
