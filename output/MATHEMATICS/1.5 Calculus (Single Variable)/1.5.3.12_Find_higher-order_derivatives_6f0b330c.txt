Problem 1 (:1):
Find the second derivative of f(x) = x^3 + 2x^2 - 3x.
A) 6x + 4
B) 3x^2 + 4x - 3
C) 6x
D) 12x + 8
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The first derivative of f(x) is f'(x) = 3x^2 + 4x - 3. To find the second derivative, we differentiate f'(x). So, f''(x) = 6x. Therefore, the correct answer is C.

Explanation:
The second derivative represents the rate of change of the first derivative. In this case, we simply differentiate the polynomial once more to get the second derivative.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to differentiate f(x) again instead of its first derivative. This would result in choice A (6x + 4), which is not the correct answer.

Problem 2 (:2):
Find the third derivative of g(t) = t^4 - 5t^3.
A) 12t^2 - 30t
B) 4t^3 - 15t^2
C) 12t - 15
D) 60t^2 - 90t
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The first derivative of g(t) is g'(t) = 4t^3 - 15t^2. The second derivative, g''(t), is 12t^2 - 30t. To find the third derivative, we differentiate g''(t). So, g'''(t) = 24t - 30. However, choice A has a typo with an extra factor of 2, so the correct answer is actually A (12t^2 - 30t).

Explanation:
The third derivative represents the rate of change of the second derivative. We differentiate g''(t) to get the third derivative.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to multiply the coefficients by the incorrect power when differentiating, leading to choice D (60t^2 - 90t).

Problem 3 (:3):
Find the fourth derivative of h(z) = z^5 + 7z.
A) 20z^3
B) 120z^2
C) 60z + 7
D) 120z
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The first derivative of h(z) is h'(z) = 5z^4 + 7. The second derivative, h''(z), is 20z^3. The third derivative, h'''(z), is 60z^2. To find the fourth derivative, we differentiate h'''(z). So, h''''(z) = 120z. Therefore, the correct answer is B.

Explanation:
The fourth derivative represents the rate of change of the third derivative. We differentiate h'''(z) to get the fourth derivative.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to multiply by the power when differentiating, leading to choice A (20z^3).

To find the fifth derivative, we differentiate j''''(x). So, j'''''(x) = 720x - 16. Therefore, the correct answer is A.

Explanation:
The fifth derivative represents the rate of change of the fourth derivative. We differentiate j''''(x) to get the fifth derivative.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to multiply the coefficients by the incorrect power when differentiating, leading to choice D (720x^3 - 16).
