Problem 1 (Q1):
What is the capital of France?
A) London
B) Paris
C) Berlin
D) Rome
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The capital of France is Paris.

Explanation:
Paris is the capital city of France, known for its iconic landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower and Louvre Museum.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting London (the capital of England) or Berlin (the capital of Germany).

Problem 2 (Q2):
What is photosynthesis?
A) The process by which plants convert sunlight into energy
B) The movement of animals during seasonal changes
C) The breakdown of complex molecules into simpler ones
D) The growth and development of an organism from birth to maturity
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Photosynthesis is the process by which plants, algae, and some bacteria convert sunlight, carbon dioxide, and water into glucose and oxygen.

Explanation:
During photosynthesis, chlorophyll in plant cells captures light energy, which is then used to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen. This process is essential for the survival of plants and plays a critical role in Earth's atmosphere.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting processes like migration (B) or cellular respiration (C), which are not related to plant energy production.

Problem 3 (Q3):
What is the boiling point of water in degrees Celsius?
A) 100
B) 212
C) -273.15
D) 0
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The boiling point of water at standard atmospheric pressure is 100 degrees Celsius or 212 degrees Fahrenheit.

Explanation:
Water boils when its temperature reaches 100°C (212°F) under normal atmospheric pressure. This is the temperature at which the vapor pressure of water equals the surrounding air pressure, causing bubbles to form within the liquid and rise to the surface.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting the boiling point in Fahrenheit (B), the freezing point of water in Celsius (D), or absolute zero (C).

Problem 4 (Q4):
What is the smallest planet in our solar system?
A) Mercury
B) Earth
C) Mars
D) Venus
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The smallest planet in our solar system, both in terms of diameter and mass, is Mercury.

Explanation:
Mercury is the innermost planet and has a diameter of about 4,880 kilometers (3,032 miles), making it smaller than Earth, Mars, and Venus. Despite its small size, Mercury has a relatively large iron core which contributes to its high density.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting Earth (B) or Venus (D), both of which are larger in diameter.

Problem 5 (Q5):
What is the primary function of white blood cells?
A) To transport oxygen throughout the body
B) To fight infections and remove foreign substances
C) To clot blood and prevent excessive bleeding
D) To produce hormones and regulate body functions
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The primary function of white blood cells, also known as leukocytes, is to defend the body against infections and remove foreign substances.

Explanation:
White blood cells are an essential part of the immune system. They can identify and destroy bacteria, viruses, and other pathogens, helping to protect the body from disease. Red blood cells (A) transport oxygen, platelets (C) help with blood clotting, and endocrine glands (D) produce hormones.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting functions related to red blood cells or platelets.

Problem 6 (Q6):
What is the chemical formula for water?
A) H2O
B) CO2
C) NaCl
D) CH4
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The chemical formula for water is H2O.

Explanation:
Water is composed of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom, forming a molecule with the chemical formula H2O. This simple molecule plays a crucial role in many biological processes and is essential for life on Earth.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting formulas for other compounds like carbon dioxide (B), table salt (C), or methane (D).

Problem 7 (Q7):
What is the process by which a caterpillar becomes a butterfly?
A) Metamorphosis
B) Hibernation
C) Molting
D) Photosynthesis
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The process by which a caterpillar becomes a butterfly is called metamorphosis.

Explanation:
Metamorphosis is a biological transformation that occurs in some insects, including butterflies and moths. Caterpillars undergo a complete change in form, going through stages such as larva, pupa (chrysalis), and adult to become fully grown butterflies.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include selecting processes like hibernation (B) or molting (C), which are not specific to butterfly development.
