Problem 1 (Asdf):
Find the derivative of f(x) = x^3 - 4x^2 + 3x at x=2.
A) 16
B) 8
C) 20
D) 12
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
f'(x) = 3x^2 - 8x + 3. To find the derivative at x=2, substitute x with 2: f'(2) = 3(2)^2 - 8(2) + 3 = 12 - 16 + 3 = -1. The correct answer is not among the choices provided.

Explanation:
The question asks for the derivative at a specific point, but there's an error in the solution: f'(2) should be calculated as -1. This mistake demonstrates a lack of attention to detail when substituting values into the derivative.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Incorrectly calculating the derivative or substituting values.

Problem 2 (Bghj):
Find the derivative of g(x) = 5x^4 - 2x^3 + x at x=1.
A) 20
B) 16
C) 24
D) 15
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
g'(x) = 20x^3 - 6x^2 + 1. To find the derivative at x=1, substitute x with 1: g'(1) = 20(1)^3 - 6(1)^2 + 1 = 20 - 6 + 1 = 15. The correct answer is D (15), which was incorrectly labeled as 18 in the choices.

Explanation:
The question asks for the derivative at a specific point, but there's a typo in one of the answer choices: 18 instead of 15. This mistake tests students' ability to identify and correct errors in provided information.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Selecting the incorrect choice due to a typo.

Problem 3 (Qwer):
Find the derivative of h(x) = x^2 sin(x) at x=π/2.
A) π
B) 0
C) π^2
D) 1
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
h'(x) = 2xsin(x) + x^2cos(x). To find the derivative at x=π/2, substitute x with π/2: h'(π/2) = 2(π/2)sin(π/2) + (π/2)^2cos(π/2) = π. The correct answer is A.

Explanation:
The question asks for the derivative of a product at a specific point, testing students' understanding of the product rule and evaluating trigonometric functions at special angles.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Forgetting to apply the product rule or misapplying it.

Problem 4 (Yuiop):
Find the derivative of j(x) = e^x cos(x) at x=0.
A) 1
B) -1
C) 2
D) 0
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
j'(x) = e^x cos(x) - e^x sin(x). To find the derivative at x=0, substitute x with 0: j'(0) = e^(0) cos(0) - e^(0) sin(0) = 1. The correct answer is A.

Explanation:
The question asks for the derivative of a product at a specific point, testing students' understanding of the product rule and evaluating exponential and trigonometric functions at special angles.

Problem 5 (Bnm):
Find the derivative of k(x) = ln(x^2 + 1) at x=1.
A) 2
B) 1
C) 0
D) 2/3
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
k'(x) = (2x)/(x^2 + 1). To find the derivative at x=1, substitute x with 1: k'(1) = (2*1)/(1^2 + 1) = 1/2. The correct answer is B.

Explanation:
The question asks for the derivative of a logarithmic function at a specific point, testing students' understanding of the chain rule and evaluating logarithms.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Forgetting to apply the chain rule or misapplying it.

Problem 6 (Vbnm):
Find the derivative of l(x) = x^(1/3) at x=8.
A) 2/3
B) 1/4
C) 1/6
D) 1/9
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
l'(x) = (1/3)x^(-2/3). To find the derivative at x=8, substitute x with 8: l'(8) = (1/3)(8)^(-2/3) = 2/3. The correct answer is A.

Explanation:
The question asks for the derivative of a power function at a specific point, testing students' understanding of the power rule and evaluating fractional exponents.

Common mistake note:
