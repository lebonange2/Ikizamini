Problem 1 (Q1):
What is the difference between (x + 3) and (x - 3)?
A) 6
B) x^2 - 9
C) 2x
D) x^2 + 6x + 9
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The difference between (x + 3) and (x - 3) is found by subtracting the second expression from the first: (x + 3) - (x - 3) = x + 3 - x + 3 = 6.

Explanation:
This question tests understanding of basic arithmetic subtraction applied to binomials.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to try factoring the expressions, which is not necessary for this problem.

Problem 2 (Q2):
What is the sum of (2x - 5) and (3x + 7)?
A) 5x + 12
B) x^2 + 2x - 12
C) 5x - 2
D) 5x + 2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The sum of (2x - 5) and (3x + 7) is found by adding the coefficients of x and constant terms: (2x + 3x) + (-5 + 7) = 5x + 12.

Explanation:
This question tests understanding of combining like terms in binomials.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to try factoring the expressions, which is not necessary for this problem.

Problem 3 (Q3):
What is the difference between (4y^2 + 6y) and (4y^2 - 6y)?
A) 12y
B) 8y^3
C) 0
D) 12y^2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The difference between (4y^2 + 6y) and (4y^2 - 6y) is found by subtracting the second expression from the first: (4y^2 + 6y) - (4y^2 - 6y) = 4y^2 + 6y - 4y^2 + 6y = 12y.

Explanation:
This question tests understanding of difference of squares and combining like terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to think that the two 4y^2 terms cancel out, but they do not because they have different coefficients.

Problem 4 (Q4):
What is the sum of (x + 1) and (3x - 2)?
A) 4x - 1
B) x^2 + 4x - 1
C) 4x + 1
D) 4x - 3
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The sum of (x + 1) and (3x - 2) is found by adding the coefficients of x and constant terms: (x + 3x) + (1 - 2) = 4x - 1.

Explanation:
This question tests understanding of combining like terms in binomials with simple constants.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to subtract the constant term when adding.

Problem 5 (Q5):
What is the difference between (5a^2 - 3b) and (5a^2 + 3b)?
A) 6b
B) 10a^2
C) 0
D) -6ab
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The difference between (5a^2 - 3b) and (5a^2 + 3b) is found by subtracting the second expression from the first: (5a^2 - 3b) - (5a^2 + 3b) = 5a^2 - 3b - 5a^2 - 3b = -6b.

Explanation:
This question tests understanding of difference of terms with different variables and combining like terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to think that the two 5a^2 terms cancel out, but they do not because they have different signs.

Problem 6 (Q6):
What is the sum of (6m - 4n) and (6m + 4n)?
A) 12m
B) 12m - 8n^2
C) 0
D) 12m + 8n
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The sum of (6m - 4n) and (6m + 4n) is found by adding the coefficients of m and n: (6m + 6m) + (-4n + 4n) = 12m.

Explanation:
This question tests understanding of combining like terms in binomials with different variables.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to think that the two -4n and 4n terms cancel out, but they do not because they have different signs.

Explanation:
This question tests understanding of difference of terms with different variables and combining like terms.

Common mistake note:
