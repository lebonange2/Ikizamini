Problem 1 (Asd123):
A company produces and sells widgets at a cost of $5 per widget. If they sell each widget for $8, what is their profit if they sell 100 widgets?
A) $250
B) $300
C) $400
D) $500
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Profit = Revenue - Cost. Total cost = 100 widgets * $5/widget = $500. Total revenue = 100 widgets * $8/widget = $800. Profit = $800 - $500 = $300.

Explanation:
Profit is the difference between total revenue and total cost. In this case, the company makes a profit of $300 on 100 widgets sold.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to multiply the profit per widget ($8 - $5 = $3) by the number of widgets (100), which would result in $300 instead of calculating total revenue and subtracting total cost.

Problem 2 (Xyz456):
A bakery sells cupcakes for $2 each. If the bakery spends $0.50 on ingredients to make each cupcake, how many cupcakes must they sell to break even?
A) 10 cupcakes
B) 20 cupcakes
C) 30 cupcakes
D) 40 cupcakes
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To break even, revenue equals cost. Profit per cupcake = Selling price - Cost per cupcake = $2 - $0.50 = $1.50. Total cost to break even = Ingredient cost per cupcake * x cupcakes, where x is the number of cupcakes needed. x = Total cost / Profit per cupcake = $0.50 / $1.50 = 20 cupcakes.

Explanation:
Breaking even means that revenue equals costs. The bakery needs to sell enough cupcakes to cover their ingredient costs of $0.50 per cupcake.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to divide the total cost by the selling price instead of calculating the profit per cupcake and then dividing by that amount.

Problem 3 (Qwe789):
A clothing store has a sale where they sell all items at a 20% discount. If an item originally costs $60, what is the revenue from selling 5 of these items during the sale?
A) $240
B) $300
C) $360
D) $480
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Revenue = Number of items sold * Discounted price per item. Discounted price = Original price - (Discount percentage * Original price) = $60 - (20% of $60) = $48. Total revenue = 5 items * $48/item = $240.

Explanation:
Revenue is calculated by multiplying the number of items sold by their discounted price. In this case, the store sells 5 items at a 20% discount off the original price of $60.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to apply the discount directly to the total cost instead of calculating the discounted price per item and then multiplying by the number of items sold.

Problem 4 (Rty101):
An electronics store sells a television for $800 with a 15% markup on its cost. If the store offers an additional 10% discount during a sale, what is the final selling price?
A) $640
B) $720
C) $760
D) $800
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
First, calculate the cost of the television: Cost = Marked-up price / (1 + Markup percentage) = $800 / 1.15 ≈ $695.71. Next, apply the additional discount: Final selling price = Cost + (Original markup * Cost) - (Discount percentage * Original marked-up price) = $695.71 + ($800 - $695.71) - (10% of $800) ≈ $760.

Explanation:
The final selling price is calculated by applying the initial markup to the cost and then offering an additional discount on the marked-up price.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to apply both discounts consecutively without first calculating the marked-up price, which would result in a lower final selling price than the correct answer.

Problem 5 (Uij112):
A florist sells bouquets for $30 each. The cost of flowers and other materials to make each bouquet is $15. If the florist sells 75 bouquets, what is their total profit?
A) $900
B) $1,200
C) $1,800
D) $2,250
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Profit = Total revenue - Total cost. Total cost = Number of bouquets * Cost per bouquet = 75 bouquets * $15/bouquet = $1,125. Total revenue = Number of bouquets * Selling price per bouquet = 75 bouquets * $30/bouquet = $2,250. Profit = $2,250 - $1,125 = $900.

Explanation:
Profit is calculated by subtracting the total cost from the total revenue. In this case, the florist makes a profit of $900 on 75 bouquets sold.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to multiply the profit per bouquet ($30 - $15 = $15) by the number of bouquets (75), which would result in $1,125 instead of calculating total revenue and subtracting total cost.

Problem 6 (Kol113):
A furniture store buys a table for $200 and sells it with a 40% markup. If the customer uses a 10% off coupon, what is the final selling price?
A) $280
B) $294
C) $300
D) $320
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
First, calculate the marked-up price: Marked-up price = Cost + (Markup percentage * Cost) = $200 + 0.40 * $200 = $280. Next, apply the discount: Final selling price = Marked-up price - (Discount percentage * Marked-up price) = $280 - 0.10 * $280 = $252.

Explanation:
The final selling price is calculated by applying the initial markup to the cost and then offering a discount on the marked-up price.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to apply both the markup and the discount directly to the original cost, which would result in a different final selling price than the correct answer.

Problem 7 (Mno114):
An online retailer sells a book for $25 with a 30% profit margin. If they decide to lower the price by 20%, what is the new selling price?
A) $20
B) $24
C) $28
D) $30
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
First, calculate the cost of the book: Cost = Selling price / (1 + Profit margin percentage) = $25 / 1.3 ≈ $19.23. Next, lower the price: New selling price = Original selling price - (Discount percentage * Original selling price) = $25 - 0.20 * $25 = $20. Then, add the original profit margin to get the new selling price: New selling price + (Original profit margin * Cost) ≈ $28.

Explanation:
The new selling price is calculated by finding the cost of the book based on the given profit margin and then reducing the original price by 20%.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to apply the 20% discount directly to the original selling price without first calculating the cost, which would result in a lower new selling price than the correct answer.
