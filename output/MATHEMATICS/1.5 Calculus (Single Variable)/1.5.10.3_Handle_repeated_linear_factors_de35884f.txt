Problem 1 (:Q1):
Factor the expression: x^2 + 6x + 9
A) (x + 3)^2
B) x(x + 3)
C) (x + 9)(x + 1)
D) (x + 4)(x + 5)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The expression is a perfect square trinomial, which can be factored as (x + 3)^2.

Explanation:
Perfect square trinomials have the form x^2 + 2ax + a^2, where a is the square root of the constant term. In this case, a = 3.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to factor it as x(x + 6), which is incorrect because the middle term should be twice the product of the square root and x.

Problem 2 (:Q2):
Factor the expression: 12x^3 - 8x^2
A) 4x^2(3x - 2)
B) 2x(6x^2 - 4)
C) 4x(x + 2)(x - 1)
D) 2x^2(6x - 4)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The greatest common factor of the terms is 4x^2, which can be factored out.

Explanation:
When factoring by GCF, we look for the largest number that divides both coefficients and can also be written as a product of the variables. In this case, 4x^2 works.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to factor it as 2x(6x^2 - 4), which is incorrect because the GCF should be factored out completely.

Problem 4 (:Q4):
Factor the expression: 9a^3b - 6ab^2
A) 3ab(3a^2 - 2b)
B) 3ab^2(3a - 2)
C) 3ab(a + b)(a - b)
D) 3ab(3a - 2b)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The greatest common factor of the terms is 3ab, and the remaining factors are (3a) and (-2b), which form a difference of squares.

Explanation:
When factoring by GCF, we look for the largest number that divides both coefficients and can also be written as a product of the variables. In this case, 3ab works, and the remaining terms form a difference of squares (3a - 2b).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to factor it as 3ab^2(3a - 2), which is incorrect because the GCF should be factored out completely.

Problem 5 (:Q5):
Factor the expression: x^4 - 16
A) (x + 4)(x - 4)
B) $(x + 2)^2(x - 2)^2$
C) $(x + 2)(x - 2)$
D) $(x + 4)^2(x - 4)^2$
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The expression is a difference of squares squared, which can be factored as (x^2 - 4)^2 = (x + 2)^2(x - 2)^2.

Explanation:
A difference of squares has the form a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b). In this case, we have x^2 - 16, which is a difference of squares with a = x and b = 4. Squaring both sides gives us (x + 2)^2(x - 2)^2.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to factor it as (x + 4)(x - 4), which is incorrect because the expression is actually a difference of squares squared.

Common mistake note:

Problem 7 (:Q7):
Factor the expression: 25a^2b^2 - c^2d^2
A) $(5ab + cd)(5ab - cd)$
B) $(5ab + cd)^2(5ab - cd)^2$
C) $(5ab - cd)^2$

Solution:

Explanation:
A difference of squares has the form a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b). In this case, we have (5ab)^2 - (cd)^2, which is a difference of squares with a = 5ab and b = cd.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to factor it as (5ab - cd)^2, which is incorrect because the expression is actually a simple difference of squares.
