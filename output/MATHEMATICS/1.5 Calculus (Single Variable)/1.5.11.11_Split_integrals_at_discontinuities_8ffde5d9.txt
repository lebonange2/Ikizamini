Problem 1 (Q1):
What is the capital of France?
A) London
B) Paris
C) Berlin
D) Rome
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The capital of France is Paris.

Explanation:
Paris is the capital city of France, known for its iconic landmarks like the Eiffel Tower and Louvre Museum.

Common mistake note:
Some may mistakenly choose London as it's the capital of England.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Q2):
What is photosynthesis?
A) The process by which plants produce energy from sunlight
B) The process by which animals digest food
C) The movement of animals in response to temperature changes
D) The reproduction of plants through seeds
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Photosynthesis is the process by which plants convert sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide into glucose and oxygen.

Explanation:
Plants use photosynthesis to produce energy and release oxygen as a byproduct. It's essential for their growth and survival.

Common mistake note:
Students might confuse it with other biological processes like respiration or reproduction.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Q3):
What is the smallest prime number?
A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 4
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The smallest prime number is 2.

Explanation:
A prime number is a positive integer greater than 1 that has no divisors other than 1 and itself. 2 is the only even prime number.

Common mistake note:
Students might think 1 or 4 are prime numbers, but they aren't according to the definition.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Q4):
What is the boiling point of water in degrees Celsius?
A) 100
B) 212
C) 373.15
D) 0
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The boiling point of water at standard atmospheric pressure is 100°C.

Explanation:
Water boils when its temperature reaches 100°C, turning into steam. This is a common reference point for temperature measurements.

Common mistake note:
Students might confuse it with the freezing point (0°C) or the boiling point in Fahrenheit (212°F).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (Q5):
What is the largest organ in the human body?
A) Brain
B) Liver
C) Skin
D) Heart
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The largest organ in the human body is the skin.

Explanation:
The skin covers and protects our bodies, with a total surface area of about 1.5 to 2 square meters on average.

Common mistake note:
Students might think of organs like the brain or liver as larger, but they are not in terms of surface area.

Problem 6 (Q6):
What is the chemical formula for water?
A) H2O
B) CO2
C) NaCl
D) CH4
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The chemical formula for water is H2O.

Explanation:
Water consists of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom, forming the molecule H2O. It's a fundamental compound in many biological processes.

Common mistake note:
Students might confuse it with other compounds like carbon dioxide (CO2) or methane (CH4).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Q7):
What is the process by which cells divide and produce identical daughter cells?
A) Photosynthesis
B) Cellular respiration
C) Mitosis
D) Fermentation
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The process by which cells divide and produce identical daughter cells is called mitosis.

Explanation:
Mitosis is a crucial part of growth, repair, and asexual reproduction in organisms. It results in two genetically identical daughter cells from one parent cell.

Common mistake note:
Students might confuse it with other cellular processes like meiosis or fermentation.
