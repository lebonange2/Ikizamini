Problem 1 (Asdf1):
Reflect the point (3, 4) across the x-axis. What are the coordinates of the reflected point?
A) (3, -4)
B) (4, 3)
C) (-3, 4)
D) (-3, -4)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
When reflecting a point across the x-axis, only the y-coordinate changes its sign. So, the reflected coordinates are (3, -4).

Explanation:
Reflecting across the x-axis means keeping the x-coordinate unchanged and changing the sign of the y-coordinate.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to change the sign of the y-coordinate.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Asdf2):
Reflect the line segment connecting points (1, 2) and (5, 6) across the x-axis. What are the coordinates of the endpoints of the reflected line segment?
A) (1, -2), (5, -6)
B) (-1, 2), (-5, 6)
C) (1, 2), (5, 6)
D) (-1, -2), (-5, -6)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Reflecting a line segment across the x-axis means reflecting each point on the segment. The endpoints become (1, -2) and (5, -6).

Explanation:
Each point on the original line segment is reflected by changing only the sign of the y-coordinate.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that both endpoints must be reflected.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Asdf3):
Reflect the triangle with vertices (2, 1), (4, 3), and (6, 5) across the x-axis. What are the coordinates of the reflected vertex (6, 5)?
A) (6, -5)
B) (-6, 5)
C) (-6, -5)
D) (2, 1)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Reflecting a vertex across the x-axis means changing only the sign of its y-coordinate. The reflected point is (6, -5).

Explanation:
The x-coordinate remains unchanged while the y-coordinate changes sign.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to mix up the x- and y-coordinates.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Asdf4):
Reflect the parabola defined by the equation y = x^2 across the x-axis. What is the equation of the reflected parabola?
A) y = -x^2
B) y = x^2
C) y = -(x + 1)^2
D) y = (x - 1)^2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Reflecting a parabola across the x-axis changes the sign of the y-value. The new equation is y = -x^2.

Explanation:
The reflection affects only the function part of the equation, not the independent variable x.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that the entire function changes sign.

Problem 5 (Asdf5):
Reflect the circle with center (3, 4) and radius 2 across the x-axis. What are the coordinates of the center of the reflected circle?
A) (3, -4)
B) (4, 3)
C) (-3, 4)
D) (-3, -4)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Reflecting a circle across the x-axis changes only the sign of its y-coordinate. The center becomes (3, -4).

Explanation:
The reflection affects only the center coordinates, not the radius.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that only the center's y-coordinate changes.

Problem 6 (Asdf6):
Reflect the rectangle with vertices (1, 2), (3, 2), (3, 4), and (1, 4) across the x-axis. Which of the following best describes the relationship between the original and reflected rectangles?
A) They are congruent.
B) They are similar but not congruent.
C) They are identical.
D) They are neither similar nor congruent.
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Reflecting a rectangle across the x-axis produces a new rectangle that is congruent to the original one, as it maintains the same shape and size with flipped orientation.

Explanation:
Reflection preserves shape and size but may change orientation.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to think that reflection changes the size or shape of the figure.

Problem 7 (Asdf7):
Reflect the function f(x) = 2x + 3 across the x-axis. What is the equation of the reflected function?
A) f(x) = -2x - 3
B) f(x) = 2x + 3
C) f(x) = -(2x + 1)
D) f(x) = (2x - 1)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Reflecting a linear function across the x-axis changes the sign of the coefficient and constant term. The new equation is f(x) = -2x - 3.

Explanation:
Reflection affects both the slope and the y-intercept of the line.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that both the slope and the y-intercept change sign.
