Problem 1 (:1):
Plot the point (3, 4) on the Cartesian plane.
A) (4, 3)
B) (3, -4)
C) (-3, 4)
D) (3, 4)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The point (3, 4) has an x-coordinate of 3 and a y-coordinate of 4. The correct plot is at the intersection of the line representing x = 3 and the line representing y = 4.

Explanation:
In Cartesian coordinates, the first number represents the x-coordinate and the second number represents the y-coordinate.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is reversing the order of the coordinates. Remember that (x, y) means 'x goes first, then y'.

Problem 2 (:2):
Which point lies on the x-axis?
A) (0, 5)
B) (5, 0)
C) (5, 5)
D) (-3, 0)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
A point lies on the x-axis if its y-coordinate is zero. In this case, (5, 0) has a y-coordinate of 0.

Explanation:
The x-axis is where all points have a y-value of 0.

Common mistake note:
Be cautious not to choose a point with a non-zero y-coordinate, like (0, 5), which lies on the y-axis.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (:3):
What is the y-coordinate of the point located 2 units to the right and 3 units up from the origin?
A) -3
B) 1
C) 3
D) 5
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Starting at the origin (0, 0), moving 2 units to the right increases the x-coordinate by 2 and moving 3 units up increases the y-coordinate by 3. So, the point is at (2, 3). The y-coordinate is 3.

Explanation:
When moving from the origin, positive changes in the x-direction correspond to an increase in the x-coordinate, and positive changes in the y-direction correspond to an increase in the y-coordinate.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to add or subtract the distances incorrectly. Remember that each direction is independent.

Problem 4 (:4):
Plot the point (-2, -3) on the Cartesian plane.
A) (2, 3)
B) (2, -3)
C) (-2, 3)
D) (-2, -3)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The point (-2, -3) has an x-coordinate of -2 and a y-coordinate of -3. The correct plot is at the intersection of the line representing x = -2 and the line representing y = -3.

Explanation:
In Cartesian coordinates, negative numbers represent positions to the left on the x-axis and below the origin on the y-axis.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is reversing the signs of the coordinates. Remember that (-x, -y) means 'negative x goes first, then negative y'.

Problem 6 (:6):
What is the x-coordinate of a point that is 4 units above and 5 units to the right of the point (1, -2)?
A) -3
B) 0
C) 4
D) 6
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Starting at (1, -2), moving 5 units to the right increases the x-coordinate by 5 and moving 4 units up increases the y-coordinate by 4. So, the new point is at (6, 2). The x-coordinate is 6.

Explanation:
When moving from a given point, positive changes in the x-direction correspond to an increase in the x-coordinate, and positive changes in the y-direction correspond to an increase in the y-coordinate.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to add or subtract the distances incorrectly. Remember that each direction is independent.

Problem 7 (:7):
Plot the point (1/2, 3/4) on the Cartesian plane.
A) (2/3, 1/4)
B) (1/2, 3/4)
C) (3/4, 1/2)
D) (1, 1)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The point (1/2, 3/4) has an x-coordinate of 1/2 and a y-coordinate of 3/4. The correct plot is at the intersection of the line representing x = 1/2 and the line representing y = 3/4.

Explanation:
In Cartesian coordinates, fractions can be used to represent points as well. Just like whole numbers, the first fraction represents the x-coordinate and the second fraction represents the y-coordinate.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is simplifying or converting the fractions incorrectly. Remember to plot the exact coordinates given.
