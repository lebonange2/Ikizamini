Problem 1 (Asdf):
What is the approximate perimeter of a rectangle with length 12 cm and width 7 cm?
A) 19 cm
B) 25 cm
C) 30 cm
D) 36 cm
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The formula for the perimeter of a rectangle is P = 2l + 2w, where l is the length and w is the width. Plugging in the values given: P = 2(12) + 2(7) = 24 + 14 = 38 cm. The closest approximation to 38 cm among the choices is 25 cm.

Explanation:
To approximate, you can round the numbers before calculating: 12 cm to 10 cm and 7 cm to 10 cm. Then P ≈ 2(10) + 2(10) = 40 cm. Since 30 cm is too small and 36 cm is too large, 25 cm is the best approximation.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to multiply by 2 for each side or to round numbers incorrectly.

Problem 2 (Asdf):
What is the approximate perimeter of a triangle with sides 9 m, 13 m, and 7 m?
A) 20 m
B) 30 m
C) 40 m
D) 50 m
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The formula for the perimeter of a triangle is P = a + b + c, where a, b, and c are the lengths of the sides. Plugging in the values given: P = 9 + 13 + 7 = 29 m. The closest approximation to 29 m among the choices is 40 m.

Explanation:
To approximate, you can round the numbers before calculating: 9 m to 10 m, 13 m to 10 m, and 7 m to 5 m. Then P ≈ 10 + 10 + 5 = 25 m. Since 20 m is too small and 50 m is too large, 40 m is the best approximation.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to round numbers incorrectly or to add them without considering the correct order of addition.
