Problem 1 (?):
Convert the quadratic equation to general form: y = 2(x - 3)^2 + 5.
A) y = 2x^2 - 12x + 23
B) y = 2x^2 - 6x + 7
C) y = 2x^2 - 12x + 35
D) y = 2x^2 + 6x + 17
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To convert the given equation to general form, we expand and simplify. The general form is y = ax^2 + bx + c.

Explanation:
y = 2( x - 3 )^2 + 5
y = 2( x^2 - 6x + 9 ) + 5
y = 2x^2 - 12x + 18 + 5
y = 2x^2 - 12x + 23

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is forgetting to distribute the 2 to all terms inside the parentheses in the expansion.

Problem 2 (?):
Convert the quadratic equation to general form: y = -(x + 4)(x - 1).
A) y = -x^2 - 3x - 4
B) y = -x^2 - 5x - 4
C) y = -x^2 - 3x + 4
D) y = -x^2 + 5x - 4
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To convert the given equation to general form, we multiply the binomials.

Explanation:
y = -( x + 4 )( x - 1 )
y = -( x^2 - x + 4x - 4 )
y = -( x^2 + 3x - 4 )
y = -x^2 - 3x + 4

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is not distributing the negative sign correctly when multiplying.

Problem 3 (?):
Convert the quadratic equation to general form: y = (x - 5)(x + 7).
A) y = x^2 + 12x - 35
B) y = x^2 - 2x - 35
C) y = x^2 + 2x - 35
D) y = x^2 - 12x - 35
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To convert the given equation to general form, we multiply the binomials.

Explanation:
y = ( x - 5 )( x + 7 )
y = x( x + 7 ) - 5( x + 7 )
y = x^2 + 7x - 5x - 35
y = x^2 + 2x - 35

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is not distributing one of the binomials correctly.

Problem 4 (?):
Convert the quadratic equation to general form: y = 3(x - 1/2)^2 - 2.
A) y = 3x^2 - 3x + 7/4
B) y = 3x^2 - 3x - 5/4
C) y = 3x^2 - 3x - 11/4
D) y = 3x^2 - 3x - 9/4
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To convert the given equation to general form, we expand and simplify.

Explanation:
y = 3( x - 1/2 )^2 - 2
y = 3( x^2 - x + 1/4 ) - 2
y = 3x^2 - 3x + 3/4 - 2
y = 3x^2 - 3x - 5/4

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is not correctly squaring the binomial, especially when dealing with fractions.

Problem 5 (?):
Convert the quadratic equation to general form: y = 4(x + 1/2)^2 + 3.
A) y = 4x^2 + 4x + 7/4
B) y = 4x^2 + 4x - 5/4
C) y = 4x^2 + 4x + 11/4
D) y = 4x^2 + 4x + 9/4
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
To convert the given equation to general form, we expand and simplify.

Explanation:
y = 4( x + 1/2 )^2 + 3
y = 4( x^2 + x + 1/4 ) + 3
y = 4x^2 + 4x + 1 - 3
y = 4x^2 + 4x + 9/4

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is not correctly squaring the binomial, especially when dealing with fractions.

Problem 6 (?):
Convert the quadratic equation to general form: y = -2(x - 3)(x + 2).
A) y = -2x^2 + 4x - 6
B) y = -2x^2 + 4x + 14
C) y = -2x^2 - 10x + 12
D) y = -2x^2 - 10x - 12
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To convert the given equation to general form, we multiply the binomials and distribute the negative sign.

Explanation:
y = -2( x - 3 )( x + 2 )
y = -2( x^2 + 2x - 3x - 6 )
y = -2( x^2 - x - 6 )
y = -2x^2 + 2x + 12
y = -2x^2 - 10x + 12

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is not distributing the negative sign correctly when multiplying.

Problem 7 (?):
Convert the quadratic equation to general form: y = (2x - 3)(x + 4).
A) y = 2x^2 + 5x - 12
B) y = 2x^2 + 5x - 8
C) y = 2x^2 + 5x + 8
D) y = 2x^2 + 5x + 12
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
To convert the given equation to general form, we multiply the binomials.

Explanation:
y = ( 2x - 3 )( x + 4 )
y = 2x( x + 4 ) - 3( x + 4 )
y = 2x^2 + 8x - 3x - 12
y = 2x^2 + 5x + 8

Common mistake note:
