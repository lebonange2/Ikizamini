Problem 1 (x1):
Given three points A(1, 2), B(3, 4), and C(5, 6), are they collinear?
A) Yes
B) No
C) Cannot determine without additional information
D) Depends on the coordinate system
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To check collinearity, compare slopes between A and B: (4-2)/(3-1) = 1, and B and C: (6-4)/(5-3) = 1. Since both slopes are equal, the points are collinear.

Explanation:
Collinearity means all points lie on a single straight line. If any two pairs of points have the same slope, they're collinear.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is assuming that if x- or y-coordinates increase at the same rate, the points are collinear; it's slopes that matter.

Problem 2 (x2):
Points D(0, 0), E(4, 8), and F(8, 16) form a straight line. Which best explains this?
A) The x-coordinates increase by 4 for each point.
B) The y-coordinates double for each point.
C) Both A and B
D) None of the above
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Both statements describe a linear relationship: x increases by 4, and y doubles (also a 4x increase). This confirms collinearity.

Explanation:
Linear relationships between coordinates indicate collinearity. Both A and B describe the same progression, confirming the points are on a line.

Common mistake note:
A common error is to choose D if not recognizing that both statements describe the same linear relationship.

Problem 3 (x3):
Points G(-2, 1), H(0, 3), and I(2, 5) are collinear. Which point can be removed without affecting collinearity?
A) G
B) H
C) I
D) All three points are necessary for collinearity
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Removing G doesn't affect collinearity as the slope between H and I remains 1 (5-3)/(2-0), same as G to H: (3-1)/(0+2).

Explanation:
Collinearity depends on slopes. If removing a point maintains slopes, it不影响collinearity.

Common mistake note:
A common error is thinking that removing any point disrupts collinearity; if slopes stay the same, removal doesn't affect it.

Problem 4 (x4):
Points J(1, 0), K(2, 2), and L(3, 4) lie on a straight line. Which point, when added, maintains collinearity?
A) (0, 0)
B) (4, 6)
C) (5, 8)
D) (6, 10)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Adding M(6, 10) maintains collinearity as the slope between J and K is 2, and between K and M is 8/1 (also 8), maintaining a ratio.

Explanation:
New points must follow existing slope relationships. Here, J to K's slope is twice that of K to L; M preserves this relationship.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be choosing C, thinking an equal slope maintains collinearity. It's the ratio that matters.

Problem 5 (x5):
Points N(1, 2), O(3, 4), and P(q, r) are collinear if which condition is met?
A) q = 2r
B) r = 2q
C) q + r = 6
D) q - r = 0
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
For collinearity, (4-2)/(3-1) = (r-2)/(q-3), simplifying to q = 2r - 1. A is closest as it represents a linear relationship.

Explanation:
Slope equality gives the condition: q = 2r - 1. While not exact, A best represents this linear relationship.

Common mistake note:
A common error would be to choose C or D based on coordinate sums; it's slopes that matter.

Problem 6 (x6):
Points Q(a, b), R(c, d), and S(e, f) are collinear if which condition is met?
A) a = c = e
B) b = d = f
C) ad = be
D) bc = ef
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Setting slopes equal gives ad - bc = be - cd, simplifying to ad = be. Thus, C is the correct condition.

Explanation:
Equating slopes leads to an algebraic relationship: ad = be. Individual coordinate values don't matter; it's their products that do.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be choosing B if thinking equal y-coordinates imply collinearity. It's the slope relationship that counts.

Problem 7 (x7):
Points T(x1, y1), U(x2, y2), and V(x3, y3) are collinear if the area of their triangle is:
A) 0
B) 1
C) Depends on coordinates
D) Cannot determine without additional information
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Collinearity means points lie on a line, forming a zero-area triangle. So, the area is 0.

Explanation:
Zero area indicates collinearity; points don't form a closed shape with non-zero sides.

Common mistake note:
A common error would be to choose C or D if not realizing that collinearity implies a zero-area triangle.
