Problem 1 (:1):
Find the lateral surface area of a cylinder with radius 2 units and height 5 units using cylindrical coordinates.
A) 4π 
(2)(5) = 40π
B) 16π 
(2)^2(5) = 160π
C) 40π 
(2)(5) = 40π
D) 80π 
(2)^2(5) = 160π
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The lateral surface area of a cylinder is given by the formula A = 2πrh. Plugging in r = 2 units and h = 5 units gives us A = 2π(2)(5) = 40π square units.

Explanation:
The lateral surface area of a cylinder is the part that wraps around it, not including the top and bottom circles. Choice A is incorrect because it uses the wrong formula (it's for the total surface area). Choices B and D are incorrect because they use the radius squared in the formula, which is unnecessary when calculating only the lateral surface area.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Using the total surface area formula instead of the lateral surface area formula.

Problem 2 (:2):
Calculate the volume of a right circular cone with height 6 units and base radius 3 units using cylindrical coordinates.
A) 18π 
rac{1}{3}π(3)^2(6)
B) 18π 
rac{1}{3}π(3)(6)
C) 54π 
rac{1}{3}π(3)^2(6)
D) 72π 
rac{1}{3}π(3)(6)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The volume of a right circular cone is given by the formula V = rac{1}{3}πr^2h. Plugging in r = 3 units and h = 6 units gives us V = rac{1}{3}π(3)^2(6) = 18π cubic units.

Explanation:
The volume of a cone is calculated using the formula provided, not the volume of a cylinder. Choice B is incorrect because it multiplies the radius by the height instead of squaring the radius. Choices C and D are incorrect because they use the wrong values for either the radius or the height.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Using the volume formula for a cylinder instead of a cone.
