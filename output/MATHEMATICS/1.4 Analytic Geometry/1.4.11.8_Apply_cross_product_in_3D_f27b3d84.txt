Problem 1 (:Q1):
Find the cross product of vector A = <2, 3, 4> and vector B = <-1, 5, 6>.
A) <-7, 10, 7>
B) <7, -10, 7>
C) <7, 10, -7>
D) <-7, -10, -7>
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The cross product of two vectors A and B is calculated as follows: A x B = (Ay * Bz - Az * By)i - (Ax * Bz - Az * Bx)j + (Ax * By - Ay * Bx)k. Plugging in the values, we get: A x B = (3 * 6 - 4 * 5)i - (2 * 6 - 4 * (-1))j + (2 * 5 - 3 * (-1))k = <-7, 10, 7>. Therefore, the correct answer is A.

Explanation:
The cross product results in a vector perpendicular to both input vectors. The formula involves multiplying corresponding components and taking differences, then applying the right-hand rule for orientation.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be swapping the order of multiplication or forgetting to negate one of the terms when calculating the components of the resulting vector.

Explanation:
Remember to treat each component of the vectors separately and apply the cross product formula correctly. This ensures that the resulting vector is perpendicular to both input vectors.
