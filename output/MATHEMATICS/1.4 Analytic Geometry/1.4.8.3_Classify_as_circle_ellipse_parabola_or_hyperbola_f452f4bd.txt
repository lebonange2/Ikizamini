Problem 1 (:1):
Which equation represents a circle?
A) (x - 3)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 25
B) y = x^2 + 2x + 1
C) xy = 9
D) x^2 - y^2 = 1
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation of a circle is in the form (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2, where (h, k) is the center and r is the radius. Answer A fits this format with center at (3, 4) and radius 5.

Explanation:
Answers B and D represent parabolas, while C represents an ellipse.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B or D because they involve x and y squared terms, but those equations are not in the standard form of a circle.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (2):
Which equation represents an ellipse with its major axis along the x-axis?
A) (x - 3)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 16
B) x^2/9 + y^2 = 1
C) y = x^2 - 4x + 4
D) x^2 - y^2 = 9
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The equation of an ellipse with the major axis along the x-axis is in the form (x - h)^2/a^2 + (y - k)^2/b^2 = 1, where a > b. Answer B fits this format with a = 3 and b = 1.

Explanation:
Answer A represents a circle, C represents a parabola, and D represents a hyperbola.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A because it looks similar to an ellipse equation but has equal values for a and b (indicating a circle).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (3):
Which of the following equations represents a parabola opening upwards?
A) y = -x^2 + 4x - 3
B) y = x^2 + 6x + 9
C) y = (x - 2)^2
D) y = rac{1}{2}(x - 3)^2
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The equation of a parabola opening upwards is in the form y = ax^2 + bx + c, where a > 0. Answer B has a = 1.

Explanation:
Answer A opens downwards, C and D are written in vertex form (y = a(x - h)^2 + k) which may be confusing but still represent parabolas opening upwards with a > 0.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A because it has a negative coefficient for x^2, indicating a downwards opening.

Problem 4 (4):
Which equation represents a hyperbola centered at the origin with its transverse axis along the x-axis?
A) x^2 - y^2 = 16
B) 9x^2 - y^2 = 81
C) (x - 3)^2/4 + (y - 4)^2/9 = 1
D) y = x^2 - 4x + 4
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation of a hyperbola centered at the origin with its transverse axis along the x-axis is in the form x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2 = 1, where a and b are positive. Answer A fits this format with a = 4 and b = 4.

Explanation:
Answer B has a > b, indicating an ellipse; C has its center at (3, 4), not the origin; D represents a parabola.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing C because it looks like an equation of a hyperbola but has a center other than the origin.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (5):
Which of the following equations represents a circle with radius 3?
A) (x - 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 9
B) y = x^2 - 6x + 9
C) xy = 9
D) x^2 + y^2 = 9
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The equation of a circle with radius 3 is in the form x^2 + y^2 = r^2, where r = 3. Answer D fits this format.

Explanation:
Answer A has a different center, B represents a parabola, and C represents an ellipse.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A because it has a radius of 5 (not 3) or choosing B because it involves x and y squared terms but is not in the standard form of a circle.

Problem 7 (7):
Which of the following equations represents a parabola with vertex at (2, 3)?
A) y = (x - 2)^2 + 3
B) y = x^2 - 4x + 5
C) y = -2(x - 2)^2 + 3
D) y = rac{1}{2}(x - 2)^2 + 3
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation of a parabola in vertex form is y = a(x - h)^2 + k, where (h, k) is the vertex. Answer A fits this format with vertex at (2, 3).

Explanation:
Answer B has a vertex at (2, 1), C opens downwards, and D opens upwards but does not have the correct vertex coordinates.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B because it has a vertex at (2, 1) or choosing C because it opens downwards.
