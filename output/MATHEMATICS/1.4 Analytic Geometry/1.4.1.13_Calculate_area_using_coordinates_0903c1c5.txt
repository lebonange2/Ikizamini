Problem 1 (:1):
Find the area of a rectangle with vertices at (0, 0), (6, 0), (6, 4), and (0, 4).
A) 18
B) 24
C) 30
D) 36
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The area of a rectangle is given by multiplying the length by the width. The length is 6 units, and the width is 4 units. So, the area = 6 × 4 = 24 square units.

Explanation:
The correct calculation uses the dimensions of the rectangle to find the area.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to add lengths and widths instead of multiplying them, which results in choice A (18).

Problem 2 (:2):
Determine the area of a triangle with vertices at (0, 0), (5, 0), and (3, 7).
A) 15.5
B) 21
C) 24
D) 35
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The area of a triangle is given by 0.5 × base × height. The base is 5 units, and the height is 6 units (drawn from point (3, 7) perpendicular to the base). So, the area = 0.5 × 5 × 6 = 15 square units. Since the given triangle is actually two congruent right triangles, we double the value: 2 × 15 = 30 square units. Choice B (21) is closest to the correct answer.

Explanation:
The correct calculation uses the base and height of the triangle, considering it's a right triangle split into two equal parts.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to calculate the area of only one half of the triangle (15 square units) without realizing it's actually a right triangle.

Problem 3 (:3):
Calculate the area of a parallelogram with vertices at (0, 0), (4, 0), (4, 6), and (0, 6).
A) 12
B) 18

D) 32
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The area of a parallelogram is given by multiplying the base by the height. The base is 4 units, and the height is 6 units (drawn from point (4, 6) perpendicular to the base). So, the area = 4 × 6 = 24 square units.

Explanation:
The correct calculation uses the base and height of the parallelogram.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to use the wrong side as the base or calculate the area of a rectangle instead, which results in choices A (12), B (18), or D (32).

Problem 4 (:4):
Find the area of a trapezoid with vertices at (0, 0), (6, 0), (9, 5), and (3, 5).
A) 18
B) 27
C) 30
D) 36
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The area of a trapezoid is given by the formula: area = 0.5 × (sum of bases) × height. The sum of bases is 12 units, and the height is 5 units. So, the area = 0.5 × 12 × 5 = 27 square units.

Explanation:
The correct calculation uses the formula for the area of a trapezoid with the given bases and height.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to use the wrong sum of bases or calculate the area of a rectangle instead, which results in choices A (18), C (30), or D (36).

Problem 5 (:5):
Calculate the area of a circle with center at (0, 0) and radius 3 units.
A) 9π
B) 18π
C) 27π
D) 36π
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The area of a circle is given by the formula: area = πr², where r is the radius. In this case, r = 3 units. So, the area = π(3)² = 9π square units.

Explanation:
The correct calculation uses the formula for the area of a circle with the given radius.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to square the radius or use the wrong value for π, which results in choices B (18π), C (27π), or D (36π).

Problem 6 (:6):
Determine the area of a sector of a circle with radius 5 units and central angle of 60°.
A) 2.5π
B) 5π
C) 7.5π
D) 10π
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The area of a sector is given by the formula: area = (central angle/360°) × πr², where r is the radius and the central angle is in degrees. In this case, r = 5 units and the central angle is 60°. So, the area = (60/360) × π(5)² = 2.5π square units.

Explanation:
The correct calculation uses the formula for the area of a sector with the given radius and central angle.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to use the wrong fraction for the central angle or forget to multiply by π, which results in choices B (5π), C (7.5π), or D (10π).

Problem 7 (:7):
Find the area of a regular hexagon with side length 4 units.
A) 24
B) 36
C) 48
D) 72
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The area of a regular hexagon can be calculated using the formula: area = (3√3/2) × s², where s is the length of one side. In this case, s = 4 units. So, the area = (3√3/2) × (4)² = 24√3 square units. Choice A (24) is closest to the approximate value: 24 × (1.732) ≈ 42 square units.

Explanation:
The correct calculation uses the formula for the area of a regular hexagon with the given side length.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to use the wrong formula or forget to multiply by √3, which results in choices B (36), C (48), or D (72).
