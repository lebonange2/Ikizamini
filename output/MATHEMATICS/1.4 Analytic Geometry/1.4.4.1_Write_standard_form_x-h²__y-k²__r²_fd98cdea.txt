Problem 1 ([1]):
What is the standard form equation of a circle with center (3, -2) and radius 4?
A) (x-4)² + (y+2)² = 16
B) (x-3)² + (y+2)² = 16
C) (x+3)² + (y-2)² = 16
D) (x-3)² + (y-2)² = 4
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The standard form equation of a circle is (x-h)² + (y-k)² = r², where (h, k) is the center and r is the radius. Given a center at (3, -2) and a radius of 4, we substitute h=3, k=-2, and r=4 into the standard form.

Explanation:
The correct equation is (x-3)² + (y+2)² = 16 because it correctly represents the given center and radius.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to mix up the coordinates of the center, using (4, -2) instead of (3, -2). This would result in answer A, which is incorrect.
