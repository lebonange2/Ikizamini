Problem 1 (:1):
A car moves from point A (0, 0) to point B (3, 4). What is the displacement vector of the car?
A) [4, 3]
B) [3, 4]
C) [7, 7]
D) [-3, -4]
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The displacement vector is the difference between the final position and the initial position. In this case, the car starts at (0, 0) and ends at (3, 4). So, the displacement vector is (3, 4) - (0, 0) = (3, 4).

Explanation:
The correct answer represents the change in position from the starting point to the ending point. Choice A has the coordinates flipped, choice C adds the positions instead of finding the difference, and choice D reverses the direction.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Confusing displacement with the original coordinates or adding them instead of subtracting.

Problem 2 (2):
A person walks from point P (2, -1) to point Q (-3, 5). What is the displacement vector?
A) [5, 6]
B) [-5, -6]
C) [-1, 4]
D) [1, -4]
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The displacement vector is calculated by subtracting the initial position from the final position. So, for this scenario, it's (-3, 5) - (2, -1) = (-5, 6).

Explanation:
Choice A has the wrong sign on both components, choice C only subtracts the x-coordinates and adds the y-coordinates, and choice D reverses the direction of the displacement.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Forgetting to change the signs when subtracting coordinates.

Problem 3 (3):
A plane flies from airport A (10, 20) to airport B (-5, -10). What is the displacement vector?
A) [15, 10]
B) [-15, -30]
C) [-15, 30]
D) [5, 10]
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The displacement vector is the difference between the coordinates of airport B and airport A: (-5, -10) - (10, 20) = (-15, -30).

Explanation:
Choice A has the wrong sign on both components, choice C uses the absolute values instead of differences, and choice D only subtracts one component correctly.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Mixing up the order of subtraction or not considering negative coordinates.
