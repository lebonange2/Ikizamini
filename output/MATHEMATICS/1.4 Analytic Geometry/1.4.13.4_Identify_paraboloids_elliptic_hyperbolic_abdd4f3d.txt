Problem 1 (Asdf):
Which equation represents an elliptic paraboloid?
A) (x^2)/4 + (y^2)/9 = z
B) x^2 - y^2 = 4z
C) x^2 + y^2 = z^2
D) x^2 - z^2 = 4y
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
An elliptic paraboloid has an equation where the variables x and y are squared with positive coefficients. The correct choice is (x^2)/4 + (y^2)/9 = z.

Explanation:
In A, both x and y have positive squared terms, indicating an elliptic paraboloid. B represents a hyperbolic paraboloid, C represents a cone, and D represents a hyperboloid of one sheet.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake is to confuse the equation for a hyperbolic paraboloid (B) with an elliptic paraboloid.

Problem 3 (Asdf):
Which equation represents a hyperbolic paraboloid?
A) (x^2)/4 - (y^2)/9 = z
B) x^2 + y^2 = 4z
C) x^2 - y^2 = z^2
D) x^2 + z^2 = 4y
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
A hyperbolic paraboloid has an equation where the squares of x and y have opposite signs. The correct choice is (x^2)/4 - (y^2)/9 = z.

Explanation:
In A, x and y have squared terms with opposite signs, indicating a hyperbolic paraboloid. B represents a cone, C represents an elliptic paraboloid, and D represents a hyperboloid of one sheet.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake is to confuse the equation for an elliptic paraboloid (C) or a hyperboloid of one sheet (D) with a hyperbolic paraboloid.

Problem 4 (Asdf):
Which surface has the equation x^2 + y^2 - z^2 = 0?
A) Elliptic paraboloid
B) Hyperbolic paraboloid
C) Cone
D) Hyperboloid of one sheet
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The equation x^2 + y^2 - z^2 = 0 represents a cone, as it is the difference of two squares.

Explanation:
In C, the equation forms a cone with its vertex at the origin. A represents an elliptic paraboloid when the signs are positive, B represents a hyperbolic paraboloid when the signs are opposite, and D represents a hyperboloid of one sheet.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake is to confuse the equation for an elliptic paraboloid (A) or a hyperbolic paraboloid (B) with a cone.
