Problem 1 (Andoid):
Find the area of a rectangle with length 8 cm and width 3 cm.
A) 16π cm²
B) 24π cm²
C) 96π cm²
D) 24 cm²
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The area of a rectangle is given by A = length × width. Given length = 8 cm and width = 3 cm, we can calculate the area as follows: A = πab = π(8)(3) = 24π cm².

Explanation:
The formula for the area of a rectangle uses the product of its length and width, not just one dimension. The correct formula includes π to account for the circular constant in the general area calculation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or C by using only one dimension (8π cm² or 96π cm²) instead of multiplying both dimensions.

Problem 2 (Cat):
Calculate the area of a circle with diameter 10 inches.
A) 25π in²
B) 50π in²
C) 78.54π in²
D) 100π in²
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The area of a circle is given by A = πr², where r is the radius. Since the diameter is twice the radius, we have r = 10/2 = 5 inches. Now we can calculate the area: A = π(5)² = 25π in².

Explanation:
The formula for the area of a circle uses the square of the radius, not just the diameter. The correct formula includes π to account for the circular constant in the general area calculation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B or D by using the diameter instead of the radius squared (50π in² or 100π in²) or not including π at all.

Problem 3 (Dog):
Find the area of a parallelogram with base 6 m and height 4 m.
A) 24π m²
B) 96π m²
C) 24 m²
D) 12 m²
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The area of a parallelogram is given by A = base × height. Given base = 6 m and height = 4 m, we can calculate the area as follows: A = 6 × 4 = 24 m².

Explanation:
The formula for the area of a parallelogram uses the product of its base and height, not π. There is no need to include π in this calculation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or B by including π (24π m² or 96π m²), which is unnecessary for a parallelogram.

Problem 4 (Bird):
What is the area of a square with side length 7 cm?
A) 14π cm²
B) 49π cm²
C) 49 cm²
D) 28 cm²
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The area of a square is given by A = s², where s is the side length. Given side length = 7 cm, we can calculate the area as follows: A = (7)² = 49 cm².

Explanation:
The formula for the area of a square uses the square of its side length, not π. There is no need to include π in this calculation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B or D by including π (49π cm² or 28 cm²), which is unnecessary for a square.

Problem 5 (Fish):
Calculate the area of a sector with radius 5 cm and central angle of 60°.
A) 1.57π cm²
B) 7.85π cm²
C) 24π cm²
D) 39.27π cm²
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The area of a sector is given by A = (θ/360°) × πr², where θ is the central angle in degrees and r is the radius. Given radius = 5 cm and central angle = 60°, we can calculate the area as follows: A = (60/360) × π(5)² = 7.85π cm².

Explanation:
The formula for the area of a sector uses the fraction of the circle represented by the central angle and the radius squared, not just one or the other. The correct formula includes π to account for the circular constant in the general area calculation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or C by using only the radius (1.57π cm² or 24π cm²) or not including the central angle fraction, or choosing D by not converting the angle to degrees.

Problem 6 (Horse):
Find the area of a triangle with base 9 ft and height 6 ft.
A) 54π ft²
B) 108π ft²
C) 27 ft²
D) 54 ft²
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The area of a triangle is given by A = (1/2) × base × height. Given base = 9 ft and height = 6 ft, we can calculate the area as follows: A = (1/2) × 9 × 6 = 54 ft².

Explanation:
The formula for the area of a triangle uses one-half the product of its base and height, not π. There is no need to include π in this calculation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or B by including π (54π ft² or 108π ft²), which is unnecessary for a triangle.

Problem 7 (Cow):
Calculate the area of an equilateral triangle with side length 12 cm.
A) 36π cm²
B) 72π cm²
C) 36√3 cm²
D) 72 cm²
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The area of an equilateral triangle is given by A = (sqrt(3)/4) × s², where s is the side length. Given side length = 12 cm, we can calculate the area as follows: A = (sqrt(3)/4) × (12)² = 36√3 cm².

Explanation:
The formula for the area of an equilateral triangle uses a specific constant involving √3, not π. There is no need to include π in this calculation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or B by including π (36π cm² or 72π cm²), which is incorrect for an equilateral triangle.
