Problem 1 (Asdf):
Find the distance between points A(1, 2, 3) and B(-2, 5, -1).
A) 2.8284271247461903
B) 5
C) 3.605551275463989
D) 4.242640687119285
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The distance formula in 3D space is given by: d = sqrt((x2 - x1)^2 + (y2 - y1)^2 + (z2 - z1)^2). Plugging in the coordinates A(1, 2, 3) and B(-2, 5, -1), we get: d = sqrt((-2 - 1)^2 + (5 - 2)^2 + (-1 - 3)^2) = sqrt(9 + 9 + 16) ≈ 5.8284. The closest approximation is option A.

Explanation:
The distance formula calculates the straight-line distance between two points in 3D space using their x, y, and z coordinates.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget one or more terms when squaring the differences or to use the wrong coordinates. For example, using the distances (1-5)^2, (2-(-1))^2, and (3-(-2))^2 would lead to an incorrect result.
