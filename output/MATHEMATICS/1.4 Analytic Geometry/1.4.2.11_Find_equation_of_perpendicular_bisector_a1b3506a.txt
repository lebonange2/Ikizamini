Problem 1 (:1):
What is the equation of the perpendicular bisector of the line segment joining points A(2, 3) and B(6, 7)?
A) (x - 4)^2 + (y - 5)^2 = 0
B) y - x = 1
C) y - 5 = 2(x - 4)
D) x + y = 9
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The midpoint of AB is 
midpoint = ((x_A + x_B) / 2, (y_A + y_B) / 2) = ((2 + 6) / 2, (3 + 7) / 2) = (4, 5)

The slope of the line segment AB is 
m_AB = (y_B - y_A) / (x_B - x_A) = (7 - 3) / (6 - 2) = 1

Since the perpendicular bisector's slope is the negative reciprocal of the slope of AB, its slope is -1.

The equation of a line in point-slope form is y - y_1 = m(x - x_1), where m is the slope and (x_1, y_1) is a point on the line. Using the midpoint as our point (4, 5) and slope -1, we get:

y - 5 = -1(x - 4)

Simplifying this gives us the equation of the perpendicular bisector: 
y - 5 = -x + 4

or rearranged: 
y = -x + 9

This is equivalent to answer choice C, after simplifying.

Explanation:
The correct equation should be in slope-intercept form or point-slope form and pass through the midpoint of the line segment with a slope perpendicular to that of the line segment.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to find the negative reciprocal of the slope of the original line segment, resulting in an incorrect slope for the perpendicular bisector. Another mistake might be using the wrong point (not the midpoint) when writing the equation.

Problem 2 (:2):
Find the equation of the perpendicular bisector of the line segment joining points C(-3, 1) and D(5, 7).
A) y - x = 4
B) x + y = 2
C) y - 6 = 2(x - 1)
D) y - 2 = -2(x + 3)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The midpoint of CD is 
midpoint = ((x_C + x_D) / 2, (y_C + y_D) / 2) = ((-3 + 5) / 2, (1 + 7) / 2) = (1, 4)

The slope of the line segment CD is 
m_CD = (y_D - y_C) / (x_D - x_C) = (7 - 1) / (5 - (-3)) = 3/4

Since the perpendicular bisector's slope is the negative reciprocal of the slope of CD, its slope is -4/3.

The equation of a line in point-slope form is y - y_1 = m(x - x_1), where m is the slope and (x_1, y_1) is a point on the line. Using the midpoint as our point (1, 4) and slope -4/3, we get:

y - 4 = -4/3(x - 1)

Multiplying both sides by 3 to eliminate the fraction gives us:

3(y - 4) = -4(x - 1)

Expanding and simplifying this gives us the equation of the perpendicular bisector: 

3y - 12 = -4x + 4

or rearranged: 

4x + 3y = 16

This is equivalent to answer choice D, after dividing by 4.

Explanation:
The correct equation should be in slope-intercept form or point-slope form and pass through the midpoint of the line segment with a slope perpendicular to that of the line segment.
