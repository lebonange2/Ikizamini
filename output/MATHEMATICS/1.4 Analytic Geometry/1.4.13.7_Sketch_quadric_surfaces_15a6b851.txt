Problem 1 (One):
Sketch the surface defined by the equation: x^2 + y^2 - z^2 = 1
A) Ellipsoid
B) Hyperboloid of one sheet
C) Paraboloid
D) Hyperboloid of two sheets
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The equation represents a hyperboloid of one sheet when k > 0. Sketch the surface accordingly.

Explanation:
An ellipsoid has positive coefficients for all variables; a paraboloid has only one variable squared on one side; and a hyperboloid of two sheets has k < 0.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A (ellipsoid) because it's the most familiar quadric surface. Remember to check the signs of the coefficients.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (Two):
Sketch the surface defined by the equation: x^2 + y^2 - z^2 = 0
A) Ellipsoid
B) Hyperboloid of one sheet
C) Paraboloid
D) Plane
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The equation simplifies to (x^2 + y^2) = z^2, which represents two parallel planes when set equal to zero. Sketch the planes.

Explanation:
An ellipsoid has positive coefficients for all variables; a hyperboloid requires at least one variable squared on each side; and a paraboloid has only one variable squared.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B (hyperboloid of one sheet) because it's similar to the previous question. However, setting the equation equal to zero eliminates the hyperbolic behavior.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Three):
Sketch the surface defined by the equation: x^2 + y^2 = 4z
A) Ellipsoid
B) Hyperboloid of one sheet
C) Paraboloid (vertical)
D) Cylinder
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The equation represents a vertical paraboloid opening upwards. Sketch the paraboloid.

Explanation:
An ellipsoid has all variables squared and positive coefficients; a hyperboloid requires at least one variable squared on each side; and a cylinder does not have z as a quadratic term.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing D (cylinder) because it's similar to the previous question. However, the presence of z^2 indicates a paraboloidal shape.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Four):
Sketch the surface defined by the equation: x^2 + y^2 = 9 - z^2
A) Ellipsoid
B) Hyperboloid of one sheet
C) Paraboloid (horizontal)
D) Sphere
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The equation represents a sphere with radius 3. Sketch the sphere.

Explanation:
An ellipsoid has all variables squared and positive coefficients; a hyperboloid requires at least one variable squared on each side; and a paraboloid does not have z^2 as part of the quadratic term.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing C (horizontal paraboloid) because of the form x^2 + y^2, but the presence of z^2 indicates a spherical shape.

Problem 5 (Five):
Sketch the surface defined by the equation: x^2 - y^2 = z
A) Ellipsoid
B) Hyperboloid of two sheets
C) Paraboloid (vertical)
D) Hyperboloid of one sheet
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The equation represents a hyperboloid of two sheets. Sketch the oblique hyperboloid.

Explanation:
An ellipsoid has all variables squared and positive coefficients; a paraboloid requires only one variable squared; and a hyperboloid of one sheet has z^2 instead of z.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing D (hyperboloid of one sheet) because it's similar to the previous question. However, the lack of z^2 indicates a different type of hyperboloid.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (Six):
Sketch the surface defined by the equation: x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 4
A) Ellipsoid
B) Hyperboloid of one sheet
C) Paraboloid (horizontal)
D) Cone
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation represents an ellipsoid with radii 2, 2, and 2. Sketch the ellipsoid.

Explanation:
A hyperboloid requires at least one variable squared on each side; a paraboloid does not have all variables squared; and a cone has only two variables squared.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing D (cone) because of the form x^2 + y^2, but the presence of z^2 indicates an ellipsoidal shape.
