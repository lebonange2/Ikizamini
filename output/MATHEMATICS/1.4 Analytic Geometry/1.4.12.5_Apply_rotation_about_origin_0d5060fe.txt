Problem 1 (Asdf):
What are the new coordinates of point (3, 4) after a counterclockwise rotation of 90 degrees around the origin?
A) (4, -3)
B) (-4, 3)
C) (-3, -4)
D) (3, 4)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
When a point is rotated counterclockwise by 90 degrees around the origin, its x-coordinate becomes the negative of the y-coordinate and its y-coordinate becomes the x-coordinate. So (3, 4) becomes (-4, 3).

Explanation:
This question tests understanding of rotating points about the origin.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that the x-ordinate becomes the negative of the y-ordinate and vice versa.

Problem 2 (Bghj):
What are the new coordinates of point (5, -2) after a clockwise rotation of 180 degrees around the origin?
A) (5, 2)
B) (-5, -2)
C) (-5, 2)
D) (5, -2)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
A rotation of 180 degrees around the origin flips the point across the origin, so (x, y) becomes (-x, -y). Thus, (5, -2) becomes (-5, 2).

Explanation:
This question assesses knowledge of rotating points by a full turn.

Common mistake note:
Students might accidentally apply a counterclockwise rotation instead of clockwise, leading to the incorrect answer A.

Problem 3 (Qwer):
What are the new coordinates of point (-1, 3) after a rotation of 270 degrees counterclockwise around the origin?
A) (3, -1)
B) (-3, 1)
C) (1, 3)
D) (-1, 3)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
A rotation of 270 degrees counterclockwise around the origin is equivalent to a clockwise rotation of 90 degrees. The x-coordinate becomes the negative of the y-coordinate and the y-coordinate remains unchanged. So (-1, 3) becomes (3, -1).

Explanation:
This question tests understanding of rotating points by different angles.

Common mistake note:
Students might confuse the clockwise and counterclockwise rotations, leading to an incorrect choice.
