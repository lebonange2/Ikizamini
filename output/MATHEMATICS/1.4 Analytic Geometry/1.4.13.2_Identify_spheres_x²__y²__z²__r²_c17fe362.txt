Problem 1 (:1):
What is the equation of a sphere with radius 5 centered at the origin?
A) (x - 5)² + (y - 5)² + (z - 5)² = 25
B) x² + y² + z² = 25
C) (x + 5)² + (y + 5)² + (z + 5)² = 25
D) x² + y² + z² = 5
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The equation of a sphere centered at the origin with radius r is x² + y² + z² = r². In this case, r = 5.

Explanation:
This question tests understanding of the standard form of a sphere's equation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or C because they include the center (0, 0, 0) but have an incorrect radius. Remember that the origin has coordinates (0, 0, 0), not (5, 5, 5).

Problem 2 (2):
Find the radius of a sphere given by the equation x² + y² + z² = 36.
A) 9
B) 6
C) 18
D) 3
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The radius r can be found by taking the square root of both sides of the equation: √(x² + y² + z²) = √36, which gives us r = 6.

Explanation:
This question tests the ability to extract the radius from a sphere's equation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or C because they are multiples of 36 but do not represent the square root. Remember that the radius is the value inside the square root.

Problem 3 (3):
Which of the following represents a sphere with radius 4 and centered at (2, 3, -1)?
A) (x + 2)² + (y + 3)² + (z - 1)² = 16
B) (x - 2)² + (y - 3)² + (z + 1)² = 16
C) x² + y² + z² = 4
D) (x - 2)² + (y - 3)² + (z - 1)² = 16
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The sphere is centered at (2, 3, -1), so the equation should have x - 2, y - 3, and z - 1 in it. The radius is given as 4, so r² = 16.

Explanation:
This question tests the application of the sphere's equation to a specific center point.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or B because they have the incorrect signs for the center coordinates. Remember that subtracting the center coordinates from x, y, and z is used in the equation.

Problem 4 (4):
What is the standard form of a sphere's equation?
A) (x - h)² + (y - k)² + (z - l)² = r²
B) x² + y² + z² = r
C) r = √(x² + y² + z²)
D) h² + k² + l² = r²
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The standard form of a sphere's equation is (x - h)² + (y - k)² + (z - l)² = r², where (h, k, l) is the center and r is the radius.

Explanation:
This question tests knowledge of the general form of a sphere's equation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B because it omits the center coordinates. Remember that the standard form includes the center point (h, k, l).

Problem 5 (5):
Find the center and radius of the sphere given by the equation 9(x - 1)² + 25(y + 3)² + 4(z - 2)² = 100.
A) Center: (1, -3, 2), Radius: 10
B) Center: (-1, 3, -2), Radius: 10
C) Center: (1, -3, 2), Radius: √(9 + 25 + 4)
D) Center: (1, -3, 2), Radius: 5
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Divide both sides by the coefficients to get standard form: (x - 1)² + (y + 3)² + (z - 2)² = 10/9 + 4/25 + 1/4. The center is (1, -3, 2), and the radius is √(10/9 + 4/25 + 1/4) = 5.

Explanation:
This question tests the ability to rearrange a sphere's equation into standard form.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or B because they have the incorrect center coordinates. Remember to divide by the coefficients to isolate (x - h), (y - k), and (z - l).

Problem 6 (6):
Which of the following equations represents a sphere that does not intersect the xy-plane?
A) x² + y² + z² = 1
B) x² + y² + z = 1
C) x² + y² = 1
D) x² + z² = 1
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
A sphere that does not intersect the xy-plane must have z ≠ 0. The equation x² + y² = 1 represents a circle in the xy-plane, and thus does not intersect it.

Explanation:
This question tests understanding of how a sphere's intersection with planes affects its equation.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A because it might seem like a valid sphere. However, it still intersects the xy-plane at z = 0. Remember that if z² is not present in the equation, there is no intersection.

Problem 7 (7):
Find the equation of a sphere with center (3, -2, 1) and passing through the point (5, -4, 2).
A) (x - 3)² + (y + 2)² + (z - 1)² = 2
B) x² + y² + z² = 2
C) (x - 3)² + (y + 2)² + (z - 1)² = 6
D) (x - 5)² + (y + 4)² + (z - 2)² = 6
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The distance from the center to a point on the sphere is equal to the radius. Using the distance formula, we get √((5 - 3)² + (-4 + 2)² + (2 - 1)²) = √(4 + 4 + 1) = √9 = 3. The equation of the sphere is thus (x - 3)² + (y + 2)² + (z - 1)² = 3².

Explanation:
This question tests finding a sphere's equation given its center and a point it passes through.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A because it has the correct center but an incorrect radius. Remember to calculate the distance from the center to the point, which gives the radius.
