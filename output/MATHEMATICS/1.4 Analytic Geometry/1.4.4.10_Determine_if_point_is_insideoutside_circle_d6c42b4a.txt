Problem 1 (1.4.4.10-1):
Is the point (3, 4) inside, outside, or on the circle with center (0, 0) and radius 5?
A) Inside
B) Outside
C) On the circle
D) Cannot be determined
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
(3, 4) is a distance of π√10 from the origin (0, 0), which is less than the radius of 5. Therefore, the point is on the circle.

Explanation:
The distance between the center and the point can be calculated using the distance formula: sqrt((x2-x1)^2 + (y2-y1)^2).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to assume that because the coordinates of the point are less than the radius, the point must be inside the circle. This is not true; the point could also lie on the circle if its distance from the center equals the radius.

Problem 2 (1.4.4.10-2):

A) Inside
B) Outside
C) On the circle
D) Cannot be determined
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
(-6, -8) is a distance of 10 from the center (2, 3), which is greater than the radius of 5. Therefore, the point is outside the circle.

Explanation:
The distance between the center and the point can be calculated using the distance formula: sqrt((x2-x1)^2 + (y2-y1)^2).

Common mistake note:
