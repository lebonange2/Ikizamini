Problem 1 (:1):
A cube has sides measuring 2 meters each. Calculate its volume in cubic meters.
A) 6
B) 8
C) 16
D) 24
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The formula for the volume of a cube is V = s^3, where s is the length of a side. Given that each side measures 2 meters, we can calculate the volume as follows: V = (2 m)^3 = 8 m^3.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by cubing the length of one side.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to multiply the sides instead of cubing them. This would result in choice A, which is the surface area of the cube rather than its volume.

Problem 2 (2):
A rectangular prism has dimensions 3 meters by 4 meters by 5 meters. What is its total surface area?
A) 90
B) 120
C) 150
D) 180
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The formula for the surface area of a rectangular prism is A = 2(lw + lh + wh), where l, w, and h are the length, width, and height, respectively. Given that l = 3 m, w = 4 m, and h = 5 m, we can calculate the surface area as follows: A = 2(3 × 4 + 3 × 5 + 4 × 5) = 120 m^2.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by applying the formula for the surface area of a rectangular prism and plugging in the given dimensions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to multiply the lengths, widths, and heights together, which would result in the volume rather than the surface area. This would lead to choice D.

Problem 3 (3):
An object is placed 10 meters away from a concave mirror with a focal length of 5 meters. Determine the image distance.
A) -5 m
B) 5 m
C) 10 m
D) 20 m
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The mirror equation is 1/f = 1/do + 1/di, where f is the focal length, do is the object distance, and di is the image distance. Given that f = -5 m (negative for a concave mirror) and do = 10 m, we can solve for di: 1/-5 = 1/10 + 1/di. Solving for di gives us di = -5 m.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by applying the mirror equation and considering the negative focal length for a concave mirror.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to assume that the image distance is positive, which is incorrect for a concave mirror. This would lead to choices B or C.

Problem 4 (4):
A right circular cone has a height of 6 meters and a radius of 3 meters. Calculate its slant height.
A) 3.sqrt(2) m
B) 3.sqrt(3) m
C) 6 sqrt(2) m
D) 6 sqrt(3) m
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The slant height (l) of a right circular cone can be found using the Pythagorean theorem: l = sqrt(h^2 + r^2), where h is the height and r is the radius. Given that h = 6 m and r = 3 m, we can calculate the slant height as follows: l = sqrt(6^2 + 3^2) = 3.sqrt(3) m.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by applying the Pythagorean theorem to find the hypotenuse of a right triangle formed by the height, radius, and slant height.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget to use the Pythagorean theorem or to incorrectly apply it with the wrong values. This could result in choices A, C, or D.

Problem 5 (5):
An engineer needs to design a triangular truss with sides measuring 4 meters, 5 meters, and 6 meters. Which of the following is the correct classification for this triangle?
A) Equilateral
B) Isosceles
C) Scalene
D) Right
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
A triangle with sides measuring 4 meters, 5 meters, and 6 meters is a scalene triangle because all three sides have different lengths.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by comparing the side lengths and determining that none of them are equal.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to confuse this triangle with an equilateral or isosceles triangle, which requires at least two sides to be equal. This could lead to choices A or B.

Problem 6 (6):
A solid sphere has a diameter of 10 centimeters. What is its volume in cubic centimeters?
A) 500π
B) 785π
C) 1000π
D) 1250π
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The formula for the volume of a sphere is V = (4/3)πr^3, where r is the radius. Given that the diameter is 10 cm, the radius is half of that: r = 5 cm. We can calculate the volume as follows: V = (4/3)π(5 cm)^3 = 785π cm^3.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by applying the formula for the volume of a sphere and using the given diameter to find the radius.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to use the diameter instead of the radius in the formula, which would result in an incorrect volume. This could lead to choice A.

Problem 7 (7):
A right circular cylinder has a height of 8 meters and a base circumference of 12π meters. What is its volume?
A) 96π m^3
B) 128π m^3
C) 192π m^3
D) 256π m^3
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The formula for the volume of a cylinder is V = πr^2h, where r is the radius and h is the height. Given that the circumference is 12π meters, we can find the radius using C = 2πr: 12π = 2πr, so r = 6 m. The volume can be calculated as follows: V = π(6 m)^2 × 8 m = 96π m^3.

Explanation:
The correct answer is obtained by using the circumference to find the radius and then applying the formula for the volume of a cylinder.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to use the wrong value for the radius or height in the volume formula, which could lead to choices B, C, or D.
