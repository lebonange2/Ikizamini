Problem 1 (:1):
Sketch the parabola defined by the equation: f(x) = x^2 + 2x - 3
A) An upwards-opening parabola, vertex at (-1, -4)
B) A downwards-opening parabola, vertex at (1, 0)
C) An upwards-opening parabola, vertex at (1, 0)
D) A downwards-opening parabola, vertex at (-1, -4)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation is in the form f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c. Since a = 1 > 0, the parabola opens upwards. The vertex is at h = -b / (2a), k = f(h). For this equation, a = 1, b = 2, and c = -3. Plugging in these values gives h = -1, k = -4. Vertex: (-1, -4). Correct graph: Upwards-opening parabola at (-1, -4).

Explanation:
Positive a indicates an upwards-opening parabola; use the vertex formula to find coordinates.

Common mistake note:
Misinterpreting the sign of a affects opening direction. Incorrect vertex calculation.

Explanation:
Negative a indicates a downwards-opening parabola; vertex formula used correctly.

Common mistake note:
Misinterpreting the sign of a affects opening direction. Incorrect vertex calculation.

Problem 3 (:3):
Sketch the parabola defined by the equation: h(x) = 2(x - 3)^2 + 5
A) An upwards-opening parabola, vertex at (3, 5)
B) A downwards-opening parabola, vertex at (-3, -5)
C) An upwards-opening parabola, vertex at (-3, -5)
D) A downwards-opening parabola, vertex at (3, 5)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation is in vertex form f(x) = a(x - h)^2 + k. Here, a = 2 > 0, h = 3, and k = 5. Since a is positive, the parabola opens upwards. Vertex: (h, k) = (3, 5). Correct graph: Upwards-opening parabola at (3, 5).

Explanation:
Vertex form directly gives vertex coordinates; positive a indicates upwards opening.

Common mistake note:
Misinterpreting vertex form or forgetting the effect of a on opening direction.

Problem 6 (:6):
Sketch the parabola defined by the equation: l(x) = 4x(x - 5)
A) An upwards-opening parabola, vertex at (0, 0)
B) A downwards-opening parabola, vertex at (2.5, -10)
C) An upwards-opening parabola, vertex at (2.5, -10)
D) A downwards-opening parabola, vertex at (0, 0)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Rewrite the equation in standard form: f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c. Factoring gives f(x) = 4x(x - 5) = 4x^2 - 20x. Since a = 4 > 0, the parabola opens upwards. Using h = -b / (2a), k = f(h), we get a = 4, b = -20, c = 0. Calculating gives h = 2.5, k = -10. Vertex: (2.5, -10). Correct graph: Upwards-opening parabola at (2.5, -10).

Explanation:
Rewrite equation in standard form first; positive a indicates upwards opening.

Common mistake note:
Forgetting to rewrite the equation or incorrect vertex calculation.
