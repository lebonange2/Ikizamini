Problem 1 (Asdf):
What is the result of reflecting the point (3, 4) across the y-axis?
A) (3, -4)
B) (-3, 4)
C) (4, 3)
D) (-4, 3)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
When reflecting a point across the y-axis, the x-coordinate changes sign while the y-coordinate remains unchanged. So, the reflection of (3, 4) is (-3, 4).

Explanation:
The y-axis reflection flips the sign of the x-coordinate.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to also flip the sign of the y-coordinate, which would result in choice A. However, this is incorrect because only the x-coordinate changes sign.

Problem 2 (Asdf2):
Reflect the point (-5, -7) across the y-axis and identify the coordinates.
A) (5, -7)
B) (-5, 7)
C) (7, -5)
D) (-7, -5)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The reflection of (-5, -7) across the y-axis is (5, -7), as only the x-coordinate changes sign.

Explanation:
Remember that reflecting across the y-axis only affects the x-coordinate.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to think that both coordinates change signs, which would lead to choices B and C. However, this is incorrect; only the x-coordinate should have its sign flipped.

Problem 3 (Asdf3):
What is the y-coordinate of the point reflected across the y-axis if the original point has coordinates (x, 8)?
A) -8
B) 0
C) x
D) 8
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The y-coordinate remains unchanged after reflecting across the y-axis. So, it will still be 8.

Explanation:
Reflection across the y-axis only affects the x-coordinate; the y-coordinate stays the same.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to think that the y-coordinate changes sign, which would lead to choice A. However, this is incorrect; the y-coordinate remains 8.

Problem 7 (Asdf7):
Reflect the point (0, -1) across the y-axis and state the new y- coordinate.
A) 1
B) 0
C) -1
D) undefined
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The reflection of (0, -1) across the y-axis is (0, -1), as only the x-coordinate changes sign. The y- coordinate remains -1.

Explanation:
Reflection across the y-axis does not affect the y-coordinate if it's already at 0.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to think that the y-coordinate changes sign, which would lead to choice A. However, this is incorrect; the y-coordinate remains -1.
