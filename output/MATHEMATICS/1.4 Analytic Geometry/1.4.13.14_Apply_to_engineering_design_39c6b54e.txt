Problem 1 (Assemble a robot arm):
A robotic arm needs to be assembled for an assembly line. Which of the following steps is crucial in the engineering design process?
A) Ordering coffee for the team.
B) Testing the arm's functionality after assembly.
C) Creating a detailed blueprint of the arm.
D) Watching a movie during the assembly.
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Creating a detailed blueprint is crucial in the engineering design process as it outlines the specifications, components, and assembly instructions for the robotic arm. Testing its functionality comes after assembly to ensure it meets the requirements.

Explanation:
The engineering design process involves planning, designing, building, and testing. Steps like ordering coffee or watching a movie are not part of this process.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or D, which are unrelated tasks that do not contribute to the actual design and assembly of the robotic arm.

Problem 2 (Design a bridge):
A civil engineer is tasked with designing a new suspension bridge. Which of the following aspects should be considered during the engineering design process?
A) The color of the bridge's paint.
B) The weight capacity of the bridge.
C) The favorite music genre of the construction workers.
D) The brand of cement used in the foundation.
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The weight capacity of the bridge is a critical aspect to consider during the engineering design process, as it ensures the safety and structural integrity of the structure.

Explanation:
In civil engineering, designing a bridge involves considering factors like load-bearing capabilities, materials, and construction methods. Personal preferences or aesthetic choices are not relevant to the design process.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or C, which are unrelated factors that do not impact the structural performance of the bridge.

Problem 3 (Water filtration system):
A team is designing a water filtration system for a remote village. Which step in the engineering design process involves identifying the specific contaminants to remove?
A) Gathering user feedback.
B) Prototyping different filter designs.
C) Conducting research on local water sources.
D) Determining the budget for the project.
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Conducting research on local water sources helps identify the specific contaminants present, which is essential in designing an effective filtration system.

Explanation:
Before designing a solution, engineers must understand the problem they are trying to solve. In this case, researching the water source provides crucial information about contaminants that need to be removed.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or D, which are important steps in the design process but do not directly address identifying contaminants.

Problem 6 (Air conditioning system):
An engineer is designing a new air conditioning system for a commercial building. Which of the following steps would be part of the engineering design process?
A) Scheduling maintenance appointments for existing systems.
B) Calculating the cooling load required for each floor.
C) Analyzing the building's heating requirements during winter months.
D) Comparing prices of different air conditioning brands.
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Calculating the cooling load is a crucial step in designing an air conditioning system, as it helps determine the appropriate size and capacity needed to maintain comfortable temperatures in the building.

Explanation:
In engineering design, understanding the specific requirements of the space (cooling or heating loads) ensures that the system meets the needs of the users. Maintenance scheduling, analyzing heating requirements, and comparing brands are not part of the initial design process.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A, C, or D, which are tasks related to maintenance or post-design considerations.
