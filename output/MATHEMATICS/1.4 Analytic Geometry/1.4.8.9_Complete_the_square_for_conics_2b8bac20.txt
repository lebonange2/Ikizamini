Problem 1 (Asdf):
Complete the square for the quadratic equation: x^2 + 6x + 5 = 0
A) (x + 3)^2 - 4
B) (x + 3)^2 + 4
C) (x - 3)^2 - 4
D) (x - 3)^2 + 4
A) Correct. The process involves adding and subtracting the square of half the coefficient of x.
B) Incorrect. Adding the wrong constant term results in a different parabola开口朝上。
C) Incorrect. Subtracting the wrong constant term results in a different parabola开口朝下。
D) Incorrect. Using the incorrect sign for the constant term yields an incorrect parabola开口方向错误。
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To complete the square, we need to add and subtract the square of half the coefficient of x (which is 6/2 = 3) inside the equation. The result is:
(x^2 + 6x + 9 - 9) + 5 = 0
This simplifies to:
(x + 3)^2 - 4 = 0
So, the correct completed square form is (x + 3)^2 - 4.

Explanation:
Completing the square helps us rewrite quadratic equations in vertex form, which makes it easier to analyze their properties and graph them. The process involves adding and subtracting a constant term to ensure the left side of the equation is a perfect square trinomial.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to subtract the same constant term added inside the square, leading to an incorrect parabola开口方向错误。

(3x^2 + 12x + 36 - 36) - 5 = 0
This simplifies to:
3(x + 6)^2 - 7/3 = 0
Dividing both sides by 3 gives us the correct completed square form:
(x + 2)^2 - 7/3 = 0
