Problem 1 (Asdf):
What is the equation of a parabola with vertex (2, -3) and directrix y = -1?
A) (x - 2)^2 = 4(y + 1)
B) (x - 2)^2 = 4(y - 1)
C) (x + 2)^2 = 4(y - 1)
D) (x + 2)^2 = 4(y + 1)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The vertex form of a parabola is (x - h)^2 = 4p(y - k), where (h, k) is the vertex and p determines the width of the parabola. Given vertex (2, -3), we have h = 2 and k = -3. The distance from the vertex to the directrix is |p|, which is equal to the distance between the vertex's y-coordinate and the directrix equation. In this case, |-3 - (-1)| = |-2| = 2, so p = 2 or -2. Since we want the parabola to open upwards (positive p), we choose p = 2. Plugging in h, k, and p gives us (x - 2)^2 = 4(y + 1).

Explanation:
The correct equation is obtained by plugging the vertex coordinates into the vertex form of a parabola and using the distance from the vertex to the directrix to determine the value of p.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to use the wrong sign for p, which would result in an incorrect opening direction of the parabola. In this case, choosing p = -2 would give a parabola that opens downwards.

Problem 2 (Asdf):
Find the equation of a parabola with vertex (1, 4) and directrix x = -1.
A) (y - 4)^2 = 8(x + 1)
B) (y - 4)^2 = 8(x - 1)
C) (y + 4)^2 = 8(x - 1)
D) (y + 4)^2 = 8(x + 1)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The vertex form of a parabola is (x - h)^2 = 4p(y - k), but since the directrix is parallel to the y-axis, we need to use the equivalent form: (y - k)^2 = 4p(x - h). Given vertex (1, 4), we have h = 1 and k = 4. The distance from the vertex to the directrix is |h - (-1)| = |1 + 1| = 2, so p = 2 or -2. Since we want the parabola to open to the right (positive x-direction), we choose p = 2. Plugging in h, k, and p gives us (y - 4)^2 = 8(x + 1).

Explanation:
The correct equation is obtained by using the vertex form of a parabola with the directrix parallel to the y-axis and plugging in the given vertex coordinates and distance from the vertex to the directrix.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to use the wrong sign for p, which would result in an incorrect horizontal direction of the parabola. In this case, choosing p = -2 would give a parabola that opens to the left.
