Problem 1 (InequalityToStandardForm1):
Rewrite the inequality in standard form (Ax + By = C): y > 3x - 2
A) 3x + y = -2
B) y - 3x = -2
C) 3x - y = 2
D) y - 3x = 2
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
To rewrite the inequality in standard form (Ax + By = C), isolate y. Add 3x to both sides: y + 3x > -2. Rearrange terms: y = 3x - 2. Subtract 3x: y - 3x = -2.

Explanation:
Positive coefficients for x and y, constant on right side.

Common mistake note:
Forget to change inequality sign with negative addition/subtraction.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (SlopeInterceptToStandardForm2):
Rewrite the equation in standard form (Ax + By = C): y = 4x + 5
A) 4x + y = 5
B) y - 4x = 5
C) 4x - y = -5
D) y - 4x = -5
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Convert slope-intercept form (y = mx + b) to standard form. Subtract 4x: y - 4x = 5.

Explanation:
Positive coefficients for x and y, constant on right side.

Common mistake note:
Forget to change sign when subtracting a variable.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (PointSlopeToStandardForm3):
Rewrite the equation in standard form (Ax + By = C): y - 2 = 2(x - 1)
A) 2x + y = 0
B) y - 2x = 0
C) 2x - y = 4
D) y - 2x = 4
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
Rewrite point-slope form to standard form. Distribute 2: y - 2 = 2x - 2. Add 2: y = 2x. Subtract 2x: y - 2x = 4.

Explanation:
Positive coefficients for x and y, constant on right side.

Common mistake note:
Forget to distribute slope in conversion.

Problem 4 (TwoPointFormToStandardForm4):
Rewrite the equation in standard form (Ax + By = C): 3x + 2y = 12, given points (2, 3) and (4, 0)
A) 3x + 2y = 12
B) 2x + 3y = 12
C) 3x - 2y = 12
D) 2x - 3y = 12
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation is already in standard form. No conversion needed.

Explanation:
Original equation is in standard form.

Common mistake note:
Think conversion is required, even though it's already in standard form.

Problem 5 (SystemOfEquations5):
Solve the system of equations in standard form: 2x + y = 7 and x - y = 1. Which equation should be manipulated first?
A) Manipulate the second equation to add y to both sides.
B) Manipulate the first equation to subtract 2x from both sides.
C) Manipulate the second equation to multiply by 2.
D) Manipulate the first equation to divide by 2.
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To eliminate y, add y to second equation: x = y + 1. Substitute into first equation and solve for y.

Explanation:
Add y to second equation for easier elimination of y.

Common mistake note:
Eliminate x instead of y, leading to complex manipulation.

Problem 6 (SystemOfEquations6):
Solve the system of equations in standard form: 4x + 3y = 12 and 2x - y = 5. Which method should be used?
A) Substitution
B) Elimination by addition
C) Elimination by multiplication
D) Graphing
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
Multiply second equation by 3: 6x - 3y = 15. Add to first equation: 10x = 27. Solve for x, then use substitution.

Explanation:
Multiply second equation to eliminate y with addition.

Common mistake note:
Try substitution, leading to complex manipulation.

Problem 7 (SystemOfEquations7):
Solve the system of equations in standard form: 5x - 2y = 8 and 3x + y = 1. Which method should be used?
A) Substitution
B) Elimination by addition
C) Elimination by multiplication
D) Graphing
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Solve second equation for y: y = 1 - 3x. Substitute into first equation, solve for x, then use substitution.

Explanation:
Use substitution as one equation can be easily solved for y.

Common mistake note:
Try elimination, leading to complex manipulation.
