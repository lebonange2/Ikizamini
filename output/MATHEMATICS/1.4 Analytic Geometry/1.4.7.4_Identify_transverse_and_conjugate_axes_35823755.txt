Problem 1 (Asd123):
In a hyperbola, which axis is perpendicular to the line passing through the foci?
A) Conjugate axis
B) Transverse axis
C) Major axis
D) Minor axis
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The transverse axis of a hyperbola is the line that passes through the center and intersects both vertices, while the conjugate axis is perpendicular to it and connects the two co-vertices. The major axis includes the foci but isn't perpendicular to the line connecting them. The minor axis is shorter than the transverse axis.

Explanation:
The transverse axis' orientation relative to the foci makes it the correct answer.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing 'Major axis' because it includes the foci, but it's not perpendicular to the line connecting them.

Problem 2 (Asd456):
In a hyperbola, which axis connects the vertices and is parallel to the directrix?
A) Conjugate axis
B) Transverse axis
C) Major axis
D) Minor axis
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The major axis of a hyperbola is the longest diameter, connecting the vertices and parallel to the directrix. The transverse axis is perpendicular to the major axis.

Explanation:
The term 'major axis' refers to the axis that aligns with the directrix and contains the foci.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing 'Transverse axis' because it connects the vertices, but it's not parallel to the directrix.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Asd789):
In the equation of a hyperbola β = 1/a^2 - 1/b^2, which axis is represented by 'a'?
A) Conjugate axis
B) Transverse axis
C) Major axis
D) Minor axis
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
In the standard form of a hyperbola equation, β = 1/a^2 - 1/b^2, 'a' represents the semi-major axis, which is half the length of the major axis. The major axis is parallel to the transverse axis.

Explanation:
The term 'a' in this context refers to a component of the major axis length.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing 'Transverse axis', but 'a' represents the semi-major axis, not the transverse axis.

Problem 4 (Asd012):
In an ellipse, which axis is parallel to the directrix and passes through the center?
A) Conjugate axis
B) Transverse axis
C) Major axis
D) Minor axis
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The major axis of an ellipse is the longest diameter, parallel to the directrix and passing through the center. The minor axis is perpendicular to the major axis.

Explanation:
The term 'major axis' refers to the axis that aligns with the directrix and contains the foci, in the case of an ellipse as well.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing 'Transverse axis', but it's not parallel to the directrix.

Problem 5 (Asd345):
In a hyperbola, which axis divides the shape into two congruent halves?
A) Conjugate axis
B) Transverse axis
C) Major axis
D) Minor axis
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The conjugate axis of a hyperbola is the line perpendicular to the transverse axis and divides the hyperbola into two congruent halves.

Explanation:
The conjugate axis creates symmetry by dividing the hyperbola into mirror images.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing 'Transverse axis', but it doesn't divide the hyperbola into congruent halves.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (Asd678):
In a parabola, which axis is perpendicular to the directrix and contains the focus?
A) Conjugate axis
B) Transverse axis
C) Major axis
D) Minor axis
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
In a parabola, there is no concept of conjugate or major/minor axes. The directrix and the focus are related to the transverse axis, which is perpendicular to the directrix and contains the focus.

Explanation:
A parabola has only one axis, the transverse axis, which connects the vertex with the focus.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing 'Conjugate axis' or 'Major axis', but these terms don't apply to a parabola.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (Asd901):
In the equation of an ellipse, y^2/a^2 + x^2/b^2 = 1, which axis is represented by 'b'?
A) Conjugate axis
B) Transverse axis
C) Major axis
D) Minor axis
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
In the standard form of an ellipse equation, y^2/a^2 + x^2/b^2 = 1, 'b' represents the semi-minor axis, which is half the length of the minor axis. The minor axis is perpendicular to the major axis.

Explanation:
The term 'b' in this context refers to a component of the minor axis length.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing 'Major axis', but 'b' represents the semi-minor axis, not the major axis.
