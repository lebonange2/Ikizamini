Problem 1 (:1):
Find the equation of the tangent line to the curve f(x) = x^2 at the point (2, 4).
A) (x - 2) = 4(y - 4)
B) y - 4 = 4(x - 2)
C) y - 4 = (x - 2)^2
D) x - 2 = (y - 4)^2
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The derivative of f(x) = x^2 is f'(x) = 2x. At the point (2, 4), the slope of the tangent line is m = f'(2) = 2 * 2 = 4. Using the point-slope form y - y1 = m(x - x1), we get y - 4 = 4(x - 2).

Explanation:
The derivative gives the slope of the tangent line at a given point. The point-slope form is used to find the equation of the line.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A, which represents the vertical line x = 2 instead of a tangent line with slope 4.

Problem 2 (:2):
Find the equation of the tangent line to the curve g(x) = √x at the point (9, 3).
A) y - 3 = (x - 9)/3
B) y - 3 = 3(x - 9)
C) y - 3 = (x - 9)^2
D) y - 9 = 3(x - 3)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The derivative of g(x) = √x is g'(x) = 1/(2√x). At the point (9, 3), the slope of the tangent line is m = g'(9) = 1/(2 * 3) = 1/6. Using the point-slope form y - y1 = m(x - x1), we get y - 3 = (1/6)(x - 9).

Explanation:
The derivative gives the slope of the tangent line at a given point. The point-slope form is used to find the equation of the line.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing B, which represents a line with an incorrect slope (3 instead of 1/6).

Problem 3 (:3):
Find the equation of the tangent line to h(x) = x^3 - 2x at the point (1, -1).
A) y + 1 = (x - 1)^2
B) y + 1 = 3(x - 1)
C) y + 1 = (x - 1)(x^2 + x + 1)
D) y - 1 = 3(x - 1)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The derivative of h(x) = x^3 - 2x is h'(x) = 3x^2 - 2. At the point (1, -1), the slope of the tangent line is m = h'(1) = 3 * 1^2 - 2 = 1. Using the point-slope form y - y1 = m(x - x1), we get y + 1 = 1(x - 1).

Explanation:
The derivative gives the slope of the tangent line at a given point. The point-slope form is used to find the equation of the line.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A, which represents an incorrect quadratic function instead of a linear tangent line.

Problem 4 (:4):
Find the slope of the tangent line to the curve f(x) = x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x at x = 3.
A) -1
B) 0
C) 1
D) 2
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The derivative of f(x) = x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x is f'(x) = 3x^2 - 12x + 9. To find the slope at x = 3, we evaluate f'(3): f'(3) = 3 * 3^2 - 12 * 3 + 9 = 0.

Explanation:
The derivative gives the slope of the tangent line at any point on the curve. We plug in x = 3 to find the slope.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or C, which represent slopes that are not correct for f(x) at x = 3.

Problem 5 (:5):
Find the equation of the tangent line to the curve g(x) = sin(x) at π/2.
A) y - 1 = cos(π/2)(x - π/2)
B) y - 0 = cos(π/2)(x - π/2)
C) y - 1 = sin(π/2)(x - π/2)
D) y - 0 = sin(π/2)(x - π/2)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The derivative of g(x) = sin(x) is g'(x) = cos(x). At x = π/2, the slope of the tangent line is m = g'(π/2) = cos(π/2) = 0. Using the point-slope form y - y1 = m(x - x1), we get y - 0 = 0(x - π/2).

Explanation:
The derivative gives the slope of the tangent line at any point on the curve. We plug in x = π/2 to find the slope and use the point-slope form.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or C, which represent incorrect slopes (cos(π/2) should be 0).

Problem 7 (:7):
Find the equation of the tangent line to f(x) = ln(x) at x = e.
A) y - 1 = 1/(x - e)
B) y - 0 = 1/e(1 - e)
C) y - 1 = 1/e(1 - e)
D) y - 0 = 1/(e - x)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The derivative of f(x) = ln(x) is f'(x) = 1/x. At x = e, the slope of the tangent line is m = f'(e) = 1/e. Using the point-slope form y - y1 = m(x - x1), we get y - 0 = 1/e(x - e).

Explanation:
The derivative gives the slope of the tangent line at any point on the curve. We plug in x = e to find the slope and use the point-slope form.

Common mistake note:
Common mistake: Choosing A or D, which represent incorrect slopes (1/(x - e) is not equivalent to 1/e(1 - e)).
