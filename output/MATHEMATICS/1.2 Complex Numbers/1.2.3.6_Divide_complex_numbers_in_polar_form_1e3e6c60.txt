Problem 1 (:1):
Find the quotient of the complex numbers (4cis(π/3)) and (2cis(π/6)). Express your answer in polar form.
A) (2 cis(π/2))
B) (4 cis(π/6))
C) (2 cis(π/3))
D) (√2 cis(π/3))
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
(4cis(π/3)) / (2cis(π/6)) = (2^2 cis(2π/3)) / (2cis(π/6)) = 2cis((2π/3) - (π/6)) = 2cis(π/2) = (√2 cis(π/2)). The correct answer is D.

Explanation:
To divide complex numbers in polar form, you divide the magnitudes and subtract the angles. In this case, the magnitude ratio is 4 / 2 = 2 and the angle difference is (2π/3) - (π/6) = π/2. The result is (√2 cis(π/2)).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to add the angles instead of subtracting them, which would lead to answer B.

Problem 2 (2):
Divide the complex numbers (3cis(5π/4)) and (1cis(π/4)). Express your answer in polar form.
A) (3 cis(π/2))
B) (3 cis(π))
C) (3 cis(π/4))
D) (3 cis(3π/4))
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(3cis(5π/4)) / (1cis(π/4)) = 3cis((5π/4) - (π/4)) = 3cis(π/2). The correct answer is A.

Explanation:
When dividing complex numbers in polar form, you divide the magnitudes and subtract the angles. Here, the magnitude ratio is 3 / 1 = 3, and the angle difference is (5π/4) - (π/4) = π/2. The result is (3 cis(π/2)).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to multiply the magnitudes instead of dividing them, which would lead to an incorrect answer.

Problem 3 (3):
Find the quotient of the complex numbers (6cis(-π/3)) and (2cis(π/3)). Express your answer in polar form.
A) (3 cis(0))
B) (3 cis(π))
C) (3 cis(π/3))
D) (3 cis(-π/3))
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(6cis(-π/3)) / (2cis(π/3)) = 3cis((-π/3) - (π/3)) = 3cis(0). The correct answer is A.

Explanation:
When dividing complex numbers, you divide the magnitudes and subtract the angles. Here, the magnitude ratio is 6 / 2 = 3, and the angle difference is (-π/3) - (π/3) = 0. The result is (3 cis(0)).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to add the angles instead of subtracting them, which would lead to an incorrect answer.

Problem 4 (4):
Divide the complex numbers (8cis(2π)) and (4cis(π/2)). Express your answer in polar form.
A) (2 cis(3π/2))
B) (2 cis(π))
C) (2 cis(π/2))
D) (2 cis(0))
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
(8cis(2π)) / (4cis(π/2)) = 2cis((2π) - (π/2)) = 2cis(3π/2). The correct answer is D.

Explanation:
When dividing complex numbers, you divide the magnitudes and subtract the angles. Here, the magnitude ratio is 8 / 4 = 2, and the angle difference is (2π) - (π/2) = 3π/2. The result is (2 cis(3π/2)).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that dividing by a multiple of 2π does not change the complex number, which would lead to an incorrect answer.

Problem 5 (5):
Find the quotient of the complex numbers (10cis(7π/6)) and (5cis(π/3)). Express your answer in polar form.
A) (2 cis(π))
B) (2 cis(π/2))
C) (2 cis(π/3))
D) (2 cis(5π/6))
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
(10cis(7π/6)) / (5cis(π/3)) = 2cis(((7π/6) - (π/3)) mod 2π) = 2cis(π/2). The correct answer is B.

Explanation:
When dividing complex numbers, you divide the magnitudes and subtract the angles. Here, the magnitude ratio is 10 / 5 = 2, and the angle difference is (7π/6) - (π/3) = π/2. Since we're working in polar form, we need to express the angle within the range of [0, 2π). The result is (2 cis(π/2)).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to adjust the angle to be within the range [0, 2π), which would lead to an incorrect answer.
