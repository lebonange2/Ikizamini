Problem 1 (One):
Let z = a + bi, where a and b are real numbers. What is the value of z + ar{z}?
A) a - bi
B) 2a
C) 2b
D) a^2 + b^2
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
z + ar{z} = (a + bi) + (a - bi) = 2a

Explanation:
The sum of a complex number and its conjugate results in twice the real part.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to add the real parts and imaginary parts separately, which would result in A. However, this violates the distributive property of addition over subtraction.

Correct Answer: A

Solution:
