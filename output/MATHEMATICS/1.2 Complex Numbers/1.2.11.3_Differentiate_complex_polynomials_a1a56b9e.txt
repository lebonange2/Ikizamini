Problem 1 (:1):
Find the derivative of the function f(x) = 4x^3 - 7x^2 + 9x - 2.
A) -12x^2 + 14x - 9
B) 12x^2 - 14x + 9
C) 4x^2 - 14x + 8
D) 12x - 14
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
f'(x) = d/dx(4x^3) - d/dx(7x^2) + d/dx(9x) - d/dx(2)
f'(x) = 12x^2 - 14x + 9

Explanation:
The derivative of a polynomial is found by applying the power rule to each term. The power rule states that if f(x) = x^n, then f'(x) = nx^(n-1).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is forgetting to apply the negative sign when differentiating an even-powered term with an odd exponent (e.g., -7x^2).

Problem 2 (:2):
Find the derivative of g(x) = 5x^4 - 3x^2 + 6.
A) 20x^3 - 6x
B) 15x^3 - 6x
C) 20x^3 - 6
D) 15x^3 - 6
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
g'(x) = d/dx(5x^4) - d/dx(3x^2) + d/dx(6)
g'(x) = 20x^3 - 6x

Explanation:
Again, apply the power rule to each term. Remember that when differentiating a constant term (like 6), the derivative is zero.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is forgetting to multiply the coefficient of the x-term by its exponent.

Problem 3 (:3):
Find the derivative of h(x) = -2x^5 + 4x^3 - 9x + 1.
A) -10x^4 + 12x^2 - 9
B) 10x^4 - 12x^2 + 9
C) -10x^4 + 12x^2 - 8
D) 10x^3 - 12x + 9
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
h'(x) = d/dx(-2x^5) + d/dx(4x^3) - d/dx(9x) + d/dx(1)
h'(x) = -10x^4 + 12x^2 - 9

Explanation:
Apply the power rule and remember to multiply negative coefficients by their respective exponents.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is forgetting to handle the negative sign in front of the x-term correctly.

Problem 4 (:4):
Find the derivative of p(x) = 3x^6 - 7x^4 + 9x^2 - 1.
A) 18x^5 - 28x^3 + 18x
B) 18x^5 - 28x^3 + 18
C) 18x^5 - 28x^4 + 18x
D) 18x^5 - 28x^3 - 18
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
p'(x) = d/dx(3x^6) - d/dx(7x^4) + d/dx(9x^2) - d/dx(1)
p'(x) = 18x^5 - 28x^3 + 18x

Explanation:
Apply the power rule and be careful with the coefficients and exponents.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is mixing up the exponents when differentiating terms.

Problem 5 (:5):
Find the derivative of q(x) = x^7 - 2x^5 + 3x^3 - 4x + 5.
A) 7x^6 - 10x^4 + 9x^2 - 4
B) 7x^6 - 10x^4 + 9x - 4
C) 7x^6 - 10x^4 + 9x^2 - 5
D) 7x^6 - 10x^3 + 9x - 4
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
q'(x) = d/dx(x^7) - d/dx(2x^5) + d/dx(3x^3) - d/dx(4x) + d/dx(5)
q'(x) = 7x^6 - 10x^4 + 9x^2 - 4

Explanation:
Apply the power rule and remember that the derivative of a constant term is zero.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is forgetting to include the correct exponents when differentiating.

Problem 6 (:6):
Find the derivative of r(x) = -4x^9 + 6x^7 - 8x^5 + 10x^3 - 12x.
A) -36x^8 + 42x^6 - 40x^4 + 30x^2 - 12
B) 36x^8 - 42x^6 + 40x^4 - 30x^2 - 12
C) -36x^8 + 42x^6 - 40x^4 + 30x^2 + 12
D) 36x^8 - 42x^6 + 40x^4 - 30x^2 + 12
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
r'(x) = d/dx(-4x^9) + d/dx(6x^7) - d/dx(8x^5) + d/dx(10x^3) - d/dx(12x)
r'(x) = -36x^8 + 42x^6 - 40x^4 + 30x^2 - 12

Explanation:
Apply the power rule and handle negative coefficients correctly.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is mishandling the negative sign in front of the x-term.

Problem 7 (:7):
Find the derivative of s(x) = 2x^10 - 3x^8 + 4x^6 - 5x^4 + 6x^2.
A) 20x^9 - 24x^7 + 24x^5 - 20x^3 + 12x
B) 20x^9 - 24x^7 + 24x^5 - 20x^3 - 12
C) 20x^9 - 24x^7 + 24x^5 - 20x^3 + 6
D) 20x^9 - 24x^7 + 24x^5 - 20x^3 - 12x
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
s'(x) = d/dx(2x^10) - d/dx(3x^8) + d/dx(4x^6) - d/dx(5x^4) + d/dx(6x^2)
s'(x) = 20x^9 - 24x^7 + 24x^5 - 20x^3 + 12x

Explanation:
Apply the power rule and be careful with the coefficients.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is mixing up the exponents when differentiating terms.
