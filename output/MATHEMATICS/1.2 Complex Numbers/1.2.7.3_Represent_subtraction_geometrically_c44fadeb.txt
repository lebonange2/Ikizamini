Problem 1 (IncrementalSubtraction):
Which diagram best represents the expression 5 - 2?
A) [A diagram with 5 circles, 2 crossed out, leaving 3 circles remaining]
B) [A diagram with 5 circles, 3 crossed out, leaving 2 circles remaining]
C) [A diagram with 2 circles, 5 crossed out, leaving no circles remaining]
D) [A diagram with no circles]
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The expression 5 - 2 represents taking away 2 from 5. The correct representation shows 5 circles and then 2 of them are crossed out, leaving 3 circles remaining.

Explanation:
Subtraction is the inverse operation of addition; it shows how many more are needed to reach a certain quantity. In this case, we start with 5 and remove 2, which leaves us with 3.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be choosing B, as some students might misinterpret the subtraction as adding 2 circles to an empty set instead of removing them from an existing set.

Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The expression 10 - 7 represents taking away 7 from 10. The correct representation shows 10 squares and then shades 7 of them, leaving 3 squares unshaded.

Problem 3 (NegativeSubtraction):
Which diagram represents the expression -5 - (-2)?
A) [A diagram with 5 circles, 2 crossed out, leaving 3 circles remaining]
B) [A diagram with 5 circles, 3 crossed out, leaving 2 circles remaining]
C) [A diagram with 2 circles, 5 crossed out, leaving no circles remaining]
D) [A diagram with 2 circles, 3 crossed out, leaving -1 circle (indicated by a negative sign)]
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The expression -5 - (-2) represents subtracting a negative number from another negative number. The correct representation shows 5 negative circles and then removes 2 of them, resulting in -3.

Explanation:
When subtracting a negative, it's equivalent to adding the positive value of that number. So, -5 - (-2) is the same as -5 + 2 = -3.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be choosing A or B, where students might not understand how to handle negative numbers in a subtraction context.
