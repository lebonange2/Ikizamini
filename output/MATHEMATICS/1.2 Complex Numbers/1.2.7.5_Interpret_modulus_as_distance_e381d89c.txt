Problem 1 (One):
What is the value of |3 - 7|?
A) -4
B) 4
C) 10
D) 0
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The modulus (absolute value) of a number represents its distance from zero on the number line. So, |3 - 7| is the distance between 3 and 7 on the number line. Since 7 is to the right of 3, the distance is 4 (7 - 3 = 4).

Explanation:
The modulus operation disregards the sign, so it's only concerned with how far apart the numbers are.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to subtract 3 from 7 without considering the absolute value, which would result in -4 (B is the correct answer).

Problem 2 (Two):
If |x| = 9, what could x be?
A) -9
B) 0
C) 18
D) ±9
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The modulus of x being equal to 9 means that x is either 9 or -9 because both are 9 units away from zero on the number line.

Explanation:
The absolute value represents distance, so any number with a distance of 9 from zero will have an absolute value of 9.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose only one option (like -9 or 9) without considering that both positive and negative values can have the same modulus.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (Three):
What is the distance between -5 and 3 on the number line?
A) -8
B) 2
C) 8
D) 5
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The distance between two numbers on a number line is found by taking the absolute value of their difference. So, |-5 - 3| = |-8| = 8.

Explanation:
When calculating the distance, we don't consider the direction; we only care about how far apart the points are.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to subtract -5 from 3 without considering the absolute value, which would result in 8 (but with a positive sign).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (Four):
If |x - 2| = 6, what is x?
A) 4
B) -4
C) 8
D) ±4
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The equation |x - 2| = 6 means that x is either 4 units to the left or 4 units to the right of 2 on the number line. So, x could be -4 or 6.

Explanation:
When solving an equation with modulus, we need to consider both positive and negative solutions.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to solve for only one value (like 8) without considering that there are two possible values for x.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 5 (Five):
What is the distance between 0 and -7 on the number line?
A) 7
B) -7
C) 14
D) 0
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The distance between any number and zero on the number line is simply the absolute value of that number. In this case, |-7| = 7.

Explanation:
When calculating the distance from zero, we only consider the magnitude of the number.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose -7 as the distance, not realizing that distances are always non-negative.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (Six):
If |x + 5| = 10, what is x?
A) -15
B) 5
C) ±5
D) ±15
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation |x + 5| = 10 means that x is either 15 units to the left or 15 units to the right of -5 on the number line. Since we're looking for a distance of 10, x must be -15 (since it's closer to -5).

Explanation:
When solving an equation with modulus, we need to consider both positive and negative solutions, but in this case, one solution is closer to the reference point.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose ±5 without considering that x must be further from 0 to achieve a distance of 10.

Problem 7 (Seven):
What is the modulus of -24?
A) -24
B) 24
C) 48
D) 0
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The modulus (absolute value) of a number represents its distance from zero on the number line. So, |-24| is 24 because it's 24 units away from zero.

Explanation:
The absolute value operation always returns a non-negative value, regardless of the sign of the original number.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to choose -24, not realizing that the modulus operation disregards the negative sign.
