Problem 1 (Asdf):
What is the complex conjugate of the number 3 + 4i?
A) 2 + 4i
B) 3 - 4i
C) 4 - 3i
D) 7 + 0i
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The complex conjugate of a complex number (a + bi) is obtained by changing the sign of the imaginary part. So for the number 3 + 4i, the complex conjugate is 3 - 4i.

Explanation:
The complex conjugate operation only affects the imaginary part, not the real part.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to change both parts of the complex number, which results in an incorrect conjugate.

Problem 2 (Bghj):
What is the product of a complex number and its complex conjugate?
A) a^2 + b^2
B) a^2 - b^2
C) 2ab
D) 0
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The product of a complex number (a + bi) and its conjugate (a - bi) is (a + bi)(a - bi) = a^2 - (bi)^2 = a^2 - b^2i^2. Since i^2 = -1, the result simplifies to a^2 + b^2.

Explanation:
The product of a complex number and its conjugate is always a real number.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to forget that i^2 = -1, leading to an incorrect product.

Problem 3 (Qwer):
What is the complex conjugate of the number -2i?
A) 2i
B) -2i
C) 0
D) -2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The complex conjugate of a pure imaginary number bi is simply -bi. So for the number -2i, the complex conjugate is 2i.

Explanation:
For a pure imaginary number, the conjugate only changes the sign of the coefficient.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to think that the conjugate should be positive, which is not correct for negative coefficients.

Problem 4 (Yuiop):
What is the complex conjugate of the number 5?
A) 5i
B) -5
C) 0
D) 5
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The complex conjugate of a real number is the number itself. So for the number 5, the complex conjugate is also 5.

Explanation:
A real number has no imaginary part, so its conjugate remains unchanged.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to think that the conjugate should have an imaginary part, but this is not true for real numbers.
