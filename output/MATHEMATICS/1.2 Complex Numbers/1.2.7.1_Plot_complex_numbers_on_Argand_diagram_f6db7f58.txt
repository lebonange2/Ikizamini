Problem 1 (x1):
Plot the complex number -3 + 4i on the Argand diagram.
A) [2, 3]
B) [-3, 4]
C) [-3, -4]
D) [3, -4]
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The complex number is in the form of a + bi. The real part (-3) corresponds to the x-coordinate and the imaginary part (4) corresponds to the y-coordinate on the Argand diagram. So, the correct plot is at the point [-3, 4].

Explanation:
An Argand diagram plots complex numbers as points in the Cartesian plane, with the real part on the horizontal axis and the imaginary part on the vertical axis.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to mix up the real and imaginary parts. Remember that -3 is the x-coordinate and 4 is the y-coordinate.

Problem 2 (x2):
Find the coordinates of the point representing the complex number 5 - 6i on the Argand diagram.
A) [6, -5]
B) [5, -6]
C) [-5, 6]
D) [-6, 5]
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The complex number is in the form of a + bi. The real part (5) corresponds to the x-coordinate and the imaginary part (-6) corresponds to the y-coordinate on the Argand diagram. So, the correct coordinates are [5, -6].

Explanation:
In an Argand diagram, the real part determines the horizontal position and the imaginary part determines the vertical position.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to reverse the signs of the real and imaginary parts. Ensure that you plot the correct sign for each component.

Problem 3 (x3):
Plot the complex number 2i on the Argand diagram.
A) [0, 2]
B) [2, 0]
C) [-2, 0]
D) [0, -2]
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The complex number is in the form of a + bi. Since there is no real part (a = 0), the point will be on the imaginary axis. The imaginary part (b = 2) determines the y-coordinate. So, the correct plot is at [0, 2].

Explanation:
When the real part is zero, the complex number lies solely on the imaginary axis.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to assume that the point would be at [2, 0], but remember that when there's no real part, the x-coordinate should be zero.

Problem 4 (x4):

B) [-4, 0]
C) [0, -4]
D) [0, 4]
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The complex number is in the form of a + bi. The real part (-4) corresponds to the x-coordinate and the imaginary part (0) means it lies on the horizontal axis. So, the correct coordinates are [-4, 0].

Explanation:
When the imaginary part is zero, the complex number lies solely on the real axis.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to assume that the point would be at [0, -4], but remember that when there's no imaginary part, the y-coordinate should be zero.

Solution:

Problem 6 (x6):
Find the coordinates of the point representing the complex number -2 - 3i on the Argand diagram.
A) [2, 3]
B) [-2, -3]
C) [2, -3]
D) [-2, 3]
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The complex number is in the form of a + bi. The real part (-2) corresponds to the x-coordinate and the imaginary part (-3) corresponds to the y-coordinate on the Argand diagram. So, the correct coordinates are [-2, -3].

Explanation:
When both parts have negative signs, they represent a point in the third quadrant of the Argand diagram.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget the negative sign for one or both components. Remember that both the real and imaginary parts should retain their signs.

Problem 7 (x7):
Plot the complex number -1 + √3i on the Argand diagram.
A) [1, √3]
B) [-1, -√3]
C) [-1, √3]
D) [1, -√3]
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The complex number is in the form of a + bi. The real part (-1) corresponds to the x-coordinate and the imaginary part (√3) corresponds to the y-coordinate on the Argand diagram. So, the correct plot is at [-1, √3].

Explanation:
When plotting irrational numbers like √3i, it's important to retain the exact form.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to try to simplify √3i or convert it to a decimal. In this case, it should be plotted as is.
