Problem 1 (Asdf):
Express the complex number in exponential form: -2
correct answer: C
choices:
A) 2(cos(180°) + isin(180°))
B) 2e^(iπ)
C) 2e^(-iπ)
D) -2e^(iπ)
A) Common mistake: Using the wrong angle. The correct angle for -2 is 180°, which corresponds to π in radians.
B) Common mistake: Not considering the negative sign. Since we have -2, the argument should be in the third quadrant, leading to π instead of -π.
C) Correct answer. The exponential form of -2 is 2e^(-iπ), as it correctly represents a point on the complex plane with magnitude 2 and angle -180° or π radians counterclockwise from the positive x-axis.
D) Common mistake: Not accounting for the negative sign properly. This choice has the correct angle but not the correct magnitude.
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The exponential form of a complex number -2 is given by r * e^(iθ), where r is the magnitude and θ is the argument in radians. The magnitude of -2 is 2, and its argument in degrees is 180° or π radians counterclockwise from the positive x-axis. Since it's in the third quadrant, the angle should be negative: -π. Therefore, the exponential form is 2e^(-iπ).

Explanation:
This question tests the understanding of expressing a complex number in polar form (exponential form) and correctly accounting for the sign of the number.

Common mistake note:
Common mistakes include using the wrong angle (not considering the negative sign) or not accounting for the sign of the number when converting to exponential form.
