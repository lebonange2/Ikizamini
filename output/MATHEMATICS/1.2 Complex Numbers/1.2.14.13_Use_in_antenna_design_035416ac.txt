Problem 1 (Asd1e8f90):
What is the primary function of an antenna in a communication system?
A) To receive audio signals
B) To convert electrical energy into radio waves
C) To amplify signal strength
D) To store data for transmission
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The primary function of an antenna is to convert electrical energy into radio waves, allowing the communication system to transmit and receive signals. Choice A refers to a receiver's function, choice C is related to amplifiers, and choice D is not the role of an antenna.

Explanation:
Antennas are essential components in wireless communication systems as they facilitate the transmission and reception of electromagnetic waves.

Problem 2 (Asd1e8f91):
Which type of antenna is commonly used for long-range radio transmission?
A) Dipole antenna
B) Loop antenna
C) Yagi- Uda antenna
D) Helical antenna
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
A Yagi- Uda antenna is designed specifically for long-range radio transmission due to its high gain and directionality. Dipole antennas are basic but not as directional, loop antennas are used for receiving specific frequencies, and helical antennas are more suitable for satellite communication.

Explanation:
Yagi- Uda antennas, also known as Yagi antennas, are popular in television and radio broadcast applications because of their ability to focus energy in a particular direction.

Common mistake note:
Choosing A might happen when混淆 the performance of dipole and Yagi- Uda antennas.

Problem 5 (Asd1e8f94):
What is the relationship between an antenna's frequency and its length?
A) Frequency is directly proportional to length
B) Frequency is inversely proportional to length
C) Frequency is independent of length
D) Frequency is equal to length in meters
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The relationship between an antenna's frequency and its length is inverse; as the frequency increases, the optimal length for efficient operation decreases. This is based on the resonance principle.

Explanation:
Antenna design often involves finding the right length to achieve resonance at a specific frequency.

Problem 6 (Asd1e8f95):
In an antenna, what determines the directionality or 'beamwidth'?
A) Frequency of the signal
B) Material used in construction
C) Shape and size of the antenna elements
D) Distance between the transmitter and receiver
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The shape and size of the antenna elements determine its directionality or beamwidth. Different designs, such as dipole, Yagi- Uda, or parabolic antennas, have different beamwidths based on their structure.

Explanation:
Directional antennas are designed to focus energy in a specific direction, which is influenced by their physical design.
