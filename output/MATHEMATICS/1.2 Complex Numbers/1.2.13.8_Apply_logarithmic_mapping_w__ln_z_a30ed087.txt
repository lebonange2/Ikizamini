Problem 1 (Asdf):
Find the value of w if z = e^4.
A) w = 2
B) w = 3
C) w = 4
D) w = 5
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The logarithmic mapping is given by w = ln z. To find the value of w when z = e^4, substitute z into the equation: w = ln(e^4). Using ln(a^b) = b * ln(a), we get w = 4 * ln(e). Since ln(e) = 1, w = 4 * 1 = 4. Answer: C.

Explanation:
ln(e^x) = x is a property of natural logarithms used to solve the problem.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is choosing A: w = 2,混淆properties with ln(x^e) ≠ x.

Problem 2 (Bvbn):
Evaluate w: w = ln(100).
A) w = 2
B) w = 3
C) w = 4
D) w = 5
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To find w, recognize that e^2 ≈ 7.39 and e^3 ≈ 20.08; thus, e^2 < 100 and e^3 > 100. So, w = ln(100) = ln(10^2) = 2 * ln(10). Since ln(10) is approximately 2.30, the exact value doesn't change the result when multiplied by 2. Answer: A.

Explanation:
ln(a^b) = b * ln(a) simplifies w = ln(100) to w = 2 * ln(10).

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is choosing B: w = 3, confusing with ln(e^3) = 3.

Problem 3 (Cvbn):
Solve for w: ln(w) = 2.
A) w = e^2
B) w = e
C) w = 2e
D) w = 1/e
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Exponentiate both sides with base e: e^(ln(w)) = e^2. By definition, e^(ln(x)) = x. So, w = e^2. Answer: A.

Explanation:
The property e^(ln(x)) = x undoes the logarithmic operation to find w.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is choosing B: w = e, forgetting to exponentiate after taking ln(w).

Problem 4 (Dvbn):
Find w if z = 1/e.
A) w = -1
B) w = 0
C) w = 1
D) w = 2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Substitute z into the equation: w = ln(1/e). Using ln(a/b) = ln(a) - ln(b), we get w = ln(1) - ln(e). Since ln(1) = 0 and ln(e) = 1, w = -1. Answer: A.

Explanation:
ln(a/b) = ln(a) - ln(b) separates the logarithm of a quotient into simpler terms.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is choosing C: w = 1,混淆with ln(e) = 1.

Problem 6 (Fvbn):
Solve for w: ln(w^2) = 4.
A) w = e^2
B) w = ±e^2
C) w = 2e^2
D) w = ±2e^2
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Exponentiate both sides: w^2 = e^4. Take square root: w = ±√(e^4) = ±e^2. Answer: B.

Explanation:
ln(a^b) and √(a^b) = (√a)^b lead to two solutions, positive and negative.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is choosing A: w = e^2, forgetting that ln(w^2) affects the solution.
