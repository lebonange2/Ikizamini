Problem 1 (Asd1):
Simplify the expression: (3 + √5) / (3 - √5)
A) (3 + √5)^2
B) (3 - √5)^2
C) (3 + √5)(3 + √5)
D) (3 - √5)(3 + √5)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
To simplify the expression using conjugates, we multiply the numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator. The conjugate of 3 - √5 is 3 + √5. So we have:

(3 + √5) / (3 - √5) * (3 + √5) / (3 + √5)

This simplifies to:

[(3 + √5)^2] / [(3 - √5)(3 + √5)]

The denominator becomes 9 - (√5)^2 = 9 - 5 = 4.

So the simplified expression is (3 + √5)^2 / 4.

Explanation:
Using the conjugate in division helps eliminate the square root from the denominator by creating a difference of squares in the denominator, which simplifies to a rational number.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to multiply both the numerator and the denominator by (3 - √5) instead of its conjugate (3 + √5), resulting in an incorrect expression.
