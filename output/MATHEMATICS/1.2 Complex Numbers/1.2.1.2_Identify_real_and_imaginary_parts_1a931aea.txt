Problem 1 ([1]):
What are the real and imaginary parts of the complex number 3 + 4i?
A) (3, 0)
B) (4, 3)
C) (3, 4)
D) (0, 4)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The real part is the coefficient of the real unit (1) and the imaginary part is the coefficient of the imaginary unit (i). For the complex number 3 + 4i, the real part is 3 and the imaginary part is 4. Therefore, the correct answer is C: (3, 4).

Explanation:
Complex numbers are written in the form a + bi, where 'a' is the real part and 'b' is the coefficient of i, which represents the imaginary part.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to mix up the real and imaginary parts. Choice A has the correct imaginary part but assigns it as the real part, while choice B mixes the numbers from both parts.

Problem 2 ([2]):
What are the real and imaginary parts of the complex number -2i?
A) (0, 2)
B) (2, 0)
C) (-2, 0)
D) (0, -2)
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The complex number -2i has no real part (the coefficient of the real unit is 0) and an imaginary part of -2 (the coefficient of i). Therefore, the correct answer is D: (0, -2).

Explanation:
When a complex number only has an imaginary term, its real part is zero.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to assign the -2 as the real part instead of the imaginary part. Choice A and B both make this mistake by assigning the -2 as the real part.

Problem 3 ([3]):
What are the real and imaginary parts of the complex number 5?
A) (5, 0)
B) (0, 5)
C) (5, 1)
D) (-5, 0)
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The complex number 5 can be written as 5 + 0i. The real part is 5 and the imaginary part is 0 (since there's no i term). Therefore, the correct answer is A: (5, 0).

Explanation:
When a complex number has no imaginary term, its imaginary part is zero.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to assign 1 as the imaginary part or to assign -5 as the real part. Choices C and D make these mistakes.

Problem 5 ([5]):
What are the real and imaginary parts of the complex number -4 + 6i?
A) (4, 6)
B) (-4, -6)
C) (-4, 6)
D) (6, -4)
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The real part of the complex number -4 + 6i is -4 and the imaginary part is 6. Therefore, the correct answer is C: (-4, 6).

Explanation:
When writing a complex number in standard form (a + bi), the first term represents the real part and the second term (with i) represents the imaginary part.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake would be to mix up the signs of the real and imaginary parts. Choice B has the correct absolute values but with opposite signs.
