Problem 1 (Asymptotes):
The function f(z) = (z^2 - 4)/(z + 2) has a vertical asymptote at z =?
A) -4
B) 0
C) 2
D) ∞
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The function f(z) has a vertical asymptote where the denominator is zero. Thus, we set the denominator equal to zero and solve for z: z + 2 = 0, which gives us z = -2.

Explanation:
Vertical asymptotes occur at values of z that make the denominator zero.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to choose A or B because they are factors of the quadratic term in the numerator. However, these do not affect the location of the vertical asymptote, which is determined by the denominator.

Problem 2 (Holes):
The function g(z) = (z^3 - 1)/(z - 1)^2 has a hole at z =?
A) -1
B) 0
C) 1
D) ∞
Correct Answer: C

Solution:
The function g(z) has a hole where the denominator is zero, but it's raised to an even power (in this case 2), which means the singularity is removable. So, we look for values of z that make the denominator zero: z - 1 = 0, giving us z = 1.

Explanation:
Holes occur when a factor in the denominator is squared or raised to an even power, making the singularity removable.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to choose A or B because they are related to the cubic term in the numerator. However, these do not affect the location of the hole, which is determined by the denominator.

Problem 3 (Zeros):
The function h(z) = z^3 + 8 has a zero at z =?
A) -2
B) 0
C) 2
D) ∞
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
To find the zeros of h(z), we set it equal to zero and solve for z: z^3 + 8 = 0. Factoring out 8 gives us (z + 2)(z^2 - 2z + 4) = 0. The only real root is z = -2.

Explanation:
Zeros of a function are the values of z that make the function equal to zero.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to choose B or C because they are related to the quadratic factor in the expanded form, but those roots are complex. The question asks for real zeros only.

Problem 6 (Periodicity):
The function l(z) = cos(2z) has a period of ? π
A) 1/2
B) 1
C) 2
D) 4
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The period of l(z) = cos(bz) is 2π/b. For l(z) = cos(2z), b = 2, so the period is 2π/2 = π.

Explanation:
The period of a trigonometric function is the length of one complete cycle of its graph.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to choose A or C because they are related to the coefficient in front of z, but they do not give the correct period.

Problem 7 (Exponential Growth):
The function m(z) = 3^z has an exponential growth rate of?
A) ln(2)
B) ln(3)
C) ln(4)
D) ln(5)
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The growth rate of an exponential function m(z) = a^z is the base logarithm, ln(a). For m(z) = 3^z, a = 3, so the growth rate is ln(3).

Explanation:
The growth rate of an exponential function with base a is given by ln(a), which measures the change in output per unit increase in input.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to choose A or C because they are related to other logarithms, but they do not represent the correct growth rate for the base 3.
