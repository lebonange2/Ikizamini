Problem 1 (:num1):
Plot the complex number 3 + 4i on the complex plane.
A) [4, 3]
B) [3, 4]
C) [-4, -3]
D) [-3, -4]
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The complex number 3 + 4i has a real part of 3 and an imaginary part of 4. In the complex plane, plot the point (3, 4).

Explanation:
Remember that in the complex plane, the first number represents the real part and the second number represents the imaginary part.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to mix up the real and imaginary parts. Ensure the real part corresponds to the x-axis and the imaginary part to the y-axis.

Problem 2 (num2):
Plot the complex number -2 - 5i on the complex plane.
A) [5, -2]
B) [-2, -5]
C) [-5, 2]
D) [2, 5]
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The complex number -2 - 5i has a real part of -2 and an imaginary part of -5. Plot the point (-2, -5) on the complex plane.

Explanation:
When dealing with negative numbers, remember that a negative real part is to the left of the origin and a negative imaginary part is below the origin.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget about the sign changes for negative values. Plot negative real parts to the left and negative imaginary parts below.

Problem 3 (num3):
Plot the complex number 0 + 7i on the complex plane.
A) [7, 0]
B) [0, 7]
C) [-7, 0]
D) [0, -7]
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The complex number 0 + 7i has a real part of 0 and an imaginary part of 7. Plot the point (0, 7) on the y-axis.

Explanation:
When plotting a complex number with a zero real part, it lies exactly on the y-axis.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget that a zero real part places the point solely on the y-axis. Don't include an x-coordinate.

Problem 5 (num5):
Plot the complex number -4 + 0i on the complex plane.
A) [4, 0]
B) [-4, 0]
C) [-4, -4]
D) [0, -4]
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
The complex number -4 + 0i has a real part of -4 and an imaginary part of 0. Plot the point (-4, 0) on the x-axis.

Explanation:
When plotting a complex number with a zero imaginary part, it lies exactly on the x-axis. A negative real part places it to the left of the origin.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget about the sign change for the negative real part. Plot negative real parts to the left.

Solution:
The complex number 1 + i√3 has a real part of 1 and an imaginary part of √3. Plot the point (1, √3) on the complex plane.

Common mistake note:
