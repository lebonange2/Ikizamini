Problem 1 (:1):
Solve the equation: (3 + 4i)x = -7 + 5i
A) -1 + i
B) 1 - i
C) 2 + 3i
D) -2 - 3i
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(3 + 4i)x = -7 + 5i
Divide both sides by (3 + 4i):
x = rac{-7 + 5i}{3 + 4i}
To divide complex numbers, multiply the numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator:
x = rac{(-7 + 5i)(3 - 4i)}{(3 + 4i)(3 - 4i)}
x = rac{-21 + 12i + 15i - 20}{9 + 16}
x = rac{-41 + 27i}{25}
x = -1 + i

Explanation:
To solve the equation, we divide both sides by the coefficient of x. To avoid imaginary denominators, we multiply the numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the complex number in the denominator.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to forget to multiply the entire equation by the conjugate of the denominator when dividing by a complex number.

Problem 2 (:2):
Solve for z: (1 - i)z = 4 + 6i
A) 5 - 2i
B) 5 + 2i
C) -5 - 2i
D) -5 + 2i
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
(1 - i)z = 4 + 6i
Divide both sides by (1 - i):
z = rac{4 + 6i}{1 - i}
To divide complex numbers, multiply the numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator:
z = rac{(4 + 6i)(1 + i)}{(1 - i)(1 + i)}
z = rac{4 + 4i + 6i + 6}{1 - (-1)}
z = rac{10 + 10i}{2}
z = 5 + 5i
Divide both parts by 2:
z = 5 + 2i

Explanation:
We divide the equation by the coefficient of z, and then multiply the numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the complex number in the denominator. After simplifying, we get the correct answer.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to incorrectly distribute the multiplication when multiplying the numerator and denominator by the conjugate.

Problem 4 (:4):
Solve for z: (3i)z = 6 - 9i
A) 2 - 3i

D) 1 + i
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
(3i)z = 6 - 9i
Divide both sides by (3i):
z = rac{6 - 9i}{3i}
To divide complex numbers, multiply the numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator:
z = rac{(6 - 9i)(-i)}{(3i)(-i)}
x = rac{-6i + 9}{-3}

Explanation:
We divide the equation by the coefficient of z, and then multiply the numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the complex number in the denominator. After simplifying, we get the correct answer.
