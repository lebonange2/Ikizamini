Problem 1 (1):
Write the equation of a line with a slope of 3 and a y-intercept of -5.
A) y = 3x + 5
B) y = 3x - 5
C) y = 5x + 3
D) y = -3x - 5
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
Using the slope-intercept form y = mx + b, where m is the slope and b is the y-intercept, plug in the given slope (3) and y-intercept (-5). This results in the equation y = 3x - 5.

Explanation:
The slope-intercept form equation is derived directly from the parameters given: slope and y-intercept.

Common mistake note:
A common mistake is to mix up the sign of the y-intercept, leading to the wrong answer such as option A.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 2 (2):
Find the equation of a circle with center at (0, 0) and radius 4.
A) x^2 + y^2 = 16
B) x^2 + y^2 = 8
C) x^2 + y^2 = 4
D) x^2 + y^2 = 2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The equation of a circle with center at (h, k) and radius r is (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2. Using center (0,0) and radius 4, the equation is x^2 + y^2 = 4^2 = 16.

Explanation:
This problem uses the standard circle equation derived from its parameters: center and radius.

Common mistake note:
Students might confuse the radius with the diameter, incorrectly using a value of 8 instead of 16.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 3 (3):
Determine the equation for a parabola opening upward with vertex at (2, -3) and passing through the point (3, -1).
A) y = (x - 2)^2 - 3
B) y = (x - 2)^2 + 3
C) y = (x - 3)^2 - 1
D) y = 2(x - 2)^2 - 3
Correct Answer: D

Solution:
The equation of a parabola in vertex form is y = a(x - h)^2 + k. Using vertex (2, -3), we have y = a(x - 2)^2 - 3. To find a, substitute point (3, -1): -1 = a(3 - 2)^2 - 3. Solving gives a = 2, so the equation is y = 2(x - 2)^2 - 3.

Explanation:
This uses vertex form of a parabola. Ensure to check substitution thoroughly to find a.

Common mistake note:
A mistake might include incorrect substitution of point values, as choices are crafted to consider common errors.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 4 (4):
Write the quadratic equation with roots 2 and -4 and a leading coefficient of 1.
A) (x - 2)(x + 4)
B) x^2 + 2x - 8
C) x^2 - 2x - 8
D) x^2 - 2x + 8
Correct Answer: B

Solution:
A quadratic equation with roots p and q is given by (x - p)(x - q). Here, p = 2, q = -4, so the factored form is (x - 2)(x + 4). Expanding gives x^2 + 2x - 8.

Explanation:
The standard approach to write quadratic equations involves using the roots to form the factors, expanding to standard form.

Common mistake note:
Error arises from incorrect sign application to roots, leading to wrong factors.

Problem 5 (5):
Construct the equation for an exponential function passing through points (0, 1) and (1, 3).
A) y = 3^x
B) y = x^3
C) y = 2x + 1
D) y = 3x
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
The exponential function form is y = ab^x. Using (0, 1), we determine a = 1. With (1, 3), solve 3 = 1*b^1; hence b = 3. The equation becomes y = 3^x.

Explanation:
Points on the graph establish parameters a and b in the exponential equation.

Common mistake note:
Misinterpreting exponential form as linear is common, leading to incorrect choices like C or D.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 6 (6):
Formulate the equation of a horizontal line passing through point (4, -2).
A) y = -2
B) x = 4
C) y = 4
D) x = -2
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
Horizontal lines have the form y = k. Given it passes through (4, -2), k = -2, so the equation is y = -2.

Explanation:
A horizontal line requires y-equation consistent with the y-value of the passing point.

Common mistake note:
Confusing vertical with horizontal lines leads to x-based equations like B or D.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem 7 (7):
Write the equation of the line perpendicular to y = x + 1, passing through the origin.
A) y = -x
B) y = x
C) y = -x + 1
D) y = x - 1
Correct Answer: A

Solution:
A line perpendicular to y = x + 1 (slope 1) has a slope of -1. Since it passes through the origin, it is y = -1x or y = -x.

Explanation:
Perpendicular slopes are negative reciprocals. Substitute required point for the final equation.

Common mistake note:
Missing the perpendicular condition often leads to incorrect choices with the same slope (e.g., B).
